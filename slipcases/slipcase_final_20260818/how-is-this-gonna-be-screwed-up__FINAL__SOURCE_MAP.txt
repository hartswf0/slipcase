SOURCE MAP
How Is This Gonna Be Screwed Up? Patent Drafting and the Cultural Formation of Prompt Expertise
Final Working Paper - 2026-08-18

C1 - Prompt research often studies expertise from the point of interaction with the model, including brittleness, opportunistic prompt iteration, and human-instruction expectations.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED + INTERPRETATION
SOURCES: Zamfirescu-Pereira et al. 2023; Reynolds & McDonell 2021.
INFERENCE: Treating that interface-local frame as the whole causal history starts the clock too late.

C2 - Shambibble explicitly connected his early prompting facility to patent practice, abstract description, possible misinterpretation, and adversarial thinking.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED
SOURCE: Hartsoe-Shambibble interview, 2022, approx. 02:02-04:01.
INFERENCE: The paper names the transferable disposition a practiced suspicion of language.

C3 - Baxandall/Geertz provide a mechanism for cross-domain transfer: social practice can cultivate perceptual/discriminative capacities later used in art.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED + COMPILER SYNTHESIS
SOURCES: Baxandall 1972, especially gauging discussion; Geertz 1976.
INFERENCE: Patent drafting can be studied analogously as a training ground for distinctions that become newly consequential in prompting. This is not a genealogy or identity claim.

C4 - Patent doctrine institutionalizes external construction, amendment opportunity, and reasonable rather than absolute certainty.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED + INTERPRETATION
SOURCES: USPTO MPEP 2111; Nautilus v. Biosig; White v. Dunbar.
INFERENCE: These doctrines illuminate a transferable interpretive discipline without making models courts or prompts claims.

C5 - Shambibble's guide documents multimodal prompt practice rather than sentence-only prompt writing.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED
SOURCE: Shambibble, Image Prompting and-- (2022).
SUPPORT: image weights, multiprompting, cross-medium face banks, crude visual mockups, text and visual arguments, seed-controlled comparisons.

C6 - The guide trains comparative attention, not only prompt strings.
TYPE: COMPILER SYNTHESIS SUPPORTED BY SOURCE
SOURCES: Shambibble guide/interview; Goodwin 1994.
INFERENCE: Prompt expertise can be modeled as professional vision produced through contrasts, controls, highlighting, and representation.

C7 - Shambibble rejects confident causal folklore: users copy prompt junk; same-seed removal can test terms but interactions limit attribution; few Midjourney techniques are surefire.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED
SOURCE: Hartsoe-Shambibble interview, approx. 47:03-48:53 and 57:24 onward.
INFERENCE: Adversarial construction plus empirical revision = adversarial empiricism.

C8 - Prompt techniques can decay or become features.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED + SYNTHESIS
SOURCE: Hartsoe-Shambibble interview; Shambibble guide.
SUPPORT: workaround-to-button account; explicit expectation that techniques may mean nothing after model change.
INFERENCE: Durable expertise may lie in discrimination/diagnosis rather than syntax.

C9 - Professional transfer can also create fixation.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED + OPEN HYPOTHESIS
SOURCE: Bilalic, McLeod & Gobet 2008.
INFERENCE: Patent-trained attention may overprivilege wording when a problem requires another representation. Shambibble's multimodal practice is evidence against a simple lawyerly-prose account.

C10 - U.S. Copyright Office Part 2 says mere provision of prompts does not itself establish sufficient human authorship, while human-authored inputs, arrangement, and modifications may matter.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED
SOURCE: U.S. Copyright Office Part 2 (2025) and NewsNet 1060.
INFERENCE: "The prompt" can be too thin a factual unit for describing the human control sequence even where the legal conclusion remains noncopyrightability.

C11 - Long iteration alone does not establish control/authorship.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED + INTERPRETATION
SOURCE: U.S. Copyright Office Review Board, Theatre D'opera Spatial (2023).
INFERENCE: Prompt count is not a measure of controllable expressive causation.

C12 - Broader thesis: AI changes the adjacency map of expertise.
TYPE: COMPILER SYNTHESIS / CURRENT WAGER
SUPPORT: C2-C9.
LIMIT: One practitioner demonstrates a plausible mechanism, not population-level lawyer superiority. Comparative empirical tests are required.
