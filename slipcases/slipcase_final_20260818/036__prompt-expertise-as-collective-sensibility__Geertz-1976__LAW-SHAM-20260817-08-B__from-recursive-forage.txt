WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The statement “Shambibble was good at prompting because he was a patent attorney” may locate expertise in the wrong place.

The competence may exist partly in a temporary social system distributed across profession, platform, community, examples, interface, and model behavior.

QUESTION:
What portion of early prompt expertise came from prior professional formation, and what portion was produced collectively inside emerging prompt communities?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Could a generative platform develop something analogous to a “period eye” or “period ear”: a historically local sensitivity to distinctions that later model generations make obsolete?

MECHANISM:
Prior culture trains some distinctions.

Platform exposes new recurrent failures.

Community names and circulates those failures.

Practitioners compare results.

Techniques stabilize.

Shared examples train attention.

Practitioners begin noticing distinctions that newcomers do not.
