2026-08-17 — Prompt leadership may require speaking a practice-language one cannot personally enact.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
INTERACTIONAL EXPERTISE MAY BE THE MISSING BRIDGE BETWEEN LAWYER AND MACHINE.

LOCAL MOVE:
[[LAW-SHAM-20260817-08]] imagines the lawyer as an interpretive engineer.

But Shambibble presents a contradiction.

He repeatedly marks that he cannot build neural networks and does not possess deep programming competence.

Yet he reads papers, questions programmer friends, searches historical Discord discussions, develops working explanations, writes a manual, and diagnoses generative behavior.

The relevant expertise may therefore not be:

CAN BUILD THE SYSTEM.

It may be:

CAN PARTICIPATE COMPETENTLY IN THE LANGUAGE OF THOSE WHO BUILD AND USE THE SYSTEM.

This also sharpens Shambibble's own speculation that future creative work may require someone who can talk to an artist and a computer.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A person can become operationally important at the boundary between practices without possessing the contributory expertise of either side in full.

The prompt expert may be less a new specialist than a bilingual interactional expert.

QUESTION:
Is the emerging prompt-language expert distinguished by the ability to translate among practice-languages rather than by superior command of one technical domain?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Could lawyers become important in natural-language programming not because they are closest to computation, but because legal practice already trains participation across heterogeneous expert worlds?

MECHANISM:
Community A possesses contributory practice.

Outsider enters its discourse.

Extended linguistic immersion develops interactional expertise.

Outsider can understand distinctions, ask competent questions, and coordinate with practitioners without performing all underlying operations.

Boundary work then requires movement between multiple practice-languages.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Collins and Evans distinguish interactional expertise from contributory expertise.

Interactional expertise concerns competent understanding through immersion in a practice-language without necessarily possessing the practical ability to contribute directly to the underlying activity. ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.04423?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

OUR FORMALIZATION:

where:

The distinctive advantage may occur at the intersection rather than inside any one component.

TENSION:
Interactional expertise can explain how Shambibble talks competently about systems he cannot build.

It does not establish that linguistic competence is sufficient for controlling those systems.

Prompt execution itself may become a form of contributory practice rather than merely interactional expertise.

MISSING:
A distinction between:

talking competently about model behavior
diagnosing model behavior
reliably controlling model behavior
building the model.

These may represent different expertise classes.

BOUNDARY:
Collins and Evans develop interactional expertise primarily within studies of scientific expertise.

Applying it to prompt practice is [OUR INFERENCE].

TEST:

model engineers
domain experts
professional translators/boundary workers

Give them cross-domain agent tasks requiring coordination between a specialist user and a generative system.

Measure separately:

diagnosis of misunderstanding
successful reformulation
final task performance.

Test whether boundary performance is predicted more strongly by interactional competence than by technical construction skill.
