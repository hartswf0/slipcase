RESEARCH OBJECT:
VERNACULAR PRECEDENT.

LOCAL MOVE:
[[SHAM-20260817-05]] called the manual a bug ledger. The legal lineage sharpens the form of that ledger. PromptCraft does not begin with a complete theory of Midjourney and derive rules. It receives fact patterns: photorealistic does not produce photorealism; two subjects collapse; text will not spell; an alligator wears the wrong trench coat. Practitioners try constructions, compare outcomes, and circulate narrow holdings. Only afterward are recurring cases consolidated into FAQ entries and manuals.

This is common-law-like not because the community has courts, but because operational knowledge accumulates from disputed cases before it becomes doctrine.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The earliest prompt manual may be better understood as a reporter of decided bugs than as a textbook of first principles.

QUESTION:
How much of prompt expertise is generated by accumulated case comparison rather than explicit model theory?

DEEPER QUESTION:
When model updates change the interpreter, what happens to precedent? Does old prompt case law become overruled, merely distinguishable, or useful as evidence of durable mechanisms beneath obsolete syntax?

MECHANISM:
User presents failure fact pattern. Community reproduces or modifies it. A workaround becomes locally reliable. Similar cases recur. The workaround gains a name or example. Manual consolidates it. Platform may later absorb it into official syntax or a button. Model updates may invalidate the rule.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
The interview explicitly describes FAQs and the manual as responses to recurring user questions and specific problems. The 2022 external blog independently records the image-prompting guide as a community resource.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Prompt precedent has at least four statuses:

GOOD LAW — reproduces on current model/runtime
DISTINGUISHABLE — works only under narrower conditions
OVERRULED — model/interface change removes the behavior
CODIFIED — former workaround becomes official control

TENSION:
“Common law” is an analogy. Discord users do not create binding precedent, and frequency of a workaround does not establish causal truth. [[MJ-2022-004]] already shows how folklore can spread without ablation.

MISSING:
A dated corpus of PromptCraft threads, Discord posts, manual revisions, feature releases, and model-version changes sufficient to reconstruct actual precedence and overruling.

BOUNDARY:
The source supports a bug-led, example-driven community practice. The jurisprudential vocabulary is ours.
