TITLE:
2026-08-17 — The thing that travels between professions may be a discrimination, not a technique.

SOURCE:

PASSAGE:

RESEARCH OBJECT:
PORTABLE DISCRIMINATION.

LOCAL MOVE:
[[LAW-SHAM-20260817-08]] asks what professional practices already train people to author consequential language for interpreters they do not control.

Baxandall changes the unit of transfer.

The merchant does not transfer barrel-measuring PROCEDURE into painting.

What travels is a learned capacity to notice certain relations.

This sharpens the Shambibble case.

Patent drafting need not resemble prompting.

A patent attorney may carry into prompting a discrimination trained elsewhere:

WORD
→ POSSIBLE CONSTRUCTIONS
→ SCOPE DIFFERENCE
→ CONSEQUENCE.

The claim is therefore weaker than:

PATENT LAW CAUSES PROMPT EXPERTISE

and stronger than:

LAWYERS ARE GOOD WITH WORDS.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A profession may prepare someone for a technology that did not exist when the professional habit was acquired.

The transferable object need not be subject matter knowledge.

It can be a habitual distinction.

QUESTION:
Which discriminations cultivated in pre-AI professions become newly consequential when natural language acquires operational effects?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does generative AI reorganize the adjacency structure of expertise by making formerly remote habits of attention suddenly interoperable?

MECHANISM:
Repeated occupational problem
→ selective attention becomes practiced
→ distinction becomes easy to perceive
→ person enters a new domain
→ new domain contains structurally relevant differences
→ old discrimination becomes newly useful.

FORMAL SHIFT:
SKILL TRANSFER AS:

TECHNIQUE_A
→ TECHNIQUE_B

becomes

PRACTICE_A
→ TRAINED DISCRIMINATION_D
→ PRACTICE_B MAKES D CONSEQUENTIAL.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Baxandall's “period eye” concerns historically acquired perceptual equipment.

His merchant example connects commercial gauging with a practiced capacity to reduce irregular masses to combinations of regular forms.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

TRANSFER(P_A → P_B)

need not require:

OPERATION_A ≈ OPERATION_B.

It may require only:

DISTINCTION_D learned in P_A
AND
DISTINCTION_D consequential in P_B.

For Shambibble:

PATENT PRACTICE
→ ALTERNATIVE-CONSTRUCTION SENSITIVITY
→ GENERATIVE PROMPTING.

TENSION:
The analogy becomes misleading if it implies that patent drafting and prompting share a genealogy or mechanism.

Baxandall's argument concerns historically shared perceptual formation in fifteenth-century Italy.

The Shambibble comparison is [OUR INFERENCE], not evidence of direct historical continuity.

MISSING:
A comparative inventory of professions whose routine discriminations unexpectedly become useful in generative systems.

Possible cases remain hypotheses:

directors
programmers
lawyers
photographers

BOUNDARY:
Baxandall does not discuss AI, prompting, patents, or linguistic control.

The source supports the mechanism of practical experience cultivating portable perceptual distinctions, not the specific Shambibble analogy.

TEST:
Construct paired tasks from two unrelated domains.

For each profession, identify a discrimination repeatedly required in ordinary work before exposing participants to an unfamiliar generative-AI task.

Test whether professionals detect structurally analogous failures earlier than matched controls.

For patent attorneys specifically, measure detection of:

polysemy
scope ambiguity
unbound reference
unintended inclusion
unintended exclusion

without using legal vocabulary in the AI task.
