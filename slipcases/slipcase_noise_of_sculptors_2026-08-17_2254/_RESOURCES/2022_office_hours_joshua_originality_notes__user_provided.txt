Joshua [ARVR] — 10/12/22, 6:32 PM
Is that I don't think it's a solution we could have come to normally. And that's because we had early art that vaguely looked like this, but it just didn't seem like it'd be good enough. So we had to refine it a little away from the older direction.

And with this new style, really the only way it sold me is because it was finished. Whereas as I'm sure you know, the traditional creative process is iterative, starting with cruder representations and get more refined over time.

It showed me there exists a giant set of designs that don't seem like they'd work from the side of “thumbnail sketches” (whatever that means for a given creative domain), and only can be found through more finished exploration.

And with a normal process, most people just don't have the time to explore all these ideas to a finished state. And even if you did, it doesn't even really make sense! Why make something finished that you think wont work??

AI generation allows for more exploration, and its fast enough to give you an impression of something in a more finished way that you don't have to worry so much about what will or wont work. You can be more truly experimental, which leads to really cool places.
