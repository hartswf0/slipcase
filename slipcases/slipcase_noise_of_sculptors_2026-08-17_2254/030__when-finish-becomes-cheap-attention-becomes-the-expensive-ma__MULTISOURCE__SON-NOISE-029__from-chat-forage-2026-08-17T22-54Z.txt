SON-NOISE-029

TITLE:
WHEN FINISH BECOMES CHEAP, ATTENTION BECOMES THE EXPENSIVE MATERIAL.

SOURCE:
Sun, Zhoujun; Watson Hartsoe; Tommy Ottolin. “Sculptors of Noise: Control in Midjourney AI Art Community.” 2022; local PDF preserved in _RESOURCES/. SOURCE URL: NONE — user-provided local evidence.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The research team described entering the server as “chaotic,” “messy,” and “fast.” Hematita said work could be “washed away immediately”; the paper reports that more than 100 new images could appear in ten minutes in active generation channels. Elsewhere, users retreat into private threads and smaller groups so their work and conversations do not disappear in the flow.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
SCARCITY MIGRATION.

LOCAL MOVE:
Generative image systems reduce the cost of producing finished-looking representations.

Discord exposes the consequence at social scale.

Production ceases to be the scarce resource first.

Attention becomes scarce.

SOURCE TERMS:
chaotic
messy

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The platform can simultaneously expand creative possibility and destroy the conditions for noticing possibility.

The same abundance that makes Joshua’s finished exploration possible makes the newcomer lose their own image.

QUESTION:
What new institutions appear when production becomes cheaper faster than attention becomes cheaper?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does generative abundance change creative expertise from making rare objects toward constructing filters, archives, rooms, and stopping rules?

MECHANISM:
cheap generation
→ high-volume near-finished output
→ attention saturation
→ disappearance / weak memory
→ private threads, clubs, archives, spreadsheets, secrecy
→ new forms of selection infrastructure.

FORMAL SHIFT:
FROM:

SCARCE PRODUCTION
→ SELECT BEFORE MAKING

TO:

ABUNDANT PRODUCTION
→ MAKE BEFORE SELECTING
→ SCARCE ATTENTION.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

If generation cost G falls faster than evaluation cost E,

G ↓↓↓ while E ↓ or remains stable,

then candidate volume N rises until:

N × E > available human attention A.

The binding constraint moves from production to evaluation.

TENSION:
The public flood is not merely a defect.

It is also apprenticeship infrastructure: users see prompts, errors, styles, and other people’s branches.

Filtering the noise can destroy the serendipity that made the community generative.

MISSING:
Longitudinal measures of how much generated work is actually inspected, revisited, or reused.

Evidence on whether experts solve overload through better attention, smaller communities, stronger archives, or more aggressive deletion.

BOUNDARY:
The paper provides qualitative and descriptive evidence from one rapidly changing 2022 community. It does not establish a general economic law for all generative systems.

TEST:
For identical users, vary generated candidate volume while holding task and available review time constant.

Measure:

fraction inspected
fraction revisited
quality of selected final artifact
novelty of selected branch
memory for discarded branches.

Locate the point at which more generated possibility decreases discovered possibility.
