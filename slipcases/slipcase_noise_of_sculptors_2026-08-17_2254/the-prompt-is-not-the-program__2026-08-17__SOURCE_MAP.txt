COUNTEREVIDENCE / LIMITS PRESERVED
- Reflective artifact-driven reframing predates generative AI: [[PROMPT-PRACTICE-TALKBACK-006]].
- Incomplete executable specifications predate generative AI: [[PROMPT-PRACTICE-HOLES-004]].
- Human-in-the-loop subjective optimization predates prompt communities: [[SON-IEC-005]].
- Iterative oracle/counterexample synthesis predates LLM prompting: [[PROMPT-PRACTICE-ORACLE-007]].
- The original Midjourney paper contains proprietary-architecture claims that later cards correctly leave unresolved; the working paper does not rely on those claims.
- Not all prompt tasks need genealogical machinery; scope is strongest where specifications are incomplete, outputs generative, and failures legible enough to test.
