RESEARCH DECISION:
The previous thesis collapsed several distinct mechanisms into a tidy replacement unit (“execution lineage”). The revised paper begins from the field’s strongest surviving tension: whether user-visible language is actually the causal locus of system behavior. It retains rival mechanisms and builds discriminating tests rather than treating their coexistence as proof of one larger ontology.
