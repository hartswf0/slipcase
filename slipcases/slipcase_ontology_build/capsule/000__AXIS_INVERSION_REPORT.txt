AXIS INVERSION REPORT

THE NARCISSISM DELETED

1. Removed the old explanatory opening in which the paper patiently established a pipeline and announced a causal-attribution thesis.
2. Removed the “five fractures” survey structure as the governing intellectual form. The sources now enter only when they worsen the ontology problem.
3. Removed “the true unit is execution lineage” as a rescue. It stabilized the field too early.
4. Removed novelty/gap logic. The paper does not argue that prompts are undertheorized because nobody has studied them. The field is actively building ontologies, taxonomies, software-engineering lifecycles, and context frameworks.
5. Removed the implication that more complete provenance automatically solves the problem. More complete records can improve reproduction while leaving the identity of the operative object unresolved.

THE TARGET COMMUNITY

Prompt engineering, HCI, NLP, and software-engineering researchers who currently operationalize prompts as stable linguistic objects, programs, specifications, artifacts, templates, or independent variables.

The target is not individual authors. It is the community habit of letting the visible, versionable instruction inherit causal privilege because it is the part of the system easiest to archive and manipulate.

THE ERROR PIVOT

OLD GAP:
We need a better model of prompt execution.

NEW ERROR:
The field may be measuring a convenient inscription and attributing to it the causal identity of a situated execution event.

COST

If the error survives:
- prompt-sensitivity results can mislocate the intervention;
- version control can preserve prompt strings while losing behavioral conditions;
- reproducibility can reproduce typography rather than execution;
- authorship can be assigned to words the downstream model never received;
- “prompt evolution” can conflate artifact change with model/runtime change;
- prompt taxonomies can classify linguistic form while being silently recruited as causal ontology;
- context engineering can merely enlarge the same unstable noun.

TARGET OPENING MOVE

Confirm the community’s reality: prompt research now has mature taxonomies, datasets, programmatic frameworks, software-engineering agendas, and prompt ontologies.

Destabilize it immediately: the field is stabilizing the noun faster than it is stabilizing the identity conditions of the thing the noun names.

The rescuing move is deliberately incomplete: distinguish prompt-text from operative execution and make the boundary itself the research problem rather than proposing another totalizing unit.
