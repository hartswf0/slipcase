SOURCE MAP — THE VANISHING PROMPT

CLAIM C01
CLAIM: Prompt scholarship is actively stabilizing prompts as linguistic objects, structured discourse units, programs, and software artifacts.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED SYNTHESIS
ZETTEL: PROMPT-ONTOLOGY-001; PROMPT-ONTOLOGY-005; WORKWORDS-PROMPT-001
SOURCE: Basmov et al. 2026; Jeoung et al. 2026; Villamizar et al. 2025; Liang et al. 2025
CITEKEY: Basmov2026PromptsWild; Jeoung2026PromptPrism; Villamizar2025PromptsArtifacts; Liang2025PromptsPrograms
SUPPORT: Each source explicitly frames prompt objects in one of these ways.
INFERENCE: Their co-presence makes prompt objecthood itself available as a research tension.

CLAIM C02
CLAIM: The visible/stored prompt can be bounded and portable while remaining causally incomplete.
TYPE: COMPILER-SYNTHESIS
ZETTEL: PROMPT-ONTOLOGY-006
SOURCE: MULTISOURCE
CITEKEY: Basmov2026PromptsWild; Mei2025ContextEngineering; Lester2021PromptTuning; Suchman1987Plans
SUPPORT: Sources establish textual prompt ontology, dynamic context beyond static strings, nonlinguistic soft prompts, and the distinction between plans and situated action.
INFERENCE: “Boundedness–causality dilemma” is not source vocabulary; it is the paper’s current wager.

CLAIM C03
CLAIM: Context engineering explicitly rejects the model in which context is one monolithic static prompt string.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED
ZETTEL: PROMPT-ONTOLOGY-003
SOURCE: Mei et al. 2025
CITEKEY: Mei2025ContextEngineering
SUPPORT: The source formalizes historical C=prompt and replaces it with dynamically assembled contextual components.
INFERENCE: Enlarging context pressures object boundaries rather than automatically solving them.

CLAIM C04
CLAIM: Prompt tuning uses learned continuous conditioning objects called soft prompts rather than only discrete natural-language strings.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED
ZETTEL: PROMPT-ONTOLOGY-004; WORKWORDS-PROMPT-002
SOURCE: Lester et al. 2021; Qin & Eisner 2021
CITEKEY: Lester2021PromptTuning; QinEisner2021SoftPrompts
SUPPORT: Both papers explicitly define/train soft prompts or soft words.
INFERENCE: Textuality cannot be assumed as an inherent property of every technical object named “prompt.”

CLAIM C05
CLAIM: Prompt artifacts can be versionable while runtime behavior changes under other conditions.
TYPE: INTERPRETATION
ZETTEL: PROMPT-ONTOLOGY-005
SOURCE: Villamizar et al. 2025; Mei et al. 2025
CITEKEY: Villamizar2025PromptsArtifacts; Mei2025ContextEngineering
SUPPORT: Sources motivate artifact management and document runtime context beyond static prompt strings.
INFERENCE: artifact identity and causal identity should not be treated as automatically identical.

CLAIM C06
CLAIM: Adding explicit prompt requirements does not monotonically increase control.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED
ZETTEL: WORKWORDS-PROMPT-003
SOURCE: Yang et al. 2025/2026 revision
CITEKEY: Yang2025PromptsDontSay
SUPPORT: The study reports fragility of unspecified requirements and interference when more requirements are explicitly added.
INFERENCE: textual specification and effective behavioral constraint are distinct variables.

CLAIM C07
CLAIM: In diffusion models, requested semantics can have a temporal career and stochastic state can have structured generative effects.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED SYNTHESIS
ZETTEL: DEFAULT-IMAGES-CHI26-A-1; DEFAULT-IMAGES-CHI26-C-1
SOURCE: Agarwal et al. 2023; Mao et al. 2023
CITEKEY: Agarwal2023ASTAR; Mao2023InitialImageEditing
SUPPORT: A-STAR analyzes attention retention through denoising; Mao et al. intervene on initial latent blocks and find content preferences.
INFERENCE: final prompt-output correspondence can underdetermine where semantic control entered or disappeared.

CLAIM C08
CLAIM: Prompt scholarship risks a planning-model reconstruction in which an available instruction is substituted for situated execution.
TYPE: COMPILER-SYNTHESIS / HISTORICAL CROSSING
ZETTEL: PROMPT-ONTOLOGY-002
SOURCE: Suchman 1987
CITEKEY: Suchman1987Plans
SUPPORT: Suchman distinguishes plans from the contingent course of situated action.
INFERENCE: Applying this distinction to prompt research is the paper’s conceptual crossing; no claim of direct genealogy is made.

CLAIM C09
CLAIM: The field can manufacture causal certainty by allowing visible inscription to inherit causal privilege because it is easy to archive and manipulate.
TYPE: COMPILER-SYNTHESIS
ZETTEL: PROMPT-ONTOLOGY-006; PROMPT-ONTOLOGY-002; PROMPT-ONTOLOGY-005
SOURCE: MULTISOURCE
CITEKEY: Suchman1987Plans; Basmov2026PromptsWild; Villamizar2025PromptsArtifacts; Mei2025ContextEngineering
SUPPORT: Sources establish the archival/textual object, artifact management needs, situated-action warning, and wider runtime context.
INFERENCE: “manufacture of causal certainty” is the Axis Inversion of the field; it is the claim most exposed to opposition.

CLAIM C10
CLAIM: The paper does not establish that the prompt is unreal or epiphenomenal.
TYPE: BOUNDARY
ZETTEL: PROMPT-ONTOLOGY-006
SOURCE: MULTISOURCE
CITEKEY: Basmov2026PromptsWild; Jeoung2026PromptPrism; Yang2025PromptsDontSay
SUPPORT: Prompt text is demonstrably consequential and can be studied as language even if it is not a sufficient causal unit.
INFERENCE: The paper asks for more disciplined attribution, not abolition of prompt research.
