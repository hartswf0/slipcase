ARGUMENT

The paper reconstructs the persons, offices, artifacts, classifications, temporal arrangements, infrastructures, trained capacities, interfaces, and repair practices through which symbolic forms acquire a practical career. "Runtime" is an analytic question, not a claim that culture is software.
