OPEN EDGES

1. Does "as a cultural system" name an ontology or an analytic projection?
2. Which Geertzian mechanisms survive across religion, ideology, common sense, art, and law without being forced into one invariant engine?
3. What makes a public symbolic form operationally consequential rather than merely interpretable?
4. How should cultural analysis separate participant meaning from operational consequence without reviving crude functionalism?
5. When does a medium or infrastructure condition multiple systems rather than count as one system itself?
6. How much coordinated cultural action depends on shared meaning versus compatible interfaces among nonidentical local models?
7. Which failures reveal hidden organization, and which are artifacts created by the analytic probe?
8. When does repair restore a system, and when does it quietly create a different one?
9. What phenomena should refuse compilation into state, system, or operator form?
10. Can the runtime vocabulary produce discriminating empirical observations that thick description alone would miss? If not, discard it.
