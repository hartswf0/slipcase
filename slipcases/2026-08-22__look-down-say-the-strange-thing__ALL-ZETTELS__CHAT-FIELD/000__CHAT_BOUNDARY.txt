CHAT ARCHIVE BOUNDARY — 2026-08-22

This field preserves every conversation artifact that was available as a mounted file in the active ChatGPT runtime when this accession was assembled: all recovered Z-ENC zettels, whole-deck text, graph data, prompts/control files, raw pasted transcript/prompt files, source maps, paper sources, the latest paper PDF, and available screenshots.

A verbatim platform-level export of the entire ChatGPT message stream was not exposed to the assistant as a file or connector resource. Therefore this accession does NOT claim to be a byte-exact full-chat export. Earlier zettels whose exact original card payloads were unavailable remain explicitly marked RECONSTRUCTED. No such marker was removed.

The prior generated Slipcase ZIP checkpoints existed in the runtime and their names/sizes/SHA-256 values are recorded under _CHECKPOINTS/. Their paper and zettel text contents were recovered into this field. The binary checkpoint ZIPs themselves are not duplicated here because the GitHub connector used for this accession has text/blob payload constraints; the latest final paper PDF is included directly.

The research field remains resumable from the individual cards and sources in this folder.
