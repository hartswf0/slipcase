TITLE:
THE CONTROL/SUBMISSION BINARY FAILS: JOSHUA’S MASTERY IS ACTIVE ENOUGH TO PUSH THE SYSTEM AND OPEN ENOUGH TO LET THE RESULT CHANGE THE GOAL.

SOURCE:
MULTISOURCE: Joshua Larson interview with Watson Hartsoe, 2022-10-18; Joshua bugs-and-quirks screenshot supplied by Watson Hartsoe; Zhuangzi, Tian Dao, Wheelwright Pian passage, James Legge translation; Julianne Chung 2020. SOURCE URLs: LOCAL_FILE ; https://ctext.org/zhuangzi/tian-dao/zh ; https://psyche.co/ideas/to-be-creative-chinese-philosophy-teaches-us-to-abandon-originality

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE — JOSHUA]
“I just have to accept that I don’t have that level of control.”

[QUOTE — JOSHUA]
“the most inventive styles I’ve found have come from essentially bugs and quirks”

[PARAPHRASE — ZHUANGZI / CHUNG]
Wheelwright Pian’s skill depends on responsive adjustment that cannot be exhausted by verbal rules; Chung reads this as creativity through integration rather than imposed novelty.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
“CONTROL” IS TOO CRUDE A MEASURE OF EXPERTISE. JOSHUA DOES NOT SURRENDER TO MIDJOURNEY; HE WORKS IT AT EXTRAORDINARY VOLUME. BUT HE ALSO DOES NOT REQUIRE THE SYSTEM TO OBEY AN ANTECEDENT PLAN BEFORE AN OUTPUT CAN COUNT AS SUCCESS.

LOCAL MOVE:
The earlier paper called this “mastery without command.” The new evidence sharpens the claim. Joshua’s bugs-and-quirks remark gives noncontrol a positive creative function, while the Wheelwright Pian comparison distinguishes responsiveness from passivity. The missing concept is not mystical surrender but disciplined attunement: acting strongly enough to probe and shape a material while allowing resistance and deviation to update the next action and sometimes the goal itself.

SOURCE TERMS:
“don’t have that level of control” · “bugs and quirks” · “knack” · “integration” · “responsive” · “10,000 images” · “stumble upon”

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A practitioner can become more expert while becoming less committed to total control as the definition of expertise. That reversal matters because most interface metaphors treat a prompt as a command whose quality is measured by fidelity to prior intention.

QUESTION:
What should replace control as the measure of mastery when useful outcomes partly depend on differences the practitioner did not specify?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can creative agency be understood as the capacity to maintain a strong direction without granting the initial intention veto power over every valuable future?

MECHANISM:
PROVISIONAL INTENTION → ACTIVE PROBING → SYSTEM RETURNS RESISTANCE / VARIATION → EXPERT DISCRIMINATES → REVISES PROMPT OR REVISES GOAL → CONTINUES UNTIL A PROJECT-SCALE FIT EMERGES.

FORMAL SHIFT:
MASTERY = MAXIMUM FIDELITY TO ANTECEDENT INTENTION → MASTERY = CAPACITY TO STEER, DIAGNOSE, AND INTEGRATE UNDER PARTIAL NONCONTROL.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
[PARAPHRASE]
Joshua simultaneously reports extensive deliberate manipulation and limits to intentional lock-in. The Wheelwright Pian passage distinguishes skilled practical adjustment from fully transferable verbal instruction. Chung interprets the latter through integration rather than originality.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
SOVEREIGN CONTROL: VALUE(output) rises with conformity to fixed intention I0.
ATTUNED MASTERY: I_t+1 = UPDATE(I_t, output_t, project_constraints, expert_judgment).
The intention remains active but revisable.

TENSION:
“Attunement” can become flattering fog unless operationalized. It must predict observable differences: better anomaly recognition, better forecasts of search yield, better stopping decisions, better reproduction of useful quirks, and better integration into professional constraints. Otherwise “going with the flow” explains nothing.

MISSING:
Comparative evidence showing that expert practitioners revise goals more productively rather than merely more often; operational measures of attunement; and Joshua’s own judgment about whether this vocabulary fits his practice.

BOUNDARY:
The Zhuangzi comparison is analytic, not genealogical. Nothing here establishes that Joshua, David Holz, or Midjourney adopted Daoist philosophy as an ideology. The claim concerns a structural resemblance worth testing: mastery expressed through situated responsiveness rather than total command.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[JOSHUA-DEEP-011]] expertise first → [[JOSHUA-DEEP-012]] inventive bugs/quirks → [[JOSHUA-DEEP-013]] Wheelwright Pian’s untransferred knack → [[JOSHUA-DEEP-015]] novelty abundance and cultivation → replace control/submission binary with a testable account of attuned mastery.

TEST:
Design matched creative tasks where experts must choose after each generation among: PRESERVE GOAL / MODIFY PROMPT / MODIFY GOAL / SAVE ANOMALY / ABANDON BRANCH. Compare experts and novices on downstream project quality, search cost, anomaly recovery, and retrospective calibration. “Attuned mastery” earns explanatory value only if its decisions predict better downstream integration than either rigid goal fidelity or indiscriminate openness.
