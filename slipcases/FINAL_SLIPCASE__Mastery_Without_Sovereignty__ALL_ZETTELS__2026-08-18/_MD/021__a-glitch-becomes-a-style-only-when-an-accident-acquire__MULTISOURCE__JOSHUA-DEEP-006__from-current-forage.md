TITLE:
A GLITCH BECOMES A STYLE ONLY WHEN AN ACCIDENT ACQUIRES A LINEAGE.

SOURCE:

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE — JOSHUA, 23:58]
“it’s just a very, like, ephemeral you know, enigmatic thing that happened.”

[PARAPHRASE — CHANG ET AL.]
Some prolific text-to-image artists deliberately sought model glitches that could be developed into styles.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
THE BOUNDARY BETWEEN ANOMALY AND STYLE IS NOT NOVELTY; IT IS THE ABILITY TO STABILIZE A FAMILY OF RELATED DIFFERENCES.

LOCAL MOVE:
Joshua has an attractive anomaly he cannot recreate. Chang et al. report artists who turn glitches into styles. Put together, these cases expose a missing operation: domestication. The practitioner must transform a singular deviation into a repeatable region without eliminating the deviation that made it interesting.

SOURCE TERMS:
“ephemeral” · “enigmatic” · recreate · glitch · style · distinctive · stabilize

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Control can destroy the thing it seeks to preserve. If the anomalous output becomes fully normalized, it may cease to be a glitch; if it remains singular, it cannot function as a style.

QUESTION:
What minimum repeatability is required before a generative anomaly becomes a style rather than a lucky artifact?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can a prompt lineage preserve controlled instability—enough recurrence for authorship and reuse, enough variance for the anomaly to remain alive?

MECHANISM:
SINGULAR GLITCH → RECOGNIZE VALUED FEATURE → SEARCH LOCAL VARIATIONS / ANCESTRY → IDENTIFY INVARIANT FAMILY RESEMBLANCE → REPRODUCE WITHOUT FULL NORMALIZATION → STYLE.

FORMAL SHIFT:
STYLE AS STATIC VISUAL PROPERTY → STYLE AS REPRODUCIBLE DISTRIBUTION AROUND A VALUED ANOMALY.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
[PARAPHRASE]
Chang et al. report that some creators seek “glitches” in text-to-image systems and can turn them into artistic styles.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
A style S exists when samples from a recoverable procedure share invariant I above threshold τ while retaining nontrivial variation V. One-off anomaly has no recoverable procedure.

TENSION:
Too strict a reproducibility criterion privileges engineering control over artistic encounter; too loose a criterion makes every singular output a “style.”

MISSING:
Examples with preserved lineage showing how a practitioner moves from one glitch image to a stable family and which state variables are essential.

BOUNDARY:
Chang et al. establish that some artists cultivate glitches; they do not supply a universal threshold for when a glitch becomes a style.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[JOSHUA-DEEP-005]] → valuable but unreproducible accident → Chang et al. glitch-as-style practice → ask what operation converts anomaly into lineage.

TEST:
Present creators with a valued one-off anomaly and require generation of a 20-image family judged by outsiders for common identity and meaningful variation. Log the state variables and mutations needed to stabilize the family.

PLATFORM:
Text-to-image art · glitch practice · lineage
