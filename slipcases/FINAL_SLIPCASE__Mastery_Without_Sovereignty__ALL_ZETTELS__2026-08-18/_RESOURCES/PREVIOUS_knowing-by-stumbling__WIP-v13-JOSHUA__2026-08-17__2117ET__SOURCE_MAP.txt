PAPER SOURCE MAP — KNOWING BY STUMBLING

CLAIM 1
CLAIM: Joshua’s “spidey sense” can be operationalized as a forecast of prompt-region yield rather than a judgment of one sample.
TYPE: COMPILER-SYNTHESIS
ZETTEL: JOSHUA-DEEP-001
SOURCE: Larson interview; Pirolli & Card 1999
SUPPORT: Joshua predicts promising phrases from few samples; information-foraging theory formalizes value cues and patch allocation.
INFERENCE: Treating the intuition as forecast calibration is the paper’s synthesis.

TYPE: COMPILER-SYNTHESIS
ZETTEL: JOSHUA-DEEP-002
SOURCE: Larson interview; Pirolli & Card 1999

CLAIM 6

CLAIM 7

CLAIM 8
CLAIM: Prompt improvement may combine clearer intention with adaptation to a model-specific dialect.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED + INTERPRETATION
ZETTEL: JOSHUA-DEEP-009
SOURCE: Larson interview; Don-Yehiya et al. 2023

STRANGE STEPPING STONE
Prompt expertise is experimental instrument calibration under partial causal knowledge: the deepest skill is not wording alone but estimating yield, stopping, reproducibility, intervention validity, and drift. This proposition is not stated by a single source; it is the paper’s wager built across the eight claim families above.
