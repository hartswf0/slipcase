STRANGE STEPPING STONE
Mastery without command: in an opaque stochastic instrument, expertise may become most diagnostically valuable where it recognizes a persistent limit. The expert's admission of uncertainty is not automatically a deficit; once expertise is independently situated, calibrated noncontrol can reveal where the instrument's affordances end.
