RESEARCH OBJECT:
PROMPT COMPETENCE APPEARS FIRST AS A COMMUNITY PRACTICE BEFORE IT APPEARS AS A FORMAL TECHNIQUE.

LOCAL MOVE:
Joshua describes entering Midjourney through the newbie Discord channels, watching prompt/output pairs, and rapidly acquiring a practical sense of what words do. The language is learned in use, socially and experimentally.

SOURCE TERMS:
“language of prompting” · “community feed” · “newbie channels” · “hive mind” · “crafts person ship”

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A computational control language can emerge socially before its semantics are documented. Its first grammar may be a stream of examples rather than a manual.

QUESTION:
What is actually learned when a user “gets a feel” for prompting?

DEEPER QUESTION:
How much prompt language is model semantics, how much is community convention, and how much is socially transmitted superstition?
