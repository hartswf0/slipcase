TITLE:
The Prompt Artist Does Not Invent a Language Alone

SOURCE:
Minsuk Chang, Stefania Druga, Alex Fiannaca, Pedro Vergani, Chinmay Kulkarni, Carrie Cai, and Michael Terry, “The Prompt Artists” (2023).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.12253

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] Interviews, observations, and survey work with prolific text-to-image practitioners found prompt templates circulating as reusable style structures, artists seeking specialized vocabulary from external sources such as architecture writing, and practitioners converting model glitches into recognizable artistic styles.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Millière locates artistry in the intentional human operator.

Chang et al. make that operator difficult to keep singular.

The effective prompt is assembled from a distributed linguistic ecology:

other artists’ prompts
templates
architectural vocabulary
community experiments
documented failures
model glitches
borrowed descriptors
shared techniques

Prompt expertise is therefore not simply a person becoming fluent in speaking to a machine.

It is a community slowly building a vernacular around a machine.

LOCAL MOVE:
Move the unit of analysis from:

ARTIST ↔ MODEL

to:

COMMUNITY ↔ ARCHIVE OF EXPRESSIONS ↔ ARTIST ↔ MODEL

SOURCE TERMS:

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A model defect can become a human style.

This reverses the normal engineering relation.

In software engineering:

GLITCH → FIX

In artistic prompting:

GLITCH
→ recognition
→ repeatability
→ circulation
→ signature
→ STYLE

The machine’s failure can become the artist’s technique.

QUESTION:
When a community learns to reproduce an accidental model behavior and names it as a style, where is the artistic invention located?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Are prompt cultures best understood as authorship practices at all, or as emergent folk engineering around partially knowable technical systems?

MECHANISM:
[PARAPHRASE] Practitioners circulate reusable prompt templates, search other domains for unusually precise descriptive vocabulary, and discover recurring model behaviors that can be deliberately reincorporated into subsequent work.

FORMAL SHIFT:

→ PROMPT

SOURCE FORMALISM:
[PARAPHRASE]

The source identifies:
prompt + generated image as a possible joint art object;
prompt templates containing variable “slots”;
specialized vocabulary acquisition;
appropriation of model glitches as styles.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Let:

C = community
A = shared archive of discovered interventions
M = model
u = individual practitioner

C observes executions of M
→ deposits successful interventions into A
→ u samples/adapts from A
→ u executes M
→ novel effects return to C
→ A changes

Therefore:

PROMPT PRACTICE =
RECURSIVE CULTURAL LEARNING AROUND MODEL BEHAVIOR

not merely:

PERSON ISSUES COMMAND TO MODEL

TENSION:
Millière’s defense of AI art stresses individual skill and intentionality.

The source does not eliminate either, but it makes them porous.

Distinctive outputs may depend on communal vocabulary, inherited templates, undocumented model behavior, and other people’s discoveries.

The stronger the surrounding prompt culture becomes, the harder it is to attribute the resulting control entirely to an isolated artist.

MISSING:
We lack a genealogy of individual prompt fragments.

Which apparently personal artistic signatures were independently discovered?

Which were copied?

Which migrated from Discord, prompt repositories, technical documentation, architecture writing, photography jargon, or other practitioners?

BOUNDARY:
Evidence of communal practice does not imply that individual authorship disappears.

It establishes only that the relevant competence cannot be fully described without its social infrastructure.
