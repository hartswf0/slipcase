PAPER SOURCE MAP
TITLE: The Fill Point: Architecture, Evidence, and the YouTube Primitive Pool

CLAIM C1
CLAIM: Excavation alone cannot explain a stable subtractive architecture unless the substrate or an added system can preserve the remaining mass.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED + INTERPRETATION
ZETTEL: YPC-20260818-MANGELI-01; YPC-20260818-MANGELI-04
SOURCE: Mangeli et al. 2022
CITEKEY: mangeli2022rockcut
SUPPORT: Rock-cut architecture depends on settlement-context material, excavation technique, and a context hard enough not to fall apart.
INFERENCE: Soil-built YouTube structures therefore require empirical material characterization before their apparent monolith can be taken at face value.

CLAIM C2
CLAIM: Finished excavated geometry can preserve evidence of predecessor operations even when filming omits those operations.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED + COMPILER-SYNTHESIS
ZETTEL: YPC-20260818-MANGELI-02
SOURCE: Mangeli et al. 2022
CITEKEY: mangeli2022rockcut
SUPPORT: The authors reconstruct excavation process from architectural fabric in the absence of historical process documentation.
INFERENCE: A dependency graph can be used to identify operations that must have occurred before a finished YouTube structure could exist.

CLAIM C3
CLAIM: Filling a basin with water is a technical threshold because water makes composition, erosion resistance, cracking, and retention consequential.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED + COMPILER-SYNTHESIS
ZETTEL: YPC-20260818-NARLOCH-01; YPC-20260818-FILL-01
SOURCE: Narloch and Woyciechowski 2020
CITEKEY: narloch2020durability
SUPPORT: Durability assessment includes water erosion, wet/dry behavior, shrinkage, and stabilization.
INFERENCE: The genre's fill sequence should be treated as the handoff from visible excavation to a water-bearing construction system.

CLAIM C4
CLAIM: Completion cannot establish durability; temporal exposure produces additional architectural evidence.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED
ZETTEL: YPC-20260818-BUI-01; YPC-20260818-FILL-01
SOURCE: Bui et al. 2009
CITEKEY: bui2009durability
SUPPORT: The study evaluates erosion after twenty years of natural weathering.
INFERENCE: A final reveal is insufficient to distinguish durable earth construction from a short-lived set-like state.

CLAIM C5
CLAIM: The excavated pool should not be assumed to descend from Cambodian vernacular housing merely because production is located in Cambodia.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED + INTERPRETATION
ZETTEL: YPC-20260818-TAINTURIER-01
SOURCE: Tainturier 2006
CITEKEY: tainturier2006wood
SUPPORT: The Center for Khmer Studies documents wood building as a major Cambodian tradition.
INFERENCE: The genealogy of pit/pool/grotto forms remains open and requires comparison rather than geographic attribution.

CLAIM C6
CLAIM: Platform incentives may influence which buildable objects are selected, but evidence is insufficient to say that the algorithm directly designs the buildings.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED + OPEN-QUESTION
ZETTEL: YPC-20260818-GRUPA-01; YPC-20260818-HALLINAN-01
SOURCE: Grupa & Nalaskowski 2019; Hallinan 2023
CITEKEY: grupa2019youtube; hallinan2023nojudgment
SUPPORT: The first observes genre proliferation and viewer-attractiveness pressures; the second theorizes value optimization in YouTube cultural production.
INFERENCE: Architectural morphology may be exposed to platform selection, but the mechanism remains to be tested with chronological and performance data.
