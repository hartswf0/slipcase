TITLE:
THE REVEAL IS TOO EARLY TO PROVE THE BUILDING — DURABILITY PRODUCES EVIDENCE AFTER THE VIDEO ENDS

SOURCE:
Q. B. Bui, J.-C. Morel, B. V. Venkatarama Reddy, W. Ghayad — “Durability of rammed earth walls exposed for 20 years to natural weathering” — 2009.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The study evaluates stabilized and unstabilized rammed-earth walls after twenty years of natural weather exposure and measures erosion rather than treating initial completion as sufficient evidence of durability.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Architecture has delayed evidence.

Some claims about a building cannot be evaluated at completion.

They become answerable only after:
rain,
drying,
wetting,
erosion,
cracking,
occupation,
maintenance,
and time.

LOCAL MOVE:
Bui et al. turn weathering into an experiment.

SOURCE TERMS:
durability
natural weathering
erosion
stabilised
unstabilised
rammed earth

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The primitive-building video terminates at precisely the moment when one of architecture’s most informative experiments begins.

QUESTION:
What would these structures tell us if the camera returned after one rain, one month, one wet season, and one year?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is the genre producing buildings, or producing a state called “finished” whose duration is irrelevant to its function?

MECHANISM:
construction
→ reveal
→ [VIDEO ENDS]

but architectural performance continues:

reveal
→ rainfall
→ saturation
→ drying
→ biological growth
→ cracking
→ erosion
→ repair / abandonment

FORMAL SHIFT:
<COMPLETED FORM>
→ <TIME SERIES>
→ [WEATHER + USE]
→ <DURABILITY EVIDENCE>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

VIDEO COMPLETION ≠ ARCHITECTURAL SUCCESS

Instead:

ARCHITECTURAL SUCCESS(t)

must be evaluated over a nonzero interval.

TENSION:
The INPUT proposes that ordinary architecture asks how the building remains a building, while video architecture asks how it becomes visibly complete.

Bui et al. give that intuition a harder material criterion:

weathering changes what can be known.

MISSING:
repeat visits
post-rain footage
water loss
pool cracking
wall erosion
roof leakage
vegetation invasion
maintenance
reuse
abandonment date
demolition

BOUNDARY:
The source does not imply that short-lived structures are illegitimate architecture.

Nor does absence of longevity prove deception.

It establishes only that completion cannot evidence durability.

CITATION TRAIL:
post-occupancy evaluation
building pathology
earthen architecture weathering
temporary architecture
set construction
architectural maintenance studies

TEST:
Locate geospatially identifiable primitive-video sites.

Create an afterlife archive:
T0 final reveal
T1 first revisit
T2 wet season
T3 one year

Photogrammetrically compare geometry and erosion.

The disappearance of a wall is data.

So is repair.
