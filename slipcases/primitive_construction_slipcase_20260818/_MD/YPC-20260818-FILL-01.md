TITLE:
THE FILL IS THE HANDOFF FROM SCULPTURE TO INFRASTRUCTURE — THE VIDEO ENDS WHERE THE BUILDING MUST START PROVING ITSELF

SOURCE:
Piotr Narloch, Piotr Woyciechowski — “Assessing Cement Stabilized Rammed Earth Durability in A Humid Continental Climate” — 2020 — Abstract / Introduction; Q. B. Bui et al. — “Durability of rammed earth walls exposed for 20 years to natural weathering” — 2009 — Abstract / Introduction.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Narloch and Woyciechowski treat wetting, water erosion, shrinkage, and stabilization as measurable determinants of earthen-material durability. Bui and colleagues show that earthen construction acquires decisive evidence only through long exposure to weathering.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The act of filling a carved basin with water changes the epistemic status of the object.

Before filling, visible geometry can establish that a cavity exists.

After filling, the object makes claims about water retention, erosion resistance, cracking, drainage, and temporal performance.

LOCAL MOVE:
The two sources move architectural judgment away from appearance-at-completion and toward material behavior under exposure.

SOURCE TERMS:
durability
water erosion
wetting and drying
stabilization
natural weathering
erosion

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
In the video grammar, filling is the climax.

In building performance, filling is the beginning of a test.

QUESTION:
Why does the narrative endpoint coincide with the technical starting point?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is the genre’s characteristic architectural object best understood not as a completed building but as a completed image-state immediately before long-duration evidence can accumulate?

TENSION:
A temporary basin can perform perfectly well for a short filming interval. Durability is not a universal requirement for all architecture, and the sources do not establish what duration the creators intend.

MISSING:
water-loss measurements
post-fill duration
drainage route
membrane or render composition
hydrostatic conditions
maintenance
repeat footage
post-rain condition

BOUNDARY:
The sources concern rammed-earth material systems, not excavated YouTube pools. They establish why water exposure changes what must be measured; they do not establish concealed construction in any particular video.

TEST:
For a sample of twenty pool videos, timestamp the first moment of filling and record how many minutes or days of post-fill evidence appear. Revisit any identifiable site after rainfall or seasonal change. Compare surface condition, water level, cracking, erosion, and repair.
