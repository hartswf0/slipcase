TITLE:
SECTION MAY PRECEDE SPECTACLE — THE DESCENT SEQUENCE COULD COME FROM EXCAVATION BEFORE IT COMES FROM CAMERA GRAMMAR

SOURCE:
Mohammad Mangeli, Farshid Aram, Reza Abouei, Fatemeh Mehdizadeh Saradj — “A New Look at Excavation Techniques and Design of Rock-Cut Architectures” — 2022.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The study distinguishes excavation procedures according to whether construction begins from relatively horizontal terrain or from sloping rock faces, and reconstructs successive transitions from exterior terrain toward semi-open and enclosed spaces.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The INPUT makes a strong observation:

surface
→ descent
→ reveal
→ deeper descent
→ water

and proposes that the section may be the genre’s privileged architectural representation.

But there are two rival explanations.

READING A:
The section evolves because it performs well visually.

READING B:
The section emerges because excavation itself is a sectional operation, and the camera inherits that construction logic.

LOCAL MOVE:
Mangeli et al. make topography and excavation direction causally prior to certain spatial organizations.

SOURCE TERMS:
horizontal context
steep surface
excavation
semi-open space
entrance
room

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
“Camera-oriented architecture” may be partly true without the camera originating the form.

QUESTION:
Do primitive-building videos become sectional because YouTube rewards sectional reveals, or because subtractive construction naturally produces depth sequences that cameras later exploit?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Which formal features of the genre are platform-selected and which are material affordances that pre-existed their platform usefulness?

MECHANISM:
EXCAVATION:
surface
→ cut
→ face
→ entrance
→ cavity

CAMERA:
surface
→ approach
→ reveal
→ interior

The two sequences may coincide.

That coincidence could make excavation unusually compatible with transformation video.

A formal trait need not be invented by the platform to become amplified by it.

TENSION:
The INPUT argues that construction substitutes for architectural representation: the viewer learns the structure through successive operations rather than from drawings.

That may describe something materially specific to excavated architecture rather than a general YouTube convention.

MISSING:
comparison corpus of above-ground primitive builds
comparison corpus of non-YouTube excavated dwellings
camera-position history within channels
whether sectional complexity increased after audience growth

BOUNDARY:
Mangeli et al. provide no evidence about YouTube aesthetics or recommendation systems.

They only establish a plausible non-platform mechanism for sectional form.

CITATION TRAIL:

TEST:
Code 100 videos:

50 additive/frame-based structures
50 subtractive/excavated structures.

Measure:
number of descents,
depth reveals,
nested levels,
camera elevations,
final overhead shots,
and sectional complexity.

If sectional reveal strongly tracks excavation regardless of channel performance, material process is doing more explanatory work than the platform-only account predicts.
