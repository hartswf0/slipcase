TITLE:
WATER MAKES THE INVISIBLE MIXTURE MATTER — A POOL IS A MATERIAL TEST DISGUISED AS A REVEAL

SOURCE:
Piotr Narloch, Piotr Woyciechowski — “Assessing Cement Stabilized Rammed Earth Durability in A Humid Continental Climate” — 2020 — Abstract / introduction.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The authors assess earthen material through wet/dry strength, shrinkage, pressurized-water erosion, cyclic wetting and drying, and other durability tests. They emphasize that composition and stabilization alter performance under exposure.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The moment a primitive-video excavation becomes a pool, “earth” stops being an adequate material description.

Water makes composition consequential.

LOCAL MOVE:
Narloch and Woyciechowski replace the category EARTH with measurable mixture variables and exposure-dependent performance.

SOURCE TERMS:
cement-stabilized rammed earth
soil mixture
stabilizer
aggregate
durability
water erosion
cyclic wetting and drying

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The visually least dramatic operation may be the most architecturally consequential:

whatever happens to the surface immediately before water enters.

QUESTION:
What is the water-contact layer of a primitive-video pool actually made of?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Could the apparent monolith be structurally composite even when no joint is visually legible?

MECHANISM:
SUBSTRATE
→ trim
→ wet coating / render / stabilizer
→ curing
→ water exposure

If the coating dramatically changes:
permeability,
erosion resistance,
surface cohesion,
or crack behavior,

then the finished pool is not simply “carved earth.”

FORMAL SHIFT:
<VISIBLE BROWN SURFACE>
→ <MATERIAL COMPOSITION>
→ [EXPOSE TO WATER]
→ <SURVIVES / ERODES / LEAKS / SOFTENS>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
The source uses laboratory durability tests rather than an architectural generative formalism.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

POOL CLAIM
=
GEOMETRY
+
WATER-RETENTION SYSTEM

A cavity without the second term is a pit, not yet a durable pool.

TENSION:
The INPUT correctly isolates “surface technology” as a third layer after bulk geometry and fine geometry, and asks whether the skin could be clay slip, clay-rich render, stabilized earth, cement plaster, or a composite.

But “surface technology” may understate its role.

The surface may be what makes the object operationally a pool at all.

MISSING:
application thickness
material color before/after curing
cement fraction
lime
aggregate
clay fraction
compaction
membrane
curing interval
water-loss rate
erosion after filling

BOUNDARY:
Rammed earth compacted in formwork is not the same construction system as an excavated pool wall.

The source supplies durability variables and mechanisms, not a diagnosis of the videos.

CITATION TRAIL:
water damage in earthen structures
soil-cement stabilization
pool renders
hydraulic lime
lateritic soils
earthen plaster permeability

TEST:
For one channel, create a frame-by-frame “surface atlas.”

Record the last visible state of every pool wall before filling.

Then compare:
dry color,
wet color,
texture,
tool marks,
crack closure,
edge thickness,
and runoff behavior.

Search especially for discontinuities where a coating terminates.
