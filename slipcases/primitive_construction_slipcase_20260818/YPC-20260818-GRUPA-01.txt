TITLE:
THE GENRE DID NOT MERELY COPY A PERFORMANCE STYLE — IT BEGAN SELECTING BUILDABLE OBJECTS FOR ATTRACTIVENESS

SOURCE:
Małgorzata Grupa, Filip Nalaskowski — “‘YouTube Cavemen.’ The phenomenon of channels presenting technological development in ‘prehistory’” — 2019 — pp. 129–135.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Grupa and Nalaskowski identify a rapid proliferation of related channels, many associated with Vietnam, Cambodia, Thailand, and Bangladesh. Their survey finds shelters in multiple forms, various technical demonstrations, and even objects made from simple cement mixtures. They argue that attractiveness to viewers appears to influence selection of content and that veracity is not the overriding criterion.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
“Viewer attractiveness” may select not only which videos get made.

It may select which architectures get built.

LOCAL MOVE:
The paper treats attractiveness as a criterion operating on content.

The architectural question is whether that criterion migrates downward into morphology.

QUESTION:
Can audience optimization become an architectural design force?

DEEPER QUESTION:

TENSION:
The source does not demonstrate such a loop.

Its unit of observation remains channel content.

The INPUT pushes further by identifying recurring pools, moats, slides, terraces, stairs, niches, sculpted furniture, and underground rooms as an architectural family.

BOUNDARY:
