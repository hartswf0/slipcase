TITLE:
The real finishing move is the reversal that changes what the fight was about.

SOURCE:
Roland Barthes — “The World of Wrestling” — Mythologies — Hill and Wang, 1972. Scan pp. 22–24. fileciteturn5file0L6-L7

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]

Barthes emphasizes reversal, compensation, exaggeration, and the progressive solidification of signs. Wrestling does not merely accumulate technically successful moves. The sequence repeatedly transforms the meaning of what has already happened until the final action makes the whole moral pattern legible.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The present paper accumulates evidence toward:

CAPABILITY IS DISTRIBUTED.

A SmackDown paper needs something harder.

Each section must land an argument that appears decisive.

Then the next source must change what that victory meant.

The paper should therefore be organized by REVERSALS rather than TOPICS.

LOCAL MOVE:
Replace topical sections with booked matches.

MATCH 1:
THE MODEL HAS THE CAPABILITY
vs
PROMPT SENSITIVITY.

MATCH 2:
THE PROMPT ELICITS THE CAPABILITY
vs
OPERATOR DEPENDENCE.

MATCH 3:
THE COUPLED SYSTEM HAS THE CAPABILITY
vs
PORTABILITY.

MATCH 4:
THE BENCHMARK MEASURES THE SYSTEM
vs
PERFORMATIVE EVALUATION.

MATCH 5:
THE SCORE DECIDES
vs
GOODHART + CROWD JUSTICE.

MAIN EVENT:
IF EVERYTHING IS IN THE CONTEST,
WHAT STILL BELONGS TO THE MODEL?

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The evidence does not simply strengthen one thesis.

It repeatedly destroys the thesis’s current unit of analysis.

MODEL
fails.

PROMPT-MODEL
fails.

OPERATOR-MODEL
fails.

COUPLED SYSTEM
may fail.

EVALUATION ECOLOGY
may become too large to evaluate.

The paper’s intellectual motion is therefore not accumulation.

It is a sequence of forced enlargements followed by boundary crises.

QUESTION:
What if the actual argument of the paper is not that capability is distributed but that every stable location proposed for capability breaks under adversarial inspection?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is “capability” itself the object that should survive the paper, or is the paper converging on evidence that capability attribution is an operation performed by an evaluator?

MECHANISM:
A candidate locus of capability is proposed.

Evidence exposes a dependency outside that locus.

The boundary expands.

Expansion solves one anomaly but produces a new attribution problem.

The new boundary is attacked.

The sequence continues until either:

a stable attribution criterion survives

or

“capability location” is replaced by another research object.

FORMAL SHIFT:
M
→ M+P
→ M+P+O
→ COUPLED SYSTEM
→ ELICITATION ECOLOGY
→ ?

Each arrow is not elaboration.

Each arrow is a defeat.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

For candidate capability locus S_n:

Claim_n:
Capability ∈ S_n

Adversarial evidence discovers dependency d_n outside S_n.

Then:

S_{n+1} = S_n ∪ {d_n}

But expansion incurs attribution cost:

A(S_{n+1}) > A(S_n)

where A represents difficulty of saying what entity actually possesses the capability.

The research question becomes whether this recursion converges.

TENSION:
The current paper wants the elicitation ecology to be the answer.

The stronger paper may need to let the ecology become merely the next wrestler thrown into the ring.

Perhaps the ecology survives.

Perhaps it does not.

MISSING:
The next opponent.

Candidates:

interventionist causal attribution
mechanistic localization
organizational responsibility
user-relative affordance
task-relative competence
capability as counterfactual relation.

BOUNDARY:
Barthes supplies an account of dramatic reversals and intelligible spectacle.

The proposed sequence of theoretical defeats is [OUR FORMALIZATION], not his argument.
