TITLE:
The WWE version of the paper needs absolutes, but the research cannot afford to believe them.

SOURCE:
Roland Barthes — “The World of Wrestling” — Mythologies — Hill and Wang, 1972. Scan pp. 18, 24. fileciteturn5file0L4-L7

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]

Barthes repeatedly values the excessive clarity of wrestling’s signs. Meanings are externalized, amplified, and made immediately readable. Near the essay’s conclusion he describes a world in which actions are presented exhaustively and ambiguity is driven from the spectacle so that moral categories become momentarily unmistakable.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The current paper’s central claim is difficult precisely because it is responsible:

capability is distributed
elicitation matters
benchmarks adapt
operators matter
measurement intervenes
attribution is unstable.

Scientifically, this complexity is the point.

Rhetorically, it has no finishing move.

Barthes suggests the transformation required for a genuine SmackDown paper:

the argument must temporarily become absolute enough to fight.

LOCAL MOVE:
Create a strict separation between:

RING CLAIM

and:

SOURCE MAP.

The prose may stage a hard proposition.

The evidence apparatus must preserve every qualification capable of breaking it.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Nuance can become a form of invisibility.

If every claim immediately carries all of its qualifications, no proposition remains solid enough for the reader to test.

But if the paper suppresses uncertainty to gain force, it converts research into theatre.

The problem is not choosing one.

It is engineering their separation.

QUESTION:
Can a research paper make claims with the absolute clarity of a wrestling gesture while retaining falsifiability underneath them?

DEEPER QUESTION:
What if the scholarly paragraph and the evidentiary apparatus should operate at deliberately different levels of resolution?

MECHANISM:
The main prose states the strongest defensible claim.

The opposing claim is given equally concrete form.

Evidence forces a collision.

Qualifications do not dissolve the clash beforehand.

Instead, they determine what survives after impact.

The source map preserves the conditional structure beneath the rhetorical surface.

TENSION:
Barthesian spectacle gains force by eliminating ambiguity.

Research gains credibility by preserving ambiguity where evidence does not discriminate.

The paper becomes WWE only if it can stage absolutes without confusing performance of a claim with certainty of the claim.
