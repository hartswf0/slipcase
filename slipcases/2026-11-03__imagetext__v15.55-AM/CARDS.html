TITLE:

SOURCE:

§§2.1–2.3, Figure 3, §7

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] In the spectral setting, the learned directions

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The rule for pairing observations as a prior about which
differences matter.

LOCAL MOVE:
The proof rewrites each modality’s learning objective on the
same state space.

SOURCE TERMS:
Positive-pair distribution, world kernel, injective
rendering, spectral gap.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
[OUR INFERENCE] On Flickr, descriptions are grouped by
photograph. The photograph supplies an address for the scene

photographed event instead would change the learning problem.

QUESTION:

image?

MECHANISM:

FORMAL SHIFT:
<Observations> → <weighted edges between states> → [spectral

SOURCE FORMALISM:

RESEARCH OBJECT:
A split between geometry that makes captioning possible and a

LOCAL MOVE:

candidate selection procedure.

SOURCE TERMS:

minimum Bayes risk, CIDEr.

[OUR INFERENCE] The caption that most resembles other
plausible captions can win even when a rarer caption

QUESTION:

DEEPER QUESTION:

MECHANISM:

BOUNDARY:
