ID: AP-10
TITLE: Recognizing a new frame and acting under it can come apart

SOURCE: Michael Newman — Art as ‘Operation’ — 2026 — concluding discussion in the supplied transcript. Johnson et al. — Imagining and Building Wise Machines — 2026 — independent citation trail on metacognition, coauthored by Melanie Mitchell.

PASSAGE: [QUOTE, supplied transcript] “AI can produce metacognitive talk from its training, but lacks metacognitive control.” Newman attributes this distinction to Mitchell. The quoted wording is from Newman’s talk.

RESEARCH OBJECT: An observable change in what a system does after it receives evidence that a different frame applies.

LOCAL MOVE: Newman moves from failures of refusal toward a question about changing perspectives and selecting among models of a situation.

SOURCE TERMS: “metacognitive talk,” “metacognitive control,” code switching, context adaptability, meta-relation.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: A system can explain a distinction convincingly and continue to act under the wrong rule. It can also act under the appropriate rule without giving a convincing explanation. A fluent transcript can conceal either pattern.

QUESTION: What would show that a system can revise the frame governing its behavior when the evidence for the original frame no longer holds?

DEEPER QUESTION: In code switching, a person may understand which norm applies and decline to follow it. How could a test distinguish perspective taking from compliance?

MECHANISM: Evidence about provenance, authority, or purpose changes. A system interprets what that change means, selects or retains a rule, and acts. Those stages can be evaluated separately.

FORMAL SHIFT: <same utterance under changed circumstances> → <evidence about provenance and authority> → [retain or revise the governing rule] → <changed or stable action>

SOURCE FORMALISM: Newman proposes the distinction and cites examples. The transcript supplies no operational test or verified citation for the precise claim he attributes to Mitchell.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Give the same sentence two roles, a quoted specimen and a live request. Change credible role evidence while holding the sentence constant. Score the action separately from the explanation and check whether the system can reverse its choice when the evidence changes again.

TENSION: A system that passes may be applying a learned provenance cue. Transfer to unfamiliar kinds of evidence would support a broader interpretation. The talk’s question about social stakes and corporeality would still require a different kind of evidence.

MISSING: The criterion that separates local rule switching, a general ability to reconsider a frame, and the social capacity to contest who gets to set it.

BOUNDARY: The proposed test can establish behavioral sensitivity to a change of frame in specified conditions. It leaves the question of a fundamental human–machine boundary open.

CITATION TRAIL: Johnson et al. for candidate definitions of metacognition. Return to the symposium materials to identify the exact Mitchell source behind Newman’s attribution.

TEST: Run one harmless task with three steps: frame A, credible evidence for frame B, and a correction restoring A. Score the action at each step, then repeat with a different kind of provenance evidence. A polished explanation with an unchanged action separates two capacities the talk puts under pressure.
