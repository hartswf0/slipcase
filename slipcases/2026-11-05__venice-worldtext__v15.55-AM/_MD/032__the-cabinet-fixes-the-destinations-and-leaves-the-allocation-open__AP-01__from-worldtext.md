ID: AP-01
TITLE: The cabinet fixes the destinations and leaves the allocation open

SOURCE: Michael Newman — Art as ‘Operation’: from Lovelace and Mallarmé to Adversarial Poetry — 2026 — discussion of Le Livre in the supplied transcript. Ca’ Foscari’s program verifies the attribution and date.

PASSAGE: [QUOTE, supplied transcript] “which shelves the pages are to be placed on would be decided by a personage.” Newman says the shelf count and the number of pages per shelf are fixed.

RESEARCH OBJECT: The rule that assigns a page to a shelf.

LOCAL MOVE: Newman reads the cabinet, pages, operator, and attendees as an apparatus that brings the book into existence through performance.

SOURCE TERMS: Le Lecteur, L’Opérateur, chance, “total format.”

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: A fixed number of destinations and places per destination leaves open how anyone chooses among eligible destinations. The gap is precisely where the reader becomes an operator.

QUESTION: Does the operator draw lots, exercise judgment, or follow a sequencing rule that Newman has compressed into “chance”?

DEEPER QUESTION: If two allocation rules produce the same arrangement, do they realize the same book?

MECHANISM: The cabinet limits the possible allocations. The operator assigns pages within those limits. The significance of the resulting placement still depends on what the performance does with it.

FORMAL SHIFT: <loose pages> → <pages eligible for placement in fixed-capacity slots> → [allocate under constraints] → <a realized arrangement>

SOURCE FORMALISM: Newman reports quantities and roles. The talk supplies no sampling rule.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Model shelf capacity and the rule for selecting the next shelf as independent variables.

TENSION: Newman likens the cabinet to a Wooton filing desk. Filing ordinarily preserves an address for retrieval. The proposed performance may use placement to create a sequence for reading. The same compartments could support either operation.

MISSING: A manuscript instruction specifying how placement is chosen and what placement does to reading order. Newman’s Wooton date also needs checking against the specific model: the cited secretary design was patented in 1874.

BOUNDARY: The comparison establishes a functional resemblance worth testing. Direct influence from Wooton, Babbage, or Lovelace remains unverified.

CITATION TRAIL: Scherer’s 1957 edition of the Livre materials, as catalogued by the BnF. Wooton’s U.S. Patent 155,604, especially its specification for the compartments.

TEST: Find one manuscript leaf that permits two legal placements. Check whether an adjacent instruction specifies how the operator chooses between them and whether that choice changes the performance.
