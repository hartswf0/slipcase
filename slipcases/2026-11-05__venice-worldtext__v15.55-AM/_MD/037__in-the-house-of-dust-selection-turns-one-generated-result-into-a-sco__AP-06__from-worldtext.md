ID: AP-06
TITLE: In The House of Dust, selection turns one generated result into a score

SOURCE: Michael Newman — Art as ‘Operation’ — 2026 — Knowles discussion in the supplied transcript. BAMPFA exhibition materials and the CalArts account supply the four fields, James Tenney’s role, and the selection of one quatrain for construction.

PASSAGE: [PARAPHRASE] Knowles and Tenney generated quatrains from lists of materials, sites, light sources, and inhabitants. One quatrain became the basis of a public structure that hosted further work.

RESEARCH OBJECT: The decision to give a sampled line or stanza a new status.

LOCAL MOVE: Newman follows a generated poem into a structure and the social activities it accommodates.

SOURCE TERMS: operation, conviviality, open structure. The BAMPFA account describes the selected quatrain as a score.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: “Inhabitants” begins as a field in a vocabulary list. After construction, people can inhabit the structure, organize events, contest its presence, or leave. The building gives the word consequences the list did not have to specify.

QUESTION: Did the selected quatrain prescribe the built work, or did its selection authorize a new design process?

MECHANISM: Compile lists → generate quatrains → select one → interpret it as a design brief → build a structure → open it to further activity. The transition from sampling to selection changes who can act on the work.

FORMAL SHIFT: <lists of phrases> → <generated quatrain> → [select and interpret as a score] → <site for participation>

SOURCE FORMALISM: Four ordered fields with random selection are documented. The sources consulted do not specify the random-number procedure or a translation algorithm from verse to building.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Follow each line through the stages and mark when it functions as a descriptor, a constraint, an affordance, or an invitation.

TENSION: The CalArts account describes both opposition at the New York site and later performances at CalArts. Conviviality was a possible outcome of the social setting. It was never guaranteed by the generated text.

MISSING: A record of why this quatrain was selected and what the builders regarded as binding in each line.

BOUNDARY: The quatrain’s role as a score is documented. A one-to-one correspondence between its fields and the installation’s features remains to be established.

CITATION TRAIL: Compare the surviving printout, construction records, and evidence of activations at the two sites. Check the programming history for Tenney’s role before assigning the computational work.

TEST: Choose the light-source line. Find a drawing or installation description and determine whether the line prescribed lighting, suggested a sensor input, or acquired a different function during design.
