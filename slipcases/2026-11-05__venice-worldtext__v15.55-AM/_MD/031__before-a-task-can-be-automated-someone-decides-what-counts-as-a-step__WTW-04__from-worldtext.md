ID: WTW-04
TITLE: Before a task can be automated, someone decides what counts as a step
SOURCE: ERC AIMODELS, “The Culture of Algorithmic Models: Advancing the Historical Epistemology of Artificial Intelligence,” project homepage, Ca’ Foscari University of Venice, 2024–2028.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] The project links automation to earlier practices of formalizing and measuring labor, language, knowledge, and social relations. Its Babbage example connects the organization of mental work to the design of calculating machinery.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
A division of work that becomes a division of operations in a machine.

LOCAL MOVE:
AIMODELS traces models of intelligence through the practices that made work countable, divisible, and comparable.

SOURCE TERMS:
Formalisation, measurement, division of labour, models of intelligence, historical epistemology.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A prompt can appear to begin the operation even when the available actions and measures of success were established long before anyone wrote it.

QUESTION:
Which parts of a supposedly new linguistic operation inherit a prior division of labor, and which parts reorganize it?

MECHANISM:
A practice is divided into tasks. Some distinctions become records, measures, or instructions. A technical system can execute the represented steps while other decisions remain with operators, editors, or evaluators.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<organized work> → <named and measurable tasks> → [encode selected steps and criteria] → <executable operation with remaining human decisions>.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE. The homepage proposes a historical research programme.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] For a single operation, trace who supplies the input, defines a successful result, and repairs exceptions before and after automation.

TENSION:
A. The machine inherits a preexisting organization of work.
B. The machine’s requirements produce new tasks and new divisions of responsibility.

MISSING:
An artifact-level chain connecting a specific work instruction or measurement practice to a specific machine operation.

BOUNDARY:
The homepage motivates the genealogy. Each historical connection still needs documentary evidence of what traveled and through which artifact.

CITATION TRAIL:
Follow the project’s Babbage references and its account of Peter Damerow. Within the conference record, trace the move from language automation at Chat Token Vector (2025) to poetry as a way of probing linguistic operations at Adversarial Poetry (2026).

TEST:
Choose one calculation in the Babbage case. Compare the human work instructions, recorded intermediate results, and corresponding machine procedure. Mark every decision that survives, disappears, or changes hands.
