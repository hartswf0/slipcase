Claim-to-source map • 5 November 2026

Read the map against the manuscript’s section numbers. AP and OD are the
research lineage identifiers from the originating conversation. The cited
sources and locators are given directly here so the map also works when
detached from that conversation. COMPILER marks a new interpretive or design
claim composed for this paper. It carries no independent evidentiary weight.
