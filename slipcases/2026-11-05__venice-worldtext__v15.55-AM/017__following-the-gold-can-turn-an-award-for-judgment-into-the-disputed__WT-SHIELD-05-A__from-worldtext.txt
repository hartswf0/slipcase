TITLE:
Following the gold can turn an award for judgment into the disputed payment

SOURCE:
Homer — Iliad 18.497–508 — translated by A. T. Murray
https://www.theoi.com/Text/HomerIliad18.html
OpenTimelineIO — Writing an OTIO Adapter — “Source Range vs Available Range”
https://opentimelineio.readthedocs.io/en/latest/tutorials/write-an-adapter.html

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE — Murray] “two talents of gold”
[PARAPHRASE — Murray] The hearing concerns blood-price. The passage later describes the gold in connection with whoever gives the most just judgment.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
A close-up of gold inserted immediately after a disputant asserts that he paid.

LOCAL MOVE:
Murray’s rendering gives the disputed payment and the gold in the assembly different roles. An edit can place the image of one alongside words about the other.

SOURCE TERMS:
Blood-price, gold, judgment, source range, clip occurrence.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
[OUR INFERENCE] The parent’s frown acquires a new apparent cause through a cut. Here the cut does something different. It can make the gold appear to be the very payment under dispute. Two things can become one referent without the script ever asserting their identity.

QUESTION:
How can a videotext expose an identity claim made by an edit?

DEEPER QUESTION:
When a text and image appear together, what leads readers to infer “this is what the speaker means” rather than “this is another thing of the same kind”?

MECHANISM:
A spoken reference leaves room for a visual referent. An inserted shot offers a candidate at the moment the viewer is looking for one. Repositioning the same shot can change which proposition it seems to illustrate.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<Speech about payment and image of gold> → <Separate audio and image intervals> → [Align the insert to a line] → <An inferred identity between visible gold and the payment>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
OpenTimelineIO represents a clip’s selected range separately from its referenced media. It supplies no formal semantics for what an insert appears to identify.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Give the visible prop, the spoken referent, and the proposed identity between them separate records. Let a reader inspect the edit occurrence where the identity first becomes plausible.

TENSION:
The insert could make the role of the gold easier to understand. It could also substitute a more legible object for a payment whose material form the adaptation has left unspecified.

MISSING:
A test of whether readers infer identity from adjacency even when the words supply different roles for the objects.

BOUNDARY:
This card concerns the distinction as rendered by Murray and a proposed edit. It does not settle every interpretation of the gold in the Greek passage.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[WT-SHIELD-05]] → Murray on the dispute and the award → [[WT-ROLE-11]] on claims made by an assembly.

TEST:
Use the same close-up and dialogue in two cuts. Place the gold after “I paid” in one and after “for the most just judgment” in the other. Ask “What is this gold?” before showing the passage. Then give readers access to the edit and ask them to identify what changed their answer.
