020 The Alert Is the Treatment

Prediction used as intervention changes the outcomes later used as evidence.
Successful prevention can make untreated risk less observable; selective decisions
make alternative outcomes absent; later causal evaluation therefore depends on
preserving prediction/action history without assuming that more surveillance is free.
