SOURCE MAP — THE HOUSE THAT WORDS BUILT: PROGRAMMING AFTER THE BLUEPRINT

P01 — Incomplete ordinary-language descriptions can initiate determinate generative artifacts, but the missing determination is supplied elsewhere.
TYPE: SYNTHESIS
SOURCES: Ingold 2013 (guidance/specification); Austin 1962; Winograd 1971.
PAPER MOVE: Frames continuation as delegated completion rather than as evidence that natural language itself contains the full program.

P02 — Deferred formalization is insufficient when an artifact creates a desire that did not exist prior to generation.
TYPE: INTERPRETATION
SOURCE: Ingold 2013, Drawing the Line, pp. 126-127; design as open-ended search, pp. 71-73.
PAPER MOVE: Splits failure -> constraint from possibility -> new intention.

P03 — In architecture, many different representational regimes mediate between sentence and material consequence.
TYPE: SYNTHESIS
SOURCES: Alberti 1988; Evans 1997; Eastman et al. 1974; Eastman & Henrion 1977; Stiny & Gips 1972; Smith 1980; Goodman 1978.
PAPER MOVE: Rejects "English replacing code" in favor of a multilingual stack.

P04 — A one-way description-to-matter pipeline suppresses the transformation and resistance occurring in the middle.
TYPE: INTERPRETATION
SOURCE: Ingold 2013, pp. 25-26, via Simondon; correspondence on p. 31.
PAPER MOVE: Extends Ingold's anti-hylomorphic argument to open-loop computational fabrication.

P05 — Failure legibility depends on exposing an artifact to a world capable of producing the relevant failure.
TYPE: INTERPRETATION
SOURCE: Ingold 2013, On Building a House, pp. 47-48.
PAPER MOVE: Adds EXPOSE to the prompt loop and makes test-world construction part of programming.

P06 — Feedback toward a fixed target differs from a process in which the encounter can change the target.
TYPE: SYNTHESIS / OPEN DISTINCTION
SOURCE: Ingold 2013 correspondence; no claim that computational feedback equals Ingoldian correspondence.
PAPER MOVE: Defines the stricter criterion for "programming by correspondence."

P07 — Rules and plans can be resources for action without exhausting situated practice.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED + INTERPRETATION
SOURCES: Ingold 2013, pp. 54-57; Suchman 1987.
PAPER MOVE: Argues that a prompt can function as guidance to prior competence rather than as compressed source code.

P08 — The operative state should preserve provenance and encounter history, not only final invariants.
TYPE: INTERPRETATION
SOURCE: Ingold 2013, pp. 30-31 (properties as histories).
PAPER MOVE: Uses this as an analogy for constraint provenance; not attributed to Ingold as a programming proposal.

P09 — The philosophical "house of language" can be read as doubled rather than simply reversed.
TYPE: SYNTHESIS
SOURCES: Heidegger 1998; Wittgenstein 2009; Goodman 1978.
PAPER MOVE: Humans remain within inherited languages while increasingly using ordinary language to route computational systems that reshape material environments, which then reshape later description.

P10 — PROGRAMMING BY CORRESPONDENCE is the paper's current wager.
TYPE: AUTHORIAL SYNTHESIS
SUPPORT: P01-P09.
BOUNDARY: The paper does not establish that any current LLM-to-construction system satisfies the full criterion. The companion artifact is a research demonstrator, not evidence of autonomous physical construction.
