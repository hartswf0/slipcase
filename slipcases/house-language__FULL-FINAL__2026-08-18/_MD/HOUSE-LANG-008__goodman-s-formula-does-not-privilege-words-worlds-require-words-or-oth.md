TITLE:
GOODMAN’S FORMULA DOES NOT PRIVILEGE WORDS: WORLDS REQUIRE “WORDS OR OTHER SYMBOLS.”

SOURCE:
Nelson Goodman — Ways of Worldmaking — 1978 — “Words, Works, Worlds,” p. 6.

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
“No world without words or other symbols.”

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Whether language should be the privileged representational cause in the house argument.

LOCAL MOVE:
Goodman’s argument culminates not in linguistic exclusivity but symbolic plurality: words appear alongside other symbols.

SOURCE TERMS:
words; other symbols; world; schemata; labels; pictures; predicates

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The title “The House That Words Built” may narrow the research object just when the machinery becomes multimodal: sentences, plans, sections, diagrams, numbers, renders, coordinates, models, codes, gestures, samples, marks.

QUESTION:
Is LANGUAGE actually the research object, or is the more precise object SYMBOLIC OPERABILITY ACROSS HETEROGENEOUS REPRESENTATIONS?

DEEPER QUESTION:
When a project passes from words into geometry, images, numerical constraints, code, and machine instructions, what warrants continuing to call the process linguistic?

MECHANISM:
Different symbolic forms organize, select, classify, and relate what can count as a world.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
HOUSE-MAKING REPRESENTATIONS = TEXT ∪ DRAWING ∪ NUMBER ∪ IMAGE ∪ MODEL ∪ CODE ∪ GESTURE ∪ MATERIAL SAMPLE ∪ MACHINE INSTRUCTION. Question: which transformations preserve operability?

TENSION:
HOUSE AND LANGUAGE is elegant, but the actual technical pipeline repeatedly leaves natural language.

MISSING:
A criterion distinguishing LANGUAGE, NOTATION, DIAGRAM, MODEL, CODE, SYMBOL SYSTEM, MATERIAL INDEX, COMMAND.

BOUNDARY:
Goodman does not make every symbol a language, nor establish equivalence among verbal, pictorial, diagrammatic, and computational representation.

CITATION TRAIL:
Goodman — Languages of Art; Ways of Worldmaking; Robin Evans; research on multimodal architectural representation.

TEST:
For every artifact in a house-making chain ask: language? symbol? executable? true/false? valid/invalid? directs action? what interprets it?
