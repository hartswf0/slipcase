MELT NOTES — THE HOUSE THAT WORDS BUILT / PROGRAMMING AFTER THE BLUEPRINT

WHAT WAS MELTED

1. "LANGUAGE BECOMES INFRASTRUCTURE" as the main thesis.
   Kept as a supporting mechanism. It names routing power, but it does not yet explain what makes the new programming temporality distinctive.

2. "DEFERRED FORMALIZATION" as the final name for the phenomenon.
   Kept as one case. It still implies that missing formality is waiting to be recovered. The rewrite distinguishes constraints clarified from prior intention from desires generated by the artifact itself.

3. "DEFERRED AUTHORITY" as the endpoint.
   Kept as a consequence. Authority does not merely move from user to model; it migrates among models, formal languages, standards, workers, tests, materials, weather, and inhabitants.

4. "THE PROGRAM IS A CONSTITUTION OF CONTINUATIONS" as an abstract conclusion.
   Melted into a concrete operational claim: programming by correspondence. The relevant state is a history of partial descriptions, provisional artifacts, exposures, responses, amendments, and retained commitments.

5. THE HOUSE AS EXAMPLE.
   Rejected. The house is now the paper's experimental object. Sentence House 01 accumulates constraints and tests as the argument develops, and the companion HTML executes a small part of that history.

6. THE ONE-WAY SENTENCE-TO-WALL DIAGRAM.
   Rejected as hylomorphic. The new loop requires an arrow back from artifact/world to representation and, in the stronger case, permits the target itself to change.

CURRENT WAGER

Natural-language programming is not best understood as English replacing code or as vague specification becoming formal later. Its strongest form is PROGRAMMING BY CORRESPONDENCE: partial language produces a provisional artifact; the artifact enters tests and worlds; those encounters can create constraints or alter goals; and the resulting commitments retain provenance and remain reopenable.

THE HOUSE THAT WORDS BUILT is therefore not the output of a sentence. It is the accumulated history of who or what was allowed to continue the sentence, and of which worlds were allowed to answer back.

WHY THE COMPANION HOUSE EXISTS

The prior paper could still move too freely among language, software, drawing, fabrication, and physical building. Sentence House 01 is a deliberately limited executable argument. It does not claim buildability. It forces the paper to state what is actually implemented, what is merely represented, what is tested, and what remains outside the computational artifact.
