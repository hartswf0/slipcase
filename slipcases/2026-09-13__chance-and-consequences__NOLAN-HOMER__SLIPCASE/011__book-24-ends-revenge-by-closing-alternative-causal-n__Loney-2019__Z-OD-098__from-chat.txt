TITLE:
Book 24 ends revenge by closing alternative causal narratives, not merely by stopping violence

SOURCE:
Alexander C. Loney — The Ethics of Revenge and the Meanings of the Odyssey — 2019 — Introduction and Chapter 6.
Homer — Odyssey 24.422–548.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Loney argues that the relatives’ revenge exposes the open-endedness of tisis and that the poem’s ending requires extraordinary divine amnesia; the conclusion also closes down alternative narratives of the suitors’ deaths.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Political closure is simultaneously causal closure and narrative closure.

LOCAL MOVE:
After the slaughter there are at least two stories:

ODYSSEUS STORY:
just punishment of reckless offenders.

BEREAVED-KIN STORY:
sons and brothers killed by Odysseus.

Both can generate future action.

Zeus’s intervention suppresses the second story’s ability to remain operative.

SOURCE TERMS:
τίσις
ἔκλησις
φόνος
ὅρκια
εἰρήνη

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Ending consequence requires more than disabling weapons.

It requires disabling a description of the past.

Peace therefore has an epistemic and narrative architecture.

QUESTION:
Can a causal account remain true while being politically forbidden to generate action?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does every stable peace require some loss of causal resolution?

MECHANISM:
same past
→ rival causal narrative
→ rival claim
→ possible revenge.

authority changes memory/claim status
→ rival narrative loses executability
→ peace.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<TRUE REMEMBERED HARM>
→ <ACTIONABLE COUNTER-NARRATIVE>
→ [DIVINE CLOSURE]
→ <NON-ACTIONABLE PAST>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Loney describes the end of revenge as requiring amnesia and closure of alternative narratives; Homer couples the settlement with covenant, forgetting, friendship, and peace.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Narrative N may remain historically possible.

But:

Executable(N):
true → false.

PoliticalClosure ≠ Proof(N false).

TENSION:
If memory is literally erased rather than merely rendered non-actionable, the poem’s solution is more radical than institutional amnesty.

MISSING:
The exact semantic force of ἔκλησις in this unique formulation.

BOUNDARY:
Later legal amnesty cannot determine Homer’s exact meaning.
