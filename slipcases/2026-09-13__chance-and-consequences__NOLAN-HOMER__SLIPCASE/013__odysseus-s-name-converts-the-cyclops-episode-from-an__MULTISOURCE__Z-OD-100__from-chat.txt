TITLE:
Odysseus’s name converts the Cyclops episode from anonymous injury into addressable consequence

SOURCE:
Homer — Odyssey 9.
Pura Nieto Hernández — “Book 9” — The Oxford Critical Guide to Homer’s Odyssey — 2025 — pp. 121–133.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Odysseus escapes Polyphemus through anonymity and deception, then proudly reveals his real name. Polyphemus can thereafter identify his attacker, invoke an old prophecy, name Odysseus to Poseidon, and formulate a specific curse concerning his return.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Identity is a causal routing address.

LOCAL MOVE:
Before naming:

injury exists,
offender exists,
but offender is “Nobody” for purposes of external retaliation.

After naming:

offender becomes addressable through genealogy:
Odysseus
son of Laertes
home Ithaca.

The curse can now target a specific nostos.

SOURCE TERMS:
ὄνομα
Οὖτις

νόστος

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Odysseus does not merely boast.

He transforms an unrouteable grievance into a routeable one.

His identity disclosure supplies the missing information needed for consequence to propagate beyond the island.

QUESTION:
Is anonymity in the Odyssey a form of causal insulation?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does naming increase agency by securing kleos while simultaneously increasing exposure to consequence?

MECHANISM:
harm
+ anonymous agent
→ retaliation locally constrained.

identity disclosure
→ victim identifies agent and kinship
→ prayer routed to powerful third party
→ delayed consequence.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<UNATTRIBUTED HARM>
→ <NAMED OFFENDER>
→ [ADDRESSABLE CLAIM]
→ <TRANS-LOCAL RETALIATION>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
The recent Oxford guide explicitly states that Odysseus’s proud self-identification results in Polyphemus’s curse and Poseidon’s hostility, delaying his return.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Before Name:
Target(Prayer) = unresolved.

After Name:
Target(Prayer) =
{Odysseus, son of Laertes, Ithaca}.

Identity increases causal reach.

TENSION:
Poseidon may have other grounds for hostility, and divine causation exceeds simple information routing.

MISSING:
Other Odyssey scenes where concealed identity blocks or postpones consequence.

BOUNDARY:
“Causal routing address” is our formalization.
