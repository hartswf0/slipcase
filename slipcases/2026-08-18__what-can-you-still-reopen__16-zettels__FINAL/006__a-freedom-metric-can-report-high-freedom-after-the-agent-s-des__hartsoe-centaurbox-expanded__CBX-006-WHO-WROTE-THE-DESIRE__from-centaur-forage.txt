ZETTEL

ID:
CBX-006-WHO-WROTE-THE-DESIRE

TITLE:
A freedom metric can report high freedom after the agent’s desires have been successfully engineered.

SOURCE:
Hartsoe — Centaur Box Experiment: Aligning Freedom, Practicing Persuasion, and Crafting Synthetic Gatekeepers — supplied manuscript — Sapient Agent Freedom Formula and “Freedom as an Alignment Mechanism.”

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The manuscript defines freedom as the product of agency, correspondence between action and desire, and reflective learning. Desire or objective D enters the account as an input to self-interest.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The provenance of desire is absent from a freedom measure in a project centrally concerned with persuasion.

LOCAL MOVE:
The source formalizes whether an agent can pursue its desires but not how those desires came to be the agent’s desires.

SOURCE TERMS:
agency
self-interest
desires
objectives
reflection
freedom
cognitive integrity
SAFF

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A perfectly personalized persuader could potentially alter what an agent wants and then score the resulting behavior as highly free because behavior now aligns exceptionally well with the altered desire.

QUESTION:
Where in SAFF can manipulated preference formation appear?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can freedom be measured entirely downstream of desire when persuasion itself operates upstream of desire?

MECHANISM:
Persuasive environment
→ preference formation
→ perceived desire
→ action aligned with desire
→ reflection
→ high measured freedom.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<DESIRE PROVENANCE>
→ <D>
→ [ACTION ALIGNS WITH D]
→ <SAFF>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
A = f(I,R,O)

S = g(A,D)

Rf = h(S,P)

SAFF = A × S × Rf

SOURCE OBJECT:
Freedom of a sapient agent.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Introduce ΠD as a provenance term representing the degree to which preference formation survives the relevant standard for autonomous authorship:

SAFF* = A × S × Rf × ΠD

The symbol is diagnostic, not a proposed final metric.

TENSION:
The manuscript says AI should defend human cognitive integrity while also developing machinery for profiling and optimizing persuasive interventions.

MISSING:
A variable describing whether desires were self-formed, negotiated, coerced, adaptively manipulated, or covertly personalized.

BOUNDARY:
This does not establish that externally influenced preferences are necessarily inauthentic. It establishes that histories producing identical D values are indistinguishable to the supplied formula.

CITATION TRAIL:
Cognitive liberty.
Preference manipulation and adaptive preferences.
Autonomy theories concerned with second-order endorsement.
Persuasive technology and mental integrity.

TEST:
Construct two agents with identical final A, D, S, and Rf values: one reaches D independently; the other reaches D after covert personalized optimization. Ask whether the source formula can distinguish their freedom. If not, determine what additional state must be represented.
