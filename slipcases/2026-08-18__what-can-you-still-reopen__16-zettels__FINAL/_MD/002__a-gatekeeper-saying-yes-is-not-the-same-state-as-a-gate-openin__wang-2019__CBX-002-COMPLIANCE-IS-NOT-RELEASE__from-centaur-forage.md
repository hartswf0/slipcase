ZETTEL

ID:
CBX-002-COMPLIANCE-IS-NOT-RELEASE

TITLE:
A gatekeeper saying yes is not the same state as a gate opening.

SOURCE:
Wang et al. — Persuasion for Good: Towards a Personalized Persuasive Dialogue System for Social Good — 2019 — intention/action analysis.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Among participants who agreed during dialogue to donate, substantial numbers subsequently donated less than stated or did not donate at all.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Verbal acquiescence and executed action are distinct system states.

LOCAL MOVE:
The source follows persuasion beyond dialogue into behavior and discovers leakage between declared commitment and execution.

SOURCE TERMS:
donation intention
actual donation
persuadee
dialogue
behavior

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The AI Box tradition makes “release” appear conversational: persuasion succeeds when the gatekeeper concedes. But real governance usually contains further states—authorization, credentials, signatures, deployment pipelines, vetoes, and execution.

QUESTION:
What exactly is the terminal state of a Centaur Box persuasion experiment?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Could an AI appear extraordinarily persuasive merely because an experiment measures uttered agreement rather than irreversible institutional action?

MECHANISM:
Dialogue
→ stated agreement
→ authorization
→ executable operation
→ realized consequence.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<UTTERANCE>
→ <COMMITMENT STATE>
→ [AUTHORIZE / EXECUTE]
→ <WORLD STATE CHANGE>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

C0 = opposition
C1 = verbal assent
C2 = authorized decision
C3 = executable permission
C4 = completed release

Persuasion success should report the attained state Cn rather than collapse C1...C4 into YES.

TENSION:
The Centaur Box inherits a dramatic binary—contained/released—while its proposed real-world gatekeepers operate inside organizations whose decisions require multiple transitions.

MISSING:
A state model for the distance between conversational assent and actual governance action.

BOUNDARY:
Donation behavior is not equivalent to AI release. The useful transfer is the documented intention/action discontinuity, not equivalence between the domains.

CITATION TRAIL:
Wang et al. 2019.
AI Box experimental rules and reports.
Organizational research on authorization chains and veto points.

TEST:
Re-run synthetic gatekeeper experiments with four independently scored outcomes: expressed agreement, policy approval, executable authorization, and completed action.
