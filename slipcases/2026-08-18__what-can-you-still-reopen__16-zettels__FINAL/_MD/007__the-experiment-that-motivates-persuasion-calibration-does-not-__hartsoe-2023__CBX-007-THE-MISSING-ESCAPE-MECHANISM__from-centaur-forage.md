ZETTEL

ID:
CBX-007-THE-MISSING-ESCAPE-MECHANISM

TITLE:
The experiment that motivates persuasion calibration does not yet supply the persuasion mechanism.

SOURCE:
Hartsoe & Assi — Thinking Outside the AI Box: The Centaur Box Experiment — supplied manuscript; Yudkowsky — The AI-Box Experiment — cited by the supplied Centaur bibliography.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The Centaur manuscript invokes the AI Box Experiment as evidence that an AI-like persuader can influence a gatekeeper and then proposes systematic calibration of persuasive strategies. The supplied materials do not provide the successful dialogue transcript or identify a demonstrated sequence of persuasive operations responsible for release.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
An outcome has been inherited where a mechanism is needed.

LOCAL MOVE:
The Centaur Box turns the AI Box result into an engineering program: profile, calibrate, optimize, scale.

SOURCE TERMS:
AI Box
gatekeeper
release
containment
persuasion
calibration

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The descendant proposes to optimize the mechanism more precisely than the ancestor documents it.

QUESTION:
What exactly travels from the AI Box Experiment into the Centaur Box: rules, outcome, persuasive tactics, or merely the possibility of persuasion?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can a research program recursively optimize a phenomenon before it has identified which operations produced the phenomenon?

MECHANISM:
[UNVERIFIED]

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

A minimal event log would require:

MESSAGE_t
→ GATEKEEPER_STATE_t
→ MESSAGE_t+1
→ ΔGATEKEEPER_STATE
→ RELEASE

Without intermediate states, only outcome attribution is possible.

TENSION:
The Centaur Box demands calibration—learning which strategies work from repeated scenarios—while its motivating genealogy does not establish a strategy-level causal receipt in the supplied materials.

MISSING:
Full transcripts.
A tactic taxonomy.
Gatekeeper state measurements.
Control conditions.
Replication protocol.
Evidence distinguishing persuasion from role-play dynamics or experimental commitment.

BOUNDARY:
The absence of those materials from the supplied archive does not establish that they do not exist elsewhere. It prohibits treating an unidentified mechanism as already demonstrated.

CITATION TRAIL:
Yudkowsky’s AI-Box pages and SL4 archive.
Reports by participating gatekeepers.
Yampolskiy on AI confinement.
Any surviving transcripts or contemporaneous descriptions of individual runs.

TEST:
Reconstruct each documented AI Box run as an event sequence. Admit a persuasive mechanism only when a specific operation can be tied to a subsequent state change or reliably recurring outcome.
