SOURCE MAP — WHAT CAN YOU STILL REOPEN?
Adaptive Persuasion and the Contestability of Desire
18 August 2026

C01
CLAIM: A present-state freedom measure can certify high autonomy after a surrounding persuasive system has altered the desire used as its reference point.
TYPE: COMPILER-SYNTHESIS
ZETTEL: CBX-006-WHO-WROTE-THE-DESIRE
SOURCE: Hartsoe — Centaur Box Experiment: Aligning Freedom, Practicing Persuasion, and Crafting Synthetic Gatekeepers
CITEKEY: hartsoe_centaurbox_expanded
SUPPORT: The supplied manuscript places desire D inside a conceptual freedom formula.
INFERENCE: Coupling that formula to adaptive preference-changing influence makes D endogenous to the evaluated system.

C02

TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED

SOURCE: Hartsoe & Assi — Thinking Outside the AI Box: The Centaur Box Experiment
CITEKEY: hartsoe_assi_centaurbox
SUPPORT: Advocacy, calibration, and scaling are explicit in the supplied manuscript.
INFERENCE: NONE

C03
CLAIM: Persuasion outcomes must be decomposed because increased action can result from procedural assistance rather than changed belief.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED + INTERPRETATION
ZETTEL: CBX-001-PROCEDURAL-PERSUASION
SOURCE: Wang et al. — Persuasion for Good
CITEKEY: wang2019persuasion
SUPPORT: Donation information had a significant positive main effect in the reported strategy table.
INFERENCE: The causal mechanism may include reduced execution friction rather than attitudinal conversion.

C04
CLAIM: Conversational assent and completed action are distinct states.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED
ZETTEL: CBX-002-COMPLIANCE-IS-NOT-RELEASE
SOURCE: Wang et al. — Persuasion for Good
CITEKEY: wang2019persuasion
SUPPORT: Among a subset who agreed to donate, some later reduced or made no donation.
INFERENCE: Governance experiments should not collapse assent, authorization, permission, and execution into one terminal state.

C05
CLAIM: Baseline susceptibility and person-specific intervention effects are different quantities.
TYPE: INTERPRETATION
ZETTEL: CBX-003-SUSCEPTIBILITY-IS-NOT-TAILORING
SOURCE: Wang et al. — Persuasion for Good
CITEKEY: wang2019persuasion
SUPPORT: The source separately reports psychological predictors and strategy-by-characteristic interactions.
INFERENCE: Predicting who is likely to comply does not by itself establish which intervention causally changes that person.

C06
CLAIM: Convincingness ranking and personalized belief change optimize different objects; strategic dialogue may require dynamic state rather than static personality.
TYPE: INTERPRETATION
ZETTEL: CBX-008-CICERO-IS-A-STATE-MACHINE; CBX-009-CONVINCINGNESS-HAS-NO-PERSON
SOURCE: Gleize et al.; Meta Fundamental AI Research Diplomacy Team
CITEKEY: gleize2019convinced; meta2022diplomacy
SUPPORT: EviConv is a pairwise convincingness task; CICERO combines language with strategic reasoning in Diplomacy.
INFERENCE: Neither source alone licenses a static psychographic causal model of real-world gatekeepers.

C07
CLAIM: Higher-order endorsement does not terminate the manipulation problem if the endorsement process itself can be targeted.
TYPE: COMPILER-SYNTHESIS
ZETTEL: CBX-006B-ENDORSEMENT-REGRESS
SOURCE: Frankfurt; Christman
CITEKEY: frankfurt1971freedom; christman1991autonomy
SUPPORT: Frankfurt distinguishes higher-order volitions; Christman argues coherent/integrated motivational structures can be manipulated.
INFERENCE: Adaptive persuasion can treat the autonomy-certifying reflective layer as another possible target surface.

C08
CLAIM: Autonomy cannot be inferred solely from a present motivational state when distinct histories can converge on the same state.
TYPE: INTERPRETATION
ZETTEL: CBX-006A-AUTONOMY-HAS-A-HISTORY
SOURCE: Christman — Autonomy and Personal History
CITEKEY: christman1991autonomy
SUPPORT: Christman develops a historical account and argues that coherent motivational integration can result from manipulation.
INFERENCE: A state-only metric loses autonomy-relevant information if trajectory matters.

C09
CLAIM: Preference provenance cannot mean purity from external influence.
TYPE: INTERPRETATION
ZETTEL: CBX-006C-PROVENANCE-IS-NOT-PURITY
SOURCE: Christman — Autonomy and Personal History
CITEKEY: christman1991autonomy
SUPPORT: The source treats personal history and influence as constitutive of the autonomy problem rather than requiring self-creation.
INFERENCE: External causation alone cannot classify a preference as non-autonomous.

C10
CLAIM: Questionable preference formation does not erase the chooser's standing; preference-changing intervention can sometimes support agency.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED + INTERPRETATION
ZETTEL: CBX-006E-ADAPTIVE-DESIRES-DO-NOT-ERASE-AGENCY; CBX-006F-PERSUASION-CAN-EXPAND-AUTONOMY
SOURCE: Khader — Adaptive Preferences and Women's Empowerment
CITEKEY: khader2011adaptive
SUPPORT: Khader separates adaptive preferences from a blanket finding of autonomy deficit and develops empowerment-oriented intervention without treating agents as inert.
INFERENCE: Noninterference cannot serve as the universal opposite of manipulation.
