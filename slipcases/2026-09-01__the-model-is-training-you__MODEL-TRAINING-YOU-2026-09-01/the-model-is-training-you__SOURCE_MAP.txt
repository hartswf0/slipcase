EVIDENCE LIMITS
- No source cited here directly claims that prompt engineering is a state-like legibility project. That analogy and mechanism are the paper's wager.
- No source establishes that organizational adaptation to LLMs is globally net harmful. The paper argues that this adaptation should be measured and disclosed.
- Tao et al. use national survey aggregates as cultural benchmarks; this does not exhaust culture.
- Moon et al. study a particular writing domain and GPT-4; the population-scale argument is an invitation to test other domains.
- Reversible legibility is a compiler-created term in this paper, derived from Star's boundary-object architecture and the zettel graph.
