OPEN EDGES

1. ORGANIZATIONAL ADAPTATION LEDGER
   Measure how much real workflow restructuring precedes observed LLM performance gains.

2. REMAINDER AUDIT
   Develop a method for recording what is lost when prose becomes template, schema, or agent graph.

3. TRANSLATION VS NORMALIZATION
   Build domain-specific tests with local expert evaluation rather than national-value aggregates alone.

4. REVERSIBILITY BENCHMARK
   Test whether an AI workflow can recover from anomalies that its original schema could not represent.

5. POPULATION-SCALE PROMPT HOMOGENIZATION
   Measure whether shared prompt patterns make problem formulations converge even when outputs remain superficially diverse.
