TITLE:
THE ROUTE PRECEDES THE PLAT: REPEATED MOVEMENT CAN OUTRANK THE LEGAL MAP AS A SHOTCALLER

SOURCE:
ABIDING ACRES, BUILD-BRIEF.md and atlas/world.json, hartswf0/abiding-halfworld. SOURCE URL: https://github.com/hartswf0/abiding-halfworld

Tim Ingold, “On Building a House,” Chapter 4 of Making: Anthropology, Archaeology, Art and Architecture (Routledge, 2013). SOURCE URL: https://doi.org/10.4324/9780203559055

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE — ABIDING] The deer are defined as three white-tailed deer who cross the cul-de-sac at the same point and in the same order because their route predates the plat. The build brief says that eleven easement scenes have a cast of one and that “that strip of ground is a character.” The corrected invariant is route and order, not hour.

[PARAPHRASE — INGOLD] Buildings and inhabited worlds unfold through paths, forces, growth, decay, maintenance and situated action rather than remaining fixed realizations of crystalline prior designs.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The legally represented world and the inhabited world can occupy the same ground while possessing different geometries.

The plat divides and names property. The recurring deer crossing writes another organization through movement. Within the fiction, the route is older than the subdivision description used to contain it.

The shot should therefore not treat the deer as decorative objects moving across an architecturally prior background. The route is a persistent structure temporarily materialized by the animals.

LOCAL MOVE:
SHOTCALL FOR EASEMENT SCENES:

DO NOT BEGIN:
SHOW THREE DEER.

BEGIN:
SHOW THE ROUTE THAT CONTINUES DESPITE THE PLAT.

INVARIANTS:
preserve crossing point;
preserve crossing order;
do not glamorize;
no golden-hour exceptionalism;
no slow motion;
let ground carry subjecthood;
permit the deer to become small if the route remains legible.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A path can behave like a rule without being written as an instruction.

Repeated movement leaves a durable expectation that conditions later movement and later representation.

QUESTION:
Can a path function as an operational nonverbal rule inside House Language?

DEEPER QUESTION:
When legal, architectural, animal, cinematic, and computational descriptions disagree about the same ground, what determines which description gets authority to call the shot?

MECHANISM:
REPEATED CROSSING
→ persistent route
→ later crossing follows route
→ repetition becomes expectation
→ route acquires directorial significance.

TENSION:
READING A:
The route is simply another constraint authored into the plan.

READING B:
Encoding it later as a plan constraint should not erase the fictional ontology in which movement precedes and contests the cadastral representation.

MISSING:
Whether repeated framing can make route persistence perceptible without becoming a repeated visual icon.

How to stage scenes in which legal dispute and animal route are simultaneously active.

BOUNDARY:
The claim that the route predates the plat belongs to the fictional world-text. It is not an empirical claim about an actual subdivision.

TEST:
