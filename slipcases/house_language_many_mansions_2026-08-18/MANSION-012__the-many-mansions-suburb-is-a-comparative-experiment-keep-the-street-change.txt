ID:
MANSION-012

TITLE:
THE MANY-MANSIONS SUBURB IS A COMPARATIVE EXPERIMENT: KEEP THE STREET, CHANGE WHICH LANGUAGE HAS AUTHORITY OVER EACH HOUSE.

SOURCE:
Synthesis from Turing 1950; Wittgenstein, Philosophical Investigations §18; Ingold 2013; FHA 1938; Shelley 1948; Habraken; Weiser 1991; ABIDING Halfworld. SOURCE URL: https://github.com/hartswf0/abiding-halfworld

PASSAGE:
[OUR INFERENCE] The project can become an experimental neighborhood rather than an essay illustration: neighboring lots share climate, municipal context, and comparable material conditions while varying the linguistic constitution that governs identity, occupancy, design, repair, memory, and machine agency.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The suburb is a comparative apparatus for theories of operative language. TURING HOUSE tests inhabitable substrates for intelligence. COVENANT HOUSE holds geometry constant while changing permitted occupants. INGOLD HOUSE forbids a final complete specification. MONA HOUSE distributes machine embodiment through architecture. ESTATE HOUSE tests persistence after death. EASEMENT HOUSE lets older use cut across newer property language. UNKNOWN HOUSE protects unclassified presence. PROMPT HOUSE records every delegated decision.

LOCAL MOVE:
Build the houses in one Halfworld. Reuse one street, weather system, municipal apparatus, and rendering grammar. Change the operative language per lot and observe conflicts at boundaries.

SOURCE TERMS:
mansion; suburb; language city; dwelling; standing; covenant; route; prompt; house

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The comparison becomes stronger when the houses look similar. What differs is not style but the language that decides what may happen.

QUESTION:
What changes when the variable is not architectural form but the linguistic constitution of the house?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can one neighborhood reveal which theories of language can coexist, which require translation, and which produce incompatible inhabitants?

MECHANISM:
COMMON STREET + CONTROLLED HOUSE CONDITIONS + DIFFERENT OPERATIVE LANGUAGES → COMPARATIVE FAILURES/NEGOTIATIONS.

FORMAL SHIFT:
FROM: PAPER ABOUT HOUSES BUILT BY WORDS
TO: SUBURB THAT INSTANTIATES RIVAL THEORIES OF HOW WORDS BECOME WORLD CONDITIONS.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
LOT_i = common_environment + constitution_i. Compare boundary events, inhabitants, permissible transitions, representation failures, and revision paths.

TENSION:
The experiment risks turning complex histories into themed houses. Each lot must preserve source-specific stakes and avoid flattening legal violence, philosophy, craft, and fiction into aesthetic motifs.

MISSING:
Experimental protocol; shared street ontology; boundary-crossing events; measures of legibility, hospitality, authority, persistence, and correction.

BOUNDARY:
This is the paper’s proposed research program, not a historical claim.

CITATION TRAIL:
Turing mansions → Wittgenstein suburbs → Abiding Acres → many-mansions experiment.

TEST:
Implement at least four lots with one recurring ambiguous entity crossing lot boundaries. Record how each house classifies, admits, refuses, remembers, or revises in response.
