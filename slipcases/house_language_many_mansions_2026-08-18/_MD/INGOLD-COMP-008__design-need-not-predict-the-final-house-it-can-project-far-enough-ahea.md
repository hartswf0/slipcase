TITLE:
DESIGN NEED NOT PREDICT THE FINAL HOUSE; IT CAN PROJECT FAR ENOUGH AHEAD TO LET MAKING FIND ITS NEXT MOVE.

SOURCE:
Tim Ingold — Making: Anthropology, Archaeology, Art and Architecture — 2013 — Chapter 5, “The Sighted Watchmaker,” pp. 71–73.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Ingold asks what design becomes once it is no longer identified with a plan completed before execution; he recasts it as anticipatory, open-ended movement toward something not fully known in advance, while making remains constrained by material engagement.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
ANTICIPATION WITHOUT COMPLETE PREDICTION.

LOCAL MOVE:
Ingold refuses the choice DESIGN PRECEDES MAKING or THERE IS NO DESIGN. Design can run ahead of material work without dictating its final state.

SOURCE TERMS:
anticipation; foresight; open-ended; future; drawing; movement; gesture; hopes; dreams; material engagement

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The House That Words Built may not need WORDS → COMPLETE HOUSE SPECIFICATION. Linguistic operation might continually extend a horizon just beyond current construction.

QUESTION:
Could an architectural language build without ever containing a complete representation of the building it is producing?

DEEPER QUESTION:
What is the minimum horizon of anticipation required for coherent construction: one operation, component, room, phase, whole building?

MECHANISM:
Imagination projects ahead; current material engagement constrains possibilities; practitioner moves; new circumstances alter projection.

TENSION:
[[HOUSE-COMP-009]] places conversation inside design; Ingold suggests conversation may repeatedly extend a horizon into a future that remains genuinely undetermined.

MISSING:
Whether prompt-to-CAD and robotic systems permit goals/geometry to remain open during construction or require a fixed target before execution.

BOUNDARY:
Ingold does not present an algorithm for receding-horizon planning; control-theoretic formalization is OUR reconstruction.

TEST:
Build the same structure with a frozen complete model and with only next N operations specified; vary N and find the smallest lookahead horizon at which coherent architectural form still emerges.
