TITLE:
A HOUSE CAN CONTINUE TO READ AFTER ITS RESIDENTS LEAVE.

SOURCE:
ABIDING ACRES, “Inventory” / resident world-text. SOURCE URL: https://github.com/hartswf0/abiding-halfworld

Tim Ingold, Making, Chapter 4, “On Building a House,” 2013. SOURCE URL: https://doi.org/10.4324/9780203559055

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE — ABIDING] Years after Imogene’s death, Ruth’s unopened cards remain in the house while Mona can account for their arrival because she logged them.

[PARAPHRASE — INGOLD] Handover to residents does not end building; maintenance, weather, pests, repair, and use continue the process.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
A house persists through two different mechanisms: records continue to be read, and materials continue to produce new evidence. The first makes absent authors and residents operative; the second prevents old representations from having final authority.

LOCAL MOVE:
Track RECORD PERSISTENCE and MATERIAL PERSISTENCE separately for every house.

SOURCE TERMS:
letters; log; death; residence; handover; maintenance; weather; repair

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A dead resident can remain operationally present through correspondence, property, obligations, possessions, memories, and machine logs while being physically absent.

QUESTION:
How long can a person continue to inhabit a house through records after bodily residence ends?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can a house itself be understood as an apparatus that repeatedly reads traces left by absent people?

MECHANISM:
TRACE → PERSISTENT STORAGE → LATER READER → NEW ACTION; MATERIAL STATE → WEATHER/USE → NEW EVIDENCE → REPAIR.

FORMAL SHIFT:
FROM: RESIDENT LEAVES → HOUSE EMPTY
TO: ABSENCE CAN REMAIN OPERATIVE THROUGH RECORDS AND MATERIAL CONSEQUENCES.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
HOUSE_MEMORY = {records read later, material traces encountered later}.

TENSION:
Calling documentary persistence “dwelling” risks metaphoric inflation. The narrower claim is that absence can continue organizing present action.

MISSING:
Estate law; archives; heirlooms; smart-home data after death; mail; memorialization; digital inheritance.

BOUNDARY:
The fictional scene establishes a research object, not empirical prevalence.

CITATION TRAIL:
Abiding letters → death → machine memory → Ingold maintenance → house continues reading.

TEST:
For one house after an owner dies, inventory every trace that continues to trigger action: mail, bills, accounts, furniture, repairs, photos, sensors, deeds, debts, automated services.
