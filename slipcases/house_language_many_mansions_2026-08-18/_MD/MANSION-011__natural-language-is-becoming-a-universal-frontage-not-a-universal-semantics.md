TITLE:
NATURAL LANGUAGE IS BECOMING A UNIVERSAL FRONTAGE, NOT A UNIVERSAL SEMANTICS.

SOURCE:
Terry Winograd, “Understanding Natural Language,” 1972. SOURCE URL: https://doi.org/10.1016/0010-0285(72)90002-3

ABIDING Halfworld method. SOURCE URL: https://github.com/hartswf0/abiding-halfworld

Ludwig Wittgenstein, Philosophical Investigations §18. SOURCE URL: https://www.wiley-vch.de/en/areas-interest/humanities-social-sciences/philosophy-12pl/historical-western-philosophy-12pl4/wittgenstein-12pl45/philosophical-investigations-978-1-4051-5928-9

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] SHRDLU’s flexible English interface depends on a restricted prior world. Halfworld routes natural-language source material through typed world-text, plans, locks, concepts, state, renderers, and ledgers. Wittgenstein’s city analogy permits historically heterogeneous language districts.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Conversational AI makes heterogeneous systems look as though they share one language. The apparent universality is at the interface. Downstream, specialist ontologies, defaults, institutional permissions, and material constraints still decide what the utterance can mean as action.

LOCAL MOVE:
For every prompt-built artifact emit a DELEGATION RECEIPT listing each consequential decision not fixed by the utterance and the system or actor that supplied it.

SOURCE TERMS:
natural language; microworld; interface; plan; lock; ontology; delegation

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Fluency can make hidden jurisdiction look like direct understanding.

QUESTION:
Who or what completes the parts of the world the prompt leaves unsaid?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can conversational interfaces expose hidden delegation without collapsing back into expert-only complexity?

MECHANISM:
PROMPT → UNDERDETERMINED INTENT → DEFAULT/PRIOR/SCHEMA/AUTHORITY/MATERIAL COMPLETION → ARTIFACT.

FORMAL SHIFT:
FROM: PROMPT = PROGRAM
TO: PROMPT = FRONTAGE OVER A DISTRIBUTED COMPLETION SYSTEM.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
DEFERRED_FORMALIZATION = DEFERRED_AUTHORITY when unspecified decisions are completed downstream.

TENSION:
Some delegation is the point of abstraction and expertise. The problem is not delegation itself but invisible or unaccountable delegation.

MISSING:
Interface patterns for delegation receipts; provenance of model priors; authority negotiation; user override.

BOUNDARY:
Do not imply the user should specify every detail. The proposal is visibility of consequential completion.

CITATION TRAIL:
SHRDLU → world model → Wittgenstein suburb → Halfworld stack → hidden jurisdiction → delegation receipt.

TEST:
Issue one underspecified house prompt to three systems. Record every materially consequential difference and trace each to explicit text, model default, code rule, institution, or human judgment.
