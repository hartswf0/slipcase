INGOLD-COMP-004

TITLE:
MEDIEVAL BUILDING MAKES DESIGN AN OPERATION WITHIN CONSTRUCTION RATHER THAN A COMPLETE REPRESENTATION BEFORE IT.

SOURCE:
Tim Ingold — Making: Anthropology, Archaeology, Art and Architecture — 2013 — Chapter 4, “On Building a House,” pp. 55–56.

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
“designed as they drew, and drew as they designed”

RESEARCH OBJECT:
DESIGN DURING EXECUTION.

LOCAL MOVE:
Ingold reviews evidence that medieval drawings often functioned at full scale and for local problems rather than as complete prespecifications; drawing and building were integral to the craft.

SOURCE TERMS:
drawing; design; craft; work; tracing; stonemason; lineaments; building; taking care

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Our computational genealogy may have mistaken DESIGN FIRST / EXECUTION SECOND for the natural structure of making. A cathedral suggests DESIGN INSIDE BUILDING.

QUESTION:
What would a programming language for architecture look like if program writing continued during physical execution rather than terminating before it?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Could construction itself become a programming environment?

MECHANISM:
Local geometric problems appear; builders draw at the needed scale/location; drawing assists operation; operation generates conditions for further drawing; design develops through work.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<COMPLETE PRIOR REPRESENTATION>
→ <LOCAL DRAWING WITHIN BUILDING>
→ [DRAW / CUT / FIT / REDRAW]
→ <FORM DEVELOPING DURING CONSTRUCTION>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Full-scale tracing, templates, strings, stakes, practical geometry, local fitting, iterative work; not a formal programming system.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
WRITE₁ → EXECUTE₁ → OBSERVE₁ → WRITE₂ → EXECUTE₂ → OBSERVE₂ → ... ; DESIGN and EXECUTION interleave.

TENSION:
[[HOUSE-COMP-000]] asks how many interpreters can disappear; this evidence suggests the crucial intermediary may be the capacity to redesign while the wall is becoming.

MISSING:
Whether contemporary construction environments permit genuine modification of architectural intent during fabrication rather than parameter adjustment toward a fixed target.

BOUNDARY:
The evidence does not establish medieval cathedrals lacked all prior plans; Ingold explicitly preserves the historical disagreement.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[HOUSE-COMP-000]] → Ingold → David Turnbull → Lon Shelby → Villard de Honnecourt → Lucy Suchman → live coding → adaptive robotic construction.

TEST:
Build one component with a frozen complete model and another where rules/geometry may be rewritten after each material operation; record whether the second creates genuinely new design information.
