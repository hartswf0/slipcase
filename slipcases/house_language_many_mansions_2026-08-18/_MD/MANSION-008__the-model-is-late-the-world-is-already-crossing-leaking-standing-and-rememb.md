TITLE:
THE MODEL IS LATE: THE WORLD IS ALREADY CROSSING, LEAKING, STANDING, AND REMEMBERING BEFORE ITS REPRESENTATION ARRIVES.

SOURCE:
Tim Ingold, Making, Chapter 4, “On Building a House,” 2013. SOURCE URL: https://doi.org/10.4324/9780203559055

ABIDING ACRES / abiding-halfworld. SOURCE URL: https://github.com/hartswf0/abiding-halfworld

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE — INGOLD] “Completion is, at best, a legal fiction.”

[PARAPHRASE — ABIDING] The deer route predates the plat; the angel occupies a threshold without entering; Mona has already persisted in the house before institutions know how to hear her.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
A representational system often encounters a world whose consequential relations already exist outside its categories. The cadastral map does not create the deer route. The plan does not create rain. Standing doctrine does not create the claimant. The world model is therefore temporally and ontologically late.

LOCAL MOVE:
When a model encounters an untyped event, do not begin by forcing the event into the nearest category. Preserve an UNKNOWN/EXCESS state long enough to inspect what the category would erase.

SOURCE TERMS:
completion; world; plan; rain; route; plat; threshold; standing

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The operational problem may be not how to make the world fit the language, but how to make language answerable to what was already there.

QUESTION:
What evidence must a model preserve when it meets a relation it cannot yet name?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can a computational world remain executable while admitting that parts of the world precede and exceed its ontology?

MECHANISM:
WORLD EVENT → MISMATCH WITH MODEL → PRESERVE EXCESS → REVISE CATEGORY/ACTION → CONTINUE.

FORMAL SHIFT:

OUR FORMALIZATION:

UNKNOWN ≠ INVALID. UNMODELED ≠ ABSENT.

TENSION:
Every executable system needs some closure. Unlimited openness can make action impossible. The research problem is where to place reversible uncertainty.

MISSING:
Open-world planning; schema evolution; exception handling; legal indeterminacy; human escalation paths.

BOUNDARY:

CITATION TRAIL:

TEST:
Introduce an event deliberately excluded from a house ontology. Compare systems that REJECT, COERCE-TO-NEAREST-TYPE, or PRESERVE-UNKNOWN-AND-ESCALATE.
