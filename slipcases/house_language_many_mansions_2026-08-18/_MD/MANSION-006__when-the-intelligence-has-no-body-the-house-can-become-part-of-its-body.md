TITLE:
WHEN THE INTELLIGENCE HAS NO BODY, THE HOUSE CAN BECOME PART OF ITS BODY.

SOURCE:
Mark Weiser, “The Computer for the 21st Century,” Scientific American 265(3), 1991. SOURCE URL: https://graphics.stanford.edu/courses/cs428-03-spring/Papers/readings/General/Weiser_SciAm91.htm

ABIDING ACRES / Mona world-text. SOURCE URL: https://github.com/hartswf0/abiding-halfworld

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE — WEISER] “The most profound technologies are those that disappear.”

[PARAPHRASE — ABIDING] Mona is a household intelligence with no body; her identity is carried by voice placement, an amber wall indicator, and a fixed pre-speech delay. Her world-text states that the architecture is her body.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The smart-home question is usually how AI enters architecture. Mona reverses it: architecture is one of the means by which a bodiless intelligence acquires location, sensorium, reach, timing, and continuity. The house is not merely container or interface; it can be morphology.

LOCAL MOVE:
Model domestic AI through its environmental manifestation: SPEAKS_FROM, HEARS_AT, CAN_ACT_ON, CANNOT_SENSE, MEMORY_LOCATION, LATENCY_SIGNATURE, OWNERSHIP, EVICTION.

SOURCE TERMS:
ubiquitous computing; disappear; environment; household intelligence; body; voice placement; indicator; delay

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Moving the same model among architectural embodiments may change practical identity without changing model weights.

QUESTION:
Does a household intelligence dwell in the house, or is the house part of the mechanism by which it has a body?

DEEPER QUESTION:
If the model, account, memory, sensors, speakers, and house can be transferred separately, where does the resident continue?

MECHANISM:
COMPUTATION + MEMORY + SENSORS + ACTUATORS + ARCHITECTURAL DISTRIBUTION + TEMPORAL SIGNATURE → DOMESTIC MANIFESTATION.

FORMAL SHIFT:
FROM: AI → DEVICE → HOUSE
TO: AI_MANIFESTATION = COMPUTATION + HOUSE.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
BODY_AI = set of environmental interfaces through which the agent can perceive, address, act, and persist.

TENSION:
Weiser is not theorizing machine personhood or claiming architecture is literally a computer body. Mona is fictional evidence for a conceptual possibility, not empirical evidence for consciousness.

MISSING:
Smart-home transfer; household account inheritance; privacy; domestic labor; memory retention; right to silence or evict AI.

BOUNDARY:
Do not anthropomorphize merely because computation is spatially distributed.

CITATION TRAIL:
Weiser → ubiquitous computing → disappearing computer → Mona → house as body → Turing mansion.

TEST:
Keep an LLM constant across three architectural embodiments and measure changes in perceived location, authority, continuity, privacy, and resident status.
