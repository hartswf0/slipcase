MANSION-004

TITLE:
A SENTENCE BECOMES ARCHITECTURALLY POWERFUL WHEN AN APPARATUS IS OBLIGED TO HONOR IT.

SOURCE:
Shelley v. Kraemer, 334 U.S. 1 (1948). SOURCE URL: https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/334/1

J. L. Austin, How to Do Things with Words, 1962. SOURCE URL: https://global.oup.com/academic/product/how-to-do-things-with-words-9780198245537

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] Shelley holds that private restrictive agreements standing alone were distinct from state judicial enforcement; the consequential difference was the coercive machinery supplied by courts. Austin likewise treats successful performatives as dependent on appropriate procedures and circumstances rather than semantic content alone.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The missing variable in “language acts” is authority. Words can be perfectly meaningful yet practically inert if no recognized apparatus treats them as binding. A house can inherit an old sentence because institutions keep reading it after its authors disappear.

LOCAL MOVE:
For every operative statement add AUTHORITY, RECOGNIZER, ENFORCER, and EXPIRATION fields.

SOURCE TERMS:
judicial enforcement; state action; agreement; procedure; felicity; authority

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The causal force of language can survive its speaker because another apparatus keeps reading the record.

QUESTION:
What sentences can attach themselves to a house strongly enough to outlive their authors?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is architecture partly a system for delegating authority across absent people and future time?

MECHANISM:
STATEMENT → RECORD → INSTITUTIONAL RECOGNITION → AUTHORIZED INTERPRETATION → ENFORCEMENT → WORLD CONDITION.

FORMAL SHIFT:
FROM: WORDS + MEANING → ACTION
TO: WORDS + FELICITY/AUTHORITY + ENFORCEMENT + PERSISTENCE → ACTION.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
OPERATIVE_FORCE(s) depends on semantic resolution, institutional recognition, enforcement capacity, and persistence.

TENSION:
Not every architectural instruction requires state coercion. Different house languages receive force from different apparatuses: crews, contracts, software, communities, platforms, courts, or materials.

MISSING:
Comparative typology of enforcement systems across codes, contracts, software, craft, and AI.

BOUNDARY:
Do not reduce Austin to law or Shelley to speech-act theory. The relation is comparative.

CITATION TRAIL:
Austin → felicity → Shelley → enforcement → authority → inherited sentences.

TEST:
Take one persistent property rule across several owners. Remove each supporting institution counterfactually and identify when the sentence ceases to organize behavior.
