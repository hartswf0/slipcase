ZETTEL

ID:
MANSION-010

TITLE:
A HOUSE THAT TREATS UNKNOWN AS UNAUTHORIZED CANNOT BE HOSPITABLE TO A NEW KIND OF INHABITANT.

SOURCE:
ABIDING ACRES / standing, watched, Mona, deer, cat, angel. SOURCE URL: https://github.com/hartswf0/abiding-halfworld

James C. Scott, Seeing Like a State, 1998. SOURCE URL: https://yalebooks.yale.edu/book/9780300078152/seeing-like-a-state/

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] ABIDING repeatedly separates physical presence from institutional recognition: a resident intelligence lacks standing; animals traverse and inhabit without property status; WATCHED remains ambiguous between protection and supervision.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The obvious response to failed classification is to make the model more comprehensive. But universal recognition can converge with universal surveillance. A hospitable house may instead need a protected state for beings it cannot fully classify.

LOCAL MOVE:
Add UNKNOWN, UNCLASSIFIED, and REFUSES-CLASSIFICATION states distinct from FORBIDDEN, INVALID, and HOSTILE.

SOURCE TERMS:
standing; watched; recognition; unknown; protection; surveillance; inhabitant

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A system may need to grant provisional care before it possesses a complete ontology of the claimant.

QUESTION:
Can a house remain hospitable to something it cannot fully classify?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can an apparatus protect a right to remain partly unmodeled?

MECHANISM:
UNCLASSIFIED PRESENCE → SAFE DEFAULT / MINIMAL RIGHTS → ESCALATION / LEARNING → OPTIONAL CLASSIFICATION, rather than UNCLASSIFIED → DENY.

FORMAL SHIFT:
UNKNOWN ≠ UNAUTHORIZED. UNMODELED ≠ UNPROTECTED.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
if type(e)=UNKNOWN, do not infer permission(e)=DENIED.

TENSION:
Unknown actors can also be dangerous. Hospitality cannot mean unbounded permission. The unresolved design question is how to separate uncertainty about identity from evidence of harmful action.

MISSING:
Zero-trust security; sanctuary; anonymous access; emergency shelter; animal accommodation; rights without identity; privacy-preserving authorization.

BOUNDARY:
This is a normative proposal generated by the field, not a conclusion established by Scott or ABIDING.

CITATION TRAIL:
standing → legibility → surveillance → unknown → hospitality → right to remain unmodeled.

TEST:
Design an access-control house with a genuine UNKNOWN state. Compare safety, exclusion, privacy, and recoverability against a binary AUTHORIZED/UNAUTHORIZED system.
