MANSION-002

TITLE:
WITTGENSTEIN MAKES LANGUAGE A CITY WITH NEW REGULAR SUBURBS; PROMPT SYSTEMS MAY BE A NEW DISTRICT RATHER THAN A UNIVERSAL LANGUAGE.

SOURCE:
Ludwig Wittgenstein, Philosophical Investigations, 4th ed., §18. SOURCE URL: https://www.wiley-vch.de/en/areas-interest/humanities-social-sciences/philosophy-12pl/historical-western-philosophy-12pl4/wittgenstein-12pl45/philosophical-investigations-978-1-4051-5928-9

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE] Wittgenstein compares language to an ancient city containing old and new houses and later additions, surrounded by newer regular suburbs.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The architectural metaphor for language is not singular. A language can accumulate districts made under different historical logics. Natural-language AI therefore need not be described as English becoming code. It may be a new regular suburb whose smooth frontage connects users to older districts: legal language, CAD, databases, building codes, craft, contracts, and machine control.

LOCAL MOVE:
Replace UNIVERSAL NATURAL-LANGUAGE PROGRAMMING with SUBURBAN INTERFACE: trace every border crossing hidden behind one fluent instruction.

SOURCE TERMS:
ancient city; houses; streets; squares; additions; suburbs; language

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The apparent regularity of the newest district can conceal the heterogeneity of the city it depends on.

QUESTION:
What older linguistic districts must a prompt cross before it can change a house?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does conversational AI democratize access to specialized languages, or merely hide their jurisdictions and defaults behind a common frontage?

MECHANISM:
NATURAL-LANGUAGE FRONTAGE → TRANSLATION → DOMAIN LANGUAGES → INSTITUTIONS/MODELS/MACHINES → WORLD CHANGE.

FORMAL SHIFT:
FROM: NATURAL LANGUAGE BECOMES UNIVERSAL CODE
TO: NATURAL LANGUAGE BECOMES A NEW SUBURB CONNECTED TO OLDER OPERATIVE DISTRICTS.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
CITY_LANGUAGE = heterogeneous historical layers; PROMPT_SUBURB = interface district that routes into them.

TENSION:
Wittgenstein’s city is a comparison in an argument about language, not a literal theory of urban infrastructure or AI.

MISSING:
Border protocols; translation loss; hidden defaults; institutional gatekeeping between linguistic districts.

BOUNDARY:
The AI-suburb interpretation is ours.

CITATION TRAIL:
Wittgenstein §18 → language city → regular suburbs → prompt interface → hidden jurisdictions.

TEST:
Take one prompt such as “add a bedroom.” Enumerate every specialist language and authority required before the request can become a lawful, structurally valid, represented, and built alteration.
