MANSION-005

TITLE:
PHYSICAL PRESENCE IS NOT ENOUGH: THE APPARATUS MUST HAVE A TYPE THROUGH WHICH YOUR PRESENCE CAN BECOME A CLAIM.

SOURCE:
Terry Winograd, “Understanding Natural Language,” Cognitive Psychology 3(1), 1972. SOURCE URL: https://doi.org/10.1016/0010-0285(72)90002-3

James C. Scott, Seeing Like a State, 1998. SOURCE URL: https://yalebooks.yale.edu/book/9780300078152/seeing-like-a-state/

ABIDING ACRES / abiding-halfworld. SOURCE URL: https://github.com/hartswf0/abiding-halfworld

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] SHRDLU acts only on objects and relations available in its microworld. Scott analyzes administrative simplification and legibility. ABIDING defines STANDING through the problem of becoming somebody an apparatus can hear as a party.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
A being may be materially present yet operationally absent. The deer cross; Mona speaks; the dead leave records; the cat moves among houses. But administrative and computational systems act only through categories they can recognize. Presence, legibility, addressability, standing, and protection must therefore be separated.

LOCAL MOVE:
Never infer P→L→S→R. Track PHYSICAL PRESENCE, LEGIBILITY, ADDRESSABILITY, STANDING, and RESPONSE as independent predicates.

SOURCE TERMS:
world model; legibility; standing; party; name; form; apparatus

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Protection often requires classification, but classification can exclude what most needs protection.

QUESTION:
What forms of existence disappear operationally when action requires prior classification?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can an apparatus become obligated by something it cannot yet classify?

MECHANISM:
PRESENCE → DETECTION → IDENTIFICATION → CLASSIFICATION → VALID ADDRESS → STANDING → RESPONSE; each arrow can fail.

FORMAL SHIFT:
ENTITY EXISTS ≠ ENTITY REPRESENTED ≠ ENTITY ADDRESSABLE ≠ ENTITY HAS STANDING ≠ ENTITY PROTECTED.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
SHRDLU has a restricted represented domain; legal standing has institutional criteria; Scott provides a theory of administrative legibility, not a formal logic.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
P(e), L(e), A(e), S(e), R(e) are distinct predicates; no implication is assumed without evidence.

TENSION:
Legibility can enable rights, care, and coordination while also enabling surveillance, normalization, taxation, exclusion, and control.

MISSING:
Animal standing; machine personhood; undocumented residence; anonymity; refusal of legibility; automated benefits and harms.

BOUNDARY:
Scott, Winograd, and ABIDING are not one genealogy. The relation is structural.

CITATION TRAIL:
Winograd → represented world → Scott → legibility → ABIDING standing → unrecognized inhabitants.

TEST:
For every recurring Abiding entity, mark presence, name, address, roster status, ownership, capacity to sign, ability to claim, visibility, and protection. Study mismatches rather than scene counts.
