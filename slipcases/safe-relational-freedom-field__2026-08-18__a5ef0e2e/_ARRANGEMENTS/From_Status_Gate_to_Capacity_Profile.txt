FROM STATUS GATE TO CAPACITY PROFILE

PROVISIONAL ARRANGEMENT — DERIVED, NOT EVIDENCE

01. [[SAFE-06]] — The license needs a theory of admission before it can promise universal rights
02. [[SAFE-06-01]] — A capacity test becomes dangerous when failing it removes the standing needed to contest the test
03. [[SAFE-06-02]] — The assessment can change the environment instead of changing the person's status
04. [[SAFE-06-03]] — Legal personality is a representational technology, not a consciousness certificate
05. [[SAFE-06-04]] — The evidential threshold for protection can be lower than the threshold for certainty about what the protected being is
06. [[SAFE-06-06]] — Unknown moral status is already a governance state
07. [[SAFE-06-07]] — A serious assessment can end in an indicator profile instead of a verdict
08. [[SAFE-06-05]] — Recognition can arrive before protection and leave the newly recognized being in a legal gap
