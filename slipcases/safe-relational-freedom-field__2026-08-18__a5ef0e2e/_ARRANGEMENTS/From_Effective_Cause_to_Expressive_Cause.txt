FROM EFFECTIVE CAUSE TO EXPRESSIVE CAUSE

PROVISIONAL ARRANGEMENT — DERIVED, NOT EVIDENCE

01. [[BGS-1884-05]] — Nottage locates authorship in the “effective cause,” not necessarily the owner or beneficiary
02. [[BGS-1884-09]] — A causally pivotal intervention can still contribute zero copyrightable authorship
03. [[BGS-1884-10]] — Creative contribution is not authorship; authorship may be a position of control over the whole
04. [[BGS-1884-13]] — Unpredictability does not defeat authorship; uncontrolled expressive determination might
05. [[BGS-1884-16]] — Changeability stops being fatal when the changing states belong to an authored possibility space
