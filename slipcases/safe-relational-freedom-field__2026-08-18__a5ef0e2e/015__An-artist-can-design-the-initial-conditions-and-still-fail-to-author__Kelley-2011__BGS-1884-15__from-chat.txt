ID:
BGS-1884-15

TITLE:
An artist can design the initial conditions and still fail to author what the system later becomes

SOURCE:
U.S. Court of Appeals for the Seventh Circuit — Kelley v. Chicago Park District — 2011 — 635 F.3d 290.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Kelley intentionally designed and planted Wildflower Works, but the court held that a living garden lacked the required authorship and stable fixation. It emphasized that natural forces determine much of a garden’s changing form even though a gardener establishes its initial arrangement.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Sarony’s arrangement logic reaches an unexpected boundary.

Arrangement is not necessarily authorship when the arranged system remains active after arrangement and continues producing perceptible form through processes not attributable to the arranger.

LOCAL MOVE:
The court shifts attention from who establishes the initial configuration to who or what determines the work as it continues to exist.

SOURCE TERMS:
planted
cultivated
initial arrangement
natural forces
alive

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Kelley did what our Burrow-Giles reading seemed to privilege:

he chose the site,
selected materials,
arranged them,
initiated the work,
and cultivated it.

Yet the court says this is not the required kind of authorship.

So “constructing generative conditions” cannot by itself be enough.

QUESTION:
When does authorship of initial conditions fail to become authorship of later states?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can an authored system generate unauthored states?

MECHANISM:

TENSION:
[[BGS-1884-01]] proposed that authorship might reside in construction of generative conditions.

Kelley is a direct pressure test: constructing conditions may produce a system whose subsequent form owes too much to processes outside the constructor.

MISSING:
A criterion for determining when later system behavior remains an execution of authored constraints and when it becomes independent expressive production.

BOUNDARY:
Kelley intertwines authorship with fixation and concerns a living garden. Its reasoning cannot be mechanically transferred to computational systems.
