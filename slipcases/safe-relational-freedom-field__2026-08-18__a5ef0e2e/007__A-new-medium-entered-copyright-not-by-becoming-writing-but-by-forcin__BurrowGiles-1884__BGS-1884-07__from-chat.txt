TITLE:
A new medium entered copyright not by becoming writing but by forcing “writing” to change scale

SOURCE:
U.S. Supreme Court — Burrow-Giles Lithographic Co. v. Sarony — 1884 — 111 U.S. 53, 56–58. fileciteturn0file1L41-L69

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The constitutional challenge claimed that a photograph was neither a “writing” nor the production of an “author.” The Court rejected a narrow material definition, treating writings as forms through which authors’ ideas receive visible expression and noting that photography could not have appeared in early statutory lists because the technology did not yet exist. fileciteturn0file1L43-L53 fileciteturn0file1L61-L69

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The arrival of a new representational technology destabilized inherited legal nouns. The solution was not to decide whether photography literally resembled writing, but to redescribe the function performed by “writing.”

LOCAL MOVE:
The Court moves from medium-specific constitutional vocabulary toward a functional category broad enough to contain technologies unknown to the framers.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
“Writing” survives technological change by ceasing to name a particular inscription technology.

QUESTION:
What happens to a legal category when new machinery produces an object that performs the category’s function without sharing its historical material form?

DEEPER QUESTION:
How many technological mutations can a category absorb before reinterpretation becomes the creation of a new category under an old name?

TENSION:
The enlarged category solves one problem while relocating another. Once photography can qualify as a “writing,” the harder question becomes which photographs contain the requisite authorship.

MISSING:
A general rule specifying when functional reinterpretation legitimately extends an old legal category rather than exceeds it.

BOUNDARY:
The Court says the Constitution is broad enough to cover photographs “so far as they are representatives of original intellectual conceptions of the author.” It does not make technological novelty alone sufficient. fileciteturn0file1L65-L69
