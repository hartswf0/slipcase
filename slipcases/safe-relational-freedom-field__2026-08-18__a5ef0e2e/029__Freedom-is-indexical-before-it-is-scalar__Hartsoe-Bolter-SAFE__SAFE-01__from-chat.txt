ZETTEL

ID:
SAFE-01

TITLE:
Freedom is indexical before it is scalar

SOURCE:
Watson Hartsoe and Jay Bolter — The Sapient Agent Freedom Equation (SAFE): A Universal Framework for Quantifying Freedom in Sapient Agents — pp. 19–22.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
For scenario F9, restricting AI roles is interpreted simultaneously as potentially promoting “societal freedom” and restricting AI technological evolution; another evaluation calls the same action non-indicative of freedom precisely because it imposes limitations on AI. fileciteturn7file0L268-L302

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The FREEDOM score has no stable meaning until the bearer of freedom is specified.

LOCAL MOVE:
The source accidentally reveals that one intervention can increase one agent's freedom while decreasing another's.

SOURCE TERMS:
societal freedom
restricts
evolution
limitations
AI
freedom

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The disagreement may not be disagreement about how much freedom exists.

The models may be measuring different people's freedom.

QUESTION:
Whose freedom does F measure?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can freedom in a multi-agent system ever be represented by one scalar without hiding transfers of freedom between agents?

MECHANISM:
regulation
→ constrains AI action-space
→ may enlarge human/social protection
→ different freedom effects occur simultaneously

FORMAL SHIFT:
<ACTION>
→ <EFFECTS ON MULTIPLE AGENTS>
→ [ATTRIBUTE FREEDOM CHANGE TO EACH AGENT]
→ <DISTRIBUTION OF FREEDOMS>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
F ∈ {-4,-3,-2,-1,0,1,2,3,4}, where ratings indicate how strongly an action reflects freedom. fileciteturn5file2L137-L159

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Replace:

F(action)

with:

F(action, agent_i)

or:

ΔF =
{human: +?,
 AI: -?,
 institution: +?,
 affected third party: ?}

The primitive object may be a freedom vector rather than a freedom score.

TENSION:
SAFE defines freedom as the availability of options and absence of undue restriction, but F9 demonstrates that restricting one actor may be the mechanism by which another actor gains meaningful options or protection. fileciteturn5file4L355-L363

MISSING:
The bearer, beneficiary, and loser of every scored freedom change.

BOUNDARY:
The evidence does not show that vector-valued freedom is sufficient. It shows that the current scalar question can become ambiguous when agents' freedoms conflict.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[SAFE]]
→ F9
→ competing bearers of freedom
→ relational and multi-agent theories of liberty

TEST:
Rescore every SAFE freedom scenario separately for each materially affected agent.

Identify cases in which the sign of the freedom judgment reverses when the bearer changes.
