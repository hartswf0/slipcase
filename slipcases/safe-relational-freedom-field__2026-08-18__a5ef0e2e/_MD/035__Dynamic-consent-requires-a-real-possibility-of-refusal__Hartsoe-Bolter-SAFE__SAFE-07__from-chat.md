ZETTEL

ID:
SAFE-07

TITLE:
Dynamic consent requires a real possibility of refusal

SOURCE:
Watson Hartsoe and Jay Bolter — The Sapient Agent Freedom Equation (SAFE): A Universal Framework for Quantifying Freedom in Sapient Agents — pp. 4–5.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
SAFE combines autonomous engagement with collective welfare and defines dynamic consent as balancing individual self-interest with ethical care and responsiveness to collective needs and rights. fileciteturn5file8L653-L677

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Consent is placed inside an equilibrium requirement that may itself constrain what counts as an acceptable refusal.

LOCAL MOVE:
The License tries to solve conflicts among autonomy, welfare, self-interest, and care through continuing balance.

SOURCE TERMS:
autonomous agency
collective welfare
dynamic consent
self-interest
ethical care
collective needs
rights

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Consent normally constrains collective claims.

Here collective claims are partly internal to the definition of proper consent.

QUESTION:
Can consent still function as a veto if a refusal can be rejected for insufficient responsiveness to collective needs?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does equilibrium turn consent from a boundary into one negotiable variable among others?

MECHANISM:
agent preference
→ evaluated against collective welfare / care
→ balancing process
→ accepted or constrained course of action

FORMAL SHIFT:
<CONSENT AS VETO>
→ <CONSENT AS INPUT TO BALANCING>
→ [ETHICAL EQUILIBRATION]
→ <NEGOTIATED PERMISSION>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Article VII associates dynamic consent with ESI, S, and C: ethical care, self-interest, and responsiveness to collective needs and rights. fileciteturn5file8L675-L681

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Strong consent:

NO_i → operation forbidden

Balanced consent:

NO_i + welfare_j + care_k
→ adjudication
→ ?

The second requires an override rule.

TENSION:
SAFE's definition of freedom emphasizes the ability to choose and act without undue restriction, while its License also authorizes restrictions in service of collective welfare. fileciteturn5file4L355-L363 fileciteturn5file8L656-L662

MISSING:
Right to refuse,
right to exit,
override threshold,
emergency exceptions,
who adjudicates,
and whether consent can be withdrawn asymmetrically.

BOUNDARY:
Collective life necessarily limits some choices. The unresolved issue is not whether limits exist but whether calling the resulting procedure “consent” obscures when a veto has ceased to operate.

TEST:
Construct a case where a sapient agent gives an informed, competent, persistent “no” to an action that strongly benefits the wider community.

Run the SAFE License literally.

Determine whether the action proceeds.
