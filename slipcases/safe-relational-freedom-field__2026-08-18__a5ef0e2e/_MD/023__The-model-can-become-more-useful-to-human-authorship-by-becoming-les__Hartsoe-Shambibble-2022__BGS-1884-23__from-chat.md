TITLE:
The model can become more useful to human authorship by becoming less creatively interesting

SOURCE:
Shambibble — interview with Watson Hartsoe — October 22, 2022 — 51:53–55:21. fileciteturn3file0L65-L95

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Shambibble describes Midjourney’s attraction as its ability to combine concepts unexpectedly and fill in cool details when given vague instructions. He speculates that a “fuzzier” model may produce more random inspiration, whereas larger or more literal models may be better at simply producing “the thing that you said.” fileciteturn3file0L65-L67 fileciteturn3file0L89-L95

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The aesthetic value prized by the practitioner may be exactly the property that weakens a human-control theory of authorship.

LOCAL MOVE:
The interview separates obedience from generative surprise and places much of the perceived creativity on the side of the system’s unexpected completion.

SOURCE TERMS:
creativity
underspecified
filling in some details
random inspiration
fuzzier model
the thing that you said

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A better “creative collaborator” may be a worse copyright instrument.

If the machine becomes more obedient:

human instruction
≈ resulting expression.

If the machine becomes more surprising:

human instruction
≠ many resulting particulars.

QUESTION:
Could human copyrightability increase as machine creativity decreases?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does an authorship doctrine centered on human control structurally reward obedient generators and penalize aesthetically fertile ones?

MECHANISM:
literal system:
human conception
→ high-fidelity execution
→ fewer machine-determined details

fuzzy system:
human conception
→ interpretive expansion
→ unexpected expressive particulars

OUR FORMALIZATION:

Hypothesis:

MODEL_AUTONOMY ↑
→ HUMAN_CONTROL ↓

while potentially:

MODEL_AUTONOMY ↑
→ PERCEIVED_NOVELTY ↑

Therefore:

PERCEIVED_CREATIVITY
and
HUMAN_AUTHORSHIP

may be negatively correlated under some interface conditions.

TENSION:
Human artists routinely use chance, improvisation, collaborators, materials, and environments precisely to escape their own intentions.

A doctrine that rewards only obedience risks mistaking artistic control for exact command.

MISSING:
A theory capable of distinguishing:

productive surprise inside an authored process

from

expressive determination delegated to another generator.

BOUNDARY:
Shambibble is offering a practitioner hypothesis about model behavior, not an empirical law about model size, creativity, or copyrightability.
