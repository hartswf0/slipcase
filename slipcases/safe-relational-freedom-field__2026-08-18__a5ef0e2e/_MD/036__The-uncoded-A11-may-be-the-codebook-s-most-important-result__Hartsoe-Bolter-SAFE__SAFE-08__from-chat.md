TITLE:
The uncoded A11 may be the codebook's most important result

SOURCE:
Watson Hartsoe and Jay Bolter — The Sapient Agent Freedom Equation (SAFE): A Universal Framework for Quantifying Freedom in Sapient Agents — pp. 16–17, 24–25, 32.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
After four rounds of theme construction, the “Coded Disagreements” section assigns A11 an empty code list even though A11 concerns autonomous, self-learning LLM persuasion and is one of the paper's high-disagreement scenarios. Later sections require an extended dedicated analysis to articulate its autonomy, persuasion, and ethical dimensions. fileciteturn5file12L891-L918 fileciteturn7file0L461-L500
