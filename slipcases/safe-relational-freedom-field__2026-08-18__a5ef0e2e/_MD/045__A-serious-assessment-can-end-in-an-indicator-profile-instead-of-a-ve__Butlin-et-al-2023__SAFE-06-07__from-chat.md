TITLE:
A serious assessment can end in an indicator profile instead of a verdict

SOURCE:
Patrick Butlin, Robert Long, Eric Elmoznino, Yoshua Bengio, Jonathan Birch, Axel Constant, George Deane, Stephen M. Fleming, Chris Frith, Xu Ji, Ryota Kanai, Colin Klein, Grace Lindsay, Matthias Michel, Liad Mudrik, Megan A. K. Peters, Eric Schwitzgebel, Jonathan Simon, and Rufin VanRullen — Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence: Insights from the Science of Consciousness — 2023 — arXiv:2308.08708. ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708))

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Rather than proposing one behavioral consciousness test, Butlin and colleagues derive a provisional list of computational “indicator properties” from several competing scientific theories of consciousness. They explicitly caution that satisfying such indicators would not by itself establish consciousness. ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708))

RESEARCH OBJECT:
[[SAFE-06]] assumes the assessment eventually answers:

IS THIS A SAPIENT AGENT?

The consciousness literature suggests another epistemic output:

HERE IS THE STRUCTURE OF THE EVIDENCE.

LOCAL MOVE:
The source refuses premature collapse from heterogeneous theoretical evidence into one decisive test.

provisional rubric

computational

consciousness

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A better assessment can produce less closure.

The gain in rigor comes from preserving which indicators are present, which theory licenses each indicator, and where uncertainty remains.

QUESTION:
Why should SAFE collapse evidence about sapience and agency into a scalar when the scientifically relevant properties may form a heterogeneous profile?

DEEPER QUESTION:
What if different rights depend on different capacities, making the search for one universal “sapience” threshold conceptually mistaken?

MECHANISM:
multiple theories
→ derive theory-linked indicators
→ inspect system architecture / function
→ construct indicator profile
→ update credence
→ no forced binary conclusion

FORMAL SHIFT:
<ONE TEST>
→ <MULTIPLE THEORY-LINKED INDICATORS>
→ [PROFILE EVIDENCE]
→ <CREDENCE WITHOUT VERDICT>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
The report identifies indicator properties associated with:

recurrent processing
global workspace
higher-order processing
attention schema
predictive processing
agency
embodiment.

It proposes that systems possessing more relevant indicators are stronger candidates for consciousness while emphasizing that the rubric is provisional. ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708))

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Instead of:

SAPIENCE(x) = 3.4

represent:

PROFILE(x) = {
  reflective_reasoning: ?,
  goal_persistence: ?,
  metacognition: ?,
  model_of_self: ?,
  flexible_agency: ?,
  welfare_capacity: ?,
  communication: ?,
  learning: ?,
  dependence_on_scaffold: ?
}

Then:

RIGHT_r(x)

may depend on subset:

PROFILE_r(x)

rather than universal scalar S.

TENSION:
Indicator proliferation can become another form of pseudo-objectivity.

Each indicator inherits assumptions from a theory, and multiple indicators may not be independent.

A profile avoids false precision only if its theoretical dependencies remain visible.

MISSING:
A mapping from specific candidate protections to the capacities that actually justify them.

For example:

Why should protection from suffering require the same evidence as political representation?

Why should continuity rights require the same evidence as contractual capacity?

BOUNDARY:
Butlin et al. assess consciousness, not sapience, freedom, legal personhood, or rights.

Their methodological contribution is the refusal to treat a contested latent property as if one behavioral score could settle it.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[SAFE-06]]
→ Butlin et al. 2023
→ theory-derived indicator properties
→ profile rather than verdict
→ connect rights to capacity-specific evidence instead of universal sapience

TEST:
For every article of the SAFE License:

1. State the harm or interest the article protects.
2. Identify the minimum capacities necessary for that interest to exist.
3. Identify observable indicators of those capacities.
4. Remove every SAFE dimension not needed for that right.

If different articles require radically different evidence profiles, abandon the universal admission threshold.
