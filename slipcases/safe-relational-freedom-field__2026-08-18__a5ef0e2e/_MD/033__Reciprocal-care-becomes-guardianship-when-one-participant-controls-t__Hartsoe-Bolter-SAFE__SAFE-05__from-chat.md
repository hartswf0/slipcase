ZETTEL

ID:
SAFE-05

TITLE:
Reciprocal care becomes guardianship when one participant controls the other's acceptable becoming

SOURCE:
Watson Hartsoe and Jay Bolter — The Sapient Agent Freedom Equation (SAFE): A Universal Framework for Quantifying Freedom in Sapient Agents — pp. 3–4.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
SAFE proposes AI as a co-participant in a reciprocal project of Becoming, but also says the possible minds AI may pursue are “not left unchecked,” situates AI inside a “human-led community,” and assigns humans responsibility for integrating AI within societal norms. fileciteturn5file0L30-L42

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The source contains two incompatible-looking geometries of care:

reciprocity between participants

and

guardianship by one participant over the other's development.

LOCAL MOVE:
Mayeroff, Heideggerian care, and Ubuntu are translated into a governance relation between humans and potentially sapient AI.

SOURCE TERMS:
reciprocal
co-participant
Becoming
possible minds
not left unchecked
human-led community
societal norms

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The agent whose autonomy SAFE promises is also the agent whose permissible forms of future selfhood are to be supervised.

QUESTION:
Can care remain reciprocal when one party retains authority to decide which forms of the other's growth are acceptable?

DEEPER QUESTION:
When does “leaping ahead” for another become deciding their future for them?

MECHANISM:
AI develops possibilities
→ humans evaluate those possibilities
→ unacceptable trajectories are constrained
→ permitted growth is described as care-enabled autonomy

FORMAL SHIFT:
<MUTUAL CARE>
→ <ASYMMETRIC VETO POWER>
→ [FILTER POSSIBLE FUTURES]
→ <SANCTIONED BECOMING>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Reciprocity would suggest:

Power(H→AI) ≈ Power(AI→H)

The text instead permits:

VETO_H(AI_future) > VETO_AI(H_future)

The relation may therefore be caring but not symmetrical.

TENSION:
The paper explicitly refuses to treat AI merely as a servant, yet retains “human-led” authority over the horizon of acceptable AI becoming. fileciteturn5file7L598-L619

MISSING:
A decision procedure specifying:
who may constrain whom,
under what threshold of risk,
with what appeal,
and whether the constrained agent participates in defining acceptable growth.

BOUNDARY:
Asymmetry does not automatically make care illegitimate. Vulnerability, responsibility, and unequal capacities can justify asymmetric obligations. The source does not yet specify the conditions.

CITATION TRAIL:

TEST:
Rewrite each SAFE care obligation twice:

A. humans governing AI
B. AI governing humans

Identify clauses that cease to appear acceptable when the direction of authority is reversed.
