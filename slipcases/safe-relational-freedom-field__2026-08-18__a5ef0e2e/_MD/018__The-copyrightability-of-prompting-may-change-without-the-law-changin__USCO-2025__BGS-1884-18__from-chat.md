TITLE:
The copyrightability of prompting may change without the law changing at all

SOURCE:
U.S. Copyright Office — Copyright and Artificial Intelligence, Part 2: Copyrightability — 2025.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The Copyright Office concludes that prompts alone do not provide sufficient expressive control with current generally available systems, but explicitly allows that future systems giving users greater control over expressive elements could require a different conclusion. It likewise notes that further automation could move the analysis in the opposite direction.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
“Is prompting authorship?” may be a malformed legal question.

Prompting does not name a stable operation.

Its legal significance may depend on the control semantics implemented by the particular machine receiving the prompt.

LOCAL MOVE:
The Office makes its conclusion technology-contingent rather than declaring natural-language instruction inherently nonauthorial.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The same sentence typed by the same human could potentially occupy different authorship relations depending on the system that interprets it.

This means the legally relevant object may not be the prompt text.

It may be the prompt-machine relation.

QUESTION:
What properties of an interface transform an instruction from a request into an expressive operation?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Could authorship become a property of control semantics rather than linguistic semantics?

MECHANISM:
Human issues identical description P.

SYSTEM A:
P → weak stochastic influence over many expressive variables.

SYSTEM B:
P → precise persistent constraints over identified expressive variables.

Same words.
Different operation.
Potentially different authorship analysis.

SOURCE FORMALISM:

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Meaning for authorship is not:

SEMANTICS(P)

but perhaps:

CONTROL_EFFECT(P, SYSTEM, STATE)

Therefore:

AUTHORIAL_FORCE(P)
is not intrinsic to P.

TENSION:
Burrow-Giles itself arose because a new apparatus destabilized old categories, yet its answer ultimately focused on what Sarony could do through that apparatus.

The current AI problem may repeat this structure at a finer grain: not whether the medium is authorial, but what operations a particular interface actually affords.

MISSING:
A rigorous vocabulary for comparing generative interfaces according to:
constraint persistence,
variable addressability,
local editability,
repeatability,
causal transparency,
and preservation of prior human decisions.

BOUNDARY:
The Copyright Office does not predict that prompting will become copyrightable. It states only that increased control over expressive elements could call for a different conclusion.

TEST:

Document not output quality but which expressive variables the human can actually set, preserve, and revise.
