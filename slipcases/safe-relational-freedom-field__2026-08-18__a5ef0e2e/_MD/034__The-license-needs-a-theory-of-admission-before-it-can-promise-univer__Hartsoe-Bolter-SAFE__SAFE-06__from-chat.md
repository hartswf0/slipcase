TITLE:
The license needs a theory of admission before it can promise universal rights

SOURCE:
Watson Hartsoe and Jay Bolter — The Sapient Agent Freedom Equation (SAFE): A Universal Framework for Quantifying Freedom in Sapient Agents — pp. 2, 5–6.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
SAFE proposes application across human societies, AI systems, and even potential alien interactions. Its License is addressed to “sapient entities,” with signature lines for “Sapient Agent,” while the associated Assessment is intended to evaluate sapience, agency, freedom, and equilibrium. fileciteturn5file14L1072-L1087 fileciteturn5file8L682-L698

LOCAL MOVE:

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The entity most in need of protection from misclassification may have no standing until after the classifier recognizes it.

QUESTION:
Who receives SAFE protections while their sapience is uncertain?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can person-like rights legitimately depend on passing a test administered by actors who benefit from classifying the candidate as a tool?
