BGS-1884-05

TITLE:
Nottage locates authorship in the “effective cause,” not necessarily the owner or beneficiary

SOURCE:
Nottage v. Jackson — Court of Appeal — 1883 — 11 Q.B.D. 627, as discussed in Burrow-Giles, 111 U.S. 53, 60–61. fileciteturn0file1L83-L95

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
A photographic business sent an employee to take a team photograph. The owners claimed authorship, but the court held that the person who actually arranged and took the photograph was the author. Brett M.R. characterized the author as the “effective cause” who superintended the arrangement; Cotton L.J. described the author as the “inventive or master mind.” fileciteturn0file1L87-L95

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Authorship follows operative causal responsibility rather than ownership of the production system, commissioning power, or economic benefit.

LOCAL MOVE:
Burrow-Giles imports Nottage as supporting authority for a production-centered account of photographic authorship.

SOURCE TERMS:
effective cause
superintended the arrangement
formed the picture
inventive or master mind
originating
making
producing
gives effect to the idea

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The person with the largest institutional claim over a production can fail to be its author if another person occupies the operative position where the work is actually determined.

QUESTION:
Where, inside a distributed production pipeline, is the “effective cause” of the resulting expressive artifact?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can authorship be diagnosed by locating the point at which alternatives are actually selected and the artifact becomes this artifact rather than another?

MECHANISM:
Commissioning owners establish the project, but an employee performs the consequential arrangement. Authorship follows the latter operation.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<COMMISSION / OWNERSHIP>
→ <PRODUCTION WORKER>
→ [ARRANGE / FORM / PRODUCE]
→ <PICTURE>

AUTHORSHIP attaches downstream rather than automatically propagating from ownership.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

For production chain:

A → B → C → ARTIFACT

Do not ask only:
WHO STARTED THE CHAIN?

Ask:
AT WHICH NODE WERE EXPRESSIVE ALTERNATIVES EFFECTIVELY DETERMINED?

TENSION:
“Effective cause” and “master mind” are not obviously identical tests. One can emphasize proximal execution; the other can emphasize governing conception.

MISSING:
The cited passages do not resolve a production in which the “master mind” and the proximal “effective cause” are different people.

BOUNDARY:
The source does not prove that authorship always belongs to the person physically nearest the final act of fixation. Its own vocabulary also includes superintendence, arrangement, and giving effect to an idea.

TEST:
Recover the complete Nottage opinions and map every formulation of authorship by Brett, Cotton, and Bowen. Test whether “effective cause,” “master mind,” and “gives effect to the idea” actually converge or encode rival theories.
