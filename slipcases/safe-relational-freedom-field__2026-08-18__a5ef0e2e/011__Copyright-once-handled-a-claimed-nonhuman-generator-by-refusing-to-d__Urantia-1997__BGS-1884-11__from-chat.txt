ZETTEL

ID:
BGS-1884-11

TITLE:
Copyright once handled a claimed nonhuman generator by refusing to decide whether the generator was real

SOURCE:
U.S. Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit — Urantia Foundation v. Maaherra — 1997 — 114 F.3d 955.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Both sides maintained that celestial beings supplied the teachings later assembled into the Urantia Book. Humans formed a group, posed specific questions, received answers, and arranged the resulting material. The court explicitly refused to make copyrightability depend on resolving the work’s claimed supernatural origin and instead examined the human selection and arrangement.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The court avoids the apparently unavoidable question:

WHO REALLY GENERATED THE WORDS?

It replaces it with a narrower one:

WHAT HUMANLY CREATED STRUCTURE CAN BE IDENTIFIED REGARDLESS OF WHERE THE RAW MATERIAL CAME FROM?

LOCAL MOVE:
The court brackets metaphysics and isolates copyrightable human operations.

SOURCE TERMS:
celestial beings
specific questions
answers
transmitted
compiled
selected
coordinated
arranged
human creativity

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The genealogy of “nonhuman generation” in copyright does not begin with artificial intelligence.

Urantia presents a production story in which humans ask questions of a purported nonhuman intelligence and receive verbal outputs whose claimed source lies outside ordinary human authorship.

The court does not need to declare the nonhuman source impossible.

QUESTION:
Can authorship be adjudicated without deciding who or what generated some of the material inside the work?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does copyright become more stable when it stops seeking one ultimate origin and instead protects locally identifiable human transformations?

MECHANISM:
claimed nonhuman source
→ humans formulate questions
→ answers arrive
→ humans select / coordinate / arrange
→ compilation becomes candidate human authorship

FORMAL SHIFT:
<ONTOLOGICALLY DISPUTED SOURCE MATERIAL>
→ <IDENTIFIABLE HUMAN OPERATIONS>
→ [SELECT / COORDINATE / ARRANGE]
→ <HUMAN-AUTHORED COMPILATION LAYER>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Do not solve:

ORIGIN(material) = ?

Instead ask:

∃ human transformation H
such that
H(material) = protectable expressive structure?

TENSION:
[[BGS-1884-03]] treated authorship as a trace from mental conception into visible form.

Urantia suggests something stranger: copyright may sometimes proceed even when the upstream origin of the expressive material is deliberately left unresolved.

MISSING:
How far can this bracketing move travel?

If humans merely ask questions and receive outputs, what differentiates protected selection/arrangement from an attempt to claim the generated answers themselves?

BOUNDARY:
Urantia did not hold that the alleged celestial revelations themselves were human-authored. Its copyrightability analysis depended on human compilation—selection, coordination, and arrangement.

CITATION TRAIL:

TEST:
Remove the humans’ selection and arrangement from the Urantia production history while leaving the questions and received answers intact.

Ask exactly what copyrightable human expression remains.
