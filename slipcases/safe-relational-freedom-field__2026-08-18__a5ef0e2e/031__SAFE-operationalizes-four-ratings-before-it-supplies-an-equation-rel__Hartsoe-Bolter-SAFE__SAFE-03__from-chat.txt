ZETTEL

ID:
SAFE-03

TITLE:
SAFE operationalizes four ratings before it supplies an equation relating them

SOURCE:
Watson Hartsoe and Jay Bolter — The Sapient Agent Freedom Equation (SAFE): A Universal Framework for Quantifying Freedom in Sapient Agents — pp. 1, 9–13.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The abstract calls SAFE a “quantifiable model” and a formula, while the methodology operationalizes SAPIENT, AGENT, FREEDOM, and EQUATION as separate -4-to-4 Likert ratings and subsequently calculates disagreement among model judgments. fileciteturn6file2L13-L23 fileciteturn5file2L135-L169

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The paper presently contains a measurement vocabulary but not yet an identified composition law connecting its dimensions into the promised freedom equation.

LOCAL MOVE:
Four qualitative constructs become four numerical scales.

SOURCE TERMS:
quantifiable
formula
SAPIENT
AGENT
FREEDOM
EQUATION
Likert scale
total absolute differences

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
“Equation” appears both as the name of the whole framework and as one of the things being independently rated.

The equation is partly an input to itself.

QUESTION:
What mathematical relation is SAFE claiming exists among sapience, agency, freedom, and equilibrium?

DEEPER QUESTION:
What observation would show that these are dimensions of one construct rather than four morally interesting but independent judgments?

MECHANISM:
scenario
→ four prompts
→ four ordinal ratings
→ model comparisons

The transformation from those ratings to a single quantity of sapient-agent freedom remains unspecified in the supplied operational method.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<FOUR CONCEPTS>
→ <FOUR ORDINAL SCALES>
→ [MISSING COMPOSITION OPERATION]
→ <CLAIMED UNIVERSAL METRIC>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
S ∈ [-4,4]
A ∈ [-4,4]
F ∈ [-4,4]
E ∈ [-4,4]

Each is separately rated using a nine-point Likert scale. fileciteturn5file2L137-L159

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Candidate unresolved forms include:

SAFE = f(S,A,F,E)

but the source does not establish:

whether f is additive,
multiplicative,
lexicographic,
thresholded,
agent-relative,
or even reducible to one scalar.

TENSION:
Calling the framework an equation encourages mathematical interpretation, while treating “Equation/Equilibrium” as one independently judged component turns equilibrium itself into a scored normative property. fileciteturn5file4L347-L363

MISSING:
The mapping f(S,A,F,E), justification for its form, treatment of interactions, dimensional independence, and empirical validation.

BOUNDARY:
The evidence supports saying that the provided operational sections specify separate ratings and disagreement calculations. It does not establish that no further mathematical specification exists outside this manuscript.

TEST:
Fit competing models:

SAFE_additive
SAFE_multiplicative
SAFE_threshold
SAFE_vector

Then construct cases with identical total scores but radically different S/A/F/E profiles.

Ask whether observers regard them as ethically equivalent.
