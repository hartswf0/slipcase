ZETTEL

ID:
ENCOUNTER-004

TITLE:
Licklider’s “symbiosis” preserves a division of agency inside the partnership.

SOURCE:
J. C. R. Licklider — “Man-Computer Symbiosis” — 1960 — Summary and §I.A, pp. 4–11. ([man.computer](https://man.computer/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] Humans set goals, hypotheses, criteria, and evaluations; computers perform routinizable preparatory work. ([man.computer](https://man.computer/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Partnership language does not imply ontological or functional symmetry.

LOCAL MOVE:
Licklider imports biological symbiosis to imagine close human-computer coupling while specifying complementary functions for human and machine.

SOURCE TERMS:
symbiosis
cooperative interaction
close coupling
partnership
goals
hypotheses
criteria
evaluation
routinizable work

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
“Partner” can name a relation whose allocation of authority is sharply predetermined. The rhetoric of partnership is therefore insufficient evidence for mutual agency.

QUESTION:
At what point does complementary division of labor become an encounter rather than an instrumentally organized partnership?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does contemporary agentic AI actually undo Licklider’s allocation of goals and evaluation to the human, or merely obscure that allocation?

MECHANISM:
human formulates
→ computer processes
→ human evaluates
→ coupled cycle supports decisions.

SOURCE FORMALISM:

COMPUTER:

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
PARTNERSHIP ≠ SYMMETRY
COUPLING ≠ CO-CONSTITUTION

TENSION:
Contemporary descriptions of AI “collaboration” often treat initiative or surprise as evidence that the relation has become more reciprocal. Licklider shows that cooperation can coexist with an explicit hierarchy of functions.

MISSING:
A criterion distinguishing delegated operation, initiative, negotiation, and genuine revision of goals.

BOUNDARY:
Licklider predicts a technical arrangement; he does not claim Buberian reciprocity, Baradian intra-action, or ethical encounter.

CITATION TRAIL:
Engelbart — Augmenting Human Intellect.
Mixed-initiative interaction.
Adjustable autonomy.
Human–AI collaboration literature.

TEST:
Code modern AI products for where goals, hypotheses, criteria, action selection, and evaluation are actually assigned. Compare the resulting agency map with Licklider’s 1960 partition.
