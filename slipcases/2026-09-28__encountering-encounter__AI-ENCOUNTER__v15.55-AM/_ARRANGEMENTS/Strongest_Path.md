# Strongest current path

**Working wager:** a relational human–AI encounter is a bounded episode in which human and system contributions become recursively and task-relevantly consequential for one another, producing a path-dependent trajectory.

The decisive empirical move is not to ask whether an interaction felt social. Perturb a consequential earlier contribution and observe whether the later trajectory branches: who repairs, what constraints change, what artifact ancestry survives, whether disagreement reorganizes reasoning, and what persists after termination.

This path keeps encounter agnostic about machine consciousness while making reciprocity and emergence falsifiable.
