TITLE:
Suchman makes “human-machine interaction” itself an empirical question.

SOURCE:

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] Suchman notes that talk of human–machine interaction had become pervasive while its very basis largely escaped examination. ([cambridge.org](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/humanmachine-reconfigurations/introduction-to-the-1st-edition/46C1F6B7DA2DFF7953ACA3D2F0A8DC60?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The category “interaction” may be an attribution generated by properties of artifacts rather than a neutral description of what occurs.

LOCAL MOVE:
Suchman contrasts a planning model with situated action and examines a supposedly interactive intelligent machine. Her analysis treats successful human interaction as collaboratively produced intelligibility involving contextual resources, detection of misunderstanding, and repair. ([apice.unibo.it](https://apice.unibo.it/bin/view/Publication/SuchmanThesis85?mode=data&utm_source=chatgpt.com))

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Before asking whether AI interaction has become an “encounter,” we may need to reopen the prior question: when is it justified to describe human-machine conduct as interaction at all?

QUESTION:
Which apparent reciprocities of contemporary AI are accomplishments of the model, which are accomplishments of the human, and which arise only from the coupled situation?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can interaction be operationalized without smuggling in symmetry?

MECHANISM:
system produces behavior
→ human interprets behavior as meaningful
→ mismatch appears
→ human/system resources permit or fail to permit repair
→ intelligibility is maintained or collapses.

TENSION:
Goffman makes mutual monitoring constitutive of encounter. Suchman shows why machine participation in mutual intelligibility cannot simply be assumed from alternating turns.

MISSING:
A contemporary replication with probabilistic language models capable of generating context-sensitive repair behavior.

BOUNDARY:
Suchman’s Xerox-machine analysis does not directly settle what modern LLMs can accomplish. Its force is methodological: do not presuppose the relation you intend to study.

CITATION TRAIL:
Ethnomethodology.

Suchman 2007 Human-Machine Reconfigurations.
Repair in conversational AI.
Grounding in dialogue systems.

TEST:
Run controlled breakdowns in human-human and human-LLM dialogue. Compare who detects trouble, who initiates repair, what contextual resources are used, and whether apparent repair tracks actual shared understanding.
