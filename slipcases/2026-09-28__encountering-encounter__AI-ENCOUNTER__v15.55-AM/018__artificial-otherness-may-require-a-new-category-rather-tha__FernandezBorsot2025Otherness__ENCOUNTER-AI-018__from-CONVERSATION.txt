TITLE:
Artificial otherness may require a new category rather than a verdict of humanlike or nonhuman.

SOURCE:
Gabriel Fernandez-Borsot — Artificial intelligence and the future of otherness: what kind of other can an AI be for a human? — 2025 — AI & Society 40, 5409–5423. ([link.springer.com](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00146-025-02281-2))

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] Fernandez-Borsot characterizes “AIs as others” as serviceable, commodified, surveillant, authoritative, techno-standardized, and interiority-less, and proposes the term “analytical other” for a socially constructed category adequate to this difference. ([link.springer.com](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00146-025-02281-2))

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The relevant question may not be whether AI counts as an Other, but what kind of otherness its socio-technical organization produces.

LOCAL MOVE:
The paper rejects both simple anthropomorphism and simple dismissal, arguing that human society may need a distinct ontological and linguistic category for artificial relational entities.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
An AI other differs from a human other not only because it may lack interiority but because it is simultaneously product, service, surveillance apparatus, standardized infrastructure, and apparent interlocutor.

QUESTION:
Which of the six characteristics actually become perceptible during an encounter, and which are concealed by interface design?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is “AI otherness” produced less by model architecture than by the contradictory stacking of interlocutor and commodity roles?

MECHANISM:
system presents social cues

+ gathers/mediates information
+ carries institutional authority
+ lacks human interiority
→ familiar social category becomes unstable

TENSION:
Buberian analysis asks whether AI can genuinely say Thou. Fernandez-Borsot instead asks what sort of socially instituted other AI becomes regardless of genuine interiority.

MISSING:
Empirical evidence that users actually organize AI into a stable new ontological category rather than shifting opportunistically among person/tool/service metaphors.

BOUNDARY:
The six characteristics are a philosophical-social characterization, not demonstrated universal properties of every AI system.
