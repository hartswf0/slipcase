# Encounter Genealogy

A deliberately non-smooth map.

- [[ENCOUNTER-001]] lexical ancestry is not conceptual descent.
- [[ENCOUNTER-002]] Goffman technicalizes encounter as focused situated activity.
- [[ENCOUNTER-003]] Turing produces machine-other uncertainty through mediated separation.
- [[ENCOUNTER-005]] direct manipulation tries to make the computer transparent.
- [[ENCOUNTER-006]] Suchman reopens whether human-machine interaction is an achieved intelligibility.
- [[ENCOUNTER-007]] Pratt changes the unit from dyad to contact zone.
- [[ENCOUNTER-008]] Barad questions whether relata precede relation.
- [[ENCOUNTER-009]] Deleuze makes failed recognition productive.
- [[ENCOUNTER-010]] Althusser asks when a contingent encounter takes hold.
- [[ENCOUNTER-AI-012]] Floridi's *Encounters* is a writing experiment, not yet a general encounter ontology.
- [[ENCOUNTER-AI-016]] Encounter Ontology supplies explicit AI-era criteria, opening the operational problem.
