ENCOUNTER-007

TITLE:
Pratt changes the unit from two encountering subjects to a historically produced contact space.

SOURCE:
Mary Louise Pratt — “Arts of the Contact Zone” — 1991 — p. 34. ([jstor.org](https://www.jstor.org/stable/i25595461?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE] Contact zones are spaces where cultures “meet, clash, and grapple with each other.” ([tandfonline.com](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/14755610.2023.2269270?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Encounter can be displaced from a dyadic relation into a social space whose asymmetries pre-exist any individual meeting.

LOCAL MOVE:
Pratt defines contact zones through historically separated peoples entering relations frequently structured by colonialism, coercion, and unequal power. ([cambridge.org](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/modern-italy/article/for-me-it-was-lifechanging-the-italian-contact-zone-of-occupation-and-the-encounter-with-otherness-in-oral-history-recollections/4771544D8BF67BF624F394CBEF92B1C5?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
“You ↔ AI” may be the wrong scale of analysis. The relevant object may be the human–AI contact zone consisting of model, interface, platform, data, labor, institutional policy, and inherited classifications.

QUESTION:
What disappears analytically when human–AI encounter is represented as a conversational dyad?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can interface design create the phenomenology of reciprocity while structurally preventing reciprocal power?

TENSION:
Buberian and conversational framings privilege the relation between two apparent participants. Pratt makes power and historical infrastructure constitutive of the place in which meeting occurs.

MISSING:
An account of which AI infrastructures are experientially available to users and which remain structurally absent from the apparent encounter.

BOUNDARY:
Pratt theorizes colonial cultural contact, not computational systems. Translation to AI must preserve rather than trivialize the historical specificity of colonial asymmetry.

TEST:
Take one chatbot conversation and produce two analyses: dyadic encounter versus contact-zone analysis. Record which actors, constraints, histories, and power relations become visible only in the second.
