ENCOUNTER-OP-035

TITLE:
Resistance can be manipulated experimentally, but disagreement may make AI seem less socially other.

SOURCE:
Atakan Atamer, Olivia Pinto, and Priti Shah — “From ally to algorithm: How disagreement shapes users’ engagement with AI” — 2026 — Computers in Human Behavior Reports 23, 101252. ([sciencedirect.com](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S245195882600326X))

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] Participants were randomly assigned to an agreeing or disagreeing chatbot. Disagreement made the AI seem more machine-like and less capable of understanding human emotion and reduced willingness to reuse it; agreement increased confidence in participants’ existing views, while disagreement did not symmetrically reduce confidence. ([sciencedirect.com](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S245195882600326X))

RESEARCH OBJECT:
“Otherness” can be decomposed experimentally into behavioral resistance, perceived machine/humanness, epistemic uptake, and willingness to continue the relation.

LOCAL MOVE:
The study treats agreement versus disagreement as an experimentally manipulable property rather than inferring resistance from naturally occurring dialogue.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The Buberian intuition that resistance or refusal should make the other more real receives an immediate empirical complication: disagreement can make the artificial partner seem more like an algorithm.

QUESTION:
Does resistance strengthen encounter, terminate encounter, or merely reveal which ontology the user was already applying?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Must encounter operationalization distinguish ALTERITY from ANTHROPOMORPHISM?

TENSION:
[[ENCOUNTER-AI-017]] makes vulnerability, reciprocity, and refusal central to stronger relation. Atamer et al. show that resistance can instead trigger distancing and de-socialization.

MISSING:
Conditions under which justified disagreement produces productive epistemic engagement rather than dismissal.

BOUNDARY:
The experiment concerns hypothetical interpersonal/social dilemmas and manipulated agreement. It does not establish the effects of all forms of refusal or disagreement.
