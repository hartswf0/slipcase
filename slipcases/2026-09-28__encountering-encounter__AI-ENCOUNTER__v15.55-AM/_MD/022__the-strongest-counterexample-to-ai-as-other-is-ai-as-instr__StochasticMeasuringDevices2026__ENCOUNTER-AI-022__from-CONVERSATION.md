TITLE:
The strongest counterexample to AI-as-other is AI-as-instrument.

SOURCE:
Communicating with AI: Philosophical Perspectives — “Large Language Models Are Stochastic Measuring Devices” — 2026 — Oxford University Press. ([academic.oup.com](https://academic.oup.com/book/63302/chapter/573018260?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] The chapter proposes that so-called communication with AI may be better understood as interaction with a scientific instrument, drawing on measurement and simulation rather than the philosophy of other minds. ([academic.oup.com](https://academic.oup.com/book/63302/chapter/573018260?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Fluent response does not force an interlocutor ontology; the same behavior can be redescribed instrumentally.

LOCAL MOVE:
The chapter relocates questions about LLM interpretation into philosophy of science: neural models can function as measuring devices and simulation machinery rather than speakers.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Encounter phenomenology and instrument ontology can coexist. A human may experience being addressed while the best explanatory account treats the generating system as measurement apparatus.

QUESTION:
How could evidence discriminate AI-as-interlocutor from AI-as-instrument when both predict responsive text?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is “encounter” useful precisely because it permits the encountered entity to remain ontologically undecided?

INSTRUMENT READING:

ENCOUNTER QUESTION:
What remains invariant across both descriptions?

TENSION:
Floridi’s artificial agency thesis grants a novel non-intelligent agency. Encounter Ontology assigns emergence to the relation. The measuring-device account may eliminate both as unnecessary ontological inflation.

MISSING:
Cases where interlocutor and instrument models make different empirical predictions rather than merely supporting different vocabularies.

BOUNDARY:
The instrumental account does not imply that interactions are socially or psychologically inconsequential. It disputes how the computational participant should be characterized.
