TITLE:
Turing produces the machine encounter by preventing an ordinary encounter.

SOURCE:
A. M. Turing — “Computing Machinery and Intelligence” — 1950 — §1, pp. 433–434. ([academic.oup.com](https://academic.oup.com/mind/article/LIX/236/433/986238?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE] “The interrogator stays in a room apart from the other two.” ([cs.sfu.ca](https://www.cs.sfu.ca/~vaughan/teaching/889/papers/turing1950.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The imitation game depends on engineered non-encounter at the bodily level.

LOCAL MOVE:
Turing replaces “Can machines think?” with a game in which the interrogator must classify hidden participants through mediated linguistic responses. Identity-bearing bodily cues are deliberately removed. ([academic.oup.com](https://academic.oup.com/mind/article/LIX/236/433/986238?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The canonical AI “encounter” is architecturally produced by suppressing face-to-face encounter. The machine becomes encounterable as an uncertain other precisely because ordinary perceptual access has been blocked.

QUESTION:
Is mediation merely the channel of a human–AI encounter, or can selective deprivation of information actively manufacture the alterity attributed to the system?

DEEPER QUESTION:
How much of machine otherness is an interface effect?

MECHANISM:
participant identity hidden
→ communication channel restricted
→ behavioral evidence isolated
→ uncertainty about source maintained
→ attribution/judgment becomes possible.

TENSION:
Levinasian and Goffmanian accounts privilege presence, face, or mutual monitoring. Turing gains epistemic uncertainty by removing exactly those resources.

MISSING:
An account of what the communication medium contributes independently of machine intelligence.

BOUNDARY:
Turing does not describe the imitation game as a theory of encounter, alterity, or HCI. Calling it one is our reconstruction.

CITATION TRAIL:
Turing 1950.

TEST:
Compare judgments of the same AI across text-only, voice, avatar, visible-process, and model-internals conditions. If perceived otherness changes substantially, the “encounter” is partly generated by mediation architecture.
