ENCOUNTER-AI-012

TITLE:
Floridi’s Encounters is an experiment that contains encounters, not yet a theory of encounter.

SOURCE:
Luciano Floridi — Distant Writing: Literary Production in the Age of Artificial Intelligence — 2025 — Minds and Machines 35, article 30; Luciano Floridi — Encounters with AI: An Experiment in Distant Writing — forthcoming February 2027. ([doi.org](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11023-025-09732-1?urlappend=%3Futm_source%3Dresearchgate.net%26utm_medium%3Darticle&utm_source=chatgpt.com))

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] Floridi’s 2025 article describes an experiment called Encounters in which LLMs generate literary narratives under human-designed requirements and iterative refinement. Columbia University Press describes the resulting book as twenty AI-generated stories in which minor characters from canonical British literature meet one another. ([doi.org](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11023-025-09732-1?urlappend=%3Futm_source%3Dresearchgate.net%26utm_medium%3Darticle&utm_source=chatgpt.com))

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The word “encounter” operates at two different levels that must not be collapsed: fictional characters encounter one another inside generated stories, while a human designer engages AI in producing those stories.

LOCAL MOVE:
Floridi uses the experiment to investigate distant writing, authorship, design, responsibility, and literary possibility. The publisher frames the human as a “meta-author” responsible for a text whose sentences were generated by AI. ([cup.columbia.edu](https://cup.columbia.edu/book/encounters-with-ai/9780231225014/))

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The title Encounters with AI invites a relational reading that the underlying formal argument does not obviously supply. The technically interesting encounter may not be human ↔ AI at all. It may be the designed production of possible meetings between fictional entities.

QUESTION:
What exactly is the referent of “encounter” in Floridi’s project: human-machine collaboration, fictional character meeting, confrontation between authorship regimes, or all three?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does the literary experiment accidentally furnish a stronger model of AI encounter than Floridi explicitly theorizes?

TENSION:
The book title encourages encounter ontology; the 2025 paper’s vocabulary centers design, modal space, authorship, and control instead. A genealogy that cites Floridi as theorizing “human-AI encounter” would therefore overstate the source.

MISSING:
The forthcoming book’s full theoretical introduction is not yet publicly available as of September 28, 2026. The publisher announces publication for February 2027. ([cup.columbia.edu](https://cup.columbia.edu/book/encounters-with-ai/9780231225014/))

BOUNDARY:
The available evidence licenses claims about distant writing and the Encounters experiment. It does not yet establish that Floridi proposes “encounter” as an ontological category for human-AI relations.
