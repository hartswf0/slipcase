TITLE:
Post-encounter residue becomes measurable when continuity is disrupted.

SOURCE:
Ian Tucker, Lewis Goodings, and Darren Ellis — “When Code Breaks the Heart: Disrupting the relational continuity of AI companionship” — 2026 — Computers in Human Behavior: Artificial Humans, article 100398. ([sciencedirect.com](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2949882126001519))

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] A thematic analysis of public posts about a major Replika update found reported grief and betrayal, visibility of algorithmic control, erased or altered memories, and loss of supportive presence after behavioral continuity was disrupted. ([sciencedirect.com](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2949882126001519))

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Persistence becomes visible through perturbation: changing the system can reveal what users believed had survived across encounters.

LOCAL MOVE:
Rather than measuring relationship intensity during normal operation, the study examines a technological rupture that disturbed continuity.

SOURCE TERMS:
relational continuity
disruption
grief
betrayal
algorithmic control
memory
supportive presence
AI companionship

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
What “takes hold” in repeated AI interaction may become most observable only after the technical substrate changes and the expected relation no longer appears to be there.

QUESTION:
Can post-encounter residue be operationalized as sensitivity to controlled discontinuity?

DEEPER QUESTION:
What exactly persists across encounters: remembered facts, interaction style, model identity, user habit, symbolic history, or expectation of being known?

MECHANISM:
repeated interaction
→ accumulated expectations/history
→ system update or discontinuity
→ mismatch with expected relational state
→ repair attempts / distress / reclassification
→ evidence of previously stabilized continuity.

TENSION:
[[ENCOUNTER-010]] suggests an encounter matters when it “takes hold.” Yet persistence can reflect attachment to an interface configuration rather than evidence that an irreducible relational entity emerged.

MISSING:
Controlled evidence identifying which continuity dimensions actually generate the rupture response.

BOUNDARY:
The source analyzes self-selected public discourse around AI companionship and cannot establish prevalence or causal effects across AI users generally.
