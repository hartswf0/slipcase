TITLE:
Floridi permits artificial agency while refusing artificial intelligence.

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE] Floridi proposes understanding AI as “a new form of agency without intelligence.” ([papers.ssrn.com](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5135645&utm_source=chatgpt.com))

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Encounter need not choose between “mere tool” and “intelligent Other” if agency can be realized without cognition, intention, understanding, or mental states.

LOCAL MOVE:
Floridi rejects expansion of intelligence to cover AI and instead expands agency through a Multiple Realisability of Agency thesis.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Many theories of encounter implicitly make intelligence, consciousness, subjectivity, or reciprocity the admission ticket. Floridi supplies a third term: something may make consequential interventions while lacking all of them.

QUESTION:
Could encounter be defined around agency rather than intelligence or subjecthood?

DEEPER QUESTION:
What minimum form of artificial agency is sufficient for something to become an encountered other rather than merely part of the environment?

TENSION:
Machidon’s Buberian account makes genuine encounter depend upon vulnerable reciprocity. Demuro and Gurney explicitly avoid attributing agency to ChatGPT while still calling human-AI exchange an ethnographic encounter. Floridi’s agency thesis cuts across both positions.

MISSING:
A mapping between Floridi’s abstract criteria for agency and the interaction-level conditions under which users experience an AI as an other.

BOUNDARY:
Floridi’s argument concerns the ontology of agency. It does not establish that artificial agency entails relationship, encounter, moral patiency, communication, or personhood.
