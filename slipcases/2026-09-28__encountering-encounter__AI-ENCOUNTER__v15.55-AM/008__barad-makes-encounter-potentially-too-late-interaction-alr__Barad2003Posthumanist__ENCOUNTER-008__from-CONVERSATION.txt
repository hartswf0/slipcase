ID:
ENCOUNTER-008

TITLE:
Barad makes encounter potentially too late: interaction already assumes the entities it claims to relate.

SOURCE:
Karen Barad — “Posthumanist Performativity: Toward an Understanding of How Matter Comes to Matter” — 2003 — p. 815. ([journals.uchicago.edu](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/abs/10.1086/345321?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE] “relata do not preexist relations.” ([link.springer.com](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11229-021-03160-1?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The distinction between interaction and intra-action attacks the assumption that encounter begins with two already bounded participants.

LOCAL MOVE:
Barad makes phenomena ontologically primary and argues that determinate relata emerge through specific intra-actions and agential cuts. ([link.springer.com](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11229-021-03160-1?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

SOURCE TERMS:
phenomena
intra-action
relata

apparatus
objectivity

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Even a sophisticated theory of encounter may remain ontologically conservative if it starts by writing:

HUMAN ↔ AI

The slash between “human” and “AI” may itself be an achievement of an apparatus.

QUESTION:
Which boundaries in a human–AI episode pre-exist the episode, and which are enacted by interfaces, prompts, logging systems, institutional categories, evaluation procedures, and discourse?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is “human-AI encounter” already the output of a cut?

MECHANISM:

→ specific material-discursive apparatus
→ agential cut
→ determinate boundaries/properties
→ apparent relata.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
INTRA-ACTION contrasts with INTERACTION.
Specific intra-actions enact agential separability.
Relata emerge within phenomena.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
INTERACTION:
A + B → relation

INTRA-ACTION:
phenomenon → cut → A | B

TENSION:
Encounter theories built around alterity often require an Other already capable of confronting a self. Barad pressures the prior operation through which “self,” “other,” “human,” and “machine” become stable analytical units.

MISSING:
A non-metaphorical specification of what counts as an apparatus and agential cut in actual HCI experiments.

BOUNDARY:
Barad’s argument emerges from a philosophy of quantum physics and material-discursive practices. It cannot simply be converted into a software architecture.

CITATION TRAIL:

Barad 2007 Meeting the Universe Halfway.
Suchman on reconfiguration.
STS studies of classification and infrastructure.

TEST:
Choose one human-AI study and list every operation required before its data can contain separate columns/categories called USER and SYSTEM. Treat those operations, rather than the categories, as the initial object of inquiry.
