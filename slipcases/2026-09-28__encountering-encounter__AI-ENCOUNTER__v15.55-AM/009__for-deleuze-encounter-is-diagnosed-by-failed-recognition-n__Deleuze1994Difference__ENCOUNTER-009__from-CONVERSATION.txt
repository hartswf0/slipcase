ZETTEL

ID:
ENCOUNTER-009

TITLE:
For Deleuze, encounter is diagnosed by failed recognition, not by reciprocity.

SOURCE:
Gilles Deleuze — Difference and Repetition — 1968; English translation 1994 — “The Image of Thought,” p. 139. ([cup.columbia.edu](https://cup.columbia.edu/book/difference-and-repetition/9780231081580/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE] “Something in the world forces us to think.” The object is not recognition but “a fundamental encounter.” ([journals.sagepub.com](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0263276420966389?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Encounter can be defined by what it does to thought rather than by the social status of the encountered entity.

LOCAL MOVE:
Deleuze contrasts recognition with an encounter that cannot simply be subsumed under existing categories and therefore forces the genesis of thought.

SOURCE TERMS:
encounter
recognition
thought
force
sensible
problem
image of thought

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A machine need not be a reciprocal subject for a human to encounter it in Deleuze’s sense. A theorem, temple, error, generated image, or model output could qualify if it breaks recognition and forces a new problem.

QUESTION:
Should the test for human–AI encounter concern what the AI is, or whether the episode reorganizes the user’s space of thought?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can engineered “surprise” force thought, or does genuine encounter require an event that escapes the designer’s and user’s available recognitional schema?

MECHANISM:
recognitional schema fails
→ something is sensed as problematic
→ faculties cannot smoothly assimilate it
→ thought is forced
→ a problem/new concept becomes possible.

FORMAL SHIFT:

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Recognition is opposed to the fundamental encounter in Deleuze’s critique of the dogmatic image of thought.

TENSION:
Goffman requires mutual social organization. Deleuze does not. These are not two versions of the same encounter concept; they yield different empirical tests.

MISSING:
A measure of conceptual reorganization that is not merely self-reported surprise or output novelty.

BOUNDARY:
Deleuze is theorizing the genesis of thought, not human-computer interaction.

CITATION TRAIL:
Deleuze — Proust and Signs.
Peircean surprise/abduction.
Dewey on problematic situations.
Creativity-support-tool studies.

TEST:
Compare AI outputs rated equally surprising. Test whether any produce durable changes in the user’s subsequent questions, categories, representations, or problem formulation. Treat those transformations, not surprise ratings, as the candidate encounter variable.
