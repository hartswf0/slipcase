ENCOUNTER-002

TITLE:
Goffman technicalizes encounter by removing surprise and trouble from it.

SOURCE:
Erving Goffman — Encounters: Two Studies in the Sociology of Interaction — 1961 — Preface p. 8; “Fun in Games,” pp. 17–19. ([scribd.com](https://www.scribd.com/document/853158019/Encounters-Two-Studies-in-the-Sociology-of-Interaction-Erving-Goffman?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE] Goffman calls the unit a “focused gathering, or an encounter, or a situated activity system.” ([scribd.com](https://www.scribd.com/document/853158019/Encounters-Two-Studies-in-the-Sociology-of-Interaction-Erving-Goffman?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

RESEARCH OBJECT:
A documented case in which “encounter” is deliberately stripped of its ordinary semantic baggage and rebuilt as a sociological unit.

LOCAL MOVE:
Goffman distinguishes unfocused interaction from focused interaction, then gives three partially unsatisfactory names to the natural social unit in which focused interaction occurs. He explicitly notes that ordinary “encounter” can mean an unexpected or troublesome meeting and says those meanings must be excluded. ([scribd.com](https://www.scribd.com/document/853158019/Encounters-Two-Studies-in-the-Sociology-of-Interaction-Erving-Goffman?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
One of the strongest twentieth-century theories explicitly called “encounter” does not preserve encounter-as-surprise. It makes encounter depend instead on jointly sustained attention and ratified participation.

QUESTION:
If Goffman’s encounter requires mutual monitoring and a jointly sustained focus, can human–AI interaction qualify when only the human possesses access to the interaction as a social situation?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is mutual orientation more fundamental to encounter than contingency?

MECHANISM:
Co-presence
→ mutual orientation
→ sustained shared focus
→ preferential communication
→ monitoring of monitoring
→ emergent “we rationale”
→ bounded encounter.

TENSION:
A probabilistic AI can surprise a user without satisfying Goffman’s stronger criterion of reciprocal monitoring. Surprise alone therefore cannot distinguish an encounter from an unpredictable output.

MISSING:
An account of whether artificial systems can participate in rather than merely simulate the ratification of a shared situation.

BOUNDARY:
Goffman is describing human social arrangements in immediate physical presence. Applying the framework to AI requires argument, not simple substitution.

CITATION TRAIL:
Goffman — Behavior in Public Places.
Conversation analysis on summons, openings, repair, and closings.
Suchman on mutual intelligibility.
Human–robot “engagement” literature.

TEST:
Annotate human–LLM sessions for Goffman’s encounter conditions: initiation, ratification, mutual focus, monitoring, repair, termination. Determine which conditions are interactional effects produced solely by the human.
