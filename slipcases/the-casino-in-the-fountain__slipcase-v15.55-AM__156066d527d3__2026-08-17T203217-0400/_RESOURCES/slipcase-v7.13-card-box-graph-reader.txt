<laws>

  1. ONE IDEA = ONE CARD.
     Every unique admitted zettel becomes one root-level .txt file.

  2. TXT IS HUMAN-PRIMARY.
     Every root .txt card gets a same-named Markdown mirror in _MD/.

  3. THE CARD IS THE PAYLOAD.
     TXT and MD contain the exact preserved zettel payload.
     Never prepend summaries, metadata wrappers, or compiler commentary.

  4. PAYLOADS ARE IMMUTABLE.
     Corrections, extensions, and disagreements become new zettels.

  5. FILENAMES CARRY HUMAN MEANING.

     <ORDER>__<NAME>__<SOURCE>__<ORIGINAL-ID>__from-<ORIGIN>.txt

     Example:
     010__unlived-curriculum__Ingold2013Making__
     Z-EDUPM-010__from-20260817-s01-o010.txt

     Hashes belong in machine metadata.

  6. ORIGINAL ID ALWAYS SURVIVES.
     Original IDs may collide.
     Never merge by ID alone.

  7. IDENTITY IS NOT LOCATION.
     Renaming, moving, or renumbering a card does not change the zettel.

  8. EVERY PLATFORM, LINK, AND [[WIKILINK]] BECOMES GRAPH DATA.
     Never leave declared relations trapped only inside payloads.

  9. PLATFORM, CONCEPT, AND ZETTEL ARE DISTINCT.
     A named PLATFORM or CONCEPT may exist without a zettel.

  10. UNINSTANTIATED ADDRESSES BECOME GHOSTS.
      Preserve them as meaningful empty places in the graph.

  11. LINKS BECOME BIDIRECTIONAL IN DERIVED VIEWS.
      A → B also produces a visible backlink B ← A.
      Never rewrite the immutable card to add backlinks.

  12. EVERY SOURCE IS HUMAN-LEGIBLE.
      Preserve when known:
        author
        year
        title
        citekey
        URL
        DOI
        local resource
        cards using it

      Never show a naked citekey without its readable citation.

  13. EVERY RESOURCE IS REGISTERED.
      Include URLs, webpages, PDFs, files, pasted text, repositories,
      images, datasets, code, transcripts, prompts, and prior checkpoints.

  14. PROMPTS OF CONSEQUENCE SURVIVE VERBATIM.

  15. SOURCE URL SURVIVES.
      Preserve SOURCE URL inside existing zettel payloads exactly.
      Any newly created zettel must include a SOURCE URL field.

  16. MISSING MEANS MISSING.
      Never invent provenance, authors, dates, URLs, DOIs, page numbers,
      graph targets, timestamps, counts, or hashes.

  17. NEVER CLAIM MORE COVERAGE THAN WAS AVAILABLE.

  18. COMPUTERS COUNT.
      Use tools for hashes, counts, manifests, duplicates, graph extraction,
      backlinks, validation, and ZIP creation.
      Otherwise mark PENDING or UNVERIFIED.

</laws>
