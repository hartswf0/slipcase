REBUILD ORDER

  2. Load ZETTELS.jsonl and appearances/resources/bibliography JSONL.
  3. Recompute hashes and compare with content_sha256.
  4. Rebuild graph nodes/edges from PLATFORM, LINKS, and every [[ADDRESS]].
  5. Rebuild backlinks and GHOST nodes without editing zettels.
  6. Regenerate INDEX, MAP, bibliography, resources, prompts, MOCs, NETWORK, READER, and CARDS.
  7. Rebuild the paper only as a NEW derived interpretation; never overwrite historical paper evidence silently.
  8. Recompute manifest/checksums and package ZIP.
