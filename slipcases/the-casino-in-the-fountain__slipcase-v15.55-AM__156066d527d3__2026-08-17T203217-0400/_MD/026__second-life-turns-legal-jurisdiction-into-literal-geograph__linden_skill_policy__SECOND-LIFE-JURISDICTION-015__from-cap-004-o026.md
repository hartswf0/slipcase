SECOND-LIFE-JURISDICTION-015

TITLE:
Second Life turns legal jurisdiction into literal geography: some avatars are not permitted to enter the region.

SOURCE:
Linden Lab — Second Life Skill Gaming Policy — current official policy.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] A Skill Gaming Region is a designated full Second Life region, and a resident must be of legal age and accessing from a jurisdiction where participation is permitted in order to enter.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Legal permission is translated into spatial access.

The law does not merely govern what an avatar may DO after entering a place.

It helps determine whether the avatar may ENTER THE PLACE.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Two avatars can stand conceptually adjacent on the same global map while one is legally unable to cross the parcel boundary.

Virtual geography becomes personalized jurisdiction.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Linden Lab defines a Skill Gaming Region as a recognized Second Life Full Region.

Participation requires that the resident:
1. have appropriate legal authority;
2. reside in and access from an authorized jurisdiction;
3. be of legal age;
4. maintain qualifying account information.

TENSION:
The usual map represents places as existing independently of viewers.

Here, lawful reachability is a property of the user-place relation.

MISSING:
The precise technical enforcement chain connecting physical geolocation, account information, jurisdiction lists, and region-entry control.

BOUNDARY:
The policy describes permission and access requirements. It does not expose Linden Lab's full geolocation or enforcement implementation.
