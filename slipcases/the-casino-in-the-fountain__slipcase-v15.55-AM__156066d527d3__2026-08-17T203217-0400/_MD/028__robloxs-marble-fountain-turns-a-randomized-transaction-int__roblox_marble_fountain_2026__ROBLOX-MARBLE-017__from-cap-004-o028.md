ZETTEL

ID:
ROBLOX-MARBLE-017

TITLE:
Roblox's marble fountain turns a randomized transaction into a tiny piece of world physics.

SOURCE:
Roblox Corporation — Roblox Terms of Use — Random Virtual Items — May 19, 2026.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] Roblox illustrates its odds-disclosure requirement with a user acquiring a virtual marble and throwing it into a fountain to receive a Random Virtual Item.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The example contains a miniature compiler:

PURCHASE / ACQUIRE CHANCE
→ MARBLE
→ THROW
→ FOUNTAIN
→ RANDOM REWARD.

A financial-probabilistic operation has been translated into nouns, movement, causality, and spectacle.

LOCAL MOVE:
Roblox acknowledges that randomized acquisition can be embedded in ordinary world interaction while requiring the probability information to remain legible before the triggering action.

SOURCE TERMS:
virtual marble
throw
fountain
Random Virtual Item
odds
transaction
acquire

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A loot box does not need a box.

More radically:

a transaction does not need to present itself as a transaction.

It can become a causal story in the world.

QUESTION:
What happens when economic syntax is compiled into diegetic physics?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can world design make an economic mechanism feel natural because payment becomes an object and probability becomes environmental causation?

TENSION:
Disclosure attempts to pull the probabilistic machinery back into explicit representation.

World design can simultaneously push it into metaphor.

The interface therefore performs two opposing moves:

MAKE MECHANISM LEGIBLE.
MAKE MECHANISM DIEGETIC.

MISSING:
Whether spatial/diegetic presentation changes children's or adults' recognition of the action as a paid random transaction.

BOUNDARY:
Roblox provides the fountain only as an example of when odds must be disclosed. The source does not claim the fountain representation changes behavior.
