OPEN EDGES — THEORY LAG FIELD

1. THEORY LAG VS ORDINARY REQUIREMENTS ELICITATION
What evidence would show that a consequential criterion did not merely pre-exist but became available through realization?
TEST: pre-generation criterion elicitation + post-realization criterion genealogy.

2. TASK LOCATION VS SPECIFICATION COMPLETION
Can sparse prompting on pretrained tasks be cleanly separated from novel constraint construction?
TEST: compare familiar tasks, arbitrary in-context mappings, and novel compositions.

3. TACIT STEERING VS SPECIFICATION EXTRACTION
Can choice behavior become predictable while verbal articulation remains poor?
TEST: preference-only steering, held-out prediction, and transfer.

4. REGENERATION VS MODIFICATION COMPETENCE
Does a compressed prompt preserve theory or only a recognizable realization pattern?
TEST: regenerate first, then introduce an unforeseen world change and score principled modification.

5. HETEROGENEOUS UNRESOLVEDNESS
Which kinds of missingness can different programming regimes tolerate: parameter, procedure, structure, semantics, ontology, aesthetics, context, or evaluation rule?

6. ITERATION BANDWIDTH / EVASION
When does cheap provisional realization improve criterion discovery, and when does it let practitioners route around unresolved theory?

7. COMPUTATIONAL FELICITY
Consequence coupling should be decomposed into reference, interpretation, authority, capability, admissible state, and execution.

8. WHAT IS A SITUATION FOR A MODEL?
Do not collapse context window, tool state, interface selection, institutional setting, and embodied human situation into one variable.
