THEORY-2007-SUCHMAN

TITLE:
Plans do not prescribe situated action at every level of detail

SOURCE:
Lucy Suchman, Human-Machine Reconfigurations: Plans and Situated Actions, 2nd ed., Cambridge University Press, 2007; "Plans" chapter retains the situated-action argument.

SOURCE URL:
https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511808418.007

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
"the prescriptive significance of intentions for situated action is inherently vague."

RESEARCH OBJECT:
[PARAPHRASE]
PLANS ARE RESOURCES FOR ACTION, NOT EXHAUSTIVE DETERMINANTS OF SITUATED PERFORMANCE.

LOCAL MOVE:
Follow [[MARTINA-2022-024]] and resist treating direction as a complete blueprint that execution merely instantiates.

SOURCE TERMS:
"plans"; "situated action"; "intentions"; "inherently vague"

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A project can be purposive without its future action sequence being exhaustively specified in advance.

QUESTION:
What is genuinely new about generative direction if ordinary situated action already exceeds prior plans?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does generative AI amplify a longstanding property of action, or introduce a distinctive source of underdetermined content through model completion?

MECHANISM:
Plans project and reconstruct action while situated circumstances and practical reasoning determine concrete next steps.

FORMAL SHIFT:
PLAN as RESOURCE, not complete execution trace.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
[PARAPHRASE]
Suchman contrasts planning views with situated action and states that intentions have inherently vague prescriptive significance.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
PURPOSEFUL ACTION can proceed with plan P that underdetermines action A_t.

TENSION:
If Martina's pattern is ordinary situated action, a generative-specific theory risks overclaiming novelty.

MISSING:
Comparative cases of sketching, delegation, improvisation, prototyping, and generative prompting.

BOUNDARY:
Use as loyal opposition to claims of novelty.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[MARTINA-2022-024]] -> Suchman -> ordinary purposive underdetermination -> identify generative difference, if any.

TEST:
Match generative and non-generative design tasks; compare how much new consequential content enters from execution rather than human situated action alone.

PLATFORM:
Situated action; plans; human-machine interaction
