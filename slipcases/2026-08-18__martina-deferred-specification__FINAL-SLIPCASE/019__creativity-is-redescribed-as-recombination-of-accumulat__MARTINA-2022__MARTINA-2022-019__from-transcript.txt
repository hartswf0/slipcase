TITLE:
Creativity is redescribed as recombination of accumulated experience

SOURCE:
Martina interview with Watson Hartsoe, October 10, 2022. Unpublished transcript.

SOURCE URL:
NONE (private local source; preserved under _RESOURCES/PINK_MJ_Interview1.wh_Martina.pages and _RESOURCES/martina_transcript.txt)

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
At 31:14 Martina describes creativity through a "database" of what one has learned and experienced, emphasizing mixture and recombination rather than creation from nothing.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
[OUR INFERENCE]
ORIGINALITY IS PRESSURED BY A RECOMBINATIONAL MODEL SHARED ACROSS HUMAN AND MACHINE PRACTICE.

LOCAL MOVE:
Martina places human creativity and model generation on partially comparable ground by describing both as recombination over accumulated material.

SOURCE TERMS:
"database"; "information"; "mixture"

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
If both human and machine outcomes are understood as recombination, originality cannot be defended simply by denying antecedents.

QUESTION:
What distinctions remain between human recombination and model recombination once "creation from nothing" is abandoned?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Are selection, situated purpose, embodied history, accountability, and project continuity more discriminating than novelty?

MECHANISM:
Experience/information supplies a repertoire; creative acts combine and transform elements under present purposes and constraints.

FORMAL SHIFT:
ORIGINALITY FROM NOTHING -> situated recombination + judgment.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
[PARAPHRASE]
The interview offers a database/recombination metaphor for human creativity.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Novelty is relational to a repertoire and context, not evidence of ex nihilo origin.

TENSION:
The database metaphor may flatten embodiment, sociality, affect, and unequal provenance.

MISSING:
A richer account of what differentiates human experience from training data and learned representations.

BOUNDARY:
Do not treat metaphor as technical cognitive model.

CITATION TRAIL:
Transcript 31:14 -> recombination -> 32:20 originality anxiety -> 20:43 style ethics.

TEST:
Compare practitioner judgments of originality when provenance and transformation histories are made visible.

PLATFORM:
Midjourney; creativity; originality
