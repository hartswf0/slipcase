WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The same artifact used to increase specificity can also re-open possibility.

QUESTION:
When is a template a constraint device and when is it a proposal generator?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can a structure simultaneously increase control over execution and increase discovery by making forgotten alternatives available?

MECHANISM:
The template externalizes categories; encountering a category expands the currently considered set; selection then re-enters the prompt.
