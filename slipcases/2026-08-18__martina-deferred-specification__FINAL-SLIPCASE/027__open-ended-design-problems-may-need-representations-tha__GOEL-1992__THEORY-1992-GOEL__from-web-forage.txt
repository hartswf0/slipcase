THEORY-1992-GOEL

TITLE:
Open-ended design problems may need representations that remain ill-structured

SOURCE:
Vinod Goel, "Ill-Structured Representations for Ill-Structured Problems," Cognitive Science Society, 1992.

SOURCE URL:
https://escholarship.org/uc/item/3wz7c4nd

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
"ill-structured, open-ended problems ... need "ill-structured" diagrammatic representations."

RESEARCH OBJECT:
[PARAPHRASE]
PRECISION CAN BE PREMATURE DURING EXPLORATORY DESIGN.

LOCAL MOVE:
Pressure [[MARTINA-2022-008]]: if prompt improvement is equated with increased specificity, early design may lose representational flexibility.

SOURCE TERMS:
"ill-structured"; "open-ended"; "sketches"; "preliminary phases"

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A representation can be useful partly because it does not yet determine every relation.

QUESTION:
When does greater prompt specificity close a question before the project has learned which differences matter?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is model-resolved underspecification equivalent to human-readable ambiguity, or does the model eliminate the ambiguity too early?

MECHANISM:
Ill-structured representations support open-ended design operations before drafting-like precision becomes appropriate.

FORMAL SHIFT:
EARLY PHASE: ambiguity/reinterpretability can have utility distinct from late-stage control.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
[PARAPHRASE]
Goel argues for a relation between ill-structured problems and ill-structured external representations in preliminary design.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Optimal specificity is phase-dependent; maximizing control need not maximize inquiry value.

TENSION:
Vague language may not preserve ambiguity for the user because the generator resolves it into a specific image.

MISSING:
Comparative representation experiments across sketch and prompt modalities.

BOUNDARY:
Does not prove vague prompts are cognitively beneficial.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[MARTINA-2022-008]] -> Goel 1992 -> control/discovery split -> distinguish preserved ambiguity from model-resolved omission.

TEST:
Compare free sketch, ambiguous prompt, and high-specificity prompt while tracking conceptual branching and commitment timing.

PLATFORM:
Design cognition; ill-structured representation
