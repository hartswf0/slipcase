THEORY-1991-GOLDSCHMIDT

TITLE:
External displays can induce images rather than merely copy them

SOURCE:
Gabriela Goldschmidt, "The Dialectics of Sketching," Creativity Research Journal 4(2), 1991.

SOURCE URL:
https://doi.org/10.1080/10400419109534381

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
"creates visual displays which help induce images of the entity that is being designed."

RESEARCH OBJECT:
[PARAPHRASE]
SKETCHING CAN PARTICIPATE IN FORMING THE REPRESENTATION IT APPEARS TO EXPRESS.

LOCAL MOVE:
Follow [[MARTINA-2022-001]] and [[MARTINA-2022-002]] into design cognition without assuming a pre-existing mental picture.

SOURCE TERMS:
"visual displays"; "induce images"; "design"; "sketching"

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The external representation can be causally upstream of later internal imagery.

QUESTION:
Can a generated image help cause the mental representation against which later generations are judged?

DEEPER QUESTION:
How can research distinguish recovered intention from intention reconstructed after seeing the artifact?

MECHANISM:
Sketching creates inspectable visual structures that participate in further design reasoning.

FORMAL SHIFT:
INNER IMAGE -> SKETCH is replaced by a bidirectional or generative relation between external display and imagery.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
[PARAPHRASE]
Goldschmidt's abstract reports that designers do not simply represent images held in mind but create visual displays that help induce design images.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
C_0 -> DISPLAY_1 -> IMAGERY_1 -> C_1.

TENSION:
Generative output differs from hand sketch because the model supplies structure not motorically produced by the designer.

MISSING:
Pre/post imagery measurements around generated versus hand-produced displays.

BOUNDARY:
Formal resemblance is not equivalence of cognitive mechanism.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[MARTINA-2022-001]] -> Goldschmidt 1991 -> generated precedent -> retrospective intention test.

TEST:
Collect pre-generation structured descriptions, immediate judgments, and delayed recollections; measure drift toward generated features absent from the preregistration.

PLATFORM:
Design cognition; sketching
