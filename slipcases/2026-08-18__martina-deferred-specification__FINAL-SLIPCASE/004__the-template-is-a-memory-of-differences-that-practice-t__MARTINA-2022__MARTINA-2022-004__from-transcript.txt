RESEARCH OBJECT:
[OUR INFERENCE]
PROMPT TEMPLATE AS ACCUMULATED SPECIFICATION HISTORY.

LOCAL MOVE:
Martina observes public prompts, creates a Notion template, and adds a new element "every time" she discovers a new way to prompt through trial and error.

SOURCE TERMS:
"showing you the prompts"; "template"; "discovering a new way"; "trial and error"; "improve"

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The template is produced downstream of executions and then returns upstream to govern future executions.

QUESTION:
Is the mature prompt best understood as a standalone instruction or as a compressed residue of earlier failures, examples, and discoveries?

DEEPER QUESTION:
What forms of knowledge disappear when the history of corrections is collapsed into a final template?

MECHANISM:
Discovery -> inscription -> reuse. A consequential difference is named and preserved so later attempts begin with more structure.

FORMAL SHIFT:
EXECUTION_t -> DISCOVERY_t -> TEMPLATE_t+1 -> EXECUTION_t+1.
