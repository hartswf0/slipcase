RESEARCH OBJECT:
[OUR INFERENCE]
CAPABILITY ACCESS CAN BE RECOMPOSED WITHOUT TRANSFERRING THE DISPLACED CRAFT.

LOCAL MOVE:
Martina says AI-generated 3D assets could put her "back in the game" because lack of 3D skill currently blocks AR/VR projects she wants to direct.

SOURCE TERMS:
"back in the game"; "I don't know 3d"; "zero interest on learning"; "happy that Midjourney can do that for me"

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A technical skill can cease to be a prerequisite for directing some projects without disappearing as a capability somewhere in the system.

QUESTION:
Which practices become accessible when execution capability can be commissioned through a generative system?

DEEPER QUESTION:
What expertise must remain with the director to judge outputs from a craft she does not personally master?

MECHANISM:
Model supplies intermediate execution capability; Martina supplies project direction and acceptance criteria; downstream use still demands evaluation and integration.

FORMAL SHIFT:
PERSONAL EXECUTION REQUIREMENT -> SYSTEM-SUPPLIED CAPABILITY + HUMAN DIRECTION.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
[PARAPHRASE]
Martina explicitly ties generative 3D capability to re-entering AR/VR project work.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Accessible project set expands when capability c is externally callable, but judgment requirements may persist or change.

TENSION:
Generated assets may be technically unusable without hidden expertise; access to representation is not equivalent to production readiness.

MISSING:
Case studies of projects where capability was bypassed versus where missing expertise reappeared downstream.

BOUNDARY:
Do not call this democratization without examining new dependencies and quality gates.

CITATION TRAIL:
Transcript 1:09:03 -> capability recomposition -> direction without personal execution.

TEST:
Follow creators entering a previously inaccessible domain with generative tools; map which former prerequisites disappear, move, or return as evaluation/integration problems.

PLATFORM:
AR/VR; 3D; capability recomposition
