TITLE:
Direction may consist in continuity of consequential judgment rather than foreknowledge of form

SOURCE:
Martina interview with Watson Hartsoe, October 10, 2022. Unpublished transcript.

SOURCE URL:
NONE (private local source; preserved under _RESOURCES/PINK_MJ_Interview1.wh_Martina.pages and _RESOURCES/martina_transcript.txt)

PASSAGE:
[OUR INFERENCE]
DIRECTION WITHOUT BLUEPRINT.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
This synthesis crosses Martina's incomplete scene, epistemic generation, template accumulation, intensive selection, and capability recomposition.

LOCAL MOVE:
[OUR INFERENCE]
Relate [[MARTINA-2022-001]], [[MARTINA-2022-003]], [[MARTINA-2022-004]], [[MARTINA-2022-022]], and [[MARTINA-2022-023]] without treating leadership as an inherited conclusion.

SOURCE TERMS:
"idea"; "scenario"; "proper result"; "template"; "picky"; "back in the game"

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The project can remain recognizably directed even while its representation, specification, and some criteria evolve during execution.

QUESTION:
What must remain continuous across transformations for an evolving artifact to remain answerable to Martina's direction?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is leadership under generative uncertainty better located in initial vision, final authorship, or the authority and responsibility to decide which differences govern the next state?

MECHANISM:
Martina initiates; generation externalizes consequences; she discriminates; retained differences become constraints; the next action begins from a changed knowledge state.

FORMAL SHIFT:
DIRECTION != COMPLETE DESTINATION.
Candidate: DIRECTION = accountable continuity of consequential judgment across state changes.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
[PARAPHRASE]
The source repeatedly shows Martina initiating with partial ideas, evaluating results, accumulating prompt distinctions, and using model capability to pursue projects she cannot personally execute.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
D_t -> ACT_t -> STATE_t+1 -> JUDGE_t+1 -> RETAIN/REJECT -> D_t+1.

TENSION:
This may redescribe ordinary design iteration rather than identify a generative-specific form of direction.

MISSING:
Comparative evidence against sketching, prototyping, briefing, directing, and conventional delegation.

BOUNDARY:
Leadership is a research question here, not a praise label or a claim about organizational role.

TEST:
Compare generative and non-generative design trajectories matched for task; identify whether criteria are formed later, whether execution contributes more underdetermined content, and where accountability is assigned.
