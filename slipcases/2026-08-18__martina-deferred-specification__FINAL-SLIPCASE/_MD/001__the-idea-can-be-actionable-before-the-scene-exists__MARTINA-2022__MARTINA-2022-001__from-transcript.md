RESEARCH OBJECT:
[OUR INFERENCE]
AN INTENTION CAN BE SUFFICIENT FOR ACTION WHILE INSUFFICIENT FOR COMPLETE REPRESENTATION.

LOCAL MOVE:
Martina explicitly separates having "the idea" from having "this scenario built on my mind." The research problem is not vague prompting but the representational status of an intention that can already initiate action.

SOURCE TERMS:
"idea"; "thought"; "scenario built on my mind"; "random thoughts"

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The usual sequence INTENTION -> DESCRIPTION -> EXECUTION assumes more determinacy upstream than Martina reports possessing.

QUESTION:
What minimum structure can an intention possess while still being actionable through a generative system?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can direction remain attributable to a person when the representation that later counts as the target is partly constructed after action begins?

MECHANISM:
Martina supplies a partial linguistic intervention; the model resolves enough unspecified structure to create a perceivable candidate; the candidate then becomes available for judgment.

FORMAL SHIFT:
FROM: completed mental representation -> prompt -> output
TO: underdetermined direction -> prompt -> generated proposal -> judgment.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
[PARAPHRASE]
The source distinguishes having an idea from having the complete scenario mentally represented.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
D_0 (direction) -> P_0 (partial description) -> G_0 (generated proposal).
D_0 need not contain a complete representation of G_0.

TENSION:
Martina may possess rich tacit or nonvisual criteria that the interview cannot recover; absence of a built scene is not evidence of absence of intention.

MISSING:
Pre-generation evidence detailed enough to distinguish latent criterion from newly formed criterion.

BOUNDARY:
The interview establishes Martina's self-description, not the cognitive ontology of intention.

CITATION TRAIL:
Transcript 17:56 -> incomplete scene despite actionable idea -> test pre-generation representational content.

TEST:
Before generation, record expected objects, relations, qualities, confidence, and rejection criteria without showing images. Compare with post-generation judgments and retained features.

Midjourney; design practice; intention
