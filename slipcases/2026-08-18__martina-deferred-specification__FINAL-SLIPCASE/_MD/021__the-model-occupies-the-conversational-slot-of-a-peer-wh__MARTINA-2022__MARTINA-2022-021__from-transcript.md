MARTINA-2022-021

TITLE:
The model occupies the conversational slot of a peer who proposes another way

SOURCE:
Martina interview with Watson Hartsoe, October 10, 2022. Unpublished transcript.

SOURCE URL:
NONE (private local source; preserved under _RESOURCES/PINK_MJ_Interview1.wh_Martina.pages and _RESOURCES/martina_transcript.txt)

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
At 51:42 Martina calls the system a collaborator, says it can make ideas visual for others and produce results she might never have thought about, and compares this to classmates or friends proposing another way.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
[OUR INFERENCE]
GENERATED ALTERNATIVES FUNCTION AS COUNTERPROPOSALS IN DESIGN REASONING.

LOCAL MOVE:
Martina's collaborator metaphor is grounded not in personhood but in capability complementarity and proposal generation.

SOURCE TERMS:
"collaborator"; "visual for others"; "results that you might never thought about"; "another way"

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A system can occupy a functional peer slot without sharing human intentions or social understanding.

QUESTION:
What exactly must an artifact-generating system contribute for "collaboration" to be analytically useful rather than anthropomorphic?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can counterproposal function be separated from claims about agency, understanding, or authorship?

MECHANISM:
User provides direction; model produces alternatives outside user's immediate repertoire; user evaluates and incorporates or rejects them.

FORMAL SHIFT:
COLLABORATION_FUNCTION = proposal diversity + responsiveness + iterative incorporation, not necessarily shared intention.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
[PARAPHRASE]
Martina explicitly compares model suggestions to classmates or friends suggesting another approach.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
PEER-LIKE FUNCTION can occur without PEER-LIKE MIND.

TENSION:
The model's "larger database" metaphor can obscure systematic biases and repeated defaults.

MISSING:
Interaction-level evidence comparing human and model counterproposals.

BOUNDARY:
Does not establish social reciprocity.

CITATION TRAIL:
Transcript 51:42 -> collaborator as counterproposal -> epistemic action -> decision authority.

TEST:
Give designers matched alternative proposals from peers and models, blind source identity, and compare incorporation, surprise, and explanation.

PLATFORM:
Midjourney; collaboration; design critique
