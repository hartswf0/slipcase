TITLE:
The machine can make originality anxiety temporarily optional

SOURCE:
Martina interview with Watson Hartsoe, October 10, 2022. Unpublished transcript.

SOURCE URL:
NONE (private local source; preserved under _RESOURCES/PINK_MJ_Interview1.wh_Martina.pages and _RESOURCES/martina_transcript.txt)

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
"I actually allowed myself to not be original, because the machine created it. I didn't." (32:20)

RESEARCH OBJECT:
[OUR INFERENCE]
DELEGATED EXECUTION CAN SUSPEND THE PRACTITIONER'S SELF-DEMAND FOR ORIGINALITY.

LOCAL MOVE:
Martina reports a changed evaluative standard when she attributes generation to the machine rather than herself.

SOURCE TERMS:
"allowed myself"; "not be original"; "machine created it"

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Reduced felt authorship can paradoxically expand exploratory freedom by lowering the personal stakes of originality.

QUESTION:
Can weaker ownership increase willingness to explore derivative, strange, or unsuccessful possibilities?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does generative delegation create a protected experimental zone, or does it merely externalize responsibility for derivative outcomes?

MECHANISM:
Attribution to machine lowers self-evaluative cost; practitioner permits broader exploration; later selection may reincorporate useful results.

FORMAL SHIFT:
LOWER FELT AUTHORSHIP -> LOWER ORIGINALITY ANXIETY -> WIDER SEARCH (candidate).

SOURCE FORMALISM:
[PARAPHRASE]
Martina explicitly links machine creation to allowing herself not to be original.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Exploration cost may decrease when creator identity is weakly attached to candidates.

TENSION:
Lower stakes may also reduce care or accountability.

MISSING:
Experimental manipulation of public attribution and candidate ownership.

BOUNDARY:
One statement cannot establish a general psychological mechanism.

CITATION TRAIL:
Transcript 32:20 -> originality suspension -> ownership cards -> exploration/control tension.

TEST:
Ask participants to ideate under self-authored, machine-authored, and anonymous candidate conditions; measure risk, diversity, and later retention.

PLATFORM:
Midjourney; originality; experimentation
