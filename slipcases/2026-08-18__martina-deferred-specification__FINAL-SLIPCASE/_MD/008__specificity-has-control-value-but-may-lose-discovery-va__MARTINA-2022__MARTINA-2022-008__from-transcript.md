TITLE:
Specificity has control value but may lose discovery value

SOURCE:
Martina interview with Watson Hartsoe, October 10, 2022. Unpublished transcript.

SOURCE URL:
NONE (private local source; preserved under _RESOURCES/PINK_MJ_Interview1.wh_Martina.pages and _RESOURCES/martina_transcript.txt)

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
"if you're specific ... quite accurate to what you have in mind" and "force myself to write a longer prompt and really tell it what to do." (40:25)

RESEARCH OBJECT:
[OUR INFERENCE]
CONTROL QUALITY AND EPISTEMIC QUALITY ARE DISTINCT.

LOCAL MOVE:
Martina values specificity because it increases correspondence, while [[MARTINA-2022-005]] values alternatives exposed by the template.

SOURCE TERMS:
"specific"; "accurate"; "force myself"; "tell it what to do"

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The practice contains two objectives that can conflict: converging on an articulated target and discovering a target through variation.

QUESTION:
What should remain open now, and what has earned the right to become fixed?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can prompt quality be evaluated without specifying whether the operation is executional or epistemic?

MECHANISM:
More constraints reduce some uncertainty and can increase correspondence; retained uncertainty can produce alternatives from which new preferences emerge.

FORMAL SHIFT:
CONTROL_VALUE(S) may rise with specificity S while DISCOVERY_VALUE(S) can peak earlier.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
[PARAPHRASE]
Martina links specificity to accuracy and deliberately uses a template to force more explicit description.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Q(prompt) = f(task_phase, control_value, discovery_value), not a scalar function of specificity alone.

TENSION:
Vague text is not equivalent to productive sketch ambiguity; the model may silently fill omissions with strong defaults.

MISSING:
A phase-sensitive experiment comparing exploration and convergence tasks.

BOUNDARY:
Does not establish a universal inverse relation between specificity and creativity.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[MARTINA-2022-003]] + [[MARTINA-2022-005]] -> tension -> Goel on ill-structured representations.

TEST:
Randomize designers to low-, medium-, and high-specification prompts at early and late project stages; measure fidelity, branching, and novelty retained later.

PLATFORM:
Midjourney; design exploration; specification
