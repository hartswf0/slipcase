ZETTEL

ID:
MARTINA-2022-020

TITLE:
The generated sketch compresses the cost of making alternatives inspectable

SOURCE:
Martina interview with Watson Hartsoe, October 10, 2022. Unpublished transcript.

SOURCE URL:
NONE (private local source; preserved under _RESOURCES/PINK_MJ_Interview1.wh_Martina.pages and _RESOURCES/martina_transcript.txt)

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
At 38:13 Martina describes generated images as a sketch or first idea that can then be built, and contrasts producing several initial ideas in hours with spending days.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
[OUR INFERENCE]
GENERATION REDUCES THE COST OF EXTERNALIZING ALTERNATIVE FUTURES.

LOCAL MOVE:
The relevant acceleration is not simply faster finished art; it is faster production of inspectable intermediate representations.

SOURCE TERMS:
"sketch"; "first idea"; "build it"; "different sketches"; "hours instead of days"

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Cheaper externalization changes how many alternatives can be materially compared before commitment.

QUESTION:
What happens to judgment when the cost of producing candidates falls faster than the capacity to evaluate them?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does abundance move the bottleneck from production to discrimination?

MECHANISM:
Low-cost generation increases candidate count; evaluation/selection becomes proportionally more consequential.

FORMAL SHIFT:
PRODUCTION BOTTLENECK -> SELECTION BOTTLENECK.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
[PARAPHRASE]
Martina treats images as intermediate sketches and emphasizes time compression.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
If cost(candidate) decreases, N_candidates increases until bounded by attention/judgment.

TENSION:
More candidates can create choice overload or shallow evaluation rather than better design.

MISSING:
Observed candidate counts, evaluation time, and retained quality across cost conditions.

BOUNDARY:
No claim that generative sketches are equivalent to hand sketches in representational properties.

CITATION TRAIL:
Transcript 38:13 -> sketch/time compression -> judgment bottleneck -> leadership as discrimination.

TEST:
Hold project time fixed while varying generation cost; measure number of alternatives, evaluation depth, and final rationale quality.

PLATFORM:
Midjourney; sketching; design alternatives
