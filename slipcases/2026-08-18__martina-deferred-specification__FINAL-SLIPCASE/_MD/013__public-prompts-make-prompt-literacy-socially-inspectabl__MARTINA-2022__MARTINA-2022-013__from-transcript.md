RESEARCH OBJECT:
[OUR INFERENCE]
OPEN PROMPT CULTURE FUNCTIONS AS A SHARED LEARNING INFRASTRUCTURE.

LOCAL MOVE:
Martina describes early Midjourney as an environment where prompts were visible and where keeping her own template private "didn't make sense."

SOURCE TERMS:
"see the prompts"; "everything is open source"; "help others"; "community"

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Prompt practice is learned in public and can therefore accumulate conventions faster than solitary trial and error.

QUESTION:
What knowledge is created by a community when descriptions and outputs are jointly inspectable?

DEEPER QUESTION:
When does open sharing support collective experimentation, and when does it produce convergence and imitation?

MECHANISM:
Public prompt-output pairs act as executable exemplars; users infer causal hypotheses and import them into personal templates.

FORMAL SHIFT:
COMMUNITY EXAMPLE -> HYPOTHESIS -> PERSONAL TEST -> TEMPLATE.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
[PARAPHRASE]
Martina treats prompt openness as a norm of the early community.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Prompt culture behaves like a distributed experiment log without controlled experimental design.

TENSION:
Visibility can also create competitive comparison and style drift, as [[MARTINA-2022-014]] reports.

MISSING:
Network-level evidence tracing diffusion of prompt phrases and stylistic outcomes.

BOUNDARY:
"Open source" is Martina's analogy, not a claim that prompts have software-source legal status.

CITATION TRAIL:
Transcript 15:51 -> openness -> transcript 59:29 contradiction -> public learning versus preference contamination.

TEST:
Trace distinctive prompt phrases through public galleries and subsequent personal use; compare learning gains with convergence effects.

PLATFORM:
Midjourney community; public prompts
