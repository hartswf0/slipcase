TITLE:
The concept can precede the image without containing it

SOURCE:
Martina interview with Watson Hartsoe, October 10, 2022. Unpublished transcript.

SOURCE URL:
NONE (private local source; preserved under _RESOURCES/PINK_MJ_Interview1.wh_Martina.pages and _RESOURCES/martina_transcript.txt)

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
"I'm very conceptual, but there's nothing there. It's just thoughts. And my journey actually makes the images and it constructs this environment and this whole thing." (33:34)

RESEARCH OBJECT:
[OUR INFERENCE]
CONCEPTUAL DIRECTION AND VISUAL REPRESENTATION ARE SEPARABLE STATES.

LOCAL MOVE:
This card pressure-tests [[MARTINA-2022-001]]. Martina does not merely say the image is blurry; she says the model constructs the environment.

SOURCE TERMS:
"conceptual"; "nothing there"; "thoughts"; "constructs this environment"

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The generated environment can contain determinations that were not present as a prior image.

QUESTION:
What relation does the generated image bear to a concept that did not already contain it?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is generation expression, interpretation, completion, proposal, construction, or some changing combination across iterations?

MECHANISM:
A concept supplies orientation; generation supplies concrete relations; later judgment determines which relations are incorporated into the project.

FORMAL SHIFT:
CONCEPT != IMAGE_SPECIFICATION.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
[PARAPHRASE]
Martina distinguishes conceptual thought from a constructed visual environment.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
CONCEPT C constrains a region of acceptable futures R, without enumerating a target image I.

TENSION:
Calling the model a constructor can overstate agency; the operational claim is only that output properties exceed explicit specification.

MISSING:
A feature-level comparison between initial concept statements and generated image properties.

BOUNDARY:
Does not establish that no visual imagery exists at all; it establishes Martina's reported distinction.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[MARTINA-2022-001]] -> transcript 33:34 -> concept/image separation -> classify relations between concept and generated proposal.

TEST:
Have independent coders map each visible output property to explicit prior statement, plausible implication, or newly supplied detail.
