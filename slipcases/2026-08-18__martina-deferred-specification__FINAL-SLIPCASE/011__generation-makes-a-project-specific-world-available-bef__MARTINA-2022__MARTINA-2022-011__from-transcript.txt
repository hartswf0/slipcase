RESEARCH OBJECT:
[OUR INFERENCE]
GENERATION AS PROJECT-SPECIFIC REPRESENTATIONAL PROSTHESIS.

LOCAL MOVE:
Martina uses Midjourney for storytelling projects because she can visualize a scenario specifically for her project despite not being good at drawing.

SOURCE TERMS:
"sketching your ideas"; "visualize this scenario"; "specifically for my project"; "my idea, my point of view"

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The generated image occupies a space between generic reference imagery and personally executed illustration.

QUESTION:
What becomes possible when a person can commission a project-specific representation before possessing the craft to execute it?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does representational access redistribute leadership without eliminating the need for domain judgment?

MECHANISM:
Conceptual direction becomes shareable visual artifact through a model; artifact can then participate in further design and communication.

FORMAL SHIFT:
CONCEPT -> GENERATED REPRESENTATION -> SOCIAL/PROJECT OPERATIONS.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
[PARAPHRASE]
Martina frames Midjourney as a way to sketch and visualize narrative project scenarios she cannot readily draw.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
CAPABILITY_PATH = concept -> model-mediated representation -> critique/pitch/build.

TENSION:
Representation is not equivalent to mastery of illustration or technical implementation.

MISSING:
Observation of how generated representations are subsequently used in project decisions.

BOUNDARY:
Claims concern access to representation, not finished product capability.

TEST:
Follow one project from conceptual statement through generated imagery to downstream design decisions; identify which decisions become possible only after representation exists.
