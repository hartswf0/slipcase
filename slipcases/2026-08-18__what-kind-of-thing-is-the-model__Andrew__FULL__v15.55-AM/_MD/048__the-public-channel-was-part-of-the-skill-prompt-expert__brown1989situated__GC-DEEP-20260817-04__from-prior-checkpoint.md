TITLE:
THE PUBLIC CHANNEL WAS PART OF THE SKILL — prompt expertise was learned through situated observation of other people acting with the tool.

SOURCE:
John Seely Brown, Allan Collins, Paul Duguid — “Situated Cognition and the Culture of Learning” — Educational Researcher 18(1), 1989, 32–42. SOURCE URL: https://doi.org/10.3102/0013189X018001032

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
“Activity, concept, and culture are interdependent.”

[PARAPHRASE]
Brown, Collins, and Duguid argue that knowledge is situated in the activity, context, and culture in which it is developed and used, and describe apprenticeship as learning through participation in authentic practice.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
PROMPT-EXPERTISE-AS-SITUATED-ENCULTURATION.

LOCAL MOVE:
[[MJ-GC-002]], [[MJ-GC-007]], [[MJ-GC-014]], and [[MJ-GC-015]] describe a learning environment in which public generations, prompt visibility, imitation, remix, expert clubs, and group scale all affect what can be learned. The platform is not neutral delivery infrastructure; it is part of the curriculum.

SOURCE TERMS:
“activity”
“concept”
“culture”
“situated”
“cognitive apprenticeship”
“enculturation”

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Moving generation from a public channel to private interaction may change the knowledge being produced even when the model is unchanged, because the disappearance of ambient examples removes part of the learning environment.

QUESTION:
How much early prompt expertise depended on public observability rather than documentation or individual trial?

DEEPER QUESTION:
If prompt knowledge is situated in communal practice, what is lost when interfaces privatize interaction and separate prompts from their social histories?

MECHANISM:
PUBLIC ACTIVITY
→ observe prompt + output + response
→ imitate / vary / discuss
→ acquire local vocabulary and norms
→ participate as practitioner.

FORMAL SHIFT:
FROM: prompting skill as individual textual competence.
TO: prompting skill as participation in a situated culture of tool use.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Brown, Collins, and Duguid argue that knowledge and learning are situated and that apprenticeship embeds learners in authentic activity and social interaction.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

SKILL = individual experimentation + visible peer practice + local norms + shared exemplars.

Remove visibility and the learning system changes even if MODEL remains constant.

TENSION:
Publicness can accelerate learning while undermining privacy, ownership, concentration, or professional work. Andrew explicitly experiences both benefits.

MISSING:
Historical platform data comparing public-channel and private-mode learning trajectories.

BOUNDARY:
The source concerns learning and apprenticeship broadly, not AI prompting specifically.

TEST:
Compare novice learning in three conditions: private prompting, public prompt-output feed, and public feed plus expert commentary. Measure transfer to novel prompt problems.
