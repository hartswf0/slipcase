EPISTEMIC-TO-PRAGMATIC-CONVERSION.

LOCAL MOVE:
[[MJ-GC-025]]–[[MJ-GC-027]] do not merely complain that Midjourney lacks features. They draw a phase boundary. Exploration can flourish under variability, but consequential making requires some discoveries to harden into persistence, repeatability, editable relations, and control.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The property that makes the system compelling during exploration—its capacity to surprise—can be the same property that prevents it from becoming usable for sustained work.

QUESTION:
Which discovered regularities must become invariants before a generative system crosses from exploratory game to production tool?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is toolhood the institutionalization of successful epistemic action: turning things once learned by probing into controls that no longer have to be rediscovered?

MECHANISM:
EXPLORATION
→ discover regularity / possibility
→ stabilize identity, parameter, reference, edit, or constraint
→ reuse without rediscovery
→ pragmatic production.

FORMAL SHIFT:
FROM: CONTROL opposes creativity.
TO: CONTROL can be the sediment of earlier exploratory learning.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

TOOLHOOD = STABILIZED(EPISTEMIC_DISCOVERIES).

A capability becomes tool-like when its relevant conditions can be recalled and reused as invariants rather than reprobed each time.

TENSION:
Hardening discoveries into controls can suppress the generative deviation that produced those discoveries. Tool development can narrow the exploratory field it emerged from.

MISSING:
A longitudinal feature history connecting community-discovered techniques to later interface controls.

BOUNDARY:
“Game” and “tool” are the participant’s evaluative categories, not universal ontological classes.

CITATION TRAIL:

→ toolhood as stabilization of discoveries rather than simple increase in capability.

TEST:
For each major control feature added to an image generator, reconstruct whether an equivalent practice existed informally before productization. Test whether feature formalization reduces the need for exploratory prompting.
