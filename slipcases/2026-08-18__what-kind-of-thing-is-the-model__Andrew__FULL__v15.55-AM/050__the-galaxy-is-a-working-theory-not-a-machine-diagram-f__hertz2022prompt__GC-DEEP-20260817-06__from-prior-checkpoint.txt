TITLE:
THE GALAXY IS A WORKING THEORY, NOT A MACHINE DIAGRAM — folk mechanics can guide action while being wrong about mechanism.

SOURCE:
Amir Hertz et al. — “Prompt-to-Prompt Image Editing with Cross Attention Control” — 2022, arXiv:2208.01626; Raphael Tang et al. — “What the DAAM: Interpreting Stable Diffusion Using Cross Attention” — ACL 2023. SOURCE URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.01626 ; https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.310/

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Hertz et al. identify cross-attention as a control relation between prompt words and spatial image layout in a text-conditioned diffusion model. Tang et al. aggregate cross-attention maps to study word attribution, syntax in pixel space, and feature entanglement. These mechanisms provide technically inspectable explanations for some word-combination phenomena without requiring literal semantic galaxies or gravity wells.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
VERNACULAR-MECHANICS-VERSUS-INTERNAL-MECHANISM.

LOCAL MOVE:
[[MJ-GC-019]]–[[MJ-GC-023]] develop an unusually rich user theory: valleys, galaxies, gravity wells, black holes, and orbital mechanics. The important research move is not to ridicule or literalize it. Later diffusion work shows that words can indeed have spatially differentiated and compositionally unequal effects, while locating those effects in machinery quite unlike the folk cosmology.

SOURCE TERMS:
“cross-attention”
“spatial layout”
“each word”
“pixel space”
“feature entanglement”

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A false ontology can support true operational predictions. A practitioner may successfully learn how to steer a model with a metaphor whose entities do not exist inside the implementation.

QUESTION:
How should we evaluate a user theory that predicts interventions well but misdescribes internal mechanism?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is prompt expertise partly the construction of interface-level theories whose criterion is manipulability rather than mechanistic truth?

MECHANISM:
OBSERVED REGULARITIES
→ folk spatial metaphor
→ intervention heuristic
→ usable prediction.

Technical inspection may later provide a different causal account for overlapping phenomena.

FORMAL SHIFT:
FROM: folk theory = misconception.
TO: folk theory can be an operative model whose predictive and mechanistic adequacy must be tested separately.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Hertz et al. analyze cross-attention in a diffusion model and intervene on attention to control text-based edits. Tang et al. aggregate cross-attention into attribution maps and report syntax/semantic interaction phenomena.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Evaluate theory T on two axes:
PREDICTIVE_UTILITY(T)
MECHANISTIC_FIDELITY(T).

High utility does not entail high fidelity.

TENSION:
The external technical papers study Stable Diffusion-like systems, not the proprietary Midjourney architecture discussed in 2022. They pressure the metaphor but cannot reveal what Midjourney actually implemented.

MISSING:
Black-box experiments that test the gravity model’s predictions without assuming a diffusion architecture.

BOUNDARY:
Formal resemblance between observed word effects and cross-attention phenomena is not evidence of shared implementation.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[MJ-GC-019]] → galaxy

TEST:
Write quantitative predictions implied by the gravity metaphor before inspecting outputs. Test them across models and versions. Separately test whether attention-based measures predict the same failures in an open diffusion model.

PLATFORM:
Midjourney folk theory / Stable Diffusion interpretability
