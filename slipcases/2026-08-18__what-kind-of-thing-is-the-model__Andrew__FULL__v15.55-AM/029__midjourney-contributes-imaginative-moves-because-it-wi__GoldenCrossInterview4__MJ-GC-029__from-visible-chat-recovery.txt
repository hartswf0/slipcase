WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The model's failure to follow conventional intention can operate as a source of imaginative difference.

QUESTION:
How can we distinguish a model-generated imaginative move from an arbitrary mistake retrospectively interpreted as creative?

DEEPER QUESTION:
If a user adopts a machine-generated deviation that they “would never think to do,” has the machine become part of the idea-generation process rather than only its execution?
