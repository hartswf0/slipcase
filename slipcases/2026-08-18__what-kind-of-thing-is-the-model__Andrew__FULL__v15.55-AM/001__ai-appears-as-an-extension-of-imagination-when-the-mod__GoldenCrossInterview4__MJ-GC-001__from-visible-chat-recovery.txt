MJ-GC-001

TITLE:
AI appears as an extension of imagination when the model supplies capacities the user does not possess.

SOURCE:
GOLD_MJ_Interview_4.wh.GoldenCross_otter_ai 2.pages — Otter.ai transcript — 01:12.

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
“So the ability to take a tool and use my creativity and imagination, and extend those with those other skills by the AI. That's I think, what was interesting to me.”

RESEARCH OBJECT:
AI-AS-CAPABILITY-EXTENSION.

LOCAL MOVE:
The speaker does not describe AI primarily as autonomous creation. The attraction is that existing creativity and imagination can be extended through skills supplied by the system.

SOURCE TERMS:
“tool”
“creativity”
“imagination”
“extend”
“other skills”
“by the AI”

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The boundary between human ability and tool ability becomes difficult to locate once the user experiences the combined system as an extension of their own imagination.

QUESTION:
What exactly is being extended: imagination, execution, technique, or the range of actions available to imagination?

DEEPER QUESTION:
When a tool supplies a missing skill while the user supplies intention, where should authorship and competence be located?

MECHANISM:
USER CAPACITY
→ tool access
→ AI-supplied skill
→ expanded realizable imagination.

FORMAL SHIFT:
FROM tool as instrument
TO tool as capability-completing extension.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
IMAGINABLE(x) + AI_SKILL(x→artifact)
→ REALIZABLE(x)

TENSION:
The speaker describes extension of personal creativity, but the skill producing the realization belongs partly to the model.

MISSING:
Examples distinguishing ideas the speaker could imagine before AI from ideas that became imaginable only through interaction with AI.

BOUNDARY:
This passage establishes experienced extension, not that AI literally becomes part of cognition.

CITATION TRAIL:
Transcript 01:12
→ extension of imagination through AI-supplied skills
→ distributed capability.

TEST:
Compare later passages where the model produces outcomes the speaker explicitly says they would never have thought to produce. Determine whether the model merely executes imagination or changes imagination itself.
