PF-SITUATEDNESS-012

TITLE:
The hard limit may not be private creativity but the inability to transfer a position into context.

SOURCE:
Unattributed working manuscript — “Aphoristic Fragments,” fragments 90–96 — date not stated. fileciteturn0file0L1006-L1084

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
“What does my body know?
What does my shame know?
What does thirty years of looking at faces know without being able to state it?
I cannot pour all of this into the context window.”

[QUOTE]
“Perhaps the decisive human contribution is not a secret substance called creativity.
Perhaps it is that I am somewhere.”

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Situated remainder that cannot simply be converted into explicit prompt context.

LOCAL MOVE:
The manuscript replaces an essentialist defense of human creativity with a positional problem: embodied history supplies discriminations that may guide selection without being fully articulable.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The human/model difference proposed here is not possession of a mysterious faculty but asymmetry of situation.

QUESTION:
What parts of effective prompting depend on a history and position that cannot be exhaustively represented in the prompt?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is the context-window problem fundamentally one of insufficient capacity, or of converting lived participation into transmissible representation?

MECHANISM:
The operator makes judgments using accumulated bodily, social, temporal, and practical history. Only selected traces of that history are externalized into the interaction. The model acts on the externalized remainder, while the operator continues judging from the larger situation.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<situated history>
→ <partial explicit context>
→ [model acts on representation]
→ <operator evaluates from unreduced situation>

SOURCE FORMALISM:

OUR FORMALIZATION:

SITUATION_USER ⊃ CONTEXT_SUPPLIED_TO_MODEL

The difference is not necessarily a hidden substance.
It may be a persistent asymmetry between participation and representation.

TENSION:
Models can also receive sensor data, persistent memory, user histories, and environmental state. Increased context might narrow this asymmetry without eliminating it.

MISSING:
A distinction between information that is merely not yet encoded and information whose practical role changes when converted into an explicit representation.

BOUNDARY:
The passage does not establish an absolute metaphysical difference between humans and machines.

CITATION TRAIL:
Situated cognition; embodied cognition; tacit knowledge; deixis; indexicality; Polanyi; phenomenology; ethnomethodology.

TEST:
Identify judgments the operator can reliably make but cannot initially verbalize. Iteratively externalize cues into the model context and test which judgments become reproducible and which remain dependent on situated evaluation.
