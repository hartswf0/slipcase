ARRANGEMENT — SEVEN FAILED DEFINITIONS — 2026-08-18

1. COMMAND — catches operational mood; breaks on deixis and collaborative reference.
2. DESCRIPTION — catches wording; breaks when apparatus and felicity conditions carry the consequence.
3. PROGRAM — catches execution; breaks when programming itself exceeds program text and examples act as specification.
4. SPECIFICATION — catches constraints; breaks when failure, repair, and tacit criteria continue writing the task.
5. CONTEXT — catches surroundings; breaks because world model, history, live coupling, and situated position are not one mechanism.
6. TRACE — catches production history; breaks because provenance does not supply an attribution rule.
7. DISTRIBUTED SYSTEM — catches multiplicity; breaks unless transformations, authority, and responsibility are separately specified.

PROVISIONAL WAGER:
Prompt research has a unit-of-analysis problem. Do not define the prompt again until the boundary of the object is made explicit and tested.
