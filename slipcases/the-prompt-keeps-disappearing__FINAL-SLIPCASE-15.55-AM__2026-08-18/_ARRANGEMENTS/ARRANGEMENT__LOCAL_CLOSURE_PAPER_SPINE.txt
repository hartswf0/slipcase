ARRANGEMENT — THE PROGRAM IS WHERE AMBIGUITY STOPS — 2026-08-17

1. Reject the weak novelty claim: executable language and interactive specification are old.
2. Distinguish FORMALIZATION from CLOSURE.
3. Show early systems buying closure before runtime: SHRDLU through world restriction; ACE through language restriction.
4. Show interactive traditions delaying closure: Pygmalion, Naur, Suchman, Clark.
5. Reconstruct contemporary agents as a chain of local closure operators.
6. Name five closure dimensions: referential, structural/procedural, criterial, consequential, temporal.
7. Make the stronger wager: the operative program is the topology and ordering of these boundaries, not the initial prompt.
8. Pressure the wager with tacit criteria and situatedness: not every useful ambiguity should be eliminated.
9. Derive CONSEQUENCE-PROPORTIONAL CLOSURE: semantic slack should shrink as reversibility decreases, while human judgment remains available where the criterion cannot safely be compiled.
10. End with a testable research program: compare systems by what stays open, what closes, who/what closes it, and which state transition makes closure necessary.
