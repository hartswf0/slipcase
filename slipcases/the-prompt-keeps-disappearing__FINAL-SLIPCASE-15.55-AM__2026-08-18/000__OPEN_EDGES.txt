OPEN EDGES — SLIPCASE-20260818-UNIT-01

THESE ARE NOT A CONCLUSION. THEY ARE THE PLACES THE PAPER FAILED TO CLOSE.

1. MINIMUM EXPLANATORY UNIT
QUESTION: What is the smallest unit that can explain a prompt-conditioned effect without silently borrowing state from outside the unit?
TEST: Hold the visible utterance constant while independently varying interaction history, tool definitions, selected objects, permissions, model, and live environment. Record which “prompt effects” survive.

2. THE SAME WORDS, DIFFERENT APPARATUS
QUESTION: When identical text produces different action because the surrounding apparatus changes, what exactly belongs to the causal object under study?
TEST: Run one utterance across read-only chat, tool-enabled chat, selected-object editing, and permission-gated mutation.

3. SITUATION IS NOT A LONGER STRING
QUESTION: Which situation particulars can be represented as context without changing the practical role they played for the situated human?
TEST: Externalize tacit cues incrementally. Compare represented context with live re-perception and human-in-the-loop judgment.

4. FAILURE OR NOISE
QUESTION: When does breakdown reveal an interpretation, and when is it merely stochastic variation?
TEST: Reproduce each failure under controlled seeds/models/contexts; classify only repeatable diagnostic patterns as evidence about interpretation.

5. CRITERIA BEFORE RUBRICS
QUESTION: Can a stable criterion guide revision before it can be stated, and what is lost when it is forced into a rubric?
TEST: Collect judgments first; articulate later; measure residual decisions that the rubric cannot recover.

6. PROVENANCE WITHOUT VERDICT
QUESTION: Which attribution rules map the same production trace to different authorship judgments?
TEST: Hold one complete trace fixed and vary explicit attribution criteria across publishing, scholarship, design, and ordinary-language evaluators.

7. DISTRIBUTION WITHOUT EVASION
QUESTION: Does “distributed” identify a mechanism or merely prevent us from naming who generated, selected, authorized, revised, and endorsed what?
TEST: Trace one paragraph operation by operation and separate causal contribution from accountability.

8. HISTORICAL NOVELTY
QUESTION: Which mechanisms in LLM prompting are genuinely new, which are older mechanisms at new scale, and which old distinctions simply stopped being serviceable?
TEST: Compare SHRDLU, ACE, Pygmalion, situated-action systems, and contemporary agents operation by operation; mark novelty only where no adequate prior mechanism survives comparison.

9. WHEN SHOULD THE NOUN BE ABANDONED?
QUESTION: At what point does enlarging “prompt” to include history, tools, schemas, model state, interface state, and user judgment make the term analytically empty?
TEST: Define nested units and compare predictive/explanatory gain against added scope.
