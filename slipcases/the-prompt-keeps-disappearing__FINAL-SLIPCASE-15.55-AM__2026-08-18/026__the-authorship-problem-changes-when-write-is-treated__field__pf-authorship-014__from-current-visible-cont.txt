PF-AUTHORSHIP-014

TITLE:
The authorship problem changes when “write” is treated as a bundle of operations rather than a sovereign act.

SOURCE:
Unattributed working manuscript — “Aphoristic Fragments,” fragment 15 and fragments 100–106 — date not stated. fileciteturn0file0L210-L224 fileciteturn0file0L1116-L1174

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
“I wrote the prompt.
The model wrote the sentence.
I chose the sentence.
I changed the sentence.
I placed it here.
Which of these is writing?”

[QUOTE]
“The philosophical trouble begins only when I demand that one of them describe the whole event.”

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Decomposition of the predicate “write.”

LOCAL MOVE:
The manuscript stops asking which actor exclusively owns writing and instead breaks writing into distinguishable operations whose allocation can vary.

SOURCE TERMS:
wrote
prompt
sentence
chose
changed
placed
whole event
authorship
indivisible

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The dispute “human wrote it / model wrote it” may arise because one ordinary verb is being forced to provide a complete causal description.

QUESTION:
Which operations are ordinarily compressed into “write,” and which of them are actually relevant to different authorship disputes?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Do authorship controversies persist because different institutions silently privilege different members of the operation bundle?

MECHANISM:
A complex production event is compressed under an ordinary predicate. Different speakers select different operations as decisive. Conflict appears when one selected operation is treated as exhaustive of the event.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<heterogeneous production process>
→ <single predicate: WRITE>
→ [select one operation as decisive]
→ <apparently exclusive authorship claim>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
WRITE = family resemblance / bundled predicate over operations such as:
INITIATE
SPECIFY
GENERATE
SELECT
REJECT
REVISE
ARRANGE
ENDORSE

Authorship disputes may result from different weightings over this bundle.

TENSION:
Ordinary predicates routinely compress complex events without becoming defective. Decomposition must demonstrate analytical payoff rather than merely greater granularity.

MISSING:
Institution-specific evidence about which operations actually control attribution in publishing, scholarship, law, design, and everyday speech.

BOUNDARY:
The passage does not establish that “write” has no useful ordinary meaning. It objects to demanding that one use of the word exhaustively describe the event.

CITATION TRAIL:
Ordinary-language philosophy; authorship law; textual scholarship; sociology of creative labor; distributed cognition; speech-act analysis.

TEST:
Present identical production histories to participants while varying only the question: “Who generated?”, “Who selected?”, “Who revised?”, “Who wrote?”, “Who is responsible?” Compare attribution shifts.
