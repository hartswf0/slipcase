COUNTEREVIDENCE / RESISTANCE
- Pygmalion, Naur, and Suchman prevent claiming that post-text specification is new.
- Structured representation does not guarantee semantic correctness.
- Reviewer/commit gates can false-hold authorized actions.
- Tacit evaluative competence may be damaged by premature rubricization.
- Represented context is not equivalent to ongoing participation in a situation.

BOUNDARY
The paper does not establish a universal metric of ambiguity, a complete formal calculus of closure, or direct historical influence among the cited traditions. It proposes a comparative and testable design vocabulary. The authorship implication remains an open edge rather than a settled paper claim.
