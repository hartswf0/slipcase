SOURCE MAP — THE PROMPT KEEPS DISAPPEARING
STATUS: compiler trace; not public evidence

CENTRAL CLAIM
Every time the prompt is isolated tightly enough to explain its agency, some causal work appears outside that boundary; when the boundary expands to include the missing work, “the prompt” becomes less like a distinct explanatory object. The paper treats this recurrence as a problem, not as a completed ontology.
SUPPORT: collision among the zettels below; no single source states this synthesis.
