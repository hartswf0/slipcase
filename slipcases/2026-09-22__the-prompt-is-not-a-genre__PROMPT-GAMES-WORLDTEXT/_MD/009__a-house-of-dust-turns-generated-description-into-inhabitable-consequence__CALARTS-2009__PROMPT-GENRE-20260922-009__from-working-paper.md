TITLE:
A House of Dust turns generated description into inhabitable consequence

SOURCE:
California Institute of the Arts, "Alison Knowles, James Tenney and the House of Dust at CalArts," 2009; related CalArts account, 2018.

SOURCE URL:
https://blog.calarts.edu/2009/09/10/alison-knowles-james-tenney-and-the-house-of-dust-at-calarts/

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]\nKnowles and Tenney used FORTRAN-based chance operations with lists of materials, sites, light sources, and inhabitants to generate a poem of houses; one generated quatrain was subsequently realized as an inhabitable structure at CalArts.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
[OUR INFERENCE]\nVOCABULARY -> COMPUTATIONAL PERMUTATION -> POEM -> ARCHITECTURE -> SOCIAL SITUATION.

LOCAL MOVE:
Provide a pre-LLM bridge where generated text crosses into spatial realization without pretending that the text fully determines the result.

SOURCE TERMS:
House of Dust; FORTRAN; permutation; architecture; realization

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The description is neither merely commentary nor a complete construction program.

QUESTION:
What kind of operative object is a generated description that organizes a field of realizable relations?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Which contemporary prompt practices repeat this score-to-space relation rather than program-to-output execution?

MECHANISM:
Combinatorial text supplies relational constraints that human construction and later inhabitation elaborate.

FORMAL SHIFT:
TEXT_GENERATION -> MATERIAL_REALIZATION -> SOCIAL_FEEDBACK.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
[PARAPHRASE]\nThe project links generated quatrains to a built House of Dust and subsequent performances/teaching.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
Operativity can propagate through multiple executors rather than one compiler.

TENSION:
The built house is an interpretation, not a deterministic decode of the poem.

MISSING:
Comparative study of how different executors would realize the same generated quatrain.

BOUNDARY:
CalArts accounts document the project history; the operative-ekphrasis reading is Hartsoe's.

CITATION TRAIL:
score -> generated poem -> built house -> social world.

TEST:
Give the same House of Dust stanza to multiple human or computational builders and compare preserved relations.

PLATFORM:
Generative art; architecture; operative description
