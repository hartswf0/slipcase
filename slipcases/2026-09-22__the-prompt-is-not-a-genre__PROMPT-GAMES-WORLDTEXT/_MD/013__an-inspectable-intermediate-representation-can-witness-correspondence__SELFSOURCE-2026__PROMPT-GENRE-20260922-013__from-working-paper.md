TITLE:
An inspectable intermediate representation can witness correspondence

SOURCE:
Watson Hartsoe, The Prompt Is Not a Genre: Language Games, Operative Ekphrasis, and Worldtext, Working Paper, 22 September 2026.

SOURCE URL:
https://github.com/hartswf0/slipcase/blob/main/slipcases/2026-09-22__the-prompt-is-not-a-genre__PROMPT-GAMES-WORLDTEXT/the-prompt-is-not-a-genre__2026-09-22.pdf

PASSAGE:
[PROJECT CASE — OUR DESCRIPTION]\nIn WAG, natural-language descriptions are routed through LDraw text and explicit 3D geometry before generated imagery and motion.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
[OUR INFERENCE]\nTHE INTERMEDIATE REPRESENTATION IS A WITNESS BETWEEN INTENTION AND SYNTHESIS.

LOCAL MOVE:
Use WAG as a case study in separating structural correspondence from generative surface completion.

SOURCE TERMS:
WAG; LDraw; geometry; intermediate representation; witness

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
When structure and surface are fused in one probabilistic request, error provenance becomes hard to locate.

QUESTION:
Which intermediate state lets us tell whether failure occurred in reference, geometry, style, or synthesis?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can witness representations become a general method for prompt accountability across code, music, film, and spatial systems?

MECHANISM:
The description is compiled into a deterministic or inspectable substrate before probabilistic synthesis adds latitude.

FORMAL SHIFT:
WORDS -> STRUCTURE -> SYNTHESIS, with TEST(STRUCTURE) before SYNTHESIS.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
[PROJECT FORMALIZATION]

OUR FORMALIZATION:
A witness state makes some errors localizable before later generative stages obscure them.

TENSION:
Intermediate representations can narrow discovery and impose their own ontology.

MISSING:
Cross-domain comparison of witness representations and their control/discovery tradeoffs.

BOUNDARY:
WAG is a case study/illustration of the argument, not independent proof.

CITATION TRAIL:
word-to-world -> LDraw witness -> visible correspondence -> repair.

TEST:
Deliberately inject structural and stylistic errors at separate stages and test whether users can correctly locate the failure.

PLATFORM:
WAG; LDraw; spatial prompting; correspondence
