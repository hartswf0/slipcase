TITLE:
Thick prompting means more of the situation, not more adjectives

SOURCE:
Gilbert Ryle, "The Thinking of Thoughts: What Is 'Le Penseur' Doing?", 1968/2009. Clifford Geertz, The Interpretation of Cultures, 1973.

SOURCE URL:
https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Interpretation_Of_Cultures.html?id=Vn74xQEACAAJ

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]\nRyle's twitch/wink distinction and Geertz's thick description show that physically similar events can be different acts because intention, code, audience, and situation differ.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
[OUR INFERENCE]\nTHICK PROMPTING = MORE OF THE SITUATION, NOT MERELY MORE DESCRIPTION.

LOCAL MOVE:
Separate contextual thickness from florid specification.

SOURCE TERMS:
thin description; thick description; wink; context; situation

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A longer prompt can remain thin if it adds surface detail without specifying the relations that make the act intelligible.

QUESTION:
Which contextual relations make an intended continuation legible?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can thickness be operationalized without forcing users to verbalize context that an interface could carry directly?

MECHANISM:
The same observable behavior acquires different force under different codes, roles, histories, and success conditions.

FORMAL SHIFT:
THICKNESS != TOKEN_COUNT.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
[PARAPHRASE]\nInterpretive adequacy requires reconstructing structures of significance, not merely recording physical behavior.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
THICK_PROMPT = move + relevant world + relations + roles + invariants + evidence.

TENSION:
Over-explication can destroy useful latitude and turn a score into a brittle specification.

MISSING:
A clause-level method for distinguishing situational context from decorative descriptive detail.

BOUNDARY:
Ryle and Geertz do not propose a method called thick prompting; the term is Hartsoe's extension.

CITATION TRAIL:
Ryle twitch/wink -> Geertz thick description -> thick prompting.

TEST:
Compare prompts matched for length but differing in situational structure; evaluate controllability and interpretability.

PLATFORM:
Anthropology; thick description; prompting
