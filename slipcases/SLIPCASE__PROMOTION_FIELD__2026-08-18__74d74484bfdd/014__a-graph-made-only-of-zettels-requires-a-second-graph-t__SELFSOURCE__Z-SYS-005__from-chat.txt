TITLE:
A graph made only of zettels requires a second graph that is not made of zettels

SOURCE:
User-provided prompt system — PRIME ZETTEL FORAGE — AUTONOMOUS GRAPH INQUIRY v4.0 — supplied 2026-08-18.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] The daemon states that ZETTEL is the only research-object type, yet internally maintains ZETTEL_SET, EDGE_SET, ACTIVE_FRONTIER, VISITED_EDGES, OPEN_CONTRADICTIONS, and SOURCE_HUBS, explicitly excluding this orchestration state from zettel syntax.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Research-object monism and execution-state pluralism coexist by placing the latter outside the declared graph.

LOCAL MOVE:
The prompt protects the zettel schema from scheduler metadata.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The daemon cannot operate on “zettels alone.” It requires a substantial shadow structure whose objects are indispensable but officially not research objects.

QUESTION:
What is the ontological status of ACTIVE_FRONTIER, OPEN_CONTRADICTIONS, and SOURCE_HUBS if they can determine future research but are not nodes in the research graph?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Which state must survive a context loss for the autonomous research process—not merely its accumulated notes—to be resumable?

MECHANISM:
Zettels remain stable public research objects.
A hidden controller compiles transient higher-order state from them.
That state selects actions.
Actions create new zettels.
The controller recompiles.

TENSION:
Ancient Master explicitly preserves reconstruction state, suggesting that some of this “internal” scheduler state becomes consequential enough to archive.

MISSING:

BOUNDARY:
Derived state need not become a zettel. The problem is persistence and reproducibility, not necessarily datatype proliferation.
