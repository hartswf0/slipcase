TITLE:
The paper is forbidden to become evidence, but its novel synthesis needs a route back into inquiry

SOURCE:

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] The paper must produce a non-obvious argument from the field, including at least one synthesis not already contained in a single zettel, while PAPER remains a current wager and never evidence.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The compiler deliberately permits the paper to know something no individual zettel knew while denying that new synthesis evidentiary status.

LOCAL MOVE:
Protects scholarship from recursively laundering its own conclusions into evidence.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
If the paper discovers the strongest new question in the entire process, the archival rules do not yet say how that question becomes forageable without making the paper an evidentiary authority.

QUESTION:
How does a novel paper-level synthesis re-enter the research graph?

DEEPER QUESTION:
What is the difference between turning an interpretation into a new hypothesis and turning an interpretation into evidence?

Inference remains marked as inference.
A subsequent research object could preserve the inference as a question or hypothesis and seek independent sources.
