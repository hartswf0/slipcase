“Preserve evidence once” means canonicalize once, not physically store once

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] Ancient Master says to preserve evidence once and derive everything else, yet also requires one exact root .txt payload and an exact mirror in _MD/.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The system's “once” cannot literally mean one physical copy. It must mean one canonical evidentiary identity from which redundant representations are verified.

LOCAL MOVE:
Resolves an apparent contradiction between archival normalization and portability.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Redundancy is necessary for usability and portability, but redundancy is also the mechanism by which supposedly identical evidence can drift.

QUESTION:
What makes one representation canonical when several exact copies are deliberately shipped?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can every duplicated manifestation be mechanically proven to be a representation of one preserved evidentiary object rather than an independent copy?
