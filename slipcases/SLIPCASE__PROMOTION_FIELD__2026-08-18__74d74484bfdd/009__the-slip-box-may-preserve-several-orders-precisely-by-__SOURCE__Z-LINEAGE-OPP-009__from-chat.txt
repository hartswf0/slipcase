TITLE:
The slip box may preserve several orders precisely by refusing to choose one

SOURCE:
Niklas-Luhmann-Archiv — Zettelkasten contents overview and archival documentation.

SOURCE URL:
https://niklas-luhmann-archiv.de/bestand/zettelkasten/inhaltsuebersicht

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] The archive documents not one undifferentiated branching sequence but multiple simultaneous structures: Luhmann’s internal numbering, large thematic regions, bibliographic material, and keyword indexes.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
“Non-descriptive addresses” do not imply a non-topical archive. Semantic classification and non-semantic local addresses coexist.

LOCAL MOVE:
The archive separates the function of an address from the function of a topic, an index term, and a branch.

SOURCE TERMS:
Zettelkasten
Ordnungsnummer
Schlagwortregister
Sachgebiete
Bibliographie

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The alternative to folders is not necessarily “no categories.” It may be refusing to make one category system carry every organizational burden.

QUESTION:
What becomes possible when address, neighborhood, topic, keyword, citation, and traversal are allowed to disagree?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is the slip box powerful because it abolishes hierarchy, or because it superimposes several partial hierarchies without requiring their reconciliation?

MECHANISM:
A slip receives one durable local position while indexes and cross-references expose additional routes into the same material.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<one note>
→ <address + topical neighborhood + index entries + cross-references>
→ [retrieve through different orderings]
→ <plural access without duplicate note identity>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
The archival organization documents branching order numbers together with thematic divisions and keyword registers.

TENSION:
“Identity is not location” is true only if “location” means semantic classification. Luhmann’s identity address itself records a position in an evolving branch.

MISSING:
A historical account of how often Luhmann retrieved through branch neighborhood versus keyword indexes versus remembered cross-reference.

BOUNDARY:
The archive proves coexistence of multiple organizational mechanisms. It does not by itself prove which mechanism caused Luhmann’s productivity.

CITATION TRAIL:
Archive documentation of numbering and cross-reference conventions.
Individual keyword registers.
Luhmann’s descriptions of retrieval practice.

TEST:
Choose twenty known published arguments and trace, where possible, which routes through the archive connect their source slips. Count how many distinct ordering systems participate.
