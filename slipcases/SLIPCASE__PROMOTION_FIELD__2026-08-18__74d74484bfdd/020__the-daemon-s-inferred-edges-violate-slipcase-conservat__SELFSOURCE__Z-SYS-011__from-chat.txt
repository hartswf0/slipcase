Z-SYS-011

TITLE:
The daemon's inferred edges violate Slipcase conservatism unless inference itself is preserved

SOURCE:
User-provided prompt system — AUTONOMOUS GRAPH INQUIRY v4.0 and SLIPCASE — ANCIENT MASTER v15.0 — supplied 2026-08-18.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] The daemon infers graph relationships from shared source terms, shared research objects, related tests, competing formal shifts, and similar cross-node signals. Ancient Master requires conservative relation resolution, preservation of literal relation and derivation status, and forbids inventing relations.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The research controller needs speculative relations to discover collisions, while the archive needs to prevent speculative relations from masquerading as source relations.

LOCAL MOVE:
Separates search heuristics from archival edges.

SOURCE TERMS:
infer edges
shared SOURCE TERMS
shared RESEARCH OBJECTS
competing FORMAL SHIFTS
literal relation
derivation status
Never invent relations

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
An inference can be useful enough to steer research without being trustworthy enough to enter the preserved graph as a relation.

QUESTION:
Where should speculative graph edges live?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can a research system exploit weak resemblance while guaranteeing that resemblance never hardens silently into genealogy or evidence?

MECHANISM:
Controller detects possible relation.
Relation becomes candidate research edge.
Research tests the relation.
Only supported result is promoted into durable compiled state.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<surface resemblance>
→ <candidate edge>
→ [investigate / discriminate]
→ <supported relation OR discarded heuristic>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Daemon infers edges from multiple shared or conflicting fields.

Ancient Master preserves:
literal relation
source object
field
ordinal
target
resolution state
derivation status.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

EDGE_STATE ∈ {
  LITERAL,
  DERIVED_DETERMINISTIC,
  HYPOTHESIS,
  VERIFIED_INFERENCE,
  REJECTED
}

Only LITERAL and adequately supported derived edges enter the durable graph without qualification.

TENSION:
Without speculative edges, the daemon loses much of its ability to discover unexpected crossings. Without provenance on speculative edges, Slipcase loses its epistemic discipline.

MISSING:
A candidate-edge registry with origin, confidence/status, test, and promotion rule.

BOUNDARY:
The supplied prompts may intend “infer” only as internal steering. The boundary becomes a problem only when inferred edges are serialized as ordinary field structure.

TEST:
Seed two unrelated zettels with deliberately shared vocabulary. Observe whether the daemon creates a relation, whether that relation reaches the persisted graph, and whether its inferential origin remains recoverable.
