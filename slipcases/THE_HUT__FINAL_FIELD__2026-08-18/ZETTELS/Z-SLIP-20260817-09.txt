    TITLE:
    EACH MATERIAL STATE CAN BECOME PART OF THE SPECIFICATION FOR THE NEXT

    SOURCE:
    Tim Ingold — Making — 2013 — Chapter 4, “On building a house,” pp. 54–55.

    SOURCE URL:
    file:MAKING_INGOLD(1).pdf

    PASSAGE:
    [PARAPHRASE] Ingold compares medieval building to a patchwork quilt: stones are shaped and reshaped to fit spaces prepared by previous stones and in turn prepare spaces for those that follow.

    RESEARCH OBJECT:
    Execution can recursively create the constraint field for later execution. The artifact is not only output; its current state helps specify admissible next moves.

    LOCAL MOVE:
    Ingold replaces assembly from pre-cut pieces with piecemeal adjustment in which each placed stone changes the local problem.

    SOURCE TERMS:
    patchwork quilt; piecemeal; shaped; reshaped; fit; space prepared

    WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
    Some constraints do not merely become explicit late; they are produced by earlier material decisions.

    QUESTION:
    Could deferred formalization be defined as recursive specification by produced state?

    DEEPER QUESTION:
    Which later requirements could not exist before the current artifact existed?

    MECHANISM:
    existing state → local gap/constraint → select/reshape/place → new state → next constraint field.

    FORMAL SHIFT:
    <ARTIFACT_n> → <LOCAL CONSTRAINT FIELD> → [FIT/RESHAPE] → <ARTIFACT_n+1>

    SOURCE FORMALISM:
    NONE

    OUR FORMALIZATION:
    [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] C_{n+1} = prior constraints + constraints induced by artifact state A_n.

    TENSION:
    Software synthesis often assumes the correctness relation exists logically before synthesis; this case suggests some design requirements are genuinely emergent.

    MISSING:
    A distinction between unknown-beforehand and not-yet-existent specification.

    BOUNDARY:
    The masonry case does not prove formal software correctness can safely emerge without prior criteria.

    CITATION TRAIL:
    Ingold on design continuing into making; output-directed programming.

    TEST:
    Timestamp constraints during iterative design and distinguish remembered requirements from distinctions first made possible by an artifact.
