    TITLE:
    DESIGN CAN CONTINUE INTO MAKING RATHER THAN PRECEDING IT

    SOURCE:
    Tim Ingold — Making — 2013 — Chapter 5, “The sighted watchmaker,” p. 69.

    SOURCE URL:
    file:MAKING_INGOLD(1).pdf

    PASSAGE:
    [QUOTE] “the designing of the watch continues into its making”

    RESEARCH OBJECT:
    Ingold gives a strong alternative to the sequence complete-design → execution: parts acquire coherence through ongoing adjustment, so design remains active inside production.

    LOCAL MOVE:
    He contrasts predictive foresight with anticipatory foresight and describes making as ontogenesis and correspondence among parts.

    SOURCE TERMS:
    anticipation; anticipatory foresight; ontogenesis; correspondence; internal coherence

    WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
    “Deferred formalization” may be too weak if it implies a complete specification merely arrives later. Some design may be generated by the act of making itself.

    QUESTION:
    Is the missing specification merely unknown, or genuinely not yet formed?

    DEEPER QUESTION:
    What properties become thinkable only after the provisional thing exists?

    MECHANISM:
    initial direction → partial assembly → perceive emerging relations → adjust → increased coherence.

    FORMAL SHIFT:
    <DESIGN BEFORE MAKING> → <DESIGN WITHIN MAKING> → [ANTICIPATE + ADJUST] → <EMERGENT COHERENCE>

    SOURCE FORMALISM:
    NONE

    OUR FORMALIZATION:
    [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] SPEC_{n+1} may contain distinctions unavailable at SPEC_n because A_n changes what can be perceived.

    TENSION:
    This departs from program synthesis systems in which correctness conditions are assumed to pre-exist the search.

    MISSING:
    A test for genuinely emergent versus merely unstated requirements.

    BOUNDARY:
    Ingold’s claim concerns making and design, not safety-critical software verification.

    CITATION TRAIL:
    Sennett; Spuybroek; program synthesis.

    TEST:
    Require designers to state known requirements before generation, then record only genuinely new distinctions after each artifact.
