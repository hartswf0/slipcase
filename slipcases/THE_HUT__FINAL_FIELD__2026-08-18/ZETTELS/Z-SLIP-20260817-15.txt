    TITLE:
    THE COMPUTER MOVES FROM MANSION TO HABITAT WHEN INFRASTRUCTURE BECOMES A CONDITION OF DWELLING

    SOURCE:
    Richard Brautigan — “All Watched Over by Machines of Loving Grace” — 1967.

    SOURCE URL:
    https://brautiganarchives.xyz/machines.html

    PASSAGE:
    [QUOTE] “mammals and computers / live together in mutually / programming harmony”

    RESEARCH OBJECT:
    Brautigan’s poem distributes computing through meadow, forest, and ecology. Computation ceases to be merely an object within habitat and becomes part of the environment through which life is organized.

    LOCAL MOVE:
    The poem imagines reciprocal programming, automation, ecological integration, and machine watching without supplying an implementation.

    SOURCE TERMS:
    cybernetic meadow; cybernetic forest; cybernetic ecology; mutually programming harmony; machines of loving grace

    WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
    When infrastructure becomes ambient, interaction shifts from using a tool to inhabiting a computational condition.

    QUESTION:
    At what scale does computation become habitat rather than device?

    DEEPER QUESTION:
    What would genuine mutual programming require beyond adaptive surveillance?

    MECHANISM:
    bounded device → distributed infrastructure → ambient coordination/observation → computational habitat.

    FORMAL SHIFT:
    <COMPUTER AS OBJECT> → [DISTRIBUTE] → <COMPUTER AS HABITAT>

    SOURCE FORMALISM:
    NONE

    OUR FORMALIZATION:
    [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] mutuality requires not only feedback but reciprocal power to inspect, alter, refuse, and leave the programming relation.

    TENSION:
    The poem’s care/surveillance ambiguity means ecological integration cannot be treated as inherently emancipatory.

    MISSING:
    Criteria for reciprocity and opt-out at infrastructural scale.

    BOUNDARY:
    The poem supplies a possible world, not an engineering design or proof of mutuality.

    CITATION TRAIL:
    Turing; ubiquitous computing; cybernetics; Ingold correspondence.

    TEST:
    Instantiate one computational capability at device, room, house, neighborhood, and ambient scales and measure control, visibility, consent, and exit.
