    TITLE:
    RULES CAN GUIDE A BUILDING WITHOUT CONTAINING THE BUILDING

    SOURCE:
    Tim Ingold — Making — 2013 — Chapter 4, “On building a house,” p. 54.

    SOURCE URL:
    file:MAKING_INGOLD(1).pdf

    PASSAGE:
    [QUOTE] “The masons’ rules were maxims in this sense: they comprised resources for action, but did not determine it.”

    RESEARCH OBJECT:
    Rules can participate essentially in making without preordaining the course of action. This pressures any equation of prompt, plan, grammar, or rule set with the whole program.

    LOCAL MOVE:
    Ingold distinguishes constructive rules from the skill, judgment, and creative extemporisation required in actual masonry.

    SOURCE TERMS:
    constructive geometry; rule of thumb; maxims; resources for action; skill; inventiveness

    WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
    The alternative to deterministic rules is not rulelessness; it is rules whose meaning is completed in situated practice.

    QUESTION:
    When a prompt guides generation without determining it, is it closer to code or to a maxim?

    DEEPER QUESTION:
    What variable represents situated judgment in a theory of natural-language programming?

    MECHANISM:
    rule + situation + skilled practitioner → judgment/adaptation → next material action.

    FORMAL SHIFT:
    <RULE> → <RESOURCE FOR ACTION> → [JUDGE + ADAPT] → <CONTINGENT NEXT STATE>

    SOURCE FORMALISM:
    NONE

    OUR FORMALIZATION:
    [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] NEXT_STATE = f(rule, situation, practitioner, material response), not rule alone.

    TENSION:
    Formal shape grammars can determine transformations in representational space, while situated building rules may only orient action.

    MISSING:
    A taxonomy separating formal rule, invariant, heuristic, maxim, example, and judgment.

    BOUNDARY:
    Ingold does not discuss LLMs; the prompting comparison is an inference.

    CITATION TRAIL:
    Polanyi; Suchman; Stiny and Mitchell.

    TEST:
    Classify actions in a building or coding task as entailed, selected, improvised, or forced by local conditions.
