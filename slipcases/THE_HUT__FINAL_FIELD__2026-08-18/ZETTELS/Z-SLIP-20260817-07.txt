    TITLE:
    A GENEALOGY OF CONSTRAINTS SHOULD BE A DEPENDENCY GRAPH, NOT A DIARY

    SOURCE:
    Johan de Kleer — “Problem Solving with the ATMS” — 1986 — Artificial Intelligence 28(2), pp. 197–224.

    SOURCE URL:
    https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(86)90082-2

    PASSAGE:
    [PARAPHRASE] In the ATMS, hypotheses are assumptions, inferences are recorded as justifications, sets of assumptions form environments, and inconsistent environments are retained as nogoods rather than forgotten.

    RESEARCH OBJECT:
    A chronological transcript says what happened next; a dependency system says what survives because of what and which combinations are inconsistent.

    LOCAL MOVE:
    de Kleer provides a machinery for maintaining multiple assumption environments, justifications, labels, and contradictions.

    SOURCE TERMS:
    assumption; justification; environment; context; label; inconsistent; nogood

    WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
    Rejected generations may be valuable not as archived clutter but as reusable nogoods tied to the assumptions that caused failure.

    QUESTION:
    Should iterative generative systems maintain explicit nogoods and justifications for their constraints?

    DEEPER QUESTION:
    Can dependency be preserved while some nodes remain metaphors, examples, or unresolved judgments rather than formal propositions?

    MECHANISM:
    assumption → inference/justification → dependency label; inconsistent assumption set → nogood.

    FORMAL SHIFT:
    <CHAT SEQUENCE> → <ASSUMPTIONS + JUSTIFICATIONS + NOGOODS> → [DEPENDENCY MAINTENANCE] → <VIABLE CONTEXTS>

    SOURCE FORMALISM:
    ATMS assumptions, justifications, environments, labels, contexts, and nogoods.

    OUR FORMALIZATION:
    [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Prompt memory should preserve constraint, rationale, supporting evidence, conflicting assumptions, and known failure combinations.

    TENSION:
    Formalizing fuzzy judgments too early can destroy precisely the ambiguity deferred formalization is meant to preserve.

    MISSING:
    A mixed graph where some nodes remain non-formal evidence while relations and provenance remain explicit.

    BOUNDARY:
    ATMS is not a prompt-memory architecture; the connection is architectural inference.

    CITATION TRAIL:
    Naur; design rationale; CEGIS; provenance.

    TEST:
    Compare raw chat memory with dependency-graph memory after fifty revisions and a late conflicting requirement.
