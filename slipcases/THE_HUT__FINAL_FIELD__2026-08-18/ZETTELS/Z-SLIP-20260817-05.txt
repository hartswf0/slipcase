    Z-SLIP-20260817-05

    TITLE:
    THICKNESS IS INFORMATION GAIN, NOT PROMPT LENGTH

    SOURCE:
    Mikael Mayer et al. — “User Interaction Models for Disambiguation in Programming by Example” — 2015 — UIST.

    SOURCE URL:
    https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/user-interaction-models-for-disambiguation-in-programming-by-example/

    PASSAGE:
    [PARAPHRASE] Examples are ambiguous specifications because many programs can remain consistent with them; the paper studies navigation and active-learning interactions for reducing that ambiguity.

    RESEARCH OBJECT:
    A specification becomes “thicker” when new evidence rules out consequential interpretations, not when it simply becomes longer.

    LOCAL MOVE:
    The authors treat ambiguity as a space of programs consistent with examples and design interactions to discriminate among them.

    SOURCE TERMS:
    Programming by Example; ambiguous specification; user intent; programs consistent with examples; active learning; disambiguation

    WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
    A six-word counterexample can carry more specification than five hundred words of description.

    QUESTION:
    Can thick prompting be measured by contraction of behavioral possibility rather than token count?

    DEEPER QUESTION:
    What observable proxy for hypothesis-space reduction works for generative models whose internal candidate set is not enumerable?

    MECHANISM:
    examples → many compatible programs → discriminating question/example → reduced candidate set.

    FORMAL SHIFT:
    <AMBIGUOUS EXAMPLES> → [DISAMBIGUATE] → <FEWER CONSISTENT PROGRAMS>

    SOURCE FORMALISM:
    Interaction models for navigation among consistent programs and active-learning questions.

    OUR FORMALIZATION:
    [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] specification gain can be approximated by reduced behavioral variance across generated candidates.

    TENSION:
    LLM output distributions are not classical version spaces, so the measurement is an analogy that must be operationalized cautiously.

    MISSING:
    A robust behavioral proxy for specification gain.

    BOUNDARY:
    The source does not establish that LLM latent distributions can be treated as enumerable hypothesis sets.

    CITATION TRAIL:
    Active learning; preference elicitation; prompt evaluation.

    TEST:
    Generate samples before and after each correction and estimate how many unwanted behaviors disappear relative to token count.
