ZETTEL

ID:
DISCOVERY-002

TITLE:
The expressive variable may be born during making rather than selected from a pre-given list.

SOURCE:
Donald A. Schön — “Designing as Reflective Conversation with the Materials of a Design Situation” — Research in Engineering Design 3 — 1992.

SOURCE URL:
https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01580516

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Schön argues that designers help construct the design world in which they work. In his example, a designer discovers relations in an intermediate design and only then comes to appreciate and intentionally work with them; the problem space is not fully given at the outset.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
VARIABLE EMERGENCE.

LOCAL MOVE:
Move from assigning values to fixed variables toward tracing when distinctions themselves become available for intentional intervention.

SOURCE TERMS:
design world
reflection-in-action
move experiment
unintended consequences
variable emergence
discovery

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A retrospective analysis can invent a clean initial choice-space that no participant actually possessed. The branch may not have existed as a branch until an artifact exposed it.

QUESTION:
How can a production history represent a choice whose choice-space emerged only after an intermediate artifact?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is making a previously unavailable difference perceptible for later control itself a creative contribution?

MECHANISM:
S_t → move m_t → intermediate artifact → previously unnoticed relation r becomes salient → r becomes variable v_{t+1} → later actions can intentionally regulate v.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<FIXED POSSIBILITY SPACE> → <ARTIFACT-MEDIATED DISCOVERY> → <EXPANDED/REFRAMED POSSIBILITY SPACE>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Schön supplies a theory of reflective designing and constructed design worlds, not a formal variable ontology.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Use V_t rather than fixed V. V_{t+1}=V_t ∪ Discover(S_t,m_t). A branch can be created as an actionable distinction before it is selected.

TENSION:
Analysts can redescribe an emergent distinction using later vocabulary and make it look obvious from the beginning; Schön warns against this retrospective reconstruction.

MISSING:
A criterion distinguishing discovery of a consequential relation from merely renaming a variable that was already practically available.

BOUNDARY:
Variable emergence does not establish who authored the first material occurrence of the discovered feature.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[RETENTION-005-Y-A]] → Schön → design-world construction → the genealogy of variables becomes part of production history.

TEST:
For each consequential distinction in a workflow, timestamp the first artifact that made it perceptible, first explicit or behavioral recognition, and first intentional manipulation.
