ID:
RETENTION-005-R-C

TITLE:
A raw prompt history is provenance but becomes evidence of control only when revisions are paired with predictions about what should change.

SOURCE:
COLLISION — Don-Yehiya et al. — 2023; U.S. Copyright Office Review Board — Théâtre D’opéra Spatial — 2023.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Iterative histories show adaptation, but even a complete sequence of prompts and outputs can show what changed without showing why or what the user predicted.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
TRAJECTORY ≠ CONTROL TRACE.

LOCAL MOVE:
Apply the same critique to the path that was earlier applied to iteration count.

SOURCE TERMS:
iteration
revision
prompt
feedback
control
creative input

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Complete provenance can still be theatrical or causally opaque.

QUESTION:
What minimum metadata turns a prompt history into evidence of artistic leverage?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Must intention be recorded prospectively to prevent later narrative from laundering attractive accidents into deliberate control?

MECHANISM:
weak: p_t→y_t→p_{t+1}. stronger: p_t→declare desired Δf→generate→compare actual Δf→next intervention.

TENSION:
Artists often work tacitly; requiring verbal prospective intentions can alter practice.

MISSING:
Methods for inferring tacit control without artificial laboratory self-report.

BOUNDARY:
More process documentation is not automatically better evidence; its evidentiary structure matters.
