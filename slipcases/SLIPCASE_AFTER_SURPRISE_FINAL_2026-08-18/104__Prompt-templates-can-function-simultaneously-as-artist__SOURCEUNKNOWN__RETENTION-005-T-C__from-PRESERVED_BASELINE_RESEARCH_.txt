RETENTION-005-T-C

TITLE:
Prompt templates can function simultaneously as artistic style, executable method, and legally unprotectable procedure.

SOURCE:
Minsuk Chang et al. — “The Prompt Artists” — 2023; 17 U.S.C. §102(b).

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Prompt-art research documents reusable templates with slots producing recognizable tendencies; copyright excludes procedures/methods as such while protecting original expression.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
A PROMPT TEMPLATE CAN OCCUPY THREE ONTOLOGIES AT ONCE.

LOCAL MOVE:
Make method-like maturity concrete through parameterized prompt templates.

SOURCE TERMS:
prompt template
slots
style
generative art
procedure
method of operation

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
One object can be artistic style recipe, technical control structure, and legally partly unprotectable method.

QUESTION:
What exactly is copied when one artist copies another’s prompt template but replaces every literal descriptive word?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can a community possess a shared artistic grammar that no individual can monopolize as copyright?

MECHANISM:
T=fixed structural phrases+slots{x1...xn}; substitute values → family of prompts → family of outputs.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<ONE PROMPT> → <PARAMETERIZED GENERATIVE GRAMMAR> → <STYLE FAMILY>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Prompt Artists describes slot templates; §102(b) supplies expression/function boundary.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
T:Parameters→Prompt; G∘T:Parameters→Δ(Images). Literal wording and operative function can have different legal status.

TENSION:
Original selection/arrangement of template text can remain protected even if method cannot be monopolized.

MISSING:
Actual disputes involving copying prompt templates or recipes.

BOUNDARY:
Unprotectability of method does not imply absence of cultural ownership norms, stylistic lineage, plagiarism concerns, or craft.

TEST:
Compare literal copy, paraphrased structural copy, same method/different wording, and same wording/altered slot logic across text, operation, style, community judgments.
