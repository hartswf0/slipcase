ID:
RETENTION-005-Y-F

TITLE:
A creative interface cannot preserve distinctions it lacks enough variety to express or counteract.

SOURCE:
W. Ross Ashby — “Requisite Variety and Its Implications for the Control of Complex Systems” — 1958.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Ashby’s law of requisite variety constrains regulation: a regulator needs corresponding discriminatory/selective variety to control a sufficiently varied disturbance.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
REQUISITE EXPRESSIVE VARIETY.

LOCAL MOVE:
Reverse the expressive-freedom question: does the control surface have enough distinct responses for the differences the artist cares about?

SOURCE TERMS:
requisite variety
regulation
control
selection
disturbance
complex system

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A system may recognize a hundred distinctions while exposing only three ways to respond. Recognition variety can exceed intervention variety.

QUESTION:
How much control vocabulary must an interface expose to preserve the variety of distinctions an artist is trying to regulate?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does prompt frustration sometimes arise not from semantic misunderstanding but from a variety deficit between desired distinctions and available interventions?

MECHANISM:
desired deviations D={d_i}; regulator repertoire R={r_j}; if distinct d map to same r, they cannot be independently corrected.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<INTERFACE HAS CONTROLS> → <INTERFACE HAS REQUISITE VARIETY FOR THIS TASK?>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Ashby relates disturbance variety, regulator variety, and attainable regulation.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Effective Variety(R) must be rich enough relative to task-relevant Variety(D); many controls with collapsed consequences add little variety.

TENSION:
Raw control count is not enough; controls must be appropriately coupled to disturbances.

MISSING:
A task-relative measure of effective regulator variety for creative interfaces.

BOUNDARY:
More sliders/tokens do not guarantee requisite variety.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[RETENTION-005-Y]] → expressive freedom topology → Ashby → requisite variety → control-surface variety deficit.

TEST:
List twenty independent target distinctions; test whether interface offers an intervention changing each while preserving the other nineteen; build distinguishability matrix.
