ZETTEL

ID:
RETENTION-005-S-A

TITLE:
Stern Electronics defeats the proposition that trajectory control by itself makes the controller an author.

SOURCE:
Stern Electronics, Inc. v. Kaufman — 669 F.2d 852 (2d Cir. 1982).

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
A Scramble player controls altitude, speed, firing, bombing, and exact sequences, yet the court locates copyrightable recurring audiovisual structure upstream in the game creators.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
CAUSAL CONTROL OVER A REALIZED PATH ≠ AUTHORSHIP OF THE POSSIBILITY STRUCTURE.

LOCAL MOVE:
A player can pass many versions of CALL THE SHOT and still not thereby author the audiovisual work.

SOURCE TERMS:
player participation
controls
variation
audiovisual work
originality
fixation
repetitive sequence

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Counterfactual control is not sufficient for legal authorship.

QUESTION:
Why is a game player’s control legally different from an artist’s manipulation of a generative image system?

DEEPER QUESTION:
What additional condition beyond consequential control is required for AUTHORSHIP rather than PERFORMANCE?

MECHANISM:
designer authors G; player actions a_t produce variable trajectory y; player influences y without authoring G or recurring assets.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<COUNTERFACTUAL CONTROL> ↛ <AUTHORSHIP>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Separate PolicyAuthorship, AssetAuthorship, TrajectoryControl, TokenAuthorship.

TENSION:
A sufficiently elaborate player performance can itself be fixed and independently expressive; Stern did not decide every such claim.

MISSING:
Cases addressing copyright in recorded gameplay or highly creative player performances.

BOUNDARY:
Control is evidence in some authorship theories but cannot be sufficient.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[RETENTION-005-S]] → distributional control → Stern → skilled trajectory control without upstream audiovisual authorship.

TEST:
Compare pinball shot, speedrun, generative edit, and procedural-art parameter performance; identify upstream-authored versus newly authored elements.
