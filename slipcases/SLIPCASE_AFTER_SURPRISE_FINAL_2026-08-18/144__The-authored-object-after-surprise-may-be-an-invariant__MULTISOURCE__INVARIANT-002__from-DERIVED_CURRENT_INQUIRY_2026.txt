ZETTEL

ID:
INVARIANT-002

TITLE:
The authored object after surprise may be an invariant across transformations rather than the surprised feature itself.

SOURCE:
COLLISION — Sol LeWitt instruction-based variation; video-game fixation cases; current generative-control field.

SOURCE URL:
https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F2/669/852/149312/

PASSAGE:
[OUR INFERENCE]
Across instruction-based works and variable audiovisual systems, identity can persist through changing realizations. This suggests analyzing whether a maker prospectively preserves a relation across later transformations rather than asking only who originated a local feature.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
AUTHORED INVARIANT.

LOCAL MOVE:
Shift attribution from isolated generated features toward deliberately stabilized relations that survive a sequence of later changes.

SOURCE TERMS:
invariant
equivalence relation
identity
transformation
stabilization
variable work

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
“Keep the face” or “keep the tension” is not necessarily a command to preserve pixels. It defines a relation by which later states are judged the same in one respect while changing in others.

QUESTION:
Who defines the equivalence relation by which two generated states count as preserving the same expressive thing?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can authorship attach more plausibly to the human-defined invariance criterion and its successful maintenance than to the initial stochastic instance?

MECHANISM:
Unexpected feature x appears → human identifies relation q worth preserving → subsequent transformations alter other dimensions while q is repeatedly tested and stabilized.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<FEATURE OWNERSHIP> → <RELATIONAL INVARIANT ACROSS STATES>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
No source states this general authorship rule. Stern and related cases analyze recurring audiovisual elements under variable play; the invariant theory is a synthesis.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Given states y1...yn and human-defined equivalence ≈_q, stabilization seeks y1≈_q y2≈_q...≈_q yn while intentionally changing other dimensions.

TENSION:
The invariant may be supplied largely by model priors rather than human action. Persistence alone does not show that the maker caused it to persist.

MISSING:
A causal test separating model-default invariance from invariance maintained through human interventions.

BOUNDARY:
An authored invariant is a candidate analytic object, not a recognized copyright category.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[INVARIANT-001]] → cross-realization structure → [[FRONTIER-001]] → prospectively stabilized relation after surprise.

TEST:
Ablate the human preservation interventions while holding model/workflow constant. If q persists anyway, the claimed human stabilization is weak.
