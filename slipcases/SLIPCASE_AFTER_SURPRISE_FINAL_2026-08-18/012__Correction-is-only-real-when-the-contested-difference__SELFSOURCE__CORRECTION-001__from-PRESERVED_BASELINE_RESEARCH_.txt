CORRECTION-001

TITLE:
Correction is only real when the contested difference survives translation into a still-open intervention point.

SOURCE:
User-provided manuscript / conversation synthesis — 2026-08-16.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Systems can offer speech, participation, appeal, or visibility while remaining operationally closed if dissent is translated into categories that erase the contested difference or arrives after consequence is fixed.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
CAUSALLY EFFECTIVE CORRECTION.

LOCAL MOVE:
Separate CHANNEL FOR RESPONSE from CHANNEL CAPABLE OF ALTERING THE OPERATIVE REPRESENTATION.

SOURCE TERMS:
speak
standing
participation
visibility
correction

translation
dissent

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A correction mechanism can be perfectly available and perfectly incapable of carrying the correction.

QUESTION:
What distinguishes an appeal channel from an actual correction channel?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can STANDING be formalized as permission to modify the representation at the layer where consequential distinctions become binding?

MECHANISM:
person contests d → corrective interface → translation τ(d) → operative system; if τ deletes d or arrival exceeds deadline, formal correction occurs without material correction.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<ABILITY TO SPEAK> → <ABILITY TO MODIFY OPERATIVE STATE>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Correction succeeds iff relevant d remains representable, reaches binding layer, actor has standing, update occurs before t_close, and downstream consequence can change.

TENSION:
Some correction processes are deliberately slow for accuracy/fairness.

MISSING:
Tradeoff between correction latency and correction quality.

BOUNDARY:
Responsiveness should not be inferred from message receipt.
