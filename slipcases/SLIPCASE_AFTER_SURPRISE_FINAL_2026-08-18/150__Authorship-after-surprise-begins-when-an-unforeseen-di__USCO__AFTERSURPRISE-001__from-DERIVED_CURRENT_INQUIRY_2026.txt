ID:
AFTERSURPRISE-001

TITLE:
Authorship after surprise begins when an unforeseen difference becomes a constraint on the future.

SOURCE:
COMPILER SYNTHESIS — Schön 1992; Kirsh and Maglio 1994; U.S. Copyright Office 2025; Allen v. Perlmutter government brief 2026; Stern 1982; Midway 1983.

SOURCE URL:
https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01580516

PASSAGE:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
The synthesis proposes a temporal receipt for exploratory generative making: SURPRISE → DISCOVERY → COMMITMENT → INTERVENTION → STABILIZATION. The key evidentiary event is not the first accidental appearance but the later moment at which the discovered difference constrains what the maker subsequently allows, changes, rejects, or preserves.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
AFTER-SURPRISE RECEIPT.

LOCAL MOVE:
Narrow the field’s “call the shot” criterion so it can admit discovery without allowing retrospective intention laundering.

SOURCE TERMS:
surprise
discovery
commitment
intervention
stabilization
temporal frontier
constraint

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Prospective intention is too strict for exploratory art, while post-hoc liking is too weak. A commitment made after discovery but before later consequential transformations occupies the missing middle.

QUESTION:
What evidence shows that a surprise became a genuine constraint on future making rather than a story attached after the artifact was finished?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can behavioral stabilization after t2 provide a falsifiable receipt of human agency without reallocating authorship of the surprise at t1?

MECHANISM:
SURPRISE x at t1 → DISCOVER relation q at t2 → COMMIT q at t3 → perform interventions whose success/failure depends on q → stabilize q across later states.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<CALL THE SHOT BEFORE ALL GENERATION> → <DISCOVER, THEN MAKE THE DISCOVERY GOVERN WHAT COMES NEXT>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
No cited source states this criterion. It is a compiler synthesis designed to survive the tension between exploratory making, non-retroactive attribution, and control-not-authorship counterexamples.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Receipt R_q is strong when: (1) q first becomes perceptible at t1; (2) later action indicates q matters; (3) interventions after commitment predictably preserve/develop q; (4) alternative paths that violate q are rejected or corrected; (5) removing those interventions materially reduces q-stability.

TENSION:
A maker can perform the receipt while acting inside a possibility space whose expressive grammar comes from upstream. The receipt establishes practical agency over a subsequent relation, not automatic legal authorship.

MISSING:
Empirical thresholds for commitment and stabilization; treatment of tacit nonverbal commitments; relation to fixation and protectable expression.

BOUNDARY:
The criterion is a research wager and testable analytic proposal, not current law.

TEST:
Record complete sessions. Identify surprise events without using final outcome labels. Then ask whether subsequent behavior creates prospective constraints and whether those constraints survive intervention ablation.
