RETENTION-005-Q

TITLE:
The negative prompt is an authored refusal: it controls the work by specifying what must not survive.

SOURCE:
Beijing Internet Court — Li v. Liu — 2023.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The Li v. Liu record includes negative prompt terms identifying representational modes or features the user did not want, alongside positive prompts, parameters, revisions, and selection.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
PROMPT AUTHORSHIP CAN OPERATE THROUGH NEGATION.

LOCAL MOVE:
Human intervention can occur as AUTHORSHIP BY PRUNING BEFORE GENERATION.

SOURCE TERMS:
negative prompt
do not wish to show
creative experience
choice
selection
prompt words

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
An expressive decision can leave no visible object corresponding to itself. Its success is an absence.

QUESTION:
How can authorship evidence attach to a decision whose successful result is precisely that nothing corresponding to it appears?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is REFUSAL a more general unit of artistic control than positive specification?

MECHANISM:
possibility space Ω; positive prompt raises desired region Ω+; negative prompt suppresses Ω−; final y lies away from Ω−.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<AUTHORSHIP AS ADDING FEATURES> → <AUTHORSHIP AS REMOVING POSSIBILITIES>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
The judgment describes positive and negative prompt words as separate components of the workflow.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Treat negative prompt as a soft branch-pruning operator N_q: Ω→Ω′ where prohibited feature class q is downweighted.

TENSION:
Negative prompts do not generally implement hard logical exclusion; effects are model- and interface-dependent.

MISSING:
Feature-level experiments measuring artistic absences repeatedly controlled by negative prompting.

BOUNDARY:
Visible expressive contribution is not the only kind of intervention; makers can shape works by controlling possibilities that never appear.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[RETENTION-005]] → Li v. Liu negative prompts → refusal enters before generation → absence becomes causal evidence.

TEST:
Compare matched outputs with and without each negative constraint while controlling seed where possible; record causally attributable absences.
