ID:
RETENTION-005-M-B

TITLE:
Andrien makes the difficult boundary not tool versus author but rote realization versus intellectual modification by the intermediary.

SOURCE:
Andrien v. Southern Ocean County Chamber of Commerce — 927 F.2d 132 (3d Cir. 1991).

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Andrien accepts authorship where detailed creative direction is physically embodied by another party whose role is rote/mechanical rather than an independent intellectual modification.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
THE INTERMEDIARY’S DEGREE OF EXPRESSIVE DISCRETION may matter more than whether the intermediary is human or mechanical.

LOCAL MOVE:
Move from DID A MACHINE STAND BETWEEN AUTHOR AND WORK? to WHAT KIND OF TRANSFORMATION OCCURRED IN THE MIDDLE?

SOURCE TERMS:
author
specific detail
rote
mechanical
transcription
intellectual modification
fixation

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Machine mediation as such is not the decisive structural variable; unresolved expressive discretion is.

QUESTION:
When does generative interpretation cease to be authorship-preserving realization and become an independent source of expressive form?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can ROTE versus EXPRESSIVELY GENERATIVE TRANSFORMATION be measured technologically rather than inferred from the label AI?

MECHANISM:
C → intermediary; case A constrained realization; case B intermediary resolves consequential expressive variables.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<TOOL / NOT TOOL> → <DEGREE OF INTERMEDIARY EXPRESSIVE DISCRETION>

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

TENSION:
Andrien involved human-directed cartography, not probabilistic generation.

MISSING:
A method measuring expressive variation unresolved when a prompt reaches the model.

BOUNDARY:
Use Andrien methodologically: trace intermediary contribution, not as technological equivalence.
