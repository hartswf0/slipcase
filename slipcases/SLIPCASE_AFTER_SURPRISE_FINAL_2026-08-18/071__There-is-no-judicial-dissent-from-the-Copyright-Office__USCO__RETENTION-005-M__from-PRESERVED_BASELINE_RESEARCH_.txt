RETENTION-005-M

TITLE:
There is no judicial “dissent” from the Copyright Office prompt rule; the opposition is distributed across commenters, foreign courts, and competing attribution theories.

SOURCE:
U.S. Copyright Office — Copyright and Artificial Intelligence, Part 2 — 2025; Beijing Internet Court — Li v. Liu — 2023.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The U.S. Copyright Office report is an administrative policy analysis, not a divided appellate opinion. It records contrary scholarly and stakeholder arguments, while foreign courts apply different doctrines.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
THE DISSENT HAS TO BE RECONSTRUCTED AS A GRAPH, NOT QUOTED AS ONE OPINION.

LOCAL MOVE:
The Office’s position is opposed, but the opposition must be reconstructed across submissions and jurisdictions rather than called a formal dissent.

SOURCE TERMS:
commenters
detailed prompts
human authorship
control
specific instructions
original conception

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Some commenters accept the same technological facts but infer that sufficiently detailed prompting can embody original conception and control enough expressive detail.

QUESTION:
What premise actually separates the Office from the sophisticated-prompt position?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is the disagreement factual—HOW MUCH CONTROL CURRENT TOOLS PROVIDE—or conceptual—WHAT KIND OF CONTROL COPYRIGHT REQUIRES?

MECHANISM:
Reading A: interpretive gap → machine supplies expression → insufficient human authorship. Reading B: detailed original conception reflected in output → machine mediates realization → authorship possible.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<ONE TECHNICAL PROCESS> → {<MEDIATION BREAKS AUTHORSHIP>, <MEDIATION REALIZES AUTHORSHIP>}

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Let control estimate c and legal threshold λ be distinct. Parties may disagree about c, λ, or both.

TENSION:
Commenter submissions are advocacy/scholarship, not controlling law; foreign decisions are not U.S. authority.

MISSING:
A U.S. judicial decision squarely resolving a detailed prompt-control record.

BOUNDARY:
Call this OPPOSITION, not DISSENT, unless a formal judicial dissent exists.
