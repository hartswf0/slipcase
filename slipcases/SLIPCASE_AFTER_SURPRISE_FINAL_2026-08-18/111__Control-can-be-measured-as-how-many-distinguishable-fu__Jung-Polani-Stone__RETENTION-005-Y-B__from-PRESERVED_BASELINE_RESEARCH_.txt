ID:
RETENTION-005-Y-B

TITLE:
Control can be measured as how many distinguishable futures an action channel can reliably produce, not how many buttons an interface displays.

SOURCE:
Tobias Jung, Daniel Polani, and Peter Stone — “Empowerment for Continuous Agent-Environment Systems” — Adaptive Behavior 19(1) — 2011.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Empowerment measures the relation between possible action sequences and subsequent observable states; it is zero when different actions cannot make distinguishable differences and grows with reliable influence.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
EFFECTIVE EXPRESSIVE FREEDOM.

LOCAL MOVE:
Measure effective consequences rather than nominal interface controls.

SOURCE TERMS:
empowerment
channel capacity
actions
successor states
influence
stochastic transitions
mobility

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Ten sliders can provide less control than one consequential gesture; a thousand tokens can provide less effective variety than one mask.

QUESTION:
Could creative control be measured through the channel capacity between user interventions and perceptible future artifact states?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Would this operationalize the claim that a button is not evidence of agency?

MECHANISM:
user action A → creative system/environment → future artifact state X′; stronger reliable dependence means greater effective control channel.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<NUMBER OF CONTROLS> → <DISTINGUISHABILITY OF THEIR CONSEQUENCES>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Empowerment C(x)=max_{p(A)} I(X′;A|x) for the action-to-successor-state channel.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Creative empowerment E_artist(s)=max_{p(a)} I(Φ(Y_future);A|s), requiring task-relevant observer features Φ.

TENSION:
Information-theoretic difference can count trivial pixel changes that are artistically meaningless.

MISSING:
Observer-relative feature representation identifying meaningful differences.

BOUNDARY:
Empowerment measures effective influence, not authorship, quality, intention, or meaning.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[RETENTION-005-Y]] → expressive freedom topology → empowerment → action-to-future channel capacity.

TEST:
Systematically sample interventions in two interfaces and estimate mutual information between intervention and task-relevant future states; compare to user controllability judgments.
