TITLE:
When action must pass through representation, representation enters the causal machinery of the world.

SOURCE:
User-provided manuscript / conversation synthesis — 2026-08-16.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
A map that routes traffic, prices land, determines risk, or constrains vehicle movement does not merely depict territory; it participates in determining subsequent action.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
OPERATIVE REPRESENTATION.

LOCAL MOVE:
Move from REPRESENTATION AS DESCRIPTION to REPRESENTATION AS CAUSAL INTERMEDIARY.

SOURCE TERMS:
representation
operating machinery
causal chain
map
action
availability

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
MAP ≠ TERRITORY does not imply MAP is causally outside TERRITORY. Representation can remain ontologically distinct while becoming causally internal.

QUESTION:
What minimum condition turns a representation from observational artifact into operative intermediary?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can operativity be measured by the counterfactual difference between acting through R and through alternative R′?

MECHANISM:
world W → representation R(W) → institution/agent acts through R → action A → changed world W′.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<WORLD> → [REPRESENT] → <ACTIONABLE REPRESENTATION> → [UPTAKE] → <ACTION> → <CHANGED WORLD>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
R is operative relative to I when changing R while holding relevant W/I fixed changes the action or reachable world.

TENSION:
Almost every representation can indirectly affect behavior. A useful theory must distinguish incidental influence from obligatory passage.

MISSING:
A threshold for OBLIGATORY PASSAGE.

BOUNDARY:
Operativity does not imply that representation creates or exhausts the represented world.

CITATION TRAIL:
operative description → obligatory passage → infrastructure → causal mediation.

TEST:
Remove one map, score, registry, or model from a real decision chain and record which actions cease, change, or become impossible.
