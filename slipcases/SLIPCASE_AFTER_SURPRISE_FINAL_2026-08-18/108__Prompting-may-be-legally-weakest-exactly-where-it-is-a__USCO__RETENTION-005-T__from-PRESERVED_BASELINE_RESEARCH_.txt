ZETTEL

ID:
RETENTION-005-T

TITLE:
Prompting may be legally weakest exactly where it is artistically most interesting: as a practice of discovering a machine’s local language.

SOURCE:
COLLISION — U.S. Copyright Office 2025; Prompt-to-Prompt 2022; Textual Inversion 2022; Li v. Liu 2023.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Practitioners can acquire highly specific knowledge of words, parameters, exclusions, retained state, and model behavior. Much of that knowledge can look legally like skill, method, procedure, idea, or tool operation rather than protectable expression.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
CRAFT CAN BE REAL WITHOUT BEING COPYRIGHT.

LOCAL MOVE:
Refuse the category error: if copyright does not protect prompt technique, that does not establish that the technique is aesthetically trivial or causally ineffective.

SOURCE TERMS:
prompt
method
procedure
creative process
control
editing
personalization

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The better someone learns the machine’s operative vocabulary, the more expertise may take the form of GENERALIZABLE METHOD—the very thing copyright avoids monopolizing.

QUESTION:
Could prompt craft become culturally significant while remaining largely outside copyright protection as such?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is the absence of copyright sometimes evidence that a practice has become MORE like technique rather than LESS like authorship?

MECHANISM:
repeated practice → observe intervention effects → build tacit local model → choose better interventions → achieve repeatable differences.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<NO COPYRIGHT> ≠ <NO SKILL> ≠ <NO CONTROL> ≠ <NO ARTISTIC AGENCY>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Separate Copyrightable(x), Skilled(x), CausallyControlling(x), ArtisticallyConsequential(x). No implication ¬Copyrightable→¬Skilled.

TENSION:
Specific products of prompt craft—original prose, arrangements, modifications—may still be copyrightable even if underlying technique is not.

MISSING:
Ethnographic studies separating actual reproducible expert discriminations from superstition and post-hoc narrative.

BOUNDARY:
Copyright law is a poor instrument for deciding whether prompting constitutes a craft; it answers a narrower allocation question.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[RETENTION-005]] → copyright boundary → prompt as operative method → technical expertise → legal unprotectability and artistic significance diverge.

TEST:
Recruit experts and novices; give same models and target transformations; prohibit copied saved prompts; compare accuracy, invariant preservation, recovery, and transfer.
