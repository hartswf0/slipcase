ZETTEL

ID:
CRAFT-002

TITLE:
Prompt craft may chiefly produce a local causal model of the generator rather than a protectable expressive object.

SOURCE:
Minsuk Chang et al. — “The Prompt Artists” — 2023; Shachar Don-Yehiya, Leshem Choshen, and Omri Abend — “Human Learning by Model Feedback” — EMNLP 2023; 17 U.S.C. §102(b).

SOURCE URL:
https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.253/

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Studies of experienced prompting document specialized vocabularies, reusable practices, and learning from model feedback. Copyright law separately excludes procedures, processes, systems, and methods of operation as such from protection.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
LOCAL CAUSAL MODEL AS CRAFT.

LOCAL MOVE:
Separate learned ability to predict and steer a system from ownership of the resulting output or of the method itself.

SOURCE TERMS:
craft
know-how
prompt vocabulary
model feedback
method of operation
causal model

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A long failed trajectory may produce little protectable output while producing substantial technical-artistic knowledge about what the system will do under nearby interventions.

QUESTION:
Can prompt practice be highly skilled even where its central learned object is method-like and largely outside copyright?

DEEPER QUESTION:
What should count as evidence that a practitioner has learned a transferable local model rather than accumulated model-specific superstition?

MECHANISM:
repeated intervention → observed consequence → updated expectation Ĝ_h → prospective prediction → successful nearby steering.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<CRAFT AS FINISHED OBJECT> → <CRAFT AS LEARNED LOCAL CONTROL MODEL>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
The cited HCI/NLP studies document practice and adaptation; copyright statute supplies the method/expression boundary. Ĝ_h is our formalization.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Ĝ_h approximates local generator behavior: intervention → expected consequence. Craft evidence strengthens when Ĝ_h supports successful prospective predictions and transfer.

TENSION:
Highly model-specific causal knowledge may evaporate after version changes and may also encode accommodation to model bias rather than general artistic expertise.

MISSING:
Prospective transfer experiments across tasks, seeds, and model versions.

BOUNDARY:
Craft, causal control, copyrightable expression, and legal authorship remain separate predicates.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[RETENTION-005-T]] → craft can be real without copyright → [[EPISTEMIC-001]] → discarded trials can update Ĝ_h.

TEST:
Ask experts and novices to predict the effect of unseen nearby prompt/control changes before generation. Measure calibration, target accuracy, invariant preservation, and cross-version transfer.
