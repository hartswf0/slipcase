ID:
RETENTION-005-M-C

TITLE:
The prompt dispute contains two hidden thresholds: how much control exists and how much copyright requires.

SOURCE:
U.S. Copyright Office — Part 2 — 2025; Samuelson, Sprigman, and Sag — 2023 comment.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The Office and sophisticated-prompt advocates may differ both in how much expressive control current prompting affords and how much human determination copyright should require.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
EMPIRICAL CONTROL THRESHOLD and NORMATIVE AUTHORSHIP THRESHOLD are distinct.

LOCAL MOVE:
Split one apparent dispute into c = measured control and λ = normative authorship threshold.

SOURCE TERMS:
control
authorship
original conception
expressive elements
current technology
prompt

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Two parties can observe the same process and disagree without misunderstanding the technology.

QUESTION:
Can prompt-authorship controversies be decomposed into a factual disagreement and a normative threshold disagreement?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Would better causal-control measurements resolve the legal question or merely make the normative disagreement visible?

MECHANISM:
measure c; law supplies λ; author if c≥λ. Disagreement may be over c, λ, or both.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<PROMPT AUTHORSHIP DISAGREEMENT> → {<FACT DISAGREEMENT>, <THRESHOLD DISAGREEMENT>}

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Control should itself be vector-valued: specificity, repeatability, intervention sensitivity, invariant preservation, expressive scope, selection.

TENSION:
Copyright does not announce a numerical λ.

MISSING:
Cases with near-identical technical facts but divergent results due to conceptions of control.

BOUNDARY:
Better empirical description cannot alone determine the normative scope of copyright.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[RETENTION-005-M]] → factual versus conceptual dispute → two thresholds.

TEST:
Ask lawyers, artists, HCI researchers, and control engineers to estimate factual control before asking whether authorship exists.
