ZETTEL

ID:
ATTRIBUTION-001

TITLE:
Control decomposes into discovery, commitment, constraint, steering, stabilization, invariant origin, and possibility-space origin.

SOURCE:
Stern Electronics, Inc. v. Kaufman — 669 F.2d 852 — Second Circuit — 1982; Midway Manufacturing Co. v. Artic International, Inc. — 704 F.2d 1009 — Seventh Circuit — 1983.

SOURCE URL:
https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F2/704/1009/107395/

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The video-game cases recognize substantial player-dependent variation and control while locating protected recurring audiovisual expression upstream in the game’s creators. Player control over a trajectory therefore cannot by itself establish authorship of the possibility structure.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
FUNCTIONAL ATTRIBUTION LEDGER.

LOCAL MOVE:
Use game cases as a limiting counterexample to any theory that turns successful steering directly into authorship.

SOURCE TERMS:
player control
trajectory
possibility space
recurring expression
authorship
performance
attribution

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A person can repeatedly make the future go otherwise without being the source of the expressive grammar within which those alternatives are reachable.

QUESTION:
Which functions in a generative workflow are performed by the human, model, interface, randomness, or prior authored system?

DEEPER QUESTION:
What extra condition beyond consequential steering distinguishes authorship from performance inside an upstream-structured expressive space?

MECHANISM:
decompose production into functions: discover distinction; commit; constrain; steer; stabilize; originate invariant; originate reachable possibility structure.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<HUMAN CONTROL PERCENTAGE> → <FUNCTIONALLY TYPED ATTRIBUTION>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
The cases concern video games and fixation/copyright of audiovisual works, not generative-AI authorship. The functional ledger is our synthesis.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
For each consequential relation r record actors for Discover(r), Commit(r), Constrain(r), Steer(r), Stabilize(r), OriginInvariant(r), and OriginSpace(r). Multiple roles may differ.

TENSION:
Possibility spaces in learned generative models are not analogous to finite libraries of pre-authored game frames; model outputs are synthesized.

MISSING:
A theory of possibility-space origin suitable for learned models rather than classical game programs.

BOUNDARY:
Counterfactual control is neither trivial nor sufficient. The ledger explains agency without deciding copyright automatically.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[RETENTION-005-S-A]] → game player as poison pill for control=authorship → functional decomposition.

TEST:
Apply the ledger to one game performance, one procedural artwork, one generative image workflow, and one direct drawing. Compare where the functions diverge.
