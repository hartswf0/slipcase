ID:
DISCOVERY-001

TITLE:
The choice-space of a creative act can be produced by an earlier act rather than given in advance.

SOURCE:
Donald A. Schön — “Designing as Reflective Conversation with the Materials of a Design Situation” — Research in Engineering Design 3 — 1992 — pp. 131–147.

SOURCE URL:
https://creativetech.mat.ucsb.edu/readings/Schon1992_Article_DesigningAsReflectiveConversat.pdf

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Schön argues that design objects and relations cannot simply be read back as inputs present from the start. Designers see, move, see again, and discover qualities and consequences in intermediate products. He explicitly treats evolving intention as an output of designing.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
ENDOGENOUS CHOICE-SPACE.

LOCAL MOVE:
RETENTION-005-Y asks who closes expressive variables. Schön forces a prior question: when did the variable become available as a distinction at all?

SOURCE TERMS:
design world
historical revisionism
seeing-moving-seeing
move experiment
evolving intention
unintended consequences

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A later analyst can make exploratory work look more controlled than it was by naming from the beginning distinctions that only became perceptible after an intermediate result existed.

QUESTION:
How should authorship evidence represent distinctions that emerge only after an artifact has begun to answer back?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is discovering a consequential variable a distinct creative act from choosing its value?

MECHANISM:
S_t → move m_t → intermediate state S_{t+1} → previously unavailable distinction v becomes perceptible → later action can intentionally preserve, alter, or reject v.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<FIXED POSSIBILITY SPACE> → [INTERMEDIATE RESULT] → <EMERGENT DISTINCTION> → [LATER CONTROL]

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Schön’s descriptive sequence is seeing-moving-seeing and his “move experiment” evaluates intended and unintended consequences through appreciation.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
V_{t+1}=V_t∪Discover(S_t,m_t). A production history must therefore record the genealogy of variables, not only assignments to variables.

TENSION:
Not every newly named feature was genuinely unavailable earlier; retrospective description can overstate emergence as easily as it can erase it.

MISSING:
A behavioral criterion for distinguishing newly discovered variables from merely newly verbalized variables.

BOUNDARY:
This does not make every surprise authorship. Surprise becomes relevant only if it enters later consequential action.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[RETENTION-005-Y]] → [[RETENTION-005-R-A-A-A]] → Schön → historical revisionism → endogenous choice-space.

TEST:
In a recorded making session, timestamp the first perceptible appearance of feature v, the first evidence that the maker treats v as consequential, and the first later intervention intended to preserve or alter v. Reject any account that places v in the initial specification without earlier evidence.
