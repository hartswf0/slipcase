ZETTEL

ID:
RETENTION-005-Y-D

TITLE:
Agency in a co-creative system has two opposite directions: how much the artist can change the system and how much the system can change the artist.

SOURCE:
David Abel et al. — “Plasticity as the Mirror of Empowerment” — NeurIPS 2025.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The authors distinguish capacity to influence future observations from capacity to be influenced by past observations, defining the second as plasticity—the directional mirror of empowerment.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
BIDIRECTIONAL AGENCY.

LOCAL MOVE:
Combine human→system influence with system→human change.

SOURCE TERMS:
empowerment
plasticity
influence
observations
agency
directed information

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
An artist can become exquisitely responsive to a model while the direction of influence shifts toward the model.

QUESTION:
Should creative agency be evaluated through both empowerment and plasticity rather than output control alone?

DEEPER QUESTION:
What would HIGH PLASTICITY but LOW EMPOWERMENT mean inside a generative system?

MECHANISM:
A_t→O_{t+1} empowerment direction; O_t→change in human state/preferences H_{t+1} plasticity direction.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<AGENCY = ABILITY TO AFFECT> → <AGENCY = AFFECTING + BEING AFFECTED>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Plasticity is defined using generalized directed information as a mirror of empowerment.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
E_h→m = human influence on artifact states; P_m→h = model influence on future human choices. Analyze four high/low regimes.

TENSION:
High plasticity is not inherently bad; being changed by materials and collaborators is central to creativity.

MISSING:
A criterion distinguishing generative dialogue from asymmetric capture.

BOUNDARY:
System-to-artist influence does not show human agency disappeared; agency is bidirectional.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[RETENTION-005-Y]] + [[RETENTION-005-R-A]] → co-adaptation → empowerment/plasticity duality.

TEST:
Over long sessions measure how human interventions shift outputs and how output characteristics shift subsequent human choices; plot both.
