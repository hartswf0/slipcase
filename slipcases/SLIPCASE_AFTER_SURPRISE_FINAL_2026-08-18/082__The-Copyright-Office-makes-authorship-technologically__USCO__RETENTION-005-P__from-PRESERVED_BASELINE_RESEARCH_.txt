ZETTEL

ID:
RETENTION-005-P

TITLE:
The Copyright Office makes authorship technologically contingent: the same human intention could cross the copyright boundary after an interface upgrade.

SOURCE:
U.S. Copyright Office — Copyright and Artificial Intelligence, Part 2 — January 2025.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The Office ties its prompt conclusion to current technology and says future systems affording greater user control over expressive elements could require a different conclusion.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
LEGAL AUTHORSHIP CAN CHANGE WITHOUT THE USER BECOMING MORE CREATIVE.

LOCAL MOVE:
The boundary can move because the MACHINE’S CONTROL SURFACE changes, not because the person changes.

SOURCE TERMS:
current technology
control
expressive elements
future advances
automation
optimization

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The same artist, intention, vocabulary, and prompt can fail under one interface and potentially pass under another.

QUESTION:
Should copyrightability of an artist’s contribution depend on the accidental historical maturity of the interface they use?

DEEPER QUESTION:
If yesterday’s difficult skilled prompting becomes tomorrow’s one-click deterministic control, does authorship become stronger as skill requirements become weaker?

MECHANISM:
t1: intent → coarse interface I1 → weak feature control. t2: same intent → improved I2 → high feature control.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<AUTHORSHIP AS PERSONAL CAPACITY> → <AUTHORSHIP AS PERSON × INTERFACE RELATION>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
A(h,p,M1,I1) may differ from A(h,p,M2,I2) even with h and p fixed.

TENSION:
Copyright often depends on expressive choices actually made through available tools; technology dependence is not unique to AI.

MISSING:
A theory separating personal skill from interface-provided control when both produce identical observable command over expressive elements.

BOUNDARY:
The Office’s rule is not “PROMPTS ARE NOT AUTHORSHIP”; it is a technology-contingent claim about current prompt-to-expression control.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[RETENTION-005]] → current insufficiency → future-technology caveat → authorship boundary moves with interface.

TEST:
Give the same artist the same edit task through interfaces of increasing control. Ask whether authorship evidence rises even as task difficulty falls.
