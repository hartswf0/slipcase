EXPLORATORY-001

TITLE:
The strongest challenge to the “re-roll” account is not that iteration is labor but that intermediate results can alter subsequent human commitments.

SOURCE:
Pamela Samuelson, Christopher Jon Sprigman, and Matthew Sag — Comments in Response to the U.S. Copyright Office’s Notice of Inquiry on Artificial Intelligence and Copyright — October 30, 2023 — pp. 3–4; U.S. Copyright Office — Copyright and Artificial Intelligence, Part 2: Copyrightability — January 2025 — pp. 18–21.

SOURCE URL:
https://www.thesedonaconference.org/sites/default/files/meeting_paper/8-6%2520Academic%2520Response%2520to%2520USCO.pdf
https://www.copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-2-Copyrightability-Report.pdf

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The Copyright Office treats repeated textual revision under current systems as additional sampling without sufficient control. Samuelson, Sprigman, and Sag accept that current registrations may fail but argue that authorship can arise through iterative exploratory engagement in which alternatives are contemplated, embraced, and rejected as a work develops.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
ITERATION AS STATE-CHANGING HUMAN COMMITMENT.

LOCAL MOVE:
Move the disagreement away from iteration count. Ask whether an intermediate result merely ends search or changes the constraints governing what may happen next.

SOURCE TERMS:
re-rolling
control
iterative exploratory fashion
alternatives
embracing
rejecting
original intellectual conception

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The same act of selecting an output can have two opposite temporal roles: terminal consumption or the creation of a new prospective constraint.

QUESTION:
What observable event distinguishes exploratory making from search when both contain repeated generation and selection?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can authorship evidence attach to a commitment that did not exist before the system produced the feature that occasioned it?

MECHANISM:
SEARCH: sample → evaluate → stop when acceptable. EXPLORATION: sample → discover v → commit to v → alter later instructions/controls so v survives or develops → continue.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<ITERATION COUNT> → <ITERATION CHANGES FUTURE CONSTRAINT STATE?>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
C_{t+1}=Update(C_t,y_t). The evidentiary discriminator is whether later interventions are conditional on a newly adopted feature and measurably structure later states.

TENSION:
A shopper can also refine preferences while searching. The criterion must require consequential intervention, not merely a changed opinion.

MISSING:
A prospective or behavioral test of commitment that does not depend entirely on self-report.

BOUNDARY:
This is an evidentiary proposal, not a statement of current U.S. copyright doctrine.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[RETENTION-005-M-A-A]] → Copyright Office re-roll analysis → Schön-style exploratory making → prospective stabilization.

TEST:
After a surprising feature first appears, conceal the maker’s verbal explanation. Examine only later actions. If subsequent interventions reliably preserve or elaborate the feature while sacrificing alternatives, classify the feature as an adopted prospective constraint rather than a terminal preference.
