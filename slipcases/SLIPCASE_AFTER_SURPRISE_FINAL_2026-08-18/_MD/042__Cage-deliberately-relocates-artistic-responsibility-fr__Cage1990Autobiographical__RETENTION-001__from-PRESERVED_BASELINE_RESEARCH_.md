ID:
RETENTION-001

TITLE:
Cage deliberately relocates artistic responsibility from choosing outcomes to designing the questions that chance will answer.

SOURCE:
John Cage — “An Autobiographical Statement” — 1990 — John Cage Trust.

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
Cage describes his compositional responsibility as “asking questions instead of making choices.”

RESEARCH OBJECT:
SELECTIVE RETENTION is not necessary for intentional artistic agency.

An artist can deliberately construct a procedure whose point is precisely to prevent later preference from filtering the generated result.

LOCAL MOVE:
The parent asks where agency enters when an accident appears and the maker says:

KEEP THAT.

Cage exposes a different architecture:

ASK THE QUESTION
→ ACCEPT WHAT THE PROCEDURE RETURNS.

SOURCE TERMS:
chance operations
responsibility
questions
choices
non-intention
I Ching
composition

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The parent’s branch test privileges a maker who chooses among generated alternatives.

Cage deliberately makes NOT CHOOSING part of the work.

QUESTION:
Can control reside in specifying the space of possible outcomes while relinquishing control over which particular outcome occurs?

DEEPER QUESTION:
How should agency be attributed when an artist intentionally designs the conditions under which their own subsequent preferences lose authority?

MECHANISM:
artist chooses:

procedure
questions
materials
mapping rules
performance conditions

→ chance operation

→ particular result

→ result accepted without aesthetic reselection.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<CONTROL OF OUTCOME>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Separate:

    C_design
    =
    control over generative system G

from:

    C_selection
    =
    control over which generated outcome survives.

Cage permits:

    C_design >> 0

while deliberately making:

    C_selection ≈ 0.

TENSION:
Acceptance itself might be redescribed as selection:

the artist could always abandon the work.

But doing so too freely would violate Cage’s stated compositional discipline.

MISSING:
How Cage distinguished:

ACCEPTANCE OF PROCEDURE
from
POST-HOC CURATION OF RESULTS

in particular works.

BOUNDARY:
Absence of output selection does not imply absence of agency.

It may indicate that agency has been moved earlier in the causal path.
