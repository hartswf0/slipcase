TITLE:
Wall Drawing 65 specifies randomness itself as an artistic operation.

SOURCE:
Sol LeWitt — Wall Drawing 65 — 1971 — primary title/instruction preserved by the National Gallery of Art.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The work calls for lines that are not short and not straight, crossing and touching, drawn randomly in four colors, uniformly dispersed at maximum density across the entire wall.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
A STOCHASTIC OUTCOME CAN FOLLOW A PRECISE INSTRUCTION.

LOCAL MOVE:
RETENTION-003 separates the concept from its later realization.

Wall Drawing 65 adds:

the concept may specifically prescribe that realization include randomness.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
“Random” and “specified” are not opposites.

The instruction tightly specifies WHICH DIMENSIONS should vary randomly and WHICH GLOBAL CONDITIONS must remain invariant.

QUESTION:
How much stochastic freedom can an instruction permit before the resulting object ceases to count as execution of that instruction?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is artistic control over a probability-generating process formally different from control over one deterministic outcome?

MECHANISM:
artist fixes:

line predicates
color set
coverage
density constraints

and specifies:

local placement
→ random.

Executor realizes one stochastic arrangement satisfying global constraints.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
The instruction explicitly uses random placement constrained by uniform dispersion and maximum density.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Artist specifies something closer to:

    K(output | constraints)

than:

    output = x*.

Work identity may lie in:

    distributional / procedural constraints

rather than a predetermined trajectory.

TENSION:
Human execution of “random” placement may not instantiate mathematically uniform randomness.

Practical artistic randomness and formal probability distributions are not identical.

MISSING:
How LeWitt instructed draftsmen to operationalize “random” and “uniformly dispersed.”

BOUNDARY:
STOCHASTICITY does not imply the absence of upstream specification.
