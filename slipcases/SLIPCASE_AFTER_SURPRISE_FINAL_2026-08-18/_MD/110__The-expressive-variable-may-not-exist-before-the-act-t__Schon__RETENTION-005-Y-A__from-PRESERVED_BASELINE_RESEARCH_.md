TITLE:
The expressive variable may not exist before the act that discovers it.

SOURCE:
Donald A. Schön — “Designing as Reflective Conversation with the Materials of a Design Situation” — Knowledge-Based Systems — 1992.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Schön argues designers do not begin with a fully given problem space; through seeing, moving, and seeing again they construct the design world and develop distinctions through the process.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
VARIABLE EMERGENCE.

LOCAL MOVE:
Attack the hidden assumption that expressive variables were already there waiting to be resolved.

SOURCE TERMS:
design world
seeing-moving-seeing
move experiment
unintended consequences
historical revisionism
design situation
appreciation

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A later analyst can redescribe an emergent variable as if it were obvious from the start.

QUESTION:
How can a production history represent a choice whose choice-space did not yet exist at the beginning?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is discovering a new controllable distinction itself a stronger form of agency than choosing among values inside an already-given distinction?

MECHANISM:
S0→move m0→S1→unforeseen relation r becomes perceptible→r becomes variable v→subsequent intentional manipulation.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<GIVEN POSSIBILITY SPACE Ω> → [MOVE] → <NEW DISTINCTION> → [VARIABLE CONSTRUCTION] → <EXPANDED Ω′>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Schön rejects treating the design problem space as fully given and describes intentions/design worlds developing through intermediate products.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
V_{t+1}=V_t∪Discover(S_t,m_t). A branch can be created before selected.

TENSION:
Retrospective vocabulary can make emergent variables look preexisting—the historical revisionism problem.

MISSING:
A criterion distinguishing discovering a situation-produced distinction from naming one already functionally present.

BOUNDARY:
Agency reconstruction must include genealogy of variables, not only assignment of resolvers.

CITATION TRAIL:

TEST:
For each consequential distinction in a recorded process ask whether it was articulated before the artifact that first made it visible; timestamp exposure/recognition/manipulation.
