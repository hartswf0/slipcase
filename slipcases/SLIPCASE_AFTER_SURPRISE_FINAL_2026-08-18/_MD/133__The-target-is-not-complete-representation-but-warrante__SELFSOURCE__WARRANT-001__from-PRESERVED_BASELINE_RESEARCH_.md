The target is not complete representation but warranted passage.

[PARAPHRASE]
Because action requires reduction, trustworthiness cannot mean completeness. It means accountability for how the representation was made, what it omitted/transformed, which consequences it enters, how long its warrant lasts, burdens it transfers, and whether relevant differences can return.
