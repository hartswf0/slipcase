RETENTION-005-R-A-A

TITLE:
Iterative prompting creates an identification problem: observed preference is jointly produced by user intention and model feedback.

SOURCE:
Shachar Don-Yehiya, Leshem Choshen, and Omri Abend — “Human Learning by Model Feedback” — EMNLP 2023.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Prompt convergence reflects both intention refinement and adaptation to model-preferred language, making final prompt content a mixture of latent causes.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
USER INTENTION IS NOT IDENTIFIABLE FROM THE FINAL PROMPT ALONE.

LOCAL MOVE:
Final prompt is a post-treatment variable rather than transparent intention report.

SOURCE TERMS:
human learning
model feedback
model preferences
missing details
convergence
natural expression

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A polished final prompt can look maximally intentional while containing phrases inserted primarily because the system rewards them.

QUESTION:
Can the artist’s intended contribution be inferred without an independent measurement taken before model feedback?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does the model become a treatment that changes the very preference the researcher later tries to measure?

MECHANISM:
H0 + M → y0 → H1=f(H0,y0); p1=g(H1,M-feedback). Final pn confounds H and model treatment.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<FINAL PROMPT AS INTENTION> → <FINAL PROMPT AS POST-TREATMENT VARIABLE>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
The study tests two explanations and finds evidence for both.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Need pre-interaction proxy H_pre independent of M to estimate accommodation; otherwise Effect(M on H) is entangled.

TENSION:
Creative intention often forms through a medium; there may be no pristine pre-medium intention.

MISSING:
Theory separating intention formation, medium discovery, and unwanted capture without assuming intention preexists making.

BOUNDARY:
Final prompt is evidence of a process, not transparent evidence of original intention.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[RETENTION-005-R-A]] → co-adaptation → latent causes → identification problem → post-treatment intention.

TEST:
Collect sketches/free description/references before model interaction and compare final prompt/output to pre-interaction representation and model-preferred traits.
