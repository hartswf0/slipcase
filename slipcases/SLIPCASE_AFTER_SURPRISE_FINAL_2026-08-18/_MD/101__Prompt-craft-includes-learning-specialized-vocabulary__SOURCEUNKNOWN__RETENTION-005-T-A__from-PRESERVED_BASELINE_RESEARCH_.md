TITLE:
Prompt craft includes learning specialized vocabulary that is useful precisely because ordinary language and model-effective language are not identical.

SOURCE:
Minsuk Chang et al. — “The Prompt Artists” — 2023.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Prolific users seek specialized terms and phrases, including borrowed disciplinary vocabulary, to obtain distinctive model effects and develop reusable practices.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
PROMPT CRAFT CAN BE MODEL-MEDIATED VOCABULARY ACQUISITION.

LOCAL MOVE:
PRACTICE BUILDS DISCRIMINATION ABOUT WHICH WORDS HAVE MACHINE-SPECIFIC CONSEQUENCES.

SOURCE TERMS:
specialized vocabulary
prompt templates
community of practice
distinctive outputs
prompt artists

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A word’s useful prompt meaning may differ from ordinary semantics; craft value can lie in a learned causal association WORD→MODEL BEHAVIOR.

QUESTION:
Is prompt expertise linguistic competence, instrument technique, or empirical knowledge of a model’s idiosyncratic response surface?

DEEPER QUESTION:
What survives when the practitioner moves to a different model with a different operative lexicon?

MECHANISM:
generation experiments → observe word/effect → retain vocabulary → share/template → community stabilizes model-specific craft language.

FORMAL SHIFT:

TENSION:
Some vocabulary transfers because models share data/caption conventions/cultural priors.

MISSING:
Cross-model transfer rates for expert prompt vocabularies.

BOUNDARY:
Model-specific vocabulary skill is genuine learned discrimination even if impermanent or uncopyrightable.
