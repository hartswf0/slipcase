TITLE:
Prompt expertise can improve by adapting to a model while simultaneously becoming less faithful to the user’s natural language and original intentions.

SOURCE:
Shachar Don-Yehiya, Leshem Choshen, and Omri Abend — “Human Learning by Model Feedback” — EMNLP 2023.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Iterative users converge partly by learning model preferences, not solely by clarifying their intended image; this can bias language away from natural expression.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
CRAFT PROFICIENCY AND HUMAN SEMANTIC FIDELITY CAN DIVERGE.

LOCAL MOVE:
Separate SKILL from FAITHFUL EXPRESSION OF HUMAN INTENTION.

SOURCE TERMS:
model preferences
human learning

bias

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
An expert can become better by becoming better at accommodating the machine. That is skill; it may also be capture.

QUESTION:
When does learning a medium become learning to satisfy the medium’s defaults?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can expertise increase while expressive sovereignty decreases?

MECHANISM:
model rewards L_M → user observes → shifts prompts toward L_M → measured generation performance improves while distance from independent L_H may grow.

FORMAL SHIFT:

SOURCE FORMALISM:
The paper empirically studies convergence and competing intention-completion/model-adaptation hypotheses.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Track Performance_M(user) and Fidelity(user language, independently elicited intention); they can move oppositely.

TENSION:
All artistic media shape intention; medium influence alone is not problematic capture.

MISSING:
A criterion separating productive medium formation from systematic narrowing imposed by model defaults.

BOUNDARY:
Expert performance inside one system should not be assumed to measure general artistic agency.
