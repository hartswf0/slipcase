TITLE:
A button is not evidence of agency if the causal window has already closed.

SOURCE:
User-provided manuscript / conversation synthesis — 2026-08-16.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Visible choices may appear after upstream classifications, defaults, rankings, permissions, and exclusions have already selected much of the future.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
CAUSAL WINDOW OF AGENCY.

LOCAL MOVE:
Separate FORMAL PARTICIPATION from CAUSAL ACCESS.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
An interface can maximize apparent choice after the consequential decision has moved elsewhere.

QUESTION:
When is an interface action still causally live?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can agency be measured as expected change in reachable future states rather than number of available controls?

MECHANISM:
upstream system restricts Ω0→Ω1; interface displays actions A1...An; if Reach(Ai) are nearly identical, choice is formally visible but causally weak.

FORMAL SHIFT:

SOURCE FORMALISM:

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Leverage(a,s)=divergence between futures under do(a) and baseline.

TENSION:
Symbolic participation can matter through precedent, signaling, coordination, or delayed effects even when immediate leverage is low.

MISSING:
Short-term versus delayed leverage.

BOUNDARY:
Agency should not be reduced entirely to instantaneous outcome control.

CITATION TRAIL:

TEST:
For each button or appeal mechanism, model downstream state distributions with and without its use. Identify controls whose visibility exceeds causal effect.
