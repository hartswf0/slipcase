RETENTION-005-Y

TITLE:
The children converge on a new distinction: expressive agency depends on where freedom is located, not merely how much freedom exists.

SOURCE:
COLLISION — RETENTION-005-T-D/B/A, RETENTION-005-S-C/A, RETENTION-005-R-B/A, RETENTION-005-N-B/A, RETENTION-005-M-B/A and children.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Across practice, HCI, optimization, law, games, and iterative prompting, the same question recurs: where did unresolved expressive possibilities exist and who or what was entitled to resolve them?

RESEARCH OBJECT:
LOCUS OF EXPRESSIVE FREEDOM.

LOCAL MOVE:
Replace HOW MUCH CONTROL? with WHERE IS EXPRESSIVE FREEDOM LOCATED AND HOW IS IT CLOSED?

SOURCE TERMS:
control
possibility space
model preference
prompt expansion
rote transcription
iteration
platformization
selection

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Two workflows can produce identical artifacts while differing radically in where open variables existed and how they were resolved.

QUESTION:
Can authorship, craft, and agency be reconstructed as different patterns in the allocation and closure of expressive freedom?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does the most important artistic intervention sometimes consist not in choosing a value but in deciding WHO OR WHAT GETS TO CHOOSE IT?

MECHANISM:
At stage t partition V_t into human-fixed, human-bounded, machine-expanded, defaulted, randomized, environment-supplied, delegated, later-discovered.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<CONTROL AMOUNT> → <TOPOLOGY OF EXPRESSIVE FREEDOM>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
For each v: Freedom(v,t), Resolver(v,t), Constraint(v,t), Authority(v,t), Visibility(v,t), Reversibility(v,t).

TENSION:
Expressive variables can become visible only retrospectively; formalization risks pretending the possibility space was given beforehand.

MISSING:
VARIABLE EMERGENCE: how previously nonexistent or unnoticed distinctions become available during making.

BOUNDARY:
A topology of expressive freedom explains causal agency more precisely than labels but does not determine legal authorship or merit.

CITATION TRAIL:
selected RETENTION branches → intervention access/model accommodation/possibility navigation/machine completion/exploration → LOCUS OF EXPRESSIVE FREEDOM.

TEST:
For a complete trace ask at each meaningful change: what could go otherwise, who perceived alternatives, who resolved them, who could veto, when did the distinction become a choice?
