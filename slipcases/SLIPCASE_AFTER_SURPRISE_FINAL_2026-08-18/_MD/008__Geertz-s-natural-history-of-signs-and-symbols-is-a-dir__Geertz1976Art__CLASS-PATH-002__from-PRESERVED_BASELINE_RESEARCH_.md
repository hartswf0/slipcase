ID:
CLASS-PATH-002

TITLE:
Geertz’s “natural history of signs and symbols” is a direct methodological warrant for replacing class labels with trajectories through use.

SOURCE:
Clifford Geertz — “Art as a Cultural System” — 1976 — conclusion.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Geertz argues that semiotics must leave signs in abstraction and investigate them in the ordinary world in which people use them. He calls for a natural history of signs and symbols and an ethnography of vehicles of meaning.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
“CLASS IS NOT A PATH” has a Geertzian methodological ancestor stronger than the essay currently makes explicit.

LOCAL MOVE:
The argument can move from:

AI LABELS ARE TOO COARSE

to the more general methodological claim:

SIGNS AND TECHNICAL CATEGORIES ACQUIRE EXPLANATORY FORCE THROUGH THEIR CAREERS IN PRACTICE.

SOURCE TERMS:
natural history
signs
symbols
vehicles of meaning
life of a society
use
common world

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Geertz’s objection is not specifically to formalism.

It is to allowing formal description to substitute for tracing how the sign lives.

QUESTION:
Could “AI-generated” itself be studied as a sign whose social career changes the reception of artifacts independently of their production path?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does the technological label operate twice:
first as a causal descriptor of production,
then as a cultural sign reorganizing judgment?

MECHANISM:
artifact production
→ technical label L attached

then:

audience encounters <artifact,L>
→ label activates expectations/categories
→ perception and judgment shift
→ new career for artifact.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<PRODUCTION CLASSIFICATION>
→ <PUBLIC SIGN>
→ [UPTAKE]
→ <RECEPTION CONSEQUENCE>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Let artifact:

    A

and label:

    L = “AI-generated”.

Reception may satisfy:

    Judge(A,L)
        ≠
    Judge(A,∅)

even though:

    A

is identical.

Thus L is simultaneously:

    metadata about Causes(A)

and:

    causal input into Effects(A).

TENSION:
A label can convey genuinely relevant provenance information.

Showing that it changes judgment does not show that the judgment change is irrational.

MISSING:
Blind/labeled experiments measuring how production-class labels alter judgments while holding artifacts constant.

BOUNDARY:
The critique should not be:

IGNORE THE CLASS.

It should be:

DO NOT SUBSTITUTE THE CLASS FOR EITHER THE PRODUCTION PATH OR THE SIGN’S SUBSEQUENT CAREER.

TEST:
Randomly assign identical artifacts to conditions:

AI-GENERATED
HUMAN-MADE
UNLABELED
DETAILED HYBRID PROVENANCE.

Measure changes in:
quality judgment
authorship attribution
intentionality inference
willingness to pay
memory for details.

Then distinguish informationally justified effects from stereotype effects.
