ZETTEL

ID:
RETENTION-005-C

TITLE:
The Allen decision could not inspect the artistic object most necessary to evaluate prompt craft because Allen withheld the prompts.

SOURCE:
U.S. Copyright Office Review Board — Second Request for Reconsideration for Refusal to Register Théâtre D’opéra Spatial — September 5, 2023 — pp. 5–6. Allen described at least 624 prompt/revision cycles but declined to disclose his specific prompt strings as confidential; the Board therefore stated that it could not assess whether those prompts were independently copyrightable.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The Board received Allen's description of an elaborate prompt-development practice, including categories, style, composition, colors, and parameters, but not the actual prompt strings that instantiated that practice.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
THÉÂTRE D’OPÉRA SPATIAL IS A STRONG CASE ABOUT OUTPUT AUTHORSHIP BUT A WEAK CASE FOR STUDYING PROMPT CRAFT AS AN ARTIFACT.

LOCAL MOVE:
RETENTION-005 asks where creative intervention occurred.

The administrative record contains the scalar:

624 ITERATIONS

but lacks the object required to reconstruct the intervention path:

THE ACTUAL PROMPT TRAJECTORY.

SOURCE TERMS:
624
revisions
text prompts
parameters
confidential
creative input
authorship

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
One of the most frequently cited prompt-authorship cases contains no public sequence of prompts from which to determine whether Allen was:

re-rolling,
learning,
steering,
encoding constraints,
or creating a prompt-work in its own right.

QUESTION:
Would the Board's analysis have changed if Allen had supplied a prompt/output history demonstrating feature-specific, repeatable control?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can copyright doctrine accurately evaluate a new creative practice when the crucial process evidence is withheld as proprietary craft knowledge?

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

The evidentiary object needed to test prompt control is:

    τ =
    [(p₀,y₀),
     (p₁,y₁),
     ...,
     (pₙ,yₙ)]

plus declared intended feature changes:

    Δf_t^desired.

Without τ:

    N = 624

cannot discriminate steering from search.

TENSION:
The Board did not need to resolve whether Allen's prompt text itself was copyrightable because he did not seek registration of the prompts and the registration claim concerned the final work.

The missing prompts therefore weaken one research question without necessarily invalidating the Board's legal decision on the record before it.

MISSING:
Allen's actual prompt and image history.

BOUNDARY:
Do not turn Théâtre D’opéra Spatial into evidence that 624 iterations contained no artistic intervention.

The decision establishes what the Board concluded from the disclosed record, not what an unavailable trajectory would have shown.
