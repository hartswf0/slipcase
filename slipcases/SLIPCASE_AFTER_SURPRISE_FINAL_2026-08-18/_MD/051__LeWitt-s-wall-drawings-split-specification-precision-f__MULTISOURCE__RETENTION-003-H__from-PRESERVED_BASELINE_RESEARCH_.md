RETENTION-003-H

TITLE:
LeWitt’s wall drawings split specification precision from realization variability into independent dimensions.

SOURCE:
COLLISION — Sol LeWitt, Wall Drawings 88, 237, 274, 65, 797, and 289 — 1971–1995 — instructions and institutional installation records.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Across the works, LeWitt varies different components independently: some instructions tightly specify geometry but leave site dimensions open; some delegate decisions to draftsmen; some prescribe randomness; some deliberately amplify copying errors; some allow broad interpretive latitude; all remain recognizable instruction-based works.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
“HOW MUCH CONTROL?” IS THE WRONG SINGLE QUESTION.

LOCAL MOVE:
RETENTION-003 initially distinguishes upstream conception from downstream execution.

The children reveal a multidimensional architecture of deferred agency.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Two instructions can be equally authored and equally executable while differing radically in:

how much they specify,
what they leave variable,
who resolves those variables,
whether variation is random,
whether the environment supplies values,
and whether realization must be authorized.

QUESTION:
Can instruction-based works be classified by their CONTROL TOPOLOGY rather than by a single continuum from controlled to uncontrolled?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Would the same topology provide a better analytic language for prompts and generative-AI workflows?

MECHANISM:
operative description D partitions downstream difference into:

FIXED
DELEGATED
ENVIRONMENT-SUPPLIED
RANDOMIZED
EXECUTOR-INTERPRETED
ITERATIVELY EMERGENT
AUTHORIZED.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<CONTROL = SCALAR>
→ <CONTROL = DISTRIBUTION OF DECISION RIGHTS AND VARIABLES>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Candidate:

    D =
    <C_fixed,
     V_env,
     V_delegate,
     V_random,
     V_interpret,
     R_iteration,
     A_authority>

where each coordinate describes a different source of downstream variation.

A production path is then an instantiation trace:

    D
    + environment
    + agents
    + stochastic events
    → realization.

TENSION:
The decomposition may impose cleaner categories than actual practice supports.

One variable can simultaneously be:
environmental,
interpreted,
and delegated.

MISSING:
A representation allowing overlapping causal responsibility rather than forcing each branch into exactly one category.

BOUNDARY:
The useful question is no longer:

DID THE MAKER CONTROL THE OUTPUT?

It becomes:

WHICH DIFFERENCES WERE FIXED, WHICH WERE LEFT OPEN, WHO OR WHAT COULD RESOLVE THEM, AND UNDER WHAT AUTHORITY?
