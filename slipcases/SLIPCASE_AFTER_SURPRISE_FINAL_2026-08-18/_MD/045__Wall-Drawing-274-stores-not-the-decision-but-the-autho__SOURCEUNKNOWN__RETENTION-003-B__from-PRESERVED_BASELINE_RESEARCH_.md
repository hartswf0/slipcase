ID:
RETENTION-003-B

TITLE:
Wall Drawing 274 stores not the decision but the authority to make the decision.

SOURCE:
Sol LeWitt — Wall Drawing 274 — September 1975 — primary instruction reproduced in the MASS MoCA Sol LeWitt Wall Drawing Retrospective record.

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
“The specific locations are determined by the drafter.”

RESEARCH OBJECT:
AN INSTRUCTION CAN DELEGATE A BRANCH RATHER THAN RESOLVE IT.

LOCAL MOVE:
RETENTION-003 treated an instruction as a stored decision.

Wall Drawing 274 exposes another operation:

stored DELEGATION.

The artist decides who gets to decide later.

SOURCE TERMS:
location
six geometric figures
specific locations
determined by the drafter
construction
drafting methods

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
LeWitt remains the attributed artist even where one visually important property is intentionally left for another person to determine.

The upstream act is not:

CHOOSE LOCATION.

It is:

MAKE LOCATION A DOWNSTREAM AUTHORIZED CHOICE.

QUESTION:
Can authorship consist partly in allocating decision rights over a possibility space?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Should operative-description theory contain an explicit AUTHORITY MAP identifying which agent may instantiate which unresolved variables?

MECHANISM:
LeWitt defines:

figure vocabulary
+
construction grammar
+
general task

and assigns:

location choice
→ draftsman.

The draftsman then chooses particular locations while remaining constrained by LeWitt’s larger system.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<UNRESOLVED VARIABLE>
→ [ASSIGN DECISION AUTHORITY]
→ <AUTHORIZED DOWNSTREAM CHOICE>
→ <REALIZATION>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
The instruction explicitly delegates placement of the six figures to the drafter.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

An operative description may contain:

    <C,V,A>

where:

    C = fixed constraints
    V = unresolved variables
    A : V → Agent

assigns authority to resolve each variable.

For Wall Drawing 274:

    A(location) = draftsman.

TENSION:
The draftsman is not unrestricted.

The museum record notes that competent realization depends on familiarity with LeWitt’s geometric vocabulary and working methods.

Delegation therefore coexists with tacit constraint.

MISSING:
How much variation LeWitt personally accepted from draftsmen in installations of Wall Drawing 274.

BOUNDARY:
AUTHORSHIP OF A SYSTEM does not require authorship of every value instantiated inside that system.

TEST:
Rewrite Wall Drawing 274 in three variants:

A.

Compare what changes in:
