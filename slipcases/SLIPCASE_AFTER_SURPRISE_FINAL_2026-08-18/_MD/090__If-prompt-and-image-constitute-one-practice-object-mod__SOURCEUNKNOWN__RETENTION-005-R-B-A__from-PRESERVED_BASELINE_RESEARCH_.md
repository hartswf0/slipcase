TITLE:
If prompt and image constitute one practice object, model version becomes part of the work’s conditions of intelligibility.

SOURCE:
Minsuk Chang et al. — “The Prompt Artists” — 2023; Téo Sanchez et al. — “Artists on a Decade of AI Evolution” — CHI 2026.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Some practitioners treat prompt and image collectively as the work, while AI art practice depends on changing models, pipelines, and tool ecologies.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
RELATIONAL WORKS REQUIRE EXECUTION CONTEXT.

LOCAL MOVE:
The model identifier alone is insufficient; VERSION and WORKFLOW STATE can be constitutive provenance.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The prompt and saved image can survive after the operative relation that connected them becomes unreconstructable.

QUESTION:
Does preservation of prompt-based art require preservation of the executable environment that once made the prompt meaningful?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is model behavior to prompt art like performance practice to a musical score: historically situated machinery needed to reconstruct the relation?

MECHANISM:
p + M_v + workflow θ → y; archive <p,y> omits transformation relation; M_v disappears → relation dies.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<ARCHIVE PROMPT + IMAGE> → <ARCHIVE OPERATIVE ENVIRONMENT>

OUR FORMALIZATION:

A=<p,M_version,workflow,parameters,seed/state,y>. Preservation can target reproducibility or historical intelligibility.

TENSION:
Many artworks survive without exact reproducibility of their making process.

MISSING:
A preservation standard distinguishing work identity, historical reconstruction, and executable reenactment.

BOUNDARY:
If prompt-image relation is constitutive, image-file preservation alone is incomplete.
