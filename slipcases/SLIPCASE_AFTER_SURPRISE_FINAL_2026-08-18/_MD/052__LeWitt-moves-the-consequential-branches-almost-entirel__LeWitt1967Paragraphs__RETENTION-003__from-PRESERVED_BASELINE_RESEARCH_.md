RETENTION-003

TITLE:
LeWitt moves the consequential branches almost entirely before execution.

SOURCE:
Sol LeWitt — “Paragraphs on Conceptual Art” — Artforum — June 1967.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
LeWitt says conceptual art places planning and decisions before realization, treats execution as comparatively perfunctory, and seeks to minimize arbitrary decisions made while the work is being completed.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
THE CONSEQUENTIAL BRANCH NEED NOT OCCUR WHILE THE ARTIFACT IS VISIBLE.

LOCAL MOVE:
The parent asks:

Where could the thing have gone otherwise because somebody noticed a difference and acted?

LeWitt adds:

THE BRANCH MAY HAVE BEEN RESOLVED BEFORE THE PHYSICAL THING EXISTED.

SOURCE TERMS:
planning
decisions
execution
idea

conceptual art

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A prospective “call the shot” test aimed only at nearby output manipulation could badly underestimate an instruction-based work.

The important decisions may already be frozen into the procedure.

QUESTION:
How can a causal analysis recover consequential decisions whose visible effects occur much later than the decision itself?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is a generative instruction a stored branch decision that continues acting after the chooser has left the scene?

MECHANISM:
artist considers possible systems
→ selects concept/rule
→ decisions fixed upstream
→ executor/system instantiates rule
→ downstream variations constrained by earlier decision.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<BRANCH CHOICE AT EXECUTION>
→ <BRANCH CHOICE STORED IN DESCRIPTION>
→ [DEFERRED REALIZATION]
→ <ARTIFACT>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

At time t₀:

    choose policy π

At later times:

    a_t = π(s_t)

The artist need not personally choose each:

    a_t

for the earlier policy choice to remain causally active.

TENSION:
The realization can introduce interpretation, material contingency, fabrication differences, and executor judgment not anticipated by the plan.

MISSING:
How much variation LeWitt considered compatible with identity of one work across different realizations.

BOUNDARY:
Direct intervention at every branch is not necessary for consequential human control.

A decision can be compiled into a procedure.
