ZETTEL

ID:
RETENTION-005

TITLE:
Current U.S. copyright analysis permits human authorship to enter after generation through creative selection, arrangement, or modification.

SOURCE:
U.S. Copyright Office — Copyright and Artificial Intelligence, Part 2: Copyrightability — January 2025 — pp. iii, 24–27.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The Office distinguishes AI-generated elements standing alone from human-authored contributions made by creatively selecting, coordinating, arranging, or modifying such material. It also distinguishes prompts alone from interfaces that let users control placement or selection of individual creative elements.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
AUTHORSHIP CAN ENTER DOWNSTREAM OF GENERATION.

LOCAL MOVE:
The parent asks whether saying KEEP THAT after an accident can be consequential agency.

The Copyright Office supplies a narrower answer:

POST-GENERATION ACTION MAY CONSTITUTE HUMAN AUTHORSHIP,

but not merely because an output was accepted.

SOURCE TERMS:
selection
coordination
arrangement
modification
control
expressive elements
AI-generated material

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The legal framework does not require the person to have authored every underlying element.

But it requires the human contribution itself to contain sufficient original expression.

QUESTION:
What transforms bare retention into creative selection or arrangement?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can an empirical control test distinguish:

CHOOSING AN OUTPUT

from:

AUTHORING A RELATION AMONG OUTPUTS?

MECHANISM:
AI system
→ generates material A,B,C,...

human
→ selects subset
→ arranges relations
→ modifies elements
→ larger authored composition.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<GENERATED ELEMENT>
→ [HUMAN SELECTION / ARRANGEMENT / MODIFICATION]
→ <NEW HUMAN-CONTRIBUTED EXPRESSIVE STRUCTURE>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
The Office applies existing copyright principles concerning originality, human authorship, compilations, selection, coordination, arrangement, and modification.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Bare retention:

    Keep(x)

Structured selection:

    Choose({x_i}) + Relation({x_i}) → A

Modification:

    Transform_human(x) → x'.

These are distinct causal operations.

TENSION:
Copyright authorship is a legal category.

Aesthetic agency, causal control, craft, and philosophical authorship need not share its boundary.

MISSING:
Whether repeated selection among single outputs can itself accumulate into expressive structure before explicit arrangement occurs.

BOUNDARY:
Downstream selection can be consequential human authorship under U.S. copyright doctrine.

Bare adoption of an attractive generated result is not thereby automatically authorship of the generated expressive elements.
