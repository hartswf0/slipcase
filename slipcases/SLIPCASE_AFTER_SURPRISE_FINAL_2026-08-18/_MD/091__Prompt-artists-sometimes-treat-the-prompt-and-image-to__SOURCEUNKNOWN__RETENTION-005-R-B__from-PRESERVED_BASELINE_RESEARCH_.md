RETENTION-005-R-B

TITLE:
Prompt artists sometimes treat the prompt and image together as the artwork, making copyright’s prompt/output split descriptively incomplete.

SOURCE:
Minsuk Chang et al. — “The Prompt Artists” — 2023.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Interview/observation work reports that some practitioners understand prompt and resulting image collectively as the art piece and develop templates, specialized vocabularies, and glitch practices.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
THE PRACTICE OBJECT MAY BE A RELATION, NOT AN OUTPUT.

LOCAL MOVE:
Practitioners can refuse the clean legal atomization PROMPT here / IMAGE there.

SOURCE TERMS:
prompt artists
prompt as art
image
collectively
template
specialized vocabulary
glitch

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Copyright can separate prompt/output while artistic practice treats their relation as the meaningful work.

QUESTION:
What is lost when legal analysis decomposes an artistic relation into separately copyrightable prompt text and generated visual material?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can an artwork consist principally in an operative relation: THIS LANGUAGE → THROUGH THIS SYSTEM → PRODUCES THIS FAMILY OF VISUAL EFFECTS?

MECHANISM:
artist develops p + knowledge of M → y; artistic judgment may attach to <p,M,y> rather than y alone.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<ARTWORK = OUTPUT> → <ARTWORK = OPERATIVE RELATION BETWEEN INSCRIPTION AND CONSEQUENCE>

MISSING:

BOUNDARY:
Legal separability of prompt and output does not establish cultural separability.
