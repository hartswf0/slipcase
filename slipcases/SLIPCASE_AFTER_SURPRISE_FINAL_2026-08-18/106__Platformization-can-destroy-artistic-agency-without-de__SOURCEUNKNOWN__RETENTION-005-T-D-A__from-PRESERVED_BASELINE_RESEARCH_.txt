ZETTEL

ID:
RETENTION-005-T-D-A

TITLE:
Platformization can destroy artistic agency without deleting the model’s nominal capabilities.

SOURCE:
Téo Sanchez et al. — “Artists on a Decade of AI Evolution: An Interview Study of Affordances, Culture, and Artistic Practice with Machine Learning” — CHI 2026; Chang et al. — “The Prompt Artists” — 2023.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Long-term ML artists report that scale, complexity, and platformization can make it harder to intervene inside models even as consumer generation becomes easier. Earlier prompt-art work documents appropriation of glitches as styles.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
AFFORDANCE LOSS WITHOUT FEATURE LOSS.

LOCAL MOVE:
Craft can disappear because access to the relevant intervention layer disappears even if related computational possibilities still exist inside the system.

SOURCE TERMS:
platformization
low-level intervention
malleable material
crafting
bricolage
glitch
workflow composition

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A platform can become easier to use while becoming harder to practice deeply. The interface rises as the substrate disappears.

QUESTION:
When does simplification of a creative interface become enclosure of an artistic material?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Should persistence of an artistic medium require preservation not merely of output capabilities but of access to the intervention layers through which practitioners learned to manipulate it?

MECHANISM:
earlier model/pipeline exposes intervention → artist learns g → technique. Later platform hides intervention boundary → technique unavailable.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<CAPABILITY PRESERVATION> ≠ <AFFORDANCE PRESERVATION>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Sanchez et al. distinguish interactions atop models from interventions within models and call for low-level intervention/workflow composition.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Creative affordance A=<possible transformation, accessible intervention point, observable feedback, repeatability>.

TENSION:
Platformization lowers barriers and creates new practices too.

MISSING:
A preservation criterion for artistic affordances across software/model versions.

BOUNDARY:
Technical capability is not sufficient evidence that a previously available artistic instrument still exists.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[RETENTION-005-T-D]] → glitch as technique → platformization reduces intervention → capability vs material access.

TEST:
Archive expert workflow at model/node/conditioning/latent/parameter level and recreate after platform migration; note possible-but-inaccessible transformations.
