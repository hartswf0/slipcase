ID:
RETENTION-005-S-C

TITLE:
Authorship of a generator does not automatically imply authorship of every output it can emit.

SOURCE:
COLLISION — Stern Electronics — 1982; U.S. Copyright Office — 2025; 17 U.S.C. §102(b).

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Copyright can protect source code, recurring audiovisual expression, human-written prompt language, or other components while keeping each distinct from particular results of operation.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
GENERATOR AUTHORSHIP and EMISSION AUTHORSHIP are independent predicates.

LOCAL MOVE:
Block automatic transfer from authorship of possibility structure to every sampled realization.

SOURCE TERMS:
computer program
audiovisual work
prompt
output
procedure
expression

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A person can clearly author RULES THAT GENERATE without authoring EVERY DETAIL GENERATED.

QUESTION:
Under what conditions does authorship of generative constraints propagate into authorship of a particular realization?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does copyright need a theory of inheritance from generator to token, or should expressive elements be attributed independently?

MECHANISM:
human authors G_H → interacts with model M, randomness z, environment θ → y; attribute each feature separately.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<AUTHOR OF GENERATIVE SYSTEM> ↛ <AUTHOR OF ALL OUTPUTS>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Contribution(f)∈{human rule, model prior, random realization, external input, combination}.

TENSION:
Artistic convention may attribute an entire generative work to a designer despite random microstates; legal attribution differs.

MISSING:
A threshold for when random realization remains attributable to an authored rule.

BOUNDARY:
Distributional authorship is useful only if it does not shortcut control of possibility space into ownership of every token.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[RETENTION-005-S]] → author distribution? → program/game separation → generator/token distinction → inheritance problem.

TEST:
Compare exhaustive-output generator, structural grammar with random parameters, and weak prompt to pretrained model; identify where token authorship stops following generator authorship.
