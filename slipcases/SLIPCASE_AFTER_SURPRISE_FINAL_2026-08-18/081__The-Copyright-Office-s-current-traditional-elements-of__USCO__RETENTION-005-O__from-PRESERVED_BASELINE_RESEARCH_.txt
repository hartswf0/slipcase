TITLE:
The Copyright Office’s current “traditional elements of authorship” test began as a provisional question in its 1965 annual report.

SOURCE:

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The Office framed an emerging question: whether computer-created works were basically human-authored with the computer as assisting instrument, or whether traditional expressive, selection, and arrangement elements were conceived and executed by machine.
