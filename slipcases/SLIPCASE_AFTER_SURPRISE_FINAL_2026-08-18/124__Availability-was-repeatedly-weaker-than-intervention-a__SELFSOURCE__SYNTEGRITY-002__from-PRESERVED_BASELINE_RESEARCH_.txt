Availability was repeatedly weaker than intervention across otherwise unrelated domains.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Across omission, category correction, interface choice, authority, embodiment, time, scale, model re-entry, and plural coordination, things could exist formally while being causally dead.

AVAILABILITY ≠ INTERVENTION.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The same operational asymmetry reappeared under different vocabularies.

QUESTION:
Is availability-versus-intervention a genuine cross-domain invariant or an artifact of the simulation’s priors?

DEEPER QUESTION:
What is the minimal structure shared by record recovery, appeal standing, interface leverage, and corrective action?

MECHANISM:
resource/action channel exists formally, but precondition for affecting consequential state is absent.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<AVAILABLE> → [? CAUSALLY CONNECTED] → <INTERVENTION-CAPABLE>

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Availability: ∃a. Intervention: ∃a such that do(a) changes consequential reachable states. Intervention implies availability, not vice versa.

TENSION:
Available resources can matter cumulatively even if no individual use causes immediate change.

MISSING:
Collective and delayed intervention.

BOUNDARY:
The distinction is strongest for immediate causal leverage and can underdescribe long-term political action.
