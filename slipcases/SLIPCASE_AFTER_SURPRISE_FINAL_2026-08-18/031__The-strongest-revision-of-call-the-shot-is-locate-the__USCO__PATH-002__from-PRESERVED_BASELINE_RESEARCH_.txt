TITLE:
The strongest revision of “call the shot” is “locate the commitment.”

SOURCE:
COLLISION — John Cage, Marcel Duchamp, Sol LeWitt, Philip Galanter, U.S. Copyright Office — evidence distributed across upstream rule design, deliberate relinquishment, selection, and downstream arrangement.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
The sources show that meaningful agency need not take the form of predicting a specific visible result before generation. A maker may instead commit to a rule, a question, an autonomous system, a selection criterion, an arrangement, or a later modification.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
CALL THE SHOT is one species of a more general test:

LOCATE THE COMMITMENT.

LOCAL MOVE:
The parent’s prospective test correctly attacks retrospective intention laundering but privileges one control topology:

TARGET FIRST
→ RESULT SECOND.

The new evidence requires multiple legitimate temporal arrangements.

SOURCE TERMS:
decision
choice
question
system
autonomy
selection
arrangement
execution

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A maker can be causally decisive without being able to predict the particular local result.

What matters may be that some commitment existed BEFORE the evidence it later claims to explain.

QUESTION:
What counts as a genuine commitment rather than a post-hoc story attached after seeing the result?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can a production history distinguish discovery from retroactive intention by timestamping when constraints became binding?

MECHANISM:
at t₀:
agent creates commitment C.

after t₀:
system/world produces event x.

C is evidentially meaningful when:

1. C existed before the relevant outcome was known;
2. C constrained subsequent admissible actions/results;
3. violating C was possible;
4. the path reflects whether C was maintained, revised, or abandoned.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<CALL SPECIFIC OUTCOME>
→ <PRECOMMIT TO CONSTRAINT / RULE / SELECTION CRITERION>
→ [WORLD RESPONDS]
→ <TRACE OF COMPLIANCE, REVISION, OR FAILURE>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

For commitment C at time t:

    Admissible_C(Ω_t) ⊂ Ω_t.

Evidence of control/agency is strengthened when C changes the future branch space before the outcome that supposedly vindicates C appears.

This permits:

SHOT COMMITMENT
RULE COMMITMENT
PROCESS COMMITMENT
FEATURE COMMITMENT
SELECTION-CRITERION COMMITMENT.

TENSION:
Some creative acts genuinely arise through unplanned discovery.

Requiring prior commitment for all authorship would wrongly exclude improvisation, serendipity, and recognition.

MISSING:
A second test for legitimate agency that begins AFTER surprise rather than before it.

Candidate:

RESPONSIBLE RETENTION:
the maker recognizes an unplanned feature and subsequently reorganizes the path around preserving or developing it.

BOUNDARY:
Prospective commitment is powerful evidence against retrospective fabrication.

It is not a necessary condition for creativity.

TEST:
For one complete creative history, construct a ledger with only three event types:

COMMIT:
constraint established before outcome.

DISCOVER:
unplanned difference becomes perceptible.

RETAIN:
later action preserves/develops discovered difference.

Delete every click that is none of the three.

Ask whether the remaining graph explains the artifact better than elapsed time or iteration count.
