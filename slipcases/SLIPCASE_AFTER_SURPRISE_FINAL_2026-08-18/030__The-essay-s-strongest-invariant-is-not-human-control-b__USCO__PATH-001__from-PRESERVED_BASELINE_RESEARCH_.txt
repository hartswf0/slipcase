ZETTEL

ID:
PATH-001

TITLE:
The essay’s strongest invariant is not “human control” but a branch that was perceptible before it was selected.

SOURCE:
COLLISION — Roger Sharpe’s prospective called-shot demonstration; U.S. Copyright Office record for Théâtre D’opéra Spatial; Geertz’s performance-centered account of Moroccan poetry; Burrow-Giles v. Sarony.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Across the cases, mere causal participation is too weak. Stronger evidence appears when an agent can distinguish alternatives before or during production and intervene so that one branch rather than another survives: Sharpe names the shot before taking it; Sarony arranges specific visible variables before exposure; Geertz’s poet receives feedback while composition remains open; Allen’s large iteration count does not itself reveal which expressive differences were prospectively controlled.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
A PATH IS A HISTORY OF BRANCH RESOLUTION.

LOCAL MOVE:
The essay’s aphorism:

A CLASS IS NOT A PATH

can be sharpened into an empirical unit:

THE CONSEQUENTIAL BRANCH.

SOURCE TERMS:
control
selection
arrangement
feedback
revision
trajectory
expression

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Neither LABOR nor ITERATION nor MECHANISM is the decisive variable.

One small intervention can dominate the final path.

A thousand operations can leave it mostly unchanged.

QUESTION:
How can “branch control” be measured independently of labor time, iteration count, and output quality?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does authorship require control of branches, perception of branches, responsibility for branch selection, or some combination of all three?

MECHANISM:
possibility state Ω_t
→ agent perceives distinction d
→ forms prospective preference
→ intervention a_t
→ system response
→ subset Ω_{t+1} survives
→ repeat.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<CLASS OF GENERATING MECHANISM>
→ <LOCAL POSSIBILITY SPACE>

→ <SELECTED TRAJECTORY>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Define a consequential decision at time t when:

1. at least two nearby outcomes are possible;
2. agent can discriminate a relevant difference before final selection;
3. an intervention changes the probability of the preferred branch;
4. the resulting branch persists into the artifact/career under study.

Candidate quantity:

    Δ_control =
    P(target | do(a))
    -
    P(target | baseline).

But CONTROL alone does not equal AUTHORSHIP.

TENSION:
Prospective awareness is too demanding for many accepted creative practices involving accident, improvisation, discovery, and unconscious skill.

A later act of recognition and preservation may itself be the consequential branch.

MISSING:
A theory of RETENTION:

When an accident occurs first and the maker says “keep that,” where does authorship/control enter?

BOUNDARY:
Branch analysis can reveal where agency entered a production history.

It cannot by itself decide normative categories such as art, authorship, merit, or copyright.

Annotate every moment where:
an alternative was visible,
a preference was stated or inferable before later praise,
an intervention occurred,
and the intervention altered the surviving artifact.

Delete all operations that do not satisfy these conditions.

Compare PATH THICKNESS with raw workflow length.
