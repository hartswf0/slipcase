TITLE:
Circulation is a sequence of transformations, not neutral transport.

SOURCE:
User-provided manuscript / conversation synthesis — 2026-08-16.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE]
Compression, ranking, interface design, recommendation, and institutional interpretation can alter a representation at every transfer. Recognizability does not prove the relation that mattered survived.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A representation can remain visibly the same while losing precisely the relation for which it was warranted.

QUESTION:
What must survive a transformation for continuity to be claimed?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can provenance record transformation history without already assuming a contested theory of what deserves preservation?
