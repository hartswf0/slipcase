TITLE: THE MODEL CAN BE INSIDE THE COMMUNITY'S COGNITION WITHOUT BEING A MEMBER OF THE COMMUNITY

SOURCE: Edwin Hutchins — Cognition in the Wild — MIT Press, 1995 — https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262581462/cognition-in-the-wild/

SOURCE URL: https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262581462/cognition-in-the-wild/

PASSAGE: [PARAPHRASE] Hutchins studies cognition as organized across people, artifacts, representational media, and socially coordinated activity rather than locating all cognitive work inside an isolated individual.

RESEARCH OBJECT: [[SCOTT-COP-004]] risks an unhelpful binary: either the model is merely a tool or it is promoted to “collaborator” and quasi-member of the community. Distributed cognition opens a third description. A nonhuman artifact can be constitutive of a cognitive system without needing intention, social membership, authorship parity, or human-like understanding. In Roberts’s Midjourney riffing, the model’s outputs can materially redirect what human participants notice and propose next while the social community remains human.

LOCAL MOVE: Replace the anthropomorphic question “is the AI a member?” with the operational question “what cognitive transformations occur through the artifact, and which parts of the distributed task cannot be reconstructed from any one human alone?”

SOURCE TERMS: distributed cognition; cognitive system; socially distributed cognition; material media; representational structure; coordination

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: [OUR INFERENCE] The model can be epistemically inside and socially outside. It may participate in the causal organization of collective imagination without possessing the obligations, recognition, identity trajectory, or accountability through which humans belong to a community of practice.

QUESTION: Which properties of early Midjourney collaboration are best explained by treating the generator as part of the distributed cognitive system rather than as either inert instrument or social collaborator?

DEEPER QUESTION: What gets lost when “collaboration” collapses causal participation, cognitive contribution, social membership, authorship, and responsibility into one word?

MECHANISM: Humans formulate partial descriptions → model transforms them into perceivable artifacts → artifacts externalize possibilities no participant had fully represented beforehand → several humans inspect the same result → attention and next moves are coordinated around the artifact → new prompts arise → distributed state changes across people and machine-mediated representations.

FORMAL SHIFT: FROM: HUMAN COMMUNITY + EXTERNAL TOOL. OR: HUMAN + AI COLLABORATORS. TO: HUMAN COMMUNITY WHOSE PRACTICE IS PARTLY REALIZED THROUGH A NONHUMAN REPRESENTATIONAL TRANSFORMATION SYSTEM.

SOURCE FORMALISM: Hutchins analyzes cognitive processes distributed across members of a social group and coordinated with material/representational structures. He does not discuss generative AI or communities of practice in these terms.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] H1,H2,H3 + GENERATOR G + SHARED ARTIFACT A_t → DISTRIBUTED STATE C_t; H_i PROMPTS G → A_t+1 → HUMAN ATTENTION/INTERPRETATION CHANGES → C_t+1. MEMBERSHIP(G)=UNRESOLVED/NOT REQUIRED FOR COGNITIVE PARTICIPATION.

TENSION: Distributed-cognition language can itself overstate what the model contributes if the crucial organization lies in human interpretation and selection. A stochastic image transformation that humans use productively is causally important without necessarily warranting a strong cognitive attribution.

MISSING: Fine-grained interaction records showing which ideas were present before a generation, which arose only after inspecting it, and how those ideas propagated among people; comparison with a non-generative randomization tool to isolate what is specific to learned generative transformation.

BOUNDARY: Hutchins’s theory does not license claims that generative models understand, intend, belong, or deserve authorship. This card uses distributed cognition to separate system participation from social membership.

CITATION TRAIL: [[SCOTT-COP-004]] → machine participates in description of community using machine → anthropomorphic collaborator/tool binary → Hutchins permits cognitive distribution across people and artifacts → next edge: reconstruct a single collaborative Midjourney episode at move-level resolution and locate where novel representational state enters the human group

TEST: Record a three-person generative-improvisation session with all prompts, outputs, chat, and think-aloud commentary. Before each generation, have participants privately record predicted/desired next directions. After the output, record newly noticed possibilities. Code each subsequent proposal as PREEXISTING, HUMAN-TO-HUMAN TRANSMITTED, OUTPUT-EVOKED, or UNRESOLVED. Compare against an otherwise matched session using shuffled human-made images as provocations.
