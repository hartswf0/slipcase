TITLE: EIGHT MONTHS SEPARATE THE EXQUISITE-CORPSE GAME FROM THE UNIVERSITY TALK

SOURCE: Scott Roberts — Midjourney exquisite-corpse post, August 31, 2022 — https://www.instagram.com/p/Ch6jYdhOw0j/ ; DePaul University Center for Teaching and Learning — 28th Annual Teaching and Learning Conference, May 5, 2023 — https://resources.depaul.edu/teaching-commons/events/teaching-learning-conference/Pages/28th-Annual-Teaching-and-Learning.aspx ; DePaul University — Scott Roberts faculty biography — https://www.depaul.edu/faculty/scott-roberts

PASSAGE: [PARAPHRASE] Roberts publicly described an ongoing Midjourney exquisite-corpse game on August 31, 2022. On May 5, 2023, Roberts, Andres Fiz, and Max Hammer presented “The Cyborg Muse” at DePaul’s Teaching and Learning Conference. DePaul states that Roberts created a generative-AI art course for animation, film, and theater in 2023.

RESEARCH OBJECT: The chronology is unusually compressed. Public evidence places Roberts inside playful collaborative Midjourney experimentation in August 2022 and inside formal university circulation of lessons from an experimental generative-AI course by May 2023. Whether or not one directly caused the other, informal practice, classroom research, and institutional pedagogy occupy a window of roughly eight months. The community did not have to stabilize before it began becoming curriculum.

LOCAL MOVE: Follow the parent from a private/social Midjourney community into Roberts’s subsequent educational practice. Instead of treating “community of practice” as one bounded Discord community, trace the migration of practice across social sites: artist collaboration → classroom research team → university teaching conference.

SOURCE TERMS: ongoing game; experimental course; student research collaborators; Teaching and Learning Conference; share knowledge; teaching practices; generative AI art tools

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: [OUR INFERENCE] Traditional curriculum often follows stabilization: practice develops, experts consolidate it, textbooks encode it, then students learn it. Here teaching appears while the underlying tools, norms, terminology, and workflows are still being invented. Pedagogy becomes one of the places stabilization happens rather than the downstream recipient of stabilized knowledge.

QUESTION: What gets taught when the thing being taught does not yet possess a stable canon?

DEEPER QUESTION: Can curriculum function as an instrument for making a community of practice rather than merely transmitting one?

MECHANISM: Emerging tool produces informal practitioner experiments → techniques circulate socially → educator imports unresolved practices into experimental course → students become additional experimenters → provisional findings are demonstrated and named → university conference circulates those findings to another professional community → practices acquire institutional legitimacy while remaining mutable.

FORMAL SHIFT: FROM: PRACTICE → MATURITY → CANON → CURRICULUM. TO: PRACTICE ↔ EXPERIMENTAL CURRICULUM ↔ COMMUNITY DISCOURSE, ALL DEVELOPING CONCURRENTLY.

SOURCE FORMALISM: DePaul’s 2023 conference page states that the annual event brings roughly 300 members of the university community together to “share knowledge” and promote teaching practices. “The Cyborg Muse” was one of its concurrent offerings. Roberts’s faculty biography states that he created a course in 2023 devoted to exploring generative AI art tools for animation, film, and theater.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] AUG_2022 SOCIAL PLAY → ? → 2023 EXPERIMENTAL COURSE → MAY_2023 FACULTY PRESENTATION → WIDER PEDAGOGICAL REPERTOIRE; “?” IS THE GENEALOGY TO RECOVER, NOT ASSUME.

TENSION: The temporal sequence is real; the causal chain remains unproven. Roberts could have independently developed the course without translating techniques from the Midjourney exquisite-corpse community. The interesting research object is therefore the missing middle rather than a claimed lineage.

MISSING: Course proposal date; syllabus versions; assignments; correspondence about creating the course; early lesson plans; which tools were introduced when; whether Yelmo, exquisite corpse, communal prompting, remixing, character consistency, or other 2022 practices explicitly entered the curriculum.

BOUNDARY: Chronological proximity does not prove transmission. This ZETTEL establishes the compressed timeline and proposes a genealogy to test.

CITATION TRAIL: [[PARENT-ZETTEL-ID]] → August 2022 collaborative Midjourney play → 2023 experimental generative-AI course → May 5, 2023 Cyborg Muse presentation to DePaul teaching community → missing middle becomes research object → next edge: recover syllabus/version history and identify actual migrations of practices, words, examples, and people

TEST: Build a dated corpus of Roberts materials from June 2022 through December 2023: Instagram, Midjourney posts, Discord/Office Hours references, syllabi, assignment sheets, student projects, conference slides, and interviews. For each distinctive practice—EXQUISITE CORPSE, RIFFING, CHARACTER CONSISTENCY, REMIX, TOOL COMBINATION, COLLABORATIVE DISCOVERY—record earliest occurrence in SOCIAL PRACTICE and earliest occurrence in COURSE/PEDAGOGY. Require textual or artifact-level continuity before claiming transmission.
