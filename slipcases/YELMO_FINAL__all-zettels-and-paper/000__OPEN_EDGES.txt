OPEN EDGES — ORIGINAL YELMO PROJECT

1. RECOVER THE PROPOSAL BEFORE YELMO.
   Was there a literal Campbell / hero's-journey sequence? Recover stage list, prompt plan, messages, or earliest proposal language.

2. IDENTIFY SCOTT.
   Confirm or falsify transcript Scott = Scott Roberts from original records or Roberts himself.

3. RECOVER THE FIRST YELMO.
   Who typed it, who named it, and what output first became canonical enough that later images could be called mutations of “the same dude”?

4. RECOVER THE HUMAN IDENTITY CRITERION.
   What did Roberts and collaborators permit to vary? Which changes killed identity? Was there a model-sheet logic, an attitude/performance logic, or a looser social judgment?

5. FIND THE CONTINUITY APPARATUS.
   Seeds, prompt templates, image prompts, remix chains, Photoshop repair, reference boards, canonical generations, model sheets, naming conventions.

6. FIND THE MULTI-CHARACTER FAILURES.
   The notes say additional characters altered one another. Recover images and prompts. This may be the clearest practical evidence that story required stable entity boundaries, not only a recurring face.

7. RECONSTRUCT THE CONTROL / SURPRISE TENSION.
   Who wanted more intentionality? Who valued being led by Midjourney? Were these different participants or simultaneous demands held by the same artist?

8. TEST THE SURREALIST GENEALOGY.
   Did Roberts consciously bring exquisite corpse or other improvisational methods into Yelmo, or is that a later resemblance? Recover pre-2022 exercises and the full Surrealist Storytelling chapter.

9. TRACE YELMO INTO PEDAGOGY — OR KILL THE LINK.
   Did character consistency, Yelmo, collaborative prompting, or “game not tool” enter the 2023 DePaul course?

10. RERUN THE ORIGINAL QUESTION.
   Persistence / transformation / re-entry / multiplicity / interaction / improvisation across model generations. Keep the original human criterion rather than substituting contemporary benchmarks.
