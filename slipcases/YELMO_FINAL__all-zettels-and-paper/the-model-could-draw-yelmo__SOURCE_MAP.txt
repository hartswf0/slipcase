THE MODEL COULD DRAW YELMO. IT COULD NOT KEEP HIM.
SOURCE MAP — Scott-facing revision — 2026-08-17

STATUS
Working paper. Connective claims below marked INTERPRETATION are arguments of this paper, not source facts.
The Yelmo archive identifies a participant named Scott as an animator; the researcher identifies him as Scott Roberts. No public source located for this revision explicitly connects Roberts by surname to Yelmo. That bridge remains UNVERIFIED and is stated as such in the manuscript.

TECHNICAL DESCENDANTS / COMPARATORS
T1 — Gal et al. 2022, Textual Inversion. https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.01618
Supports: learning a reusable representation of a user-provided concept from a small image set and composing it into new scenes.

T2 — Chen et al. 2023, DisenBooth. https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.03374
Supports: explicit distinction between identity-relevant subject information and identity-irrelevant variation such as pose/background.

T3 — Tewel et al. 2024, ConsiStory. https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03286
Supports: consistent subjects across diverse prompts through shared generation-time information without per-subject optimization.

T4 — He et al. 2024, IR-Diffusion. https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.19261
Supports: multi-subject interference/fusion in diffusion attention and an isolation intervention to reduce identity contamination.

T5 — Singh et al. 2025, StoryBooth. https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.05800
Supports: multi-subject consistency as a visual-storytelling problem; reports leakage/interference among characters.

MAJOR CONNECTIVE CLAIMS
C1 — “A model can succeed at depiction while failing at reference.” INTERPRETATION grounded in Y1-Y3.
C2 — “Yelmo asked for identity-preserving transformation.” INTERPRETATION grounded in the Campbell-story proposal (Y1/Y4) plus the observed continuity failures (Y2), sharpened by T1-T3.
C3 — “The community was the rig.” INTERPRETATION grounded in Y1-Y4: persistence/history were maintained by participants across otherwise independent generations. This is not a claim that participants themselves used the word rig.
C4 — “A story requires entity boundaries that survive relation.” INTERPRETATION grounded in Y3 and sharpened by T4-T5.
C5 — “The instability that made Midjourney weak as a production tool also made it generative as an improvisational social game.” INTERPRETATION grounded in Y2-Y4 and Roberts’s exquisite-corpse evidence R4-R5.
C6 — Roberts’s earlier artistic practice may make Yelmo legible as an older problem entering a new medium. HYPOTHESIS grounded in R1-R6. No direct causal genealogy is claimed.
C7 — “Yelmo was an informal benchmark before the product had a benchmark for the thing that mattered.” INTERPRETATION comparing Y1-Y3 with M1-M2. No causal influence claimed.
C8 — The proposed rerun (persistence / transformation / re-entry / multiplicity / interaction / improvisation) is a COMPILER-DERIVED experimental program, not present as such in any source.
