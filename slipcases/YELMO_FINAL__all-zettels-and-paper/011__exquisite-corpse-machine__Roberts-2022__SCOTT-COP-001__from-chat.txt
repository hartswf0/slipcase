TITLE: THE MIDJOURNEY COMMUNITY DID NOT JUST SHARE PROMPTS; IT TURNED THE MODEL INTO AN EXQUISITE-CORPSE MACHINE

SOURCE: Scott Roberts — Instagram post, August 31, 2022 — https://www.instagram.com/p/Ch6jYdhOw0j/ ; @onetoomanyoptions — Instagram post, August 16, 2022 — https://www.instagram.com/p/ChWLLqvuOXe/

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] Roberts: “an on-going exquisite corpse game I'm playing with @onetoomanyoptions.” [QUOTE] @onetoomanyoptions: “an exquisite corpse collaboration with @mrscottroberts through #MidJourney”

RESEARCH OBJECT: Scott Roberts’s own August 2022 posts identify a concrete social protocol for early Midjourney use: the “exquisite corpse.” This changes the interpretation of the parent transcript’s descriptions of people riffing on one another’s generations. The practice was not merely several individuals independently learning prompt craft in the same Discord. At least one Roberts collaboration explicitly imported a named artistic game in which creation proceeds through partial inheritance, response, surprise, and continuation. The generative model occupied the middle of that exchange.

LOCAL MOVE: Follow the parent transcript’s description of prompting as “riff[ing] off each other,” almost like a musical experience, rather than treating it only as individual prompt optimization. The transcript describes one person proposing a Muppet, another extending it to space, and another producing a “muppet space pirate.” Roberts’s own public record independently documents an “on-going exquisite corpse game” conducted through Midjourney. The match is structural; it does not by itself prove that the Instagram collaboration and the transcript episode are the identical event.

SOURCE TERMS: exquisite corpse; game; collaboration; MidJourney; on-going; work

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: [OUR INFERENCE] The prompt may not be the fundamental unit of early generative practice. In an exquisite-corpse workflow, the operative unit is a TURN: receive an artifact or provocation, transform it through the model, hand the result onward, and let another participant continue. What persists is not a prompt but a lineage of mutations.

QUESTION: What exactly was passed between Roberts and his collaborators during these Midjourney exquisite-corpse games: prompts, images, concepts, partial descriptions, aesthetic constraints, or some combination?

DEEPER QUESTION: If each participant responds to an artifact partially produced by both another human and an opaque model, whose move is the next participant actually answering?

MECHANISM: [OUR INFERENCE] PARTICIPANT_A establishes provisional material → Midjourney transforms it → PARTICIPANT_B inherits some visible residue rather than a complete plan → PARTICIPANT_B introduces another instruction or variation → Midjourney transforms again → continuity emerges retrospectively through the sequence.

FORMAL SHIFT: FROM: COMMUNITY OF PRACTICE = PEOPLE EXCHANGE TIPS ABOUT HOW TO OPERATE A TOOL. TO: COMMUNITY OF PRACTICE = PEOPLE PASS PARTIALLY MACHINE-MADE OBJECTS THROUGH A SOCIAL GAME WHOSE SUCCESS DEPENDS ON MUTATION.

SOURCE FORMALISM: NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] HUMAN_A → MODEL → ARTIFACT_1 → HUMAN_B → MODEL → ARTIFACT_2 → HUMAN_A/B/... ; WHERE EACH ARTIFACT IS BOTH OUTPUT AND NEXT PLAYER’S INPUT

TENSION: “Exquisite corpse” could be merely a playful label for exchanging generated images rather than evidence of a rigorously followed Surrealist protocol. Conversely, if the rules actually constrained what each participant was permitted to see or change, then the social architecture of the game—not just Midjourney—helped generate the surprise being attributed to AI.

MISSING: The complete August 2022 sequence; turn order; original prompts; whether participants saw previous prompts or only images; whether outputs were remixed directly; rules stated between participants; Discord or message history explaining the game.

BOUNDARY: These Instagram posts establish that Roberts publicly participated in a Midjourney exquisite-corpse collaboration in August 2022. They do NOT establish that this collaboration was Yelmo, that @onetoomanyoptions was part of the Yelmo group, or that the transcript’s unnamed “Scott” has been independently proven from these posts alone to be Roberts.
