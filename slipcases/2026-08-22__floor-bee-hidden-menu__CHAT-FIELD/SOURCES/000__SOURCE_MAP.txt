- “A blank box does not promise that anything can happen. It promises that almost anything can be addressed.”
- “The obligation that follows is not accommodation but correspondence.”
- Generative mystery as uncertainty over the genealogy of a revelation.
- Probing agency as the ability to invent interventions that disclose a hidden action boundary.
