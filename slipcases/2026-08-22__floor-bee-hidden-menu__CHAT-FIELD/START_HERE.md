## Central wager

The floor and the bee are the same test. A 360-degree film gives a viewer freedom to look without implying that looking changes plot. A free-text narrative system gives a participant freedom to speak without implying that speech changes canon. In both cases the useful question is not how much freedom exists, but what kind of consequence the invited action can produce.

The blank box therefore hides a menu. It expands **address** before it reveals **alteration**. The empirical method is to perturb the encounter and **follow the dent**.
