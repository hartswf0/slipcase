RECOVERY BOUNDARY

Packaging date: 2026-08-22

At packaging time the working container exposed 56 raw mounted files from the active conversation. The full local package preserves those files and explodes five major ZIP checkpoints, producing a 207-file preservation field.

This repository checkpoint does not claim to be a native ChatGPT transcript export. It does not include hidden reasoning or messages unavailable to the active conversation. Some earlier in-chat zettels existed only as visible chat text and were never surfaced as standalone mounted files. Those are represented through later consolidated descendants and reconstruction rather than fabricated as byte-exact originals.

The repository transport ZIP contains the inspectable text field: paper sources, zettels, prompts, transcript material, bibliography, and provenance. The originating conversation also produced a larger binary-rich preservation ZIP containing recovered PDFs, images, render appearances, and historical ZIPs.
