## Core trajectory

1. The research began from the analogy between framed film and 360-degree film, and between menu-driven dialogue and free-text chat. The analogy was revised away from scalar “interactivity” toward delegated selection: 360 transfers view-selection to the viewer; free text transfers utterance-selection to the participant while operational boundaries remain hidden.
2. *Clouds Over Sidra* supplied the **Floor Test**: if a medium gives the participant an action, what kind of consequence is that action for? The revision rejected the crude claim that looking must alter plot. Looking may instead produce spatial witnessing, context, presence, self-directed attention, and attention uncertainty.
3. The original FOMO claim was weakened: freedom to look creates attention uncertainty; FOMO additionally depends on perceived importance, irrecoverability, and a belief that there is a correct view.
4. *Wolves in the Walls* supplied the counterexample to “meaningful consequence = branching.” Lucy can recognize and adapt to the participant while the larger narrative proceeds. Relational response can matter without plot-topology change.
5. The **Bee-Sting Test** became the conversational equivalent of looking at the floor. “You get stung by a bee” is a plausible, unnecessary intervention used to distinguish surface reply from persistent world-state integration.
6. *Façade* supplied an architectural precedent: open natural-language surface input can collapse into a smaller operational vocabulary and can be locally absorbed into a larger authored progression.
7. The FIGURES / Cass encounter supplied concrete boundary cases: identity correction is acknowledged but Maya returns; a pregnancy proposition is understood but not admitted to canon; an unreadable annotation remains unknown; a complaint about not finding what is missing appears to alter access to story evidence; Cass’s claims about her own memory cannot be treated as telemetry.
8. The resulting distinction became **address versus alteration**. A blank box expands what can be addressed before it reveals what can be changed.
9. The working metaphor became **the hidden menu**: the menu has not disappeared, it has moved behind the conversation.
10. The empirical seed became **follow the dent**: introduce a plausible intervention, stop reinforcing it, and trace where and how long its consequences persist across reply, relation, memory, knowledge, world state, disclosure, and trajectory.
11. The mystery-specific pressure became **generative mystery**: the participant sees a revelation but may not know whether it was retrieved, selected, inferred, generated, or produced through a hybrid process. For mystery, UNKNOWN must be a positive state; otherwise inquiry can manufacture its own evidence.

## Principal formulations preserved by this lineage
