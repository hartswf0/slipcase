TITLE: A century-old discipline already reads revision as evidence and named the object the corpus needs: the avant-texte

SOURCE: Critique génétique / genetic criticism; Jean Bellemin-Noël, Le Texte et l'avant-texte (1972), for the term avant-texte; Louis Hay and the Institut des Textes et Manuscrits Modernes tradition [UNVERIFIED page references]

PASSAGE: [PARAPHRASE] Genetic criticism takes as its object not the finished work but the dossier of drafts, notes and corrections that precede it, and treats that dossier — the avant-texte — as a structured record of composition rather than as discarded waste. [QUOTE] dse.json: "{ <inward_pressure>, ... <public_criterion>, <correction>, <ethical_consequence> }"

RESEARCH OBJECT: A named, methodologically developed object for exactly the evidence the struggle account needs and cannot locate. The avant-texte is inscribed, public, plural-readable, and about the process rather than the product. It satisfies the hermeneutic requirement that meaning live in the trace while carrying information about answerability.

LOCAL MOVE: This child imports a discipline rather than inventing a method. The corpus's problem — struggle is real but unobservable — was solved for literary manuscripts fifty years ago by changing the object of study.

SOURCE TERMS: avant-texte / dossier / genetic criticism / critique génétique / campaign of revision / variant

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: Genetic criticism reached a conclusion the corpus would find uncomfortable: the avant-texte does not reveal intention. It reveals *operations* — substitutions, expansions, deletions, reorderings. The discipline explicitly declines to read drafts as windows onto the mind, because variants underdetermine motives. So the draft rescues the struggle as a *process* and confirms the eviction of intention rather than reversing it.

QUESTION: Can the variant typology of genetic criticism be applied to machine-generated revision chains, and does it discriminate them from human ones?

DEEPER QUESTION: If drafts show operations and not motives, then "descriptive struggle" must be redefined as a distribution over operation types rather than as an inward condition — which keeps the concept and abandons its moral vocabulary.

MECHANISM: <COMPOSITION> -> [SUCCESSIVE STATES INSCRIBED] -> <DOSSIER> -> [CLASSIFY VARIANTS BY OPERATION TYPE] -> <PROCESS RECONSTRUCTED WITHOUT INTENTION ATTRIBUTED>

FORMAL SHIFT: <FINISHED TEXT> -> <DOSSIER OF STATES> -> [VARIANT CLASSIFICATION] -> <COMPOSITION AS AN OPERATION PROFILE>

SOURCE FORMALISM: The avant-texte as an object; variant classification as the working procedure. Specific typologies vary by school and are not quoted here.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] profile(dossier) = distribution over {substitution, expansion, deletion, reordering, reversal}. Then the struggle claim becomes comparative: do human and machine revision chains differ in profile, and specifically in the share of *reversals* — changes that undo an earlier commitment?

TENSION: READING A: importing genetic criticism gives the struggle account a rigorous evidentiary base. READING B: genetic criticism studies dossiers preserved by accident of archive practice, and machine revision chains are complete by default — so the two objects are not comparable, and the discipline's methods assume scarcity that no longer holds.

MISSING: Verified primary references. Any application of variant typology to machine output. Any statement of what a *complete* dossier does to a method built for partial ones.

BOUNDARY: This establishes that a method exists for reading revision. It does not establish that revision evidences the inward conditions dse names — genetic criticism explicitly denies the analogous claim for literary drafts.

CITATION TRAIL: [[FORAGE-PT-003]] and [[FORAGE-PT-023]] -> genetic criticism / Bellemin-Noël 1972 -> the avant-texte -> next: the discipline's own arguments against intentionalist reading of variants, which pressure the parent's moral framing.

TEST: Take ten human dossiers and ten machine revision chains on matched tasks. Classify every variant. Compare profiles. If the reversal share differs sharply, there is a public signature and it is countable; if profiles match, the struggle claim loses its last observable.
