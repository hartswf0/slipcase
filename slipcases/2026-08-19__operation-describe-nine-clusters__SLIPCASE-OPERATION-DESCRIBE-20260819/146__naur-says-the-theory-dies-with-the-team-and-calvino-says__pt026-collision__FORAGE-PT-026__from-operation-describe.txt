ZETTEL

ID: FORAGE-PT-026

TITLE: Naur says the theory dies with the team and Calvino says the inventory and rules persist, which makes constraint the transferable part of an untransferable thing

SOURCE: Collision of PROGRAMS/theory.json (program theory as living possession, text as residue) with PROGRAMS/haunted.json (narrative from a finite inventory under combination rules)

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] theory.json: "<program_text> is a residue. <documentation> is a residue. <method> is a tool. <AI_code> is a candidate artifact. <program_theory> is the living possession" [QUOTE] haunted.json: "AXIOM_02 := <narrative> [emerges_from] <finite_symbolic_inventory> [under] <rules_of_combination>" [QUOTE] haunted.json: "AXIOM_03 := <constraint> [does_not_merely_limit] <generation> <constraint> [enables] <generation> <constraint> [excludes] <generation>"

RESEARCH OBJECT: A candidate solution to the transfer problem, assembled from two instruments that do not cite each other. Naur's pessimism is about *understanding* — the mapping to the world that lets a team modify without breaking. Calvino's optimism is about *generativity* — an inventory plus rules that produces new work in new hands. Constraint is writable, checkable, and enforceable. Understanding is not.

LOCAL MOVE: Rather than resolving the pessimism, this zettel asks what *kind* of thing survives handover, and answers: not the theory, but the constraint set that made the theory's outputs coherent.

SOURCE TERMS: living possession / residue / candidate artifact / finite symbolic inventory / rules of combination / enables / excludes

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: Every serious worldbuilding practice already relies on this without saying so. A world bible is not a theory of the world; it is an inventory plus combination rules. Handed to a new writer it produces coherent new material — which is precisely the transfer Naur says is impossible. So either those practices are producing incoherence nobody notices, or constraint transfers understanding's *effects* without transferring understanding.

QUESTION: Can a stranger given only the constraint set — no explanations, no rationale — produce modifications the original holder judges coherent, at a higher rate than a stranger given prose documentation of the same system?

DEEPER QUESTION: If constraints beat explanations, then documentation has been optimising the wrong target for decades. The goal would not be to convey understanding but to specify the admissible region — and the best document would be a list of what is forbidden.

MECHANISM: <HOLDER'S THEORY> -> [EXPRESSED AS INVENTORY + COMBINATION RULES] -> <CONSTRAINT SET> -> [STRANGER GENERATES WITHIN IT] -> outputs constrained to the admissible region -> <COHERENT WITHOUT UNDERSTANDING>

FORMAL SHIFT: <UNTRANSFERABLE THEORY> -> <TRANSFERABLE CONSTRAINT SET> -> [GENERATION BY A STRANGER] -> <COHERENCE WITHOUT POSSESSION>

SOURCE FORMALISM: theory's four-tier ranking; haunted's three axioms.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Two handover conditions on the same system: PROSE = explanatory documentation; CONSTRAINT = inventory, rules, and prohibitions with no rationale. Same modification task, blind coherence scoring by the holder. Compare rates.

TENSION: READING A: constraint transfers coherence but not understanding — the stranger produces admissible work and cannot handle the case the rules do not cover, which is Naur's point exactly. READING B: constraint plus enough coverage *is* understanding, operationally, and Naur's living possession is a romantic residue.

The discriminating case is the unforeseen one: give the stranger a task the constraint set does not cover and see whether they extend it sensibly. That is the same indefinite-field problem as concept possession, arriving here from software practice.

MISSING: Any comparison of prose against constraint as handover media. Both instruments assume their own medium and never race them.

BOUNDARY: Naur writes about programs maintained over years; Calvino about literary production. Neither addresses handover of a constraint set, which is our construction from the pair.

CITATION TRAIL: Naur, "Programming as Theory Building" (1985). Calvino, "Cybernetics and Ghosts." Propp's morphology as an inventory with rules. World-bible practice in film and games. [[FORAGE-PT-016]] [[FORAGE-PT-017]] [[FORAGE-PT-001]]

TEST: Two strangers, one system. One gets prose documentation, one gets only the constraint set. Same modification task. Holder scores coherence blind. Then the crucial second round: a task neither medium covers. The first round measures transfer; the second measures whether anything was understood.
