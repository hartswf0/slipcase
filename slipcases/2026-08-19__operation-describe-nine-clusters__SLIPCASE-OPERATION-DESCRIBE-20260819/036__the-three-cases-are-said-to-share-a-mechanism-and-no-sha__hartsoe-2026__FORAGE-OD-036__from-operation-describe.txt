TITLE:
THE THREE CASES ARE SAID TO SHARE A MECHANISM AND NO SHARED QUANTITY IS EVER MEASURED IN MORE THAN ONE OF THEM

SOURCE:
Watson Hartsoe — PAPERS/operation-describe-label-00.md §11 "Comparison Questions" and PAPERS/operation-describe-label-01.md §11–12 — 2026

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
label-00 §11:
"Why these three cases? What do GitHub labels, toxicity labels, and LLM tool descriptions share? ... Are they comparable in scale, stakes, and evidence?"

[QUOTE]
label-01 §12:
"This is a single dissertation focused on a shared mechanism."

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The comparison questions are asked and the answers document replies with a *register* ("labor, speech, execution") rather than a *quantity*.

A shared mechanism claim requires at least one quantity measured the same way in at least two cases. The archive proposes none.

LOCAL MOVE:
label-01 §11 offers a rhetorically strong triad — GitHub routes labor, moderation routes speech, schemas route execution. It is a good sentence and it is a taxonomy of domains, not evidence of a common mechanism.

SOURCE TERMS:
shared mechanism
comparable in scale, stakes, and evidence
labor / speech / execution
register of contemporary systems
routing analysis

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The archive's due-diligence document asks exactly the right question — "are they comparable?" — and its answers document changes the subject to why they are interesting.

That substitution is the single most likely point of failure at a defense, and it is documented in the archive's own files.

QUESTION:
What is the one quantity that could be measured identically in a repository, a moderation queue, and a tool schema?

DEEPER QUESTION:
If no such quantity exists, is the dissertation three papers that share a vocabulary rather than one that shares a mechanism — and would saying so plainly make it stronger?

MECHANISM:
Claim of shared mechanism requires:
<QUANTITY q DEFINED SUBSTRATE-INDEPENDENTLY>
→ measured in case 1
→ measured in case 2
→ [COMPARISON]
→ <SHARED MECHANISM SUPPORTED OR REFUTED>

Currently the archive has different quantities per case: invocation rate (schemas), resolution time (labels), queue placement (moderation). None is the same measurement.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<THREE DOMAINS>
→ <THREE DIFFERENT OUTCOME VARIABLES>
→ [NO COMMON QUANTITY]
→ <SHARED MECHANISM UNTESTED>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE. The archive's formal apparatus is substrate-independent in notation (O, D, Act) and has never been instantiated in more than one case.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Four candidate cross-case quantities, in increasing order of difficulty:

  1. λ, the half-life of a description
     turns / frames / days — same estimator, three units (FORAGE-OD-019)
  2. h, hysteresis after removal
     does the route revert when the description is withdrawn (FORAGE-OD-028)
  3. the position/semantics variance share
     how much of the route is layout versus meaning (FORAGE-OD-009)
  4. the legibility complement
     ΔG as a function of how illegible the name is to the operator (FORAGE-OD-005)

Each is defined without reference to substrate. Each is measurable in at least two of the three cases. Any one of them, measured twice, converts the comparability claim from assertion to evidence.

The strongest candidate is λ, because it needs no counterfactual, no consent, and no platform cooperation — only time series.

TENSION:
READING A: comparative media studies routinely compares cases without a shared metric; demanding one imports a social-science standard the field does not use.
READING B: the archive itself claims a *mechanism*, supplies a formal model, and demands falsification metrics in five documents. Having chosen that register, it owes a shared quantity.

MISSING:
Any instance in the archive of the same measurement performed in two cases. Thirty-six papers, and no cross-case table.

BOUNDARY:
This is a claim about evidential structure. The three cases may well share a mechanism; the point is that nothing currently in the archive could show it.

CITATION TRAIL:
PAPERS/section-comparison-bins.md — an existing comparison document that compares *drafts* rather than cases, which is a revealing displacement.
Mieke Bal, Travelling Concepts, on what transfers between domains and what does not.
FORAGE-OD-019, FORAGE-OD-028, FORAGE-OD-009, FORAGE-OD-005.

TEST:
Build the cross-case table with one row per case and one column: λ.

Estimate λ in turns for a system prompt, in days for a repository label. Two cells. If the two λ values are within an order of magnitude of each other after normalization by operator cycle time, the shared-mechanism claim has its first evidence. If they differ by five orders, the archive has learned something more valuable than agreement.
