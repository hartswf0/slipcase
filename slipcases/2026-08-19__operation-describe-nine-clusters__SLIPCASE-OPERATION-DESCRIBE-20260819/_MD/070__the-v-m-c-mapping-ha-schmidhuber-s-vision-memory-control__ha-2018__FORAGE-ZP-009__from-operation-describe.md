TITLE: The V/M/C mapping: Ha & Schmidhuber's Vision/Memory/Controller architecture read onto Homer's shield

SOURCE: Z-Port/final-draft-v2.md §3 (identical in v3, final-assembled §3, watson-sections-final.md); Z-Port/Ekphrasis, AI, and World Models.md ("Homer's Shield of Achilles: The First World-Specification" — origin of the mapping, with technical detail); Z-Port/section-comparison.md (provenance: "E's strongest unique contribution")

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] "Crucially, Ha and Schmidhuber's architecture comprises three core components: a Vision model that compresses high-dimensional observations into a compact latent vector, a Memory model that predicts future states as probability distributions, and a Controller that maps the current vision and memory states to action. Homer's shield anticipates this tripartite structure. The visual descriptions of the metalwork parallel the Vision model, providing the sensory surface. The procedural algorithms of the harvest, the battle, and the trial function as the Memory model, predicting how the environment changes over time. The warrior who carries the shield, or the audience who visualizes it, functions as the Controller, learning to navigate the complexities of mortal life by exploring the world generated by the artifact." (final-draft-v2.md §3) [QUOTE] "A world model is not a picture of the world. It is a predictive engine for the world — a learned model of how states succeed one another in time, how actions produce consequences, and how the environment responds to intervention." (final-draft-v2.md §3)

RESEARCH OBJECT: The corpus's single most original technical-humanistic bridge (so identified by section-comparison.md: "the paper's most original technical contribution... it only appears in E"): a component-by-component mapping of Ha & Schmidhuber 2018 (V = VAE compressing observations to latent vector z; M = MDN-RNN outputting probability distributions over future states; C = linear controller trained by CMA-ES, per the E file) onto three registers of the Homeric ekphrasis (metalwork surfaces / procedural scenes / warrior-audience).

LOCAL MOVE: Convert a suggestive analogy (shield ≈ world model) into an articulated homology with three matched joints, so the claim gains apparent precision — and simultaneously acquires three separate points where it can fail.

SOURCE TERMS: Vision model; Memory model; Controller; compressed spatiotemporal representation; predictive engine; internal simulation ("hallucinated dream" in the research report's gloss of Ha & Schmidhuber); tripartite structure; anticipates

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: The audience of an oral poem is recast as a Controller — reception becomes policy learning: to hear the shield described is to be trained on a simulated environment "for navigating mortal life." Literary experience is redescribed as offline reinforcement learning without the drafts flagging how radical that identification is.

QUESTION: Does the mapping's middle joint hold — are the harvest, trial, and battle scenes really "procedural algorithms" that predict state transitions, or are they narrative vignettes onto which prediction is projected?

DEEPER QUESTION: The word "anticipates" carries the argument: is this a homology (same functional organization discovered twice), an analogy (structural resemblance), or a retrojection (the ML architecture used as a reading grid that would fit any sufficiently rich artifact)? What evidence could distinguish these?

MECHANISM: Functional decomposition transfer: identify the target system's decomposition (V/M/C), locate candidate counterparts in the source artifact, and assert correspondence at the level of function (sensing / predicting / acting) rather than substrate.

FORMAL SHIFT: The shield analogy moves from holistic ("a miniature world model") to compositional (three components with distinct jobs) — the only place in the corpus where the Homer/AI comparison has internal articulation rather than global resemblance.

SOURCE FORMALISM: Ha & Schmidhuber 2018: V = VAE, z_t latent; M = MDN-RNN modeling P(z_{t+1} | a_t, z_t, h_t) as mixture of Gaussians; C = single-layer linear policy a_t = W_c [z_t h_t] + b_c, trained via CMA-ES (technical detail preserved in the E research file).

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Mapping μ: {metalwork description ↦ V, procedural scenes ↦ M, warrior/audience ↦ C}. Adequacy conditions: (i) each Homeric register performs the matched function; (ii) the registers interact as V→M→C do (surface feeds prediction feeds action). The drafts argue (i) per component; (ii) is nowhere argued — the shield's scenes do not feed the warrior's actions in any stated loop.

TENSION: READING A (E file, v2–final): the shield "anticipates this exact tripartite architecture" — strong structural precedence. READING B (deep-research-report): "the shield is a verbal artifact that organizes a total environment for imagination"; the relation is "analogical, not genealogically continuous," and the literal claim "risks collapsing" the ancient into the technical. The guardian-grade report scores the mapping as the strongest technical element (E: C3=85) while the research report is the same corpus's own brake on it.

MISSING: Interaction between the mapped components (condition (ii) above); any acknowledgment that Ha & Schmidhuber's M predicts in a learned latent space while the shield's "procedures" live in cultural convention; no engagement with the shield's maker (Hephaestus) in the mapping — the generative process itself has no V/M/C slot.

BOUNDARY: The mapping is asserted for the shield episode only, and for the 2018 architecture specifically — not for JEPA/LeCun-style world models, whose different decomposition would break the tripartite fit.

CITATION TRAIL: Ha & Schmidhuber 2018 (arXiv:1803.10122; worldmodels.github.io) → LeCun 2022 (JEPA, world models as foundation for machine intelligence) → Bruce et al. 2024 (Genie 2, 11B-parameter foundation world model) → World Labs Marble (Gaussian splats, exportable meshes) → mapped onto Homer via Taplin/Seaford structural readings.

TEST: Apply the same μ to a control artifact that is NOT claimed to be a world model (e.g., a medieval tapestry description or Keats's urn): if V/M/C counterparts are equally findable, the mapping is a reading grid, not a discovery about the shield.

PLATFORM: Ha & Schmidhuber 2018 reference implementation (VAE + MDN-RNN + CMA-ES controller); Genie 2; World Labs Marble.
