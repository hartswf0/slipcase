TITLE: Calling AI an *intensified* symbolic-control apparatus is a quantitative claim about a difference everyone else argues qualitatively — and no unit is given

SOURCE: PROGRAMS/thick.json — THICK_PROGRAMMING v2.0, <Initial_Interpretation>, after Clifford Geertz

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] "<human> [is-not] <natural essence hidden beneath culture> <human> [is] <unfinished animal completed through cultural programs>" [QUOTE] "<culture> [is-not] <ornament> <culture> [is] <extra-genetic instruction system>" [QUOTE] "<AI> [is-not] <break from culture> <AI> [is] <intensified symbolic-control apparatus>" [QUOTE] "<thick programming> [reads] <symbols> by asking what they mean and what they make happen>"

RESEARCH OBJECT: The word intensified. It refuses the rupture story — AI is not a break from culture — and substitutes a magnitude claim. Magnitude claims need units, and none is supplied. Intensified in *rate*, in *reach*, or in *granularity* are three different theses with three different politics.

LOCAL MOVE: The theory makes culture an instruction system and the human its output, then places AI on a continuum with it rather than outside it. That is the anti-exceptionalist move, and it is the strongest thing here.

SOURCE TERMS: unfinished animal / cultural programs / extra-genetic instruction system / intensified symbolic-control apparatus / thick programming / what they mean and what they make happen

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: The final clause is a two-part reading protocol — meaning *and* effect — which is precisely the distinction the rest of this corpus struggles to hold. Here it is stated in one line as a method, and nowhere is it noted that the second half requires a counterfactual the first half does not.

QUESTION: In what unit has symbolic control intensified — instructions received per day, proportion of conduct scripted, or granularity of the smallest scriptable act?

DEEPER QUESTION: The unfinished-animal thesis is *constitutive*: programs make the person over developmental time. Operative routing is *causal*: a description changes an action now. If AI is the same apparatus intensified, are those one mechanism at two speeds, or has an equivocation been built into the continuum?

MECHANISM: <SYMBOLIC PROGRAM> -> [ACQUIRED OVER TIME] -> <CAPACITY> -> conduct; versus <SYMBOLIC INSTRUCTION> -> [READ NOW] -> <ACTION>. The first makes an agent; the second steers one. Intensification silently blends them.

FORMAL SHIFT: <CULTURE AS PROGRAM> -> <AI AS MORE OF THE SAME> -> [NO UNIT SUPPLIED] -> <CONTINUUM UNTESTABLE>

SOURCE FORMALISM: The is-not/is contrast pairs; the two-part reading protocol.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Three candidate units: rate = scripted instructions encountered per day; reach = fraction of daily acts with an explicit script; granularity = smallest act with a script. Intensification is then three separate empirical questions, and they may not move together.

TENSION: READING A: one apparatus, more of it; the continuum is the insight and rupture talk is inflation. READING B: constitutive and causal control are different mechanisms, and the continuum hides an equivocation between making a person and steering one.

MISSING: Any unit. Any comparison across eras. Any acknowledgement that the two-part protocol's second half needs a counterfactual.

BOUNDARY: Geertz argues that culture completes the human; he makes no comparative claim about AI. Intensification is this theory's addition.

CITATION TRAIL: Geertz, "The Impact of the Concept of Culture on the Concept of Man" and "Thick Description," in The Interpretation of Cultures (1973). Bourdieu on habitus for the missing middle timescale. [[FORAGE-PT-007]] [[FORAGE-PT-024]]

TEST: Pick one unit — proportion of daily acts accompanied by an explicit script — and estimate it for two settings a generation apart. If it has not risen, "intensified" is doing rhetorical work and the continuum needs restating.
