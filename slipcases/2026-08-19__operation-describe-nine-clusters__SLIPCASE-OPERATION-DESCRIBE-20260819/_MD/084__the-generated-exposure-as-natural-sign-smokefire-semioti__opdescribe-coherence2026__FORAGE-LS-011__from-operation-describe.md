TITLE: The generated exposure as natural sign: smoke→fire semiotics retrofitted onto model outputs

SOURCE: 2026_04_27_NATURAL SIGN/Worldtext Coherence Theory and Design.md — Part I (natural sign lineage); 2026_04_27_NATURAL SIGN/a.md — "natural sign version vs seamless" exchange (12:21 PM) and "Exposure logic"

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] "in Augustinian semiotics, the concept of the *vestige*—a trace or a footprint—emerged as a natural sign characterized by a triadic structure: the perceived footprint, the absent animal that caused it, and the interpreting mind that establishes the meaningful connection between the two." [QUOTE] "a natural sign signifies by causal necessity, in the same incontrovertible manner that the presence of smoke dictates the existence of fire." [QUOTE] "The resulting output is rendered not as an isolated digital painting, but as an \<exposure\>—a natural sign that implies the larger, unseen causal structure." [QUOTE from a.md] "smoke → fire / footprint → animal passed here / scar → wound happened / fossil → ancient life / glitch → system underneath" [QUOTE from a.md] "the world is never exhausted by one output / every output is an exposure / multimodality is the plurality of exposures / the operator binds the exposures together / prompts are requests for further exposure"

RESEARCH OBJECT: The semiotic status of generated artifacts: reading each output as evidence of a hidden world-cause rather than as a self-contained picture.

LOCAL MOVE: Grafts the oldest sign-relation in the record (Mesopotamian portents, Augustine's vestige, Deely's virtual semiosis, biosemiotics) onto generative outputs, but with the causal arrow deliberately faked: the "hidden cause" is a designed worldtext, not nature.

SOURCE TERMS: natural sign; vestige; exposure; seamless version vs. natural sign version; glitch → system underneath; virtual semiosis (Deely, fossilized dinosaur bone example); triadic structure; hidden order / partial revelation / medium / operator / prompt (the exposure-logic five-tuple).

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: In a generative pipeline the natural sign is inverted — smoke is generated first and fire must be maintained behind it; the sign relation runs by *stipulated* causal necessity, enforced by an operator, or it does not run at all.

QUESTION: Can a sign whose "causal necessity" is enforced by canon rules still do the cognitive work of a natural sign for the user, or is it a designed icon wearing an index's clothes?

DEEPER QUESTION: Is the glitch the only genuine natural sign in the system — the one output that truly signifies by causal necessity, pointing at the substrate model rather than the fictional world?

MECHANISM: World = hidden order; each generation = partial revelation (exposure) through a medium; the operator binds plural exposures into one implied cause; the prompt requests further exposure; a continuity break "fails to be a vestige of the intended world" and must be pruned. Dual mode: "seamless traversal = the road / natural signs = the fossils, smoke, scars, ruins, and symbols along the road."

FORMAL SHIFT: From output-as-artwork (judged internally) to output-as-evidence (judged abductively against a hidden cause); quality control becomes forensic inference.

SOURCE FORMALISM: a.md's XML-ish blocks (<worldtext_logic> world/exposure/medium/operator/prompt; <natural_sign_worldtext>); the Coherence file's triad (sign, absent cause, interpreting mind).

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Exposure e is world-valid iff ∃ consistent w ∈ W(bible) with P(e | w) high and e adds constraint: W' = W ∩ {w : w ⊢ e}; a continuity break is e with W ∩ {w : w ⊢ e} = ∅.
