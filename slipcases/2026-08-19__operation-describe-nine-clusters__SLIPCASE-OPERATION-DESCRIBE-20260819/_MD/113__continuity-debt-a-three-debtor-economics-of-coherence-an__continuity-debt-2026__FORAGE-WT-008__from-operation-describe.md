TITLE: Continuity debt: a three-debtor economics of coherence and the CRI governor architecture

SOURCE: worldtext/syntheses/continuity-debt.md — "Core Thesis", "The Economics", "Accumulation Mechanics", "Repair Strategies", "The Suchman Critique", "The CRI Architecture (Speculative)"

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] "**Continuity debt** is the compounding accumulation of logic errors, entity contradictions, and world-state fractures that occurs when generative output scales faster than the coherence infrastructure maintaining it." [QUOTE] "The continuity debt is always paid. The only variable is how far downstream the bill travels before someone catches it."

RESEARCH OBJECT: An economic model of world-coherence: debt accrual mechanisms with rates (Prompt Rupture — immediate; Semantic Drift — cumulative over 10-50 generations; Entity Amnesia — proportional to context window exhaustion; Physics Violations — episodic; Style-Over-Logic Illusion — hidden), a three-party liability structure (OEM / Service Provider / Operator), and a cost-ordered repair hierarchy (prevention < detection < repair).

LOCAL MOVE: The file imports the technical-debt metaphor and then breaks it in one precise place: unlike technical debt, [QUOTE] "continuity debt **is immediately visible to the inhabitant**. A world with continuity debt does not feel broken *eventually*. It feels broken *now*." Debt in fiction has no grace period.

SOURCE TERMS: Continuity debt; three debtors (OEM, Service Provider, Operator); prompt rupture; semantic drift; entity amnesia; style-over-logic illusion; pseudo-canon; Executable World Bible; Entity Ledger; Operative String Composition (Anchors + Constraints + Motion Vectors + Negative Constraints); Same-Worldness Test; Return Probe; Canary Conditions; Lore Absorption; Closed-World Fallacy; CRI (Continuity-Regulated Intelligence).

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: Retcon — the standard franchise repair move — is priced as the worst option: "Destroys trust." And contradiction has a legitimate laundering channel: Lore Absorption "Reclassifying a contradiction as a 'myth' rather than a 'fact'" — canon damage can be converted into mythic depth, but "the lore book can only absorb so much."

QUESTION: The file's own labor question, verbatim: [QUOTE] "When generation outpaces coherence, who bears the labor cost — OEM, service provider, or user?" (Answer given: "Currently: the user. Always the user.")

DEEPER QUESTION: Is coherence infrastructure economically shiftable upward at all — can an OEM sell persistent world-state as a platform feature — or does Suchman's plans/situated-actions asymmetry mean the operator's situated thread of intent can never be fully delegated, making some floor of continuity debt structurally unshiftable?

MECHANISM: Compounding: [QUOTE] "Contradicted facts create ambiguous ground for future generation. The model has no mechanism to distinguish 'established canon' from 'error.' Downstream prompts may reference the error, hardening it into pseudo-canon." Error becomes canon by citation — the same mechanism that makes provenance graphs necessary.

FORMAL SHIFT: From "the AI is inconsistent" (a model complaint) to a debt ledger with accrual rates, liability assignment, and repair costs ordered O(1) prevention vs O(n²) manual review — coherence as an economics problem, not a capability problem.

SOURCE FORMALISM: The CRI three-layer stack diagram: CRI GOVERNOR (health signals, compliance, quality gates, canary conditions, rollback, entity reconciliation) / WORLD BIBLE (identity fields, invariants, continuity anchors, negative constraints, physics model) / LLM (produces surfaces on demand; "no persistent state, no entity memory, no self-audit capability"), with four verdicts per generation: Repairs / Rejects / Flags / Rolls back. Anti-scaling axiom: [QUOTE] "Coherence is not an emergent property of scale. It is an architectural commitment."

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Debt D(t) with dD/dt = Σᵢ aᵢ(t) − ρ(t), where aᵢ are the five accrual mechanisms and ρ repair throughput; compounding via aᵢ(t) = aᵢ⁰·(1 + λD(t)) — the pseudo-canon feedback term λ makes D super-linear whenever ρ < Σaᵢ, predicting a runaway threshold analogous to epistemic debt's irreversibility point.

TENSION: The Closed-World Fallacy section asserts scaling can never yield persistence ("A model with 1000x more compute still treats each prompt as isolated") — an architectural claim stated as fact but contested by the cited LeCun program and by long-context/stateful-agent trends; the file bets against emergence without hedging. Rival reading within the corpus: anomaly-of-return.md locates the same failure as "structural absence," but exposure-as-natural-sign.md implies every generation *does* carry latent world-state, only unreliably.

MISSING: Numbers for the rates (what is "10-50 generations" measured against?); how the CRI's own judgments avoid accruing debt (who lints the governor?); a market analysis of why OEMs would internalize the cost.

BOUNDARY: Speculative by self-label ("The CRI Architecture (Speculative)"); the economics is qualitative — no currency, no measurement instrument for D.
