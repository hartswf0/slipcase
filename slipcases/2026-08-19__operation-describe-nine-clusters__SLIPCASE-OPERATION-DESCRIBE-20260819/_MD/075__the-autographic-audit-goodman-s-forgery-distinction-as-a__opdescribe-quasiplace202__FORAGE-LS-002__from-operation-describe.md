TITLE: The autographic audit: Goodman's forgery distinction as an epistemic firewall for generated environments

SOURCE: LATENT SPACE/Latent Space as Operative Quasi-Place.md — "The Autographic Audit and the Allographic Reality" / "Goodman's Distinction and Data Materiality"; LATENT SPACE/Latent Space Cosmos Integration Report.md — allographic section

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] "The danger lies in conflating the visually plausible with the evidential." [QUOTE] "In his foundational 1968 work *Languages of Art*, philosopher Nelson Goodman established a vital theoretical distinction between autographic and allographic arts." [QUOTE] "Autographic data consists of the physical traces, imprints, and self-inscriptions left by actual phenomena in the material world—such as tree rings, ice cores, or the physical accumulation of urban pollution." (attributed to Dietmar Offenhuber's "autographic design"). Cosmos report: [QUOTE] "popular debates over AI \"forgery,\" \"plagiarism,\" or \"authenticity\" often miss the mark: they mistakenly apply autographic criteria (demanding human provenance) to inherently allographic algorithmic processes."

RESEARCH OBJECT: The evidential status of photorealistic generated exposures — what kind of sign a generated image is, and what audit separates trace from synthesis.

LOCAL MOVE: Imports an aesthetics-of-forgery distinction (Goodman) through a data-materiality relay (Offenhuber) to reclassify AI-authenticity debates as a category error.

SOURCE TERMS: autographic; allographic; autographic design; data materiality; visually plausible vs. evidential; synthetic reproductions; notational system; unforgeable provenance.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: Photorealism flips valence — the more perfectly a generated world simulates physics, the *less* evidential it is, because it severs the causal history that autographic traces carry.

QUESTION: Can an audit distinguish autographic from allographic content from the artifact alone, or does it always require provenance metadata external to the image?

DEEPER QUESTION: If the synthetic completely absorbs the autographic — the Cosmos report asks what truth-value remains — is evidentiality a property of artifacts at all, or only of custody chains?

MECHANISM: Goodman: autographic works are individuated by their physical history of production (forgery is possible); allographic works are individuated by notational compliance (any correct instantiation is genuine). Generated environments are allographic through and through, so demanding "originality" of them is incoherent; the audit instead asks which pipeline stage last touched material reality.

FORMAL SHIFT: From authenticity as originality to authenticity as compliance-plus-provenance: two orthogonal audit axes instead of one.

SOURCE FORMALISM: Goodman's autographic/allographic binary; Offenhuber's trace/inscription vocabulary.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] For artifact x: Evid(x) = f(causal chain from world-state w to x); generated x has Evid(x) ≈ 0 w.r.t. any depicted w even when perceptual similarity sim(x, w) → 1. Audit = estimate of the last autographic link in x's pipeline.

TENSION: The file names an "Autographic Audit" in a section title and supplies its rationale, but specifies no procedure — rival readings: the audit is (a) a forensic protocol awaiting steps, or (b) a purely conceptual firewall never meant to be run. Also unresolved: training data *is* a compressed archive of autographic traces (see trace archives), so generated outputs are not evidentially null, just evidentially laundered.

MISSING: Any step-by-step audit procedure; any treatment of watermarking/C2PA-style provenance standards; the criterion for mixed pipelines (photo-initialized generation).

BOUNDARY: Applies to output artifacts of generative systems; does not claim the models' internal weights lack material history.

CITATION TRAIL: Nelson Goodman, Languages of Art (1968); Dietmar Offenhuber, autographic design / data materiality; file links this to Gieryn's "truth-spot" and Messeri's surplus realities.

TEST: Partially checkable: whether human or machine auditors can classify autographic vs. synthetic exposures above chance from artifact-internal features is an empirical question with an existing deepfake-detection literature; the file's stronger claim (category error in authenticity debates) is conceptual, not testable.

PLATFORM: Any generative image/video/world pipeline; evidentiary contexts (journalism, forensics, ethnography).
