ID: FORAGE-PT-005

TITLE: "Rescues" implies something arrives intact, and nothing specifies what survives inscription and what is destroyed by it

SOURCE: PROGRAMS/mot.json — THE_MODEL_OF_THE_TRACE, <Core Thesis>, after Paul Ricoeur

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] "<speech> [vanishes as] <event> <writing> [rescues] <the-said> <action> [vanishes as] <event> <trace> [rescues] <the-done> <meaning> [does-not-live-inside] <intention> <meaning> [survives-as] <inscribed-public-consequence>"

RESEARCH OBJECT: The rescue relation. Two vanishings and two rescues are asserted with perfect symmetry, and the loss term is never written. What the trace preserves and what it necessarily destroys are both unspecified.

LOCAL MOVE: Ricoeur's distanciation is compressed into a two-line conservation law: the event goes, the said/done remains, and meaning relocates from intention to public consequence.

SOURCE TERMS: vanishes as event / rescues / the-said / the-done / does-not-live-inside intention / inscribed-public-consequence

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: A rescue with no stated loss is a conservation claim. But every trace is partial by construction — a log records what it was built to record. So "the-done" is not rescued; a projection of it is, and the projection is chosen by whoever built the recorder.

QUESTION: What is invariant under inscription — what do all independent traces of one action preserve, and what does only some preserve?

DEEPER QUESTION: If the invariant is small, then "meaning survives as inscribed public consequence" means meaning is whatever the recording apparatus happened to be able to hold. That relocates authority from actors to instrument-builders.

MECHANISM: <EVENT> -> [INSCRIPTION BY SOME APPARATUS] -> <PARTIAL TRACE> -> detached from author, audience, occasion -> readable by unforeseen readers -> <PUBLIC CONSEQUENCE>

FORMAL SHIFT: <ACTION> -> <TRACE UNDER A RECORDING CHOICE> -> [READING] -> <MEANING AS A FUNCTION OF THE APPARATUS>

SOURCE FORMALISM: The four-line rescue schema.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] For an event e with independent traces T1..Tn, define INV(e) = intersection of what all Ti support, and RESIDUE(e, Ti) = what only Ti supports. The rescue claim is strong iff INV is large. Testable wherever one event is multiply recorded.

TENSION: READING A: the invariant is substantial — the same deed is recognisable across independent records, which is what makes history possible. READING B: the invariant is thin, and agreement across traces reflects shared recording conventions rather than the event.

MISSING: Any multiply-traced case worked through. Any treatment of what a trace *cannot* record.

BOUNDARY: Ricoeur argues for semantic autonomy of the inscribed; he does not offer a conservation law. The symmetry is this theory's compression.

CITATION TRAIL: Ricoeur — "The Model of the Text: Meaningful Action Considered as a Text" (1971). [[FORAGE-PT-006]] [[FORAGE-PT-023]]

TEST: Take one action with several independent records (a meeting with minutes, a recording, and three participants' notes). Compute what all four support versus what one supports alone. The ratio is the strength of the rescue claim, measured for the first time.
