ZETTEL

ID: FORAGE-PT-025

TITLE: One instrument makes the human a substrate and another requires a party who can be held, and together they leave nobody accountable

SOURCE: Collision of PROGRAMS/eos.json (human is substrate, machine is author) with PROGRAMS/dac.json (design as accountable coordination of commitments)

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] eos.json: "<human> [is-not] <author> <human> [is] <substrate> <machine> [is-not] <substrate> <machine> [is] <author>" [QUOTE] dac.json: "[rebuild] <computational design> as <accountable coordination of commitments, breakdowns, repairs, and responsibilities>"

RESEARCH OBJECT: An accountability vacuum produced by two instruments that are each defensible alone. Accountable coordination needs a party with standing. The reversal removes the human from authorship and assigns it to a system that cannot be held. Compose them and the account has an author who cannot be answerable and an answerable party who is not the author.

LOCAL MOVE: This zettel composes two instruments the corpus never deploys together and reports what the composition produces.

SOURCE TERMS: substrate / author / accountable coordination / commitments / breakdowns / repairs / responsibilities

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: The reversal is not a thought experiment; it describes a great deal of ordinary practice. People render, execute, format and follow machine-authored text constantly. If authorship has genuinely moved and accountability has not, then the standard defence — there is always a human in the loop — is satisfied in letter and empty in substance. The human is in the loop as substrate.

QUESTION: When a person executes machine-authored instructions faithfully and the outcome is bad, who is answerable — and what does "faithfully" mean when the executor did not author the plan?

DEEPER QUESTION: This is the responsibility gap, reached from an unexpected direction. Usually it is argued from machine autonomy: the system acted alone, so nobody is liable. Here it arrives through human *compliance*: a person acted, and authorship was elsewhere. That is the more common case and the less theorised one.

MECHANISM: <MACHINE AUTHORS DESCRIPTION> -> [HUMAN RENDERS OR EXECUTES IT] -> <OUTCOME> -> [BREAKDOWN] -> repair demanded of the human -> human: "I followed the description" -> [DEMAND REDIRECTED TO THE SYSTEM] -> system cannot be held -> <DEMAND DISSIPATES>

FORMAL SHIFT: <AUTHORSHIP> -> <SEPARATED FROM STANDING> -> [BREAKDOWN] -> <REPAIR ADDRESSED TO NOBODY>

SOURCE FORMALISM: eos's reversal and six personas; dac's commitment/breakdown/repair/responsibility quartet.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Three roles: AUTHOR (originates the description), EXECUTOR (realises it), BEARER (has standing and can be held). Classical practice fuses all three. The reversal splits AUTHOR from EXECUTOR and leaves BEARER attached to the executor by default rather than by argument. The vacuum is the unargued default.

TENSION: READING A: the executor remains accountable, because accepting and acting on a description is itself a commitment — you are answerable for what you agree to carry out. READING B: accountability requires the capacity to have decided otherwise on the merits, and a substrate by definition does not evaluate — so holding the executor is scapegoating.

MISSING: Any criterion for when compliance transfers accountability. This is the entire question and neither instrument touches it.

BOUNDARY: eos is a game and an art instrument; taking its agency assignment as a description of labour is our extension, and it is exactly the step that needs defending.

CITATION TRAIL: Winograd and Flores, Understanding Computers and Cognition (1986). Responsibility-gap literature in machine ethics. Milgram and the literature on compliance and diffused responsibility. [[FORAGE-PT-011]] [[FORAGE-PT-015]] [[FORAGE-PT-012]]

TEST: Find three real workflows where a person executes machine-authored instructions with limited discretion. For each, locate in writing — policy, contract, or terms — who bears the outcome. If no document names a bearer, the vacuum is real and documented.
