TITLE: The signature of answerability is reversal — a change that undoes a prior commitment rather than extending it

SOURCE: PROGRAMS/dse.json correction condition, read through the variant classification of genetic criticism [[FORAGE-PT-030]] and the contraction gap [[FORAGE-PT-037]]

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] dse.json: "<language> [becomes] <thinking-like-description> only when it is made answerable to: { <inward_pressure>, <memory>, <intention>, <metaphor>, <fact>, <history>, <public_criterion>, <correction>, <ethical_consequence> }" [PARAPHRASE] Genetic criticism classifies variants by operation — substitution, expansion, deletion, reordering — and treats the dossier as a record of operations rather than of motives.

RESEARCH OBJECT: One operation type carrying the diagnostic weight. Expansion adds; substitution swaps; reordering rearranges. Only reversal withdraws something the author previously committed to. That is the operation that requires the author to have applied a standard against their own earlier choice — which is exactly the correction condition, and it is countable in any revision chain.

LOCAL MOVE: This child narrows the parent's proposal from "code the diffs" to a single operation with a stated rationale, making the measure cheap and the claim falsifiable.

SOURCE TERMS: correction / answerable to / variant / substitution / expansion / deletion / reordering

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: Reversal is the operation natural language has no syntax for and the operation revision chains record most legibly. Prompts cannot contract; drafts can. So the medium that preserves struggle is precisely the medium that supports the operation the medium of instruction lacks — and the corpus has been trying to find struggle in the wrong artifact.

QUESTION: What share of changes in a revision chain are reversals, and does that share distinguish chains produced under correction from chains produced by elaboration?

DEEPER QUESTION: If reversal share is the measure, then a system that never reverses has no observable answerability regardless of how much it revises. Elaboration without reversal is accumulation wearing the appearance of work — and that is a testable signature of a specific failure mode, not a moral complaint.

MECHANISM: <COMMITMENT MADE IN STATE k> -> [LATER STATE WITHDRAWS IT: the text no longer asserts what it asserted] -> [WITHDRAWAL REQUIRES A STANDARD APPLIED TO ONE'S OWN EARLIER CHOICE] -> <REVERSAL INSCRIBED AND COUNTABLE>

FORMAL SHIFT: <NINE ANSWERABILITY CONDITIONS> -> <ONE COUNTABLE OPERATION> -> [REVERSAL SHARE] -> <ANSWERABILITY WITH A NUMBER>

SOURCE FORMALISM: dse's nine conditions; variant typologies from genetic criticism, which vary by school and are not quoted.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] For a chain of states s_0..s_n, classify each transition and define rev = reversals / total transitions. Predict: chains produced under external correction have higher rev than chains produced by continuation; and rev is near zero for single-pass generation regardless of length.

TENSION: READING A: reversal is the signature and rev is a usable measure of answerability. READING B: reversal can be performed — a system can be trained to produce "on reflection, no" moves, and a writer can stage self-correction for rhetorical effect. Then rev measures a genre, and the counterfeit problem returns one level down.

Discriminating evidence: whether reversals are *consequential* — does the reversal propagate to dependent passages, or is it local and cosmetic? Genuine withdrawal should force downstream repair.

MISSING: Any reversal counts for any corpus. Any test of propagation, which is what separates a real withdrawal from a performed one.

BOUNDARY: This proposes a measure and a rationale. It does not establish that reversal indicates the inward conditions dse names — genetic criticism explicitly declines the analogous inference.

CITATION TRAIL: [[FORAGE-PT-023]] [[FORAGE-PT-030]] [[FORAGE-PT-037]] -> variant classification -> reversal as the diagnostic operation -> next: propagation as the counterfeit test, which connects directly to consequence-propagation benchmarking.

TEST: Twenty revision chains, half with recorded external objections. Count reversals and, for each, whether dependent passages were also changed. High rev with propagation in the objection group and low rev or non-propagating rev elsewhere establishes both the measure and its counterfeit test.
