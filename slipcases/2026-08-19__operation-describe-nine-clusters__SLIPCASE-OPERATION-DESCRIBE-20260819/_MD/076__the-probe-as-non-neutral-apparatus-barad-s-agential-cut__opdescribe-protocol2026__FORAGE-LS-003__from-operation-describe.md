ID: FORAGE-LS-003

TITLE: The probe as non-neutral apparatus: Barad's agential cut relocated into prompt-space

SOURCE: LATENT SPACE/Operative Ethnography Protocol Design.md — "The Non-Neutral Apparatus and the Agential Cut"

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] "the software interface, the ethnographic questionnaire, or the algorithmic probe is not a neutral, passive lens through which the researcher objectively views the latent space of the model. It is an active, material-discursive *apparatus* that participates forcefully in making the object of study what it is." [QUOTE] "Instead, they enact an *agential cut*. This agential cut temporarily stabilizes the boundary between the \"interpreter\" and the \"system,\" resolving the probabilistic superposition of the latent space into a singular, legible output. However, this resolution comes at a cost: the apparatus renders certain probabilistic features, aesthetic modalities, or cultural representations visible, while simultaneously and inevitably foreclosing the observation of countless others."

RESEARCH OBJECT: The epistemic status of prompts and interpretability probes as measuring instruments.

LOCAL MOVE: Transfers Barad's quantum-derived agential realism from laboratory apparatus to prompt/probe, making every prompt an ontology-fixing measurement rather than a query.

SOURCE TERMS: agential cut; intra-action; material-discursive apparatus; probabilistic superposition; foreclosure; entanglement; Encounter Trace (Cosmos report's name for the output as "physical manifestation of the agential cut, and the primary unit of analysis").

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: The output of a generative model stops being "the model's answer" and becomes a co-authored event that did not exist, even latently as a determinate fact, before the cut.

QUESTION: What distinguishes an agential cut from ordinary conditioning of a probability distribution — is anything empirical added by the Barad vocabulary?

DEEPER QUESTION: If every observation forecloses others, can there be a principled sampling design over cuts, or does agential realism forbid the very idea of representative measurement?

MECHANISM: Prompt/probe fixes boundary conditions → superposed latent potential resolves into one legible output → the output (Encounter Trace) is analyzed as evidence of the *configuration*, not of a pre-existing model-internal fact → visibility gained is paid for in systematically foreclosed alternatives.

FORMAL SHIFT: From representationalism (output reveals hidden state) to performativity (output enacts a phenomenon); the file tabulates this as "Representationalism (Traditional XAI)" vs. "Agential Realism (Operative Ethnography)."

SOURCE FORMALISM: A two-row comparison table: black box with immanent truths / neutral lens / direct reflection / transparency goal vs. entangled latent space of "unactualized near-forms" / cut-enacting apparatus / co-authored performance / interference-mapping goal.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Output o ~ P(· | L, A) where apparatus A is a free variable of the experiment, not a nuisance parameter; the research object is the function A ↦ P(· | L, A), never P(· | L) alone.

TENSION: Rival readings: (a) deflationary — "agential cut" is just conditional sampling dressed in quantum language; (b) strong — model properties are literally indeterminate prior to measurement configuration. The file asserts (b) but its examples (a music-style probe missing embedded biases) support only (a).

MISSING: Any criterion for when two apparatuses count as "the same cut"; error bars; engagement with interpretability work on probe sensitivity that makes the same point without Barad.

BOUNDARY: Claims scoped to qualitative study of generative models; does not claim physical quantum effects in silicon.

CITATION TRAIL: Karen Barad, agential realism / intra-action (Meeting the Universe Halfway); file routes Barad through XAI critique (LRP, saliency, LIME, SHAP) and synthetic ethnography.

TEST: Checkable in weak form: do differently-designed probes of the same latent structure yield systematically incompatible characterizations (probe-sensitivity experiments)? The strong ontological claim is not empirically decidable.

PLATFORM: LLMs, generative image/music models, linear-classifier probing of hidden layers.
