RESEARCH OBJECT:
Four self-addressed operative descriptions, each with a stated route, a dated issuance, and a measured compliance rate. Together they are the archive's only quantitative dataset on its own subject.

They were produced as housekeeping. Read as evidence, they are the negative cases the dissertation has been unable to find for two years.

LOCAL MOVE:
The dark-matter audit was performed to fix the archive. Its numbers were reported as remediation targets and the remediation was reported as success ("improved link resolution to 97%").

Nobody asked what the 93.6% *measured* — namely, the routing power of COSMIC_LAW §3 over its own author, at 6.4%.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Each of the four has a *different failure profile*, and the differences are informative rather than uniform:

  COSMIC_LAW §3 (source summaries)   6.4% compliance at audit, then remediated to 97% — a law that failed, was measured, and then worked
  COSMIC_LAW §6 step 10 (atlas)      worked for six weeks, then stopped dead — decay, not failure
  framework §8 (banned terms)        0% compliance from the first minute — immediate rejection
  red team Claim 4 (New Operator Test) specified and never run — a demand with no attempt

Four laws, four profiles: repaired-after-measurement, decay, immediate rejection, never-attempted. That is a taxonomy of non-operativity derived from data, and it does not exist anywhere in the archive's twenty numbered sections of boundary questions.

QUESTION:
What distinguishes a self-addressed description that decays from one that is rejected immediately, and from one that is repaired once it is measured?

DEEPER QUESTION:
The §3 case is the important one: compliance went from 6.4% to 97% *because it was measured*. So measurement was the operative act, not the law. If describing compliance is what produces compliance, then audit is the routing mechanism and the law is only its pretext — which would relocate the archive's entire political account from category-writers to auditors.

MECHANISM:
Repaired-after-measurement:
<LAW> → low compliance → [AUDIT MEASURES AND PUBLISHES A NUMBER] → operator repairs → <HIGH COMPLIANCE>
The measurement, not the law, closed the gap.

Decay:
<LAW> → compliance while the practice is fresh → [ATTENTION MOVES TO PAPERS] → law lapses silently → <ZERO>
No decision to stop was recorded anywhere.

Immediate rejection:
<LAW> → conflicts with a load-bearing dependency → [COMPLIANCE COST PROHIBITIVE] → <ZERO FROM THE START>
See FORAGE-OD-023.

Never-attempted:
<DEMAND> → requires an external participant → [NO PARTICIPANT AVAILABLE] → <ZERO>
The binding constraint is a resource, not a description.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
The dark-matter audit supplies two percentages and a remediation figure. That is the archive's only existing quantitative measurement of a description's effect on an operator, and it is buried in a red-team footnote.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

For each law L with issue date τ, define the compliance series c_L(t) and classify:

  REPAIRED   c low, then high after an audit event a:  c(a⁻) ≪ c(a⁺)
  DECAY      c high, then falling with no audit event:  dc/dt < 0, no a
  REJECTED   c ≈ 0 for all t ≥ τ
  UNATTEMPTED c undefined; the required resource never existed

And a prediction that follows only from the §3 case:

  **audit is operative even when the law is not.**

Testable directly: introduce a second unmeasured law, measure it once publicly, and see whether compliance rises without any change to the law's text or enforcement. If it does, the archive has demonstrated that *description of compliance* routes more powerfully than *description of the requirement* — which is a finding about audit culture with reach far beyond this repository.

TENSION:
READING A: the §3 remediation happened because the operator decided to fix it; the audit merely informed the decision, and attributing the change to measurement is over-reading a single event.
READING B: the operator had had the law for zero days longer than the audit and had not complied; the only new thing was the number. On the archive's own standards — hold everything constant, vary one thing — the number is the manipulation.

n = 1. Reading A cannot be excluded. That is precisely why the second-law experiment is worth running.

BOUNDARY:
Four laws, one repository, one operator, sixteen weeks. No generalization is licensed. What is licensed is that the archive possesses measurements of its own subject and has never read them as such.
