TITLE:
THE ARCHIVE PROMOTED THE CASE WITH THE BEST EXPERIMENTAL CONTROL AND THEREBY DEMOTED THE ONLY CASE WHERE ITS POLITICS CAN BE OBSERVED

SOURCE:
Watson Hartsoe — PAPERS/operation-describe-label-01.md §12 "Scope: The Leanest Core" and §11 "The Cases" — 2026

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
§12:
"The primary case is the LLM tool description (cleanest experimental control); the secondary case is GitHub labels (public scale). Toxicity is the political governance case; emergency triage is kept only as an introductory analogy."

[QUOTE]
§11:
"GitHub labels route human labor. Toxicity thresholds route platform speech. LLM schemas route machine execution."

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The scope decision is ordered by *measurability*. The political argument is carried entirely by the case ranked third.

Whichever way the dissertation is defended, one of its two contributions rests on a case the author has declared secondary.

LOCAL MOVE:
The ranking is presented as a scope discipline — the leanest core. It is in fact a trade: experimental cleanliness purchased with political relevance.

The trade is never stated as a trade.

SOURCE TERMS:
cleanest experimental control

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The three cases were chosen because they share a mechanism (label-01 §11: labor, speech, execution). But they differ on the variable that decides whether the dissertation is a media-politics dissertation or an HCI measurement dissertation.

That variable — who pays when the route is wrong — is maximal in the demoted case and approximately zero in the promoted one. A sandboxed mock refund harms nobody. A false toxicity flag silences somebody.

QUESTION:
Can the political claim be carried by the primary case at all, or does the dissertation need a fourth case with both control and stakes?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is there any domain where the researcher can both randomize the description and observe a real cost borne by a real party — and if not, is the archive's ethics chapter (label-01 §14: "sandboxed agency") also a confession that its politics cannot be tested?

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Place each case on two axes:

  control  = ability to randomize D and hold everything else fixed
  stakes   = magnitude of cost borne by a party who did not choose D

  tool schemas      : control HIGH,   stakes ~0
  GitHub labels     : control MEDIUM, stakes LOW-MEDIUM (labor, credit, congestion)
  toxicity queues   : control LOW,    stakes HIGH
  triage            : control ~0,     stakes MAXIMAL

Nothing sits in the high-control / high-stakes quadrant. The archive's contribution architecture (framework §9) claims both a core theory contribution and an ethical contribution; the case portfolio cannot support both from the same evidence.

Candidate for the empty quadrant: a setting where the researcher controls the description and real participants bear a real, consented, reversible cost — e.g. randomized wording of a moderation *appeal* form, or of a "good first issue" description in a live repository with contributor consent.

TENSION:
READING A: the ethical contribution is a normative argument, not an empirical one, so it does not require a high-stakes case.
READING B: the archive's own standard forbids this. Its disproof section demands falsification metrics; a normative claim exempt from evidence inside an otherwise empirical dissertation is the "elegant alchemy" its own Geertz chapter attacks (PAPERS/geertz-01.md §VIII).

MISSING:
A case in the empty quadrant. An IRB-shaped design for one. Any acknowledgement in the archive that the quadrant is empty.

BOUNDARY:
This is an argument about evidential coverage, not about the truth of the political claims. The political claims may well be right; they are currently unevidenced by the promoted case.

TEST:
Draw the 2×2 with the three cases plotted. Then attempt to name one setting in the high-control/high-stakes quadrant that passes an IRB.

If no such setting exists, the honest move is to split the dissertation's claims explicitly: routing is demonstrated, harm is argued. Saying so is stronger than implying both are demonstrated.
