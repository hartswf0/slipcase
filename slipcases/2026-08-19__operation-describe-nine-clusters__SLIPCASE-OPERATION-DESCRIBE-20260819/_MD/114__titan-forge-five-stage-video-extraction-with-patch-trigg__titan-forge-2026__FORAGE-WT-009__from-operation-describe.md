TITLE: TITAN FORGE: five-stage video extraction with patch-trigger rules and a banned-verb observation discipline

SOURCE: worldtext/syntheses/titan-forge.md — "Core Axiom", "The Five Stages", "Segmentation Protocol", "Patch Trigger Rules", "Observation Discipline", "The Four Interchange Matrices"

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] "Reject the Occult Fallacy. Words are not mirrors of hidden thought; they are physical reconfiguration levers that trigger execution. Meaning is use." [QUOTE] "Track only what the source materially presents: bodies, objects, vectors, cuts, thresholds, durations, spatial relations, lens changes, light states, and sound states."

RESEARCH OBJECT: A pipeline (PRIME ENGINE v6.0) that converts moving images into worldtext structure through five typed stages — DELIMIT, SEGMENT, TRACK, COMPRESS, TRANSMIT — over a five-level segmentation hierarchy (Diegesis > Macro Units > Beats > Syntagmas > Patches) with hard atomic-unit bounds (patches: target 8–10 s, hard limits 6–12 s).

LOCAL MOVE: The file makes behaviorism a lint rule: mental-state verbs are BANNED from descriptions ("feels, thinks, realizes, knows, understands, wants"), forcing every psychological claim into measurable kinetics — [QUOTE] "NOT: 'she fears him' / BUT: 'her shoulders pull back, gaze snaps left, feet retreat two steps'."

SOURCE TERMS: Occult Fallacy; DELIMIT/SEGMENT/TRACK/COMPRESS/TRANSMIT; diegesis; macro units; beats; syntagmas (continuous, alternating, parallel, interrupted, bracketed, accumulative, composite); patches; patch trigger rules; CINEMATIC_PATCH_GENOME (Matrix A, YAML); STORYMAP (Matrix B); EDL_BARE_METAL (CMX3600); OTIO_DEEP_STRUCTURE; generative seed; conform.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: Film-editing law (Metz-style syntagmas) is repurposed as a *physics* layer: the syntagma is defined as "Editing law governing a beat" — montage grammar becomes part of the world's measurable state, on par with light and sound regimes, rather than an interpretive category.

QUESTION: Are the ten patch-trigger rules (location shift, new kinetic vector, optical-distance change, angle/axis change, light-state change, sound-regime change, time-arrow jump, entity entry/exit, threshold crossing, subsystem dominance) jointly complete and individually necessary — i.e., do they segment any footage into the same patches for any two operators?

DEEPER QUESTION: If "patch boundaries obey physical event coherence, not convenience," is there an objective event-segmentation of continuous footage at all — or does the 6–12 second hard limit reveal that the atomicity is calibrated to generative-model context economics rather than to the ontology of events?

MECHANISM: Each patch passes through the Analytic Kernel: identify Variables/Inputs/Outputs/States/Constants/Invariants; Axioms/Primitives/Postulates; classify properties Fundamental-or-Derived, Scalar-or-Vector; track feedback loops (reinforcing or balancing), equilibrium tendencies (attractors or repellers), open/closed system type, and hard/soft constraints — a full systems-theory schema applied per 8-second window.

FORMAL SHIFT: From film analysis as interpretation to film as a reconstructible state-machine: COMPRESS emits "a concise generative seed and invariant state description" per patch, so the output's success criterion is rehydration (can a generator re-conform the footage?), not critical insight.

SOURCE FORMALISM: The five-stage table; the five-level segmentation table with patch bounds; the ten patch-trigger rules; the four interchange matrices with status flags (A CINEMATIC_PATCH_GENOME YAML — ACTIVE; B STORYMAP Markdown — ACTIVE; C EDL_BARE_METAL CMX3600 — STANDBY; D OTIO_DEEP_STRUCTURE JSON — STANDBY); the BANNED/ALLOWED observation lists.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Footage f: [0,T] → frames; a valid patching is a partition P of [0,T] with 6 ≤ |p| ≤ 12s where every boundary coincides with a trigger event e ∈ E₁₀ and no trigger event is interior to a patch (event-aligned segmentation). TITAN FORGE ≈ a lossy encoder E: f → seeds with fidelity judged by a decoder D: seeds → f′ under same-worldness rather than pixel distance.

TENSION: The core axiom "Meaning is use" (Wittgenstein) is invoked to justify strict behaviorist observation, but the corpus's own thick-description commitments (Geertz's wink vs. twitch, cited in seven-failure-modes.md) hold that identical physical motions differ in meaning by context — thin behaviorist tracking is exactly what Geertz calls inadequate. The corpus thus runs Geertz and the ban-list in opposite directions: thickness demands interpretation; TITAN FORGE bans it, allowing only "defensible inference from repeated physical evidence."

MISSING: Inter-operator segmentation agreement data; the actual Matrix A YAML schema (named, never shown); criteria for promoting Matrices C/D from STANDBY; how TRACK handles off-screen persistence (the anomaly-of-return problem inside a single shot).

BOUNDARY: Applies to temporal media with visible physics; explicitly cannot capture "theme," symbol, or interiority — by design it produces the world's Topos and event structure while leaving Mythos and Ethos to other instruments.
