TITLE: Urn vs. shield: two governing images of ekphrasis, and Lessing's temporal fabrication

SOURCE: KEEL BLOCKS/SYNTH-02_shield-as-world-model.md (opening argument); KEEL BLOCKS/SEED-02_shield-as-worldtext-evolved.md; Dry-Dock/BULKHEAD-04_shield-performance.md (Lessing paragraph); KEEL BLOCKS/deep-research-report (16).md

PASSAGE: [QUOTE from SYNTH-02] "Two artifacts compete for the title of ekphrasis's governing image. One is the urn. The other is the shield. They produce different critical programs." [QUOTE] "The 'urn' became a name for the prestige of the resolved object. Modern literary criticism, when it imagines ekphrasis, still tends to imagine an object of contemplation rather than a scene of making." [QUOTE from SEED-02] "The urn names the prestige of the resolved object. The shield names something older and now technically urgent: world-making as process." [QUOTE from BULKHEAD-04] "instead of describing the finished object, Homer describes how the god Hephaestus fashions the shield, scene by scene ... That Homer described the process of making as it unfolds in time was an important point for Lessing who argued in Laocoon that poetry is a temporal medium." [PARAPHRASE] Webb confirms from the rhetorical tradition that Theon's interest in the shield "concerned the manner in which it is made" — ancient ekphrasis accommodated process no less than object.

RESEARCH OBJECT: A structural opposition doing genealogical work: Brooks's Well Wrought Urn (1947) and Keats's Grecian Urn stand for the New Critical program (the poem as resolved autonomous object), while Homer's shield stands for a rival program suppressed by that canonization — ekphrasis as temporal fabrication. The claim: choosing the urn as emblem locked twentieth-century ekphrasis theory into product-thinking, and generative AI makes the shield's process-thinking "technically urgent."

LOCAL MOVE: Read the history of ekphrasis theory as an emblem-choice with consequences: Spitzer's definition ("the poetic description of a pictorial or sculptural work of art," Webb 1999, 10) plus Brooks's urn produced the finished-artifact paradigm; recover Lessing and Theon to show the process paradigm was always available and is what prompting actually resembles.

SOURCE TERMS: well-wrought urn; heresy of paraphrase (Brooks, via deep-research-report 16); temporal fabrication; Laocoön (Lessing 1766); progymnasmata; enargeia; manner of making (Theon via Webb 2009).

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: Lessing's 1766 media theory — poetry is temporal, painting spatial — turns out to describe the diffusion process better than any twentieth-century account: the shield, like a generated image, exists only as an unfolding of steps.

QUESTION: Why did ekphrasis theory keep the urn and lose the shield when the shield is the older and richer case?

DEEPER QUESTION: Is process-orientation an aesthetic preference or an ontological claim — i.e., is a generated image ever "finished," or is apparent finish just the arbitrary halting of an iterative procedure (no final perfect prompt, no final perfect frame)?

MECHANISM: Canon formation: rhetoric declines → ekphrasis narrows to poetry about art objects (Spitzer) → New Criticism enshrines the autonomous finished poem (Brooks 1947) → process cases become invisible → Bajohr/AI reopens the rhetorical, performative, processual lineage (Webb).

FORMAL SHIFT: From ekphrasis classified by object-type (description OF art) to ekphrasis classified by temporal structure (description AS making).

SOURCE FORMALISM: Lessing's temporal/spatial media distinction; Brooks's urn as figure for the autonomous verbal artifact.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Two paradigm functors on the category of descriptions: U (urn) maps a description to its terminal object and evaluates fidelity; S (shield) maps a description to its production trace and evaluates the unfolding. Twentieth-century theory ≈ image of U; operative ekphrasis is only visible under S.

TENSION: READING A (SYNTH-02/SEED-02): the urn/shield opposition is the paper's structural engine and should open the worldtext argument. READING B (the shipped drafts): BULKHEAD-06 and portugal-draft drop the urn entirely — Brooks survives only as a passing tag in the thick-description section, and the STATUS note admits "Brooks not formally cited in References." The strongest framing device was written twice in the KEEL BLOCKS and never installed in the paper.

MISSING: Keats's urn is named but never read; no confrontation with Heffernan's Museum of Words (cited in References, absent from argument), whose museum-frame is precisely the urn paradigm at institutional scale.

BOUNDARY: The opposition concerns emblems of critical programs, not a claim that all New Critical readings ignore process.

CITATION TRAIL: Lessing 2022 [1766] → Brooks 1947 (The Well Wrought Urn) → Spitzer via Webb 1999, 10 → Webb 2009/2016 (Theon on manner of making) → Taplin 1980 → Bajohr 2024.

TEST: Literature check: survey post-1990 ekphrasis studies for governing examples; if urn/Grecian-urn-type finished objects dominate over process cases, the canonization claim is corroborated.
