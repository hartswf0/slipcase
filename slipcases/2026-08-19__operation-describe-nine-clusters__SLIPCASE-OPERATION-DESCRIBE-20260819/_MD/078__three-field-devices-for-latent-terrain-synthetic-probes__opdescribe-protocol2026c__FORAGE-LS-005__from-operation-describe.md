TITLE: Three field devices for latent terrain: synthetic probes, latent space walks, trace archives

SOURCE: LATENT SPACE/Operative Ethnography Protocol Design.md — field devices sections and comparison table; LATENT SPACE/Latent Space Cosmos Integration Report.md — "Synthetic Probes and Latent Walks"

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] "a synthetic probe involves formalizing a specific input mechanism—such as a rigidly structured prompt pattern, a targeted image type, a logical procedure, or a deliberate jailbreaking technique—and launching it iteratively into the black-boxed model's latent space under highly controlled conditions." [QUOTE from device table] "**Latent Space Walk** | Mathematically interpolating between specific vector points. | To visualize the algorithmic transition and ambient drift between complex concepts. | Morphing an image from \"poor neighborhood\" to \"wealthy neighborhood\" to expose architectural biases." [QUOTE] "Generative models function essentially as massive, multimodal trace archives containing the mathematical representations, statistical weightings, and cultural residue of the global internet's data."

RESEARCH OBJECT: The instrumentarium of synthetic ethnography — named, repeatable devices for studying generative models as field sites.

LOCAL MOVE: Converts three engineering operations (structured prompting, vector interpolation, dataset genealogy) into ethnographic field devices with distinct objects: boundaries, drift, genealogy.

SOURCE TERMS: synthetic probe; latent space walk; trace archive; field devices; ambient drift; condition editors; digital exhaust; cultural residue; synthetic ethnography (de Seta, Pohjonen, Knuutila).

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: The model ceases to be the tool of research and becomes simultaneously field site, informant, and archive — the ethnographer's "village" is a weight matrix.

QUESTION: What are the controlled-conditions requirements that make a probe launch replicable across model versions?

DEEPER QUESTION: If the model is a trace archive of the internet, is probing it ethnography of the model or second-hand ethnography of the archived populations — and who is the informant owed ethical duties?

MECHANISM: Probe: fix an input schema, iterate at scale, read the output distribution as a map of boundaries and stereotypes (thousands of "a CEO" images → gender/racial bias map). Walk: interpolate v1→v2 in latent space, read the intermediate frames as the model's implicit ontology of the transition. Trace archive: treat weights as compressed data genealogy, audit what was amplified or excluded.

FORMAL SHIFT: From reading outputs as content to reading output *distributions and trajectories* as terrain features of a quasi-place.

SOURCE FORMALISM: A three-row device table: device / operation / purpose / example, plus the "condition editor" role description.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Probe = (schema S, iteration budget n, condition set C) → empirical distribution P_S; Walk = path γ(t) = decode((1−t)v1 + t·v2); Archive audit = inference from P_S back to training measure μ.

TENSION: The three devices pull in opposite epistemic directions: probes and walks are synchronic (what the model does now), the trace archive is diachronic (where the data came from) — and the inference from output distribution back to training corpus is exactly the step the agential-cut zettel says is foreclosed. The protocol never reconciles this.

MISSING: Sample-size guidance; inter-rater procedures for reading walks; any worked trace-archive audit (the indigenous-language exclusion example is hypothetical).

BOUNDARY: Devices specified for black-box access; white-box interpretability is explicitly the rival paradigm, not the method.

CITATION TRAIL: De Seta, Pohjonen and Knuutila, "Synthetic Probes: A Qualitative Experiment in Latent Space Exploration" (Sociologica, cited by URL in the file's works-cited); cultural probes in design research; Bolter's Augmented Environments Lab (AI as "algorithmic remediation" that "harvests, remixes, and spits back all the digitized media of the human past").

TEST: Fully specified and runnable: the probe procedure (fixed schema, iterated launch, distribution reading) is the region's most operationalized method — e.g. "Generating thousands of images of \"a CEO\" to map the model's gender and racial biases" is a checkable experiment with published analogues.

PLATFORM: Text-to-image models, LLMs, Genie 3-class world models (walks become literal navigation).
