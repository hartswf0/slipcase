ID: FORAGE-PT-027

TITLE: An audit trail records the path taken through what was available and can never record the cut that decided what was available

SOURCE: Collision of PROGRAMS/agential.json (the apparatus performs agential cuts) with PROGRAMS/memex.json (auditable intellectual traversal as the ethical standard); with PROGRAMS/mmt.json on projection

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] agential.json: "<generative_AI_system> is an <apparatus> that [performs] <agential_cuts>" and "This [hides] the deeper operation: <system> [makes] <worlds_actionable>" [QUOTE] memex.json: "Can a system show not only <what it says>, but <how its path through the record was built>?" [QUOTE] mmt.json: "What disciplined arrangement lets one system be laid against another and tested?"

RESEARCH OBJECT: A structural limit on auditability. A trail is a path through a space of available options. A cut is what constituted the space. The trail can be complete and honest about every step while remaining silent about everything the cut excluded — and the exclusions leave no trace, because a trace is made of what was there.

LOCAL MOVE: This zettel puts the corpus's ethical standard against its ontological claim and finds that the standard cannot reach the operation the claim says matters most.

SOURCE TERMS: agential cut / makes worlds actionable / hides / path through the record / auditable traversal / disciplined arrangement / laid against

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: Auditability is the field's default remedy for almost everything — provenance, logging, citation, model cards. All of it audits *within* a constituted space. The most consequential operation, deciding what counts as an available option, is systematically outside the reach of the remedy, and its invisibility is not a tooling gap but a consequence of what a trace is.

QUESTION: Can a cut be documented at all, given that documenting an exclusion requires already having the excluded thing available to name?

DEEPER QUESTION: The only apparent route is comparative: run two differently-cut apparatuses on the same material and read the difference. Cuts would then be visible only in *contrast*, never in a single system's records — which makes auditing a solitary system an in-principle inadequate method, not merely an incomplete one.

MECHANISM: <WORLD> -> [CUT: what counts as an object, a category, a candidate] -> <SPACE OF AVAILABLE OPTIONS> -> [TRAVERSAL] -> <TRAIL> -> [AUDIT READS TRAIL] -> every step accounted for -> <THE CUT NEVER APPEARS, HAVING LEFT NO ITEM BEHIND>

FORMAL SHIFT: <CUT> -> <CONSTITUTED SPACE> -> <TRAIL WITHIN IT> -> [AUDIT] -> <COMPLETE RECORD OF AN INCOMPLETE WORLD>

SOURCE FORMALISM: NONE — the collision is between an ontological claim and a procedural standard.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Let C constitute option space S, and let a trail T be a sequence in S. audit(T) verifies steps within S and is silent on S itself. Define cut_visibility = symmetric difference of S1 and S2 for two apparatuses on the same material. Cuts are measurable only across systems, never inside one.

TENSION: READING A: cuts can be partially documented by declaring the taxonomy, the exclusions, the thresholds — the corpus elsewhere proposes exactly such a levelled cut taxonomy, so the problem is tooling. READING B: declaring a cut requires standing outside it, and any declaration is made from within a further cut, so documentation regresses and comparison is the only purchase.

MISSING: Any comparative audit anywhere. Every provenance regime audits one system's internal record.

BOUNDARY: Barad's cut concerns the constitution of objects in measurement; whether a taxonomy declaration counts as documenting a cut is precisely what is in dispute and cannot be settled by citing her.

CITATION TRAIL: Barad, Meeting the Universe Halfway. Bush, "As We May Think" (1945). Bowker and Star, Sorting Things Out, on residual categories — the closest existing account of what a classification hides. [[FORAGE-PT-012]] [[FORAGE-PT-019]] [[FORAGE-PT-008]]

TEST: Two systems with different training or policy cuts, one identical query set. Record each option space. The symmetric difference is a cut made visible — the first measurement of an operation currently claimed to be central and treated as unobservable.
