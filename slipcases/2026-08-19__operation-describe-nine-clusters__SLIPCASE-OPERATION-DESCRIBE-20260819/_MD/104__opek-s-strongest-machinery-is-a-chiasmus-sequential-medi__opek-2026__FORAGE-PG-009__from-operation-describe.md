TITLE: opek's strongest machinery is a chiasmus: sequential media have pragmatics-without-semantics, connectionist media semantics-without-pragmatics — and the theory knows this distinction is its single point of failure

SOURCE: PROGRAMS/opek.json — <invariants> I07, I08, I10, I12; <states>; <Change_Test> scenario C03_REJECT_ARTIFICIAL_SEMANTICS; Theory_Code_Mapping <comments>

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] "I07: <SequentialDigitalMedium> [has] <technical pragmatics> without <machine semantics>. I08: <ConnectionistDigitalMedium> [has] <artificial semantics> without <explicit command pragmatics>."; "I12: <text/image collapse> occurs at <model-level>, not necessarily at <cultural-experience-level>."; C03: "What changes if <ArtificialSemantics> is rejected? ... <consequence> <paper> loses its strongest distinction between <sequential code> and <connectionist model>. <repair> Replace 'AI means weakly' with: <AI> [correlates] <text> and <image> operationally..."; comment: "<ArtificialSemantics> must stay weak. Its weakness prevents hype; its consequence prevents dismissal."

RESEARCH OBJECT: The three-regime media theory in opek.json (analogue / sequential digital / connectionist digital) whose hinge is a crossed pair of privatives — code executes but does not mean; models mean (weakly) but do not command — plus the theory's own contingency plan (C03) for the day its central concept is refuted.

LOCAL MOVE: Extract the chiasmus as a 2×2 and read C03 as the theory pre-writing its own obituary and repair — the only place in the corpus where a change test targets the theory's core concept rather than its application domain.

SOURCE TERMS: ArtificialSemantics; technical pragmatics; three media regimes; word-image-separated; EkphrasticHope/EkphrasticFear; model-level collapse; substrate-aware reading

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: "Weakness" is load-bearing. ArtificialSemantics is defined by four negations (weak, nonhuman, nonintentional, consequential) and the comments say its weakness is functional: too strong → hype (failure F01), too weak → dismissal (F02). The concept is engineered to sit on a knife edge, and the constraint set (<semantic_constraint>) legislates that it stay there — an invariant about a concept's rhetorical temperature, unique in the corpus.

QUESTION: Is "semantics without pragmatics" coherent? On most philosophical accounts (and on uam's), semantics divorced from any command/use context is exactly what "mere correlation" means — so is I08 a discovery or a relabeling of F02's "empty syntax" with a friendlier name?

DEEPER QUESTION: I12 places the collapse at model-level while culture keeps the distinction — but ELEPHANT_OS (eos.json) claims the operative relation is substrate-independent and instantiable in "carbon, code, gap_between_them," which would dissolve opek's regime boundaries from below. Does the fourth regime (human-as-substrate) break the 2×2 or complete it?

MECHANISM: Genealogical stress-test: visual poetry archive (typewriter poem → shell-script poem → DALL-E poem) as three experimental conditions varying the production operation while holding the artifact class fixed.

FORMAL SHIFT: From ekphrasis as representation-relation (word describes image) to ekphrasis as causal production relation with a scoping rule: "<OperativeEkphrasis> applies only when <text> [causally-produces] <image>" — a humanities concept given an applicability predicate and an overextension failure mode (F05).

SOURCE FORMALISM: Invariant list I01–I12; 43-step ArgumentProgram; failure conditions F01–F10.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Regimes as points in {sem, prag} ⊆ {0,1}²: analogue (0,0) at machine level; sequential (0,1); connectionist (1,0); human interpreter (1,1). The chiasmus is the anti-diagonal. eos.json's human-as-substrate regime routes a (1,1) agent into the executor role, occupying (0,1) by discipline — showing the coordinates attach to roles in an operation, not to substrates. That reframing preserves opek's grid but refutes its substrate indexing, which is precisely what eos claims ("Proves operative ekphrasis is substrate-independent").

TENSION: opek needs ArtificialSemantics to be real enough to differentiate model from script (contra dismissal) while uam-style critics inside the corpus (I10: not HumanSemantics; dse: fluency without accountability) push it toward the correlation-only reading that C03 concedes would cost "the paper's strongest distinction." The corpus holds both: opek's I08 and its own pre-authorized retreat.

MISSING: A criterion separating "weak semantics" from "strong correlation" that is not purely rhetorical; an account of instruction-tuned models where prompts regain command character (pragmatics returning to the connectionist cell, breaking the anti-diagonal).

BOUNDARY: The chiasmus is claimed for text↔image generation; change tests C01/C02 extend to video/sound with invariants preserved, but nothing covers text→text generation, where the corpus's other theories (tenne, argue) operate.

CITATION TRAIL: Classical ekphrasis theory (Mitchell's ekphrastic hope/fear) → opek's S02; concrete/visual poetry archive → S03; CLIP/DALL-E multimodal architecture → I06, A04; eos.json CROSS_REFERENCES dependency on opek ("introduces fourth media regime").

TEST: Operationalize I07/I08: (a) show a shell script whose output covaries with non-semantic permutations of its source (pragmatics, no semantics); (b) show a multimodal model whose outputs covary with meaning-preserving paraphrase but not with command form (semantics, no pragmatics); (c) run the same probes on an instruction-tuned model — if it covaries with both, the anti-diagonal is broken and opek needs a fifth regime.

PLATFORM: Any text-to-image API plus a scripting environment; paraphrase-invariance probes.
