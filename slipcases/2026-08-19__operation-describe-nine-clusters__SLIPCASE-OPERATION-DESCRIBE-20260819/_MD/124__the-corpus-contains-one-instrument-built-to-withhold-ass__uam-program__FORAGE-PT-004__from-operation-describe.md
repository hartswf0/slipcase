ID: FORAGE-PT-004

TITLE: The corpus contains one instrument built to withhold assent, and nothing specifies what a permanently unresolved conflict routes

SOURCE: PROGRAMS/uam.json — USE_AGAINST_MEANING, <Purpose>

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] "<paper> [must-model] <philosophical conflict> where <philosophical conflict> := <use> [claims-to-explain] <meaning>"

RESEARCH OBJECT: A diagnostic whose deliverable is a standing objection rather than a reading. Every other instrument in the set converts an artifact into a description; this one converts a claim into a suspended judgment.

LOCAL MOVE: It stages rather than settles — it models the conflict as a conflict, refusing the use-theory the neighbouring instruments presuppose.

SOURCE TERMS: philosophical conflict / claims-to-explain / use / meaning

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: The set's other instruments *need* the use-theory to work. Concept-possession is defined as factor-in-use; thick programming reads symbols by what they make happen; executable semantics turns utterance into operation. All of them assume use explains meaning. One member of the set exists to deny it.

QUESTION: When a suite deploys both this instrument and a use-theoretic one on the same artifact, what does the reading look like at the seam?

DEEPER QUESTION: Does an instrument that never concludes still route action — by making the operator hesitate — and if so, is hesitation the smallest observable unit of an operative description?

MECHANISM: <ARTIFACT> -> [USE-THEORETIC READING] -> operational account of meaning -> [UAM APPLIED] -> objection: use may describe without explaining -> <OPERATOR MUST RULE> -> ruling is the actual output

FORMAL SHIFT: <CLAIM> -> <STAGED CONFLICT> -> [NO RESOLUTION] -> <OPERATOR RULING AS THE REAL PRODUCT>

SOURCE FORMALISM: NONE beyond the conflict statement.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Distinguish DESCRIBE (use predicts meaning) from EXPLAIN (use constitutes meaning). Most operational theories only need the first; they habitually claim the second. uam is the guard on that slippage.

TENSION: READING A: uam is a necessary brake — without it the suite becomes uncritically pragmatist. READING B: an instrument that cannot conclude cannot be deployed, and its presence is decorative rigour.

MISSING: Any recorded deployment. Any rule for how a ruling at the seam is made or logged.

BOUNDARY: This is about the instrument's function inside a set, not about whether use-theories of meaning are correct.

CITATION TRAIL: Wittgenstein, Philosophical Investigations, on meaning and use; Ryle, "Use, Usage and Meaning" (1961) — the capital/trade distinction, which is a *describe* not *explain* move. [[FORAGE-PT-001]]

TEST: Deploy a use-theoretic instrument alone on one artifact, then with uam alongside. Record whether the second reading differs. If it never differs, the brake is not engaged.
