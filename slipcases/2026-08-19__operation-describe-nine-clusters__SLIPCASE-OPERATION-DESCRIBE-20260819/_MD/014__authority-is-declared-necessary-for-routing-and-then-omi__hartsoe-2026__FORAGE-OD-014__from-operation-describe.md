TITLE:
AUTHORITY IS DECLARED NECESSARY FOR ROUTING AND THEN OMITTED FROM EVERY FORMALISM IN THE ARCHIVE

RESEARCH OBJECT:
If routing requires authority, then operativity is not a property of D. It is a property of the pair ⟨D, enforcement regime⟩.

Every formal expression in the archive writes it as a property of D alone. The word "authority" appears in the political chapters and in no equation.

LOCAL MOVE:
The archive uses authority to *defend* the concept against the objection that it collapses into "all language does stuff." Authority is the scarcity condition that keeps operativity non-trivial.

Having done that work, authority is dropped before the model is written.

SOURCE TERMS:

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The archive's boundary defense and its formal model are load-bearing in opposite directions.

The boundary needs authority to be a necessary condition (so that most descriptions are non-operative).
The model needs operativity to be measurable from D and O alone (so that the experiment is runnable).

Both cannot hold. If authority is necessary, the tool-schema experiment measures something other than operativity — because a JSON schema has no authority over anything.

QUESTION:
Is the model's compliance with a tool description a case of authority at all, or is it the archive's first genuine case of routing *without* authority?

DEEPER QUESTION:
If routing without authority is possible, what remains of the political claim — and if it is not, what exactly authorizes a schema?

MECHANISM:
Hetero-authorized routing (GitHub, moderation):
<DESCRIPTION> + <INSTITUTIONAL SANCTION FOR NON-COMPLIANCE> → route

Trained compliance (tool schemas):
<DESCRIPTION> + <NO SANCTION, NO OBLIGATION, NO RECOGNITION OF THE AUTHOR> → route anyway

The second has the same output and none of the political structure. It is obedience without a sovereign.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<DESCRIPTION>
→ <ENFORCEMENT REGIME>
→ [AUTHORIZED ROUTE]
→ <SANCTIONABLE ACTION>

vs.

<DESCRIPTION>
→ <TRAINED DISPOSITION>
→ [UNAUTHORIZED ROUTE]
→ <ACTION WITH NO ADDRESSABLE AUTHORITY>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE. There is no authority term anywhere in the archive's formal apparatus.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

  ΔG(D) = g(D, O, S, κ)

where κ is the enforcement regime: sanction probability, recognition of the author's standing, availability of appeal.

Three regimes worth separating, none currently distinguished in the archive:

  κ_sanction    — comply or be punished (moderation, employment)
  κ_convention  — comply because coordination requires it (labels, standards)
  κ_disposition — comply because trained to (models; also habituated humans)

The archive's politics assumes κ_sanction. Its primary case is κ_disposition. Its scale case is κ_convention.

Three cases, three regimes, one undifferentiated word.

TENSION:
READING A: trained disposition *is* authority, exercised at training time by whoever curated the data — so authority is present, merely displaced in time.
READING B: calling a trained disposition "authority" empties the term of the sanction-and-contestation structure that made it politically interesting; it renames a statistical regularity as a power relation.

Reading A rescues the political claim at the cost of making authority unfalsifiable. Reading B keeps authority sharp at the cost of the primary case.

MISSING:
Any test that varies κ while holding D fixed. This is easy for human operators (vary whether the label is advisory or mandatory) and currently unimagined in the archive.

BOUNDARY:
This does not show the archive's politics is wrong. It shows the politics and the primary case are about different phenomena, and that the dissertation currently has no term marking the difference.

CITATION TRAIL:

PAPERS/naur.md §3 "The False Comfort of Method" — on rules that bind without sanction.

TEST:
For a human-operator case, run identical labels under two framings: advisory ("suggested triage") and mandatory ("required triage"). Same label text, same objects, different κ.

Any ΔG difference is a pure authority effect, and it would be the first measurement of κ in the archive.
