FORAGE-OD-025

TITLE:
A LAW WITH A NUMERIC THRESHOLD AND NO MEASUREMENT PROCEDURE STILL ROUTES, WHICH MEANS OPERATIVITY DOES NOT REQUIRE COMPUTABILITY

SOURCE:
OPERATION DESCRIBE repository — PROGRAMS/CLAUDE.md, "CONSTITUTIONAL LAWS," LAW 3 (mtime 2026-04-27)

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
"LAW 3: <no addition without subtraction>. Every new entity, distinction, or theory admitted to the corpus must be justified against existing coverage. If it overlaps >80% with an existing theory, it must merge or the existing theory must retire."

RESEARCH OBJECT:
An 80% threshold with no defined similarity metric, no unit, no comparison procedure, and no adjudicator other than the operator whose behavior it governs.

The law cannot be executed. It is nonetheless plausibly effective: it makes the author hesitate before adding a theory, which is exactly the route it was written to create.

A description can route by *appearing* executable.

LOCAL MOVE:
CLAUDE.md is written in the register of a specification: numbered laws, angle-bracket notation, "inviolable," a registration gate with eight pass/fail requirements.

The register borrows the authority of formal systems without incurring their obligations.

SOURCE TERMS:

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The archive's boundary work assumes operativity comes from either enforcement or from the operator processing the description's content.

LAW 3 has neither. It has no enforcement and no processable content — 80% of what, measured how? What it has is *form*. It looks like a rule that could be checked, and that appearance does the work.

This is a third source of routing power, alongside authority and semantics, and the archive has no name for it.

QUESTION:
How much of an operative description's power comes from its genre rather than its content — from looking like a specification, a policy, a schema, a law?

DEEPER QUESTION:
If genre routes, then the archive's own documents are evidence about their own subject in a second way: every time the author reached for numbered laws and angle brackets, they were exercising the mechanism they are trying to theorize.

MECHANISM:
<DESCRIPTION IN SPECIFICATION GENRE>
→ operator recognizes the genre
→ [GENRE IMPLIES A CHECK EXISTS]
→ operator pre-complies to avoid an anticipated check
→ <ROUTE OCCURS WITHOUT THE CHECK EVER RUNNING>

The check's existence is never tested, because pre-compliance removes the occasion.

FORMAL SHIFT:

→ <SPECIFICATION GENRE>
→ [ANTICIPATED ENFORCEMENT]
→ <PRE-COMPLIANCE>
→ <ROUTE>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
The document supplies notation — `<entity>`, `[morphism]`, `{group}` — and eight laws each paired with a failure condition. It supplies no metric for LAW 3 and no procedure for the "Explanation Gate" that LAW 4 requires.

Compare LAW 5 ("every theory must state what it cannot do"), which *is* checkable by inspection for a named section. The document contains both checkable and uncheckable laws, undifferentiated, in one numbered list.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Three-way split the archive lacks:

  EXECUTABLE  : a procedure exists and is run          (LAW 5: grep for the section)
  CHECKABLE   : a procedure exists and is not run      (LAW 7: pair each invariant with a failure mode)
  INVOCABLE   : no procedure exists; the form suffices (LAW 3: >80% overlap)

Prediction: invocable descriptions have *higher* apparent compliance than executable ones, because non-compliance is never detected and the operator self-reports.

That prediction, if it holds, is a warning about every audit regime that publishes thresholds without metrics — which is most of AI governance.

TENSION:
READING A: LAW 3 does route, via anticipated enforcement, and is therefore operative.
READING B: LAW 3 routes nothing; the author never added a theory that would have triggered it, so there is no observed instance and the appearance of routing is confabulated.

Smallest discriminating evidence: find a theory in /PROGRAMS/ that *was* considered and not added, or a merge that occurred. Nineteen program theories exist. If none was ever retired or merged, LAW 3 has no observed effect and Reading B holds.

MISSING:
Any record of a rejected or retired program theory. The corpus has 19 registered theories and no visible graveyard. Absence of a graveyard is evidence either that LAW 3 never bound, or that it bound perfectly — and those are indistinguishable without the author's notes.

This is the "negative space" problem in its sharpest form: a perfectly effective prohibition and a completely ineffective one leave the same trace.

BOUNDARY:
One law, one repository, no observed trigger. The three-way split is proposed, not demonstrated.

TEST:
Classify all eight CONSTITUTIONAL LAWS as executable, checkable, or invocable. Then check compliance for the executable ones only.

If compliance on executable laws is *lower* than the author's self-assessed compliance overall, the invocable laws are absorbing credit they did not earn — and the archive has measured genre-effect for the first time.
