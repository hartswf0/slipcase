ID: FORAGE-PA-007

TITLE: Computers as media of commitment — and the generative return of the rationalist dream

SOURCE: PAPERS/winnograd.md, sections 3, 6, 7 ("Language is not information"; "Computers as media of commitment"; "The return of rationalist AI in generative form")

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] "The problem is not that the machine is 'fake' and the human is 'real.' That opposition is too crude. The problem is that generative systems operate within human commitment structures while being ontologically exempt from commitment. They can trigger obligations without holding them. They can influence decisions without being answerable. They can produce language acts without being social actors in the full sense." And: [QUOTE] "Generative AI has revived the old dream in a new aesthetic. ... beneath the new surface, an old confusion returns: the confusion of linguistic performance with understanding."

RESEARCH OBJECT: Two fused claims. (1) The reframe: computers are not artificial minds but "media for human coordination" — design goal shifts from autonomy to accountability ("make commitments visible, make interpretation possible, make repair available"). Work is "a web of commitments held together by language" (Austin via conversations-for-action). (2) The diagnosis that generative AI re-runs rationalism "in probabilistic dress," making the 1986 critique more, not less, necessary — this section is effectively an unwritten paper.

LOCAL MOVE: Relocates AI alignment: [QUOTE] "'AI alignment' remains incomplete if it focuses only on model behavior. Alignment must also be a design problem about conversations, institutions, interfaces, and commitments. A model may produce a safe answer, but the surrounding system may still misallocate responsibility."

SOURCE TERMS: conversations for action; media of commitment; commitment-centered systems; ontological exemption; magic (as design pathology: "Magic hides labor, hides uncertainty, hides judgment"); prediction vs understanding; output vs commitment

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: Fluency itself — "The simulation of confidence is not the same as responsibility. The system may predict the next word; it does not thereby inhabit the consequences of the promise that word may make." Delegation becomes suspect: the question is "what happens to the structure of commitment when work passes through the system."

QUESTION: Who is speaking when an AI drafts an email — and can an interface typology (suggestion/decision, draft/declaration, prediction/verification, simulation/record, assistance/authorization) be enforced technically rather than by convention?

DEEPER QUESTION: Can commitment be re-attached to generative output at all — via signatures, provenance, institutional countersigning — or is ontological exemption structural, such that every added accountability layer merely relocates the exemption?

MECHANISM: Speech acts create social facts (promise → obligation; declaration → institutional reality). Generative systems emit act-shaped tokens into commitment webs; recipients' uptake creates real obligations; no node in the system can hold, discharge, or be sanctioned for them. Institutions then "redesign human practices around systems that simulate participation without bearing responsibility" — misallocation compounds: interface performs confidence, workflow pressures acceptance, no practice of correction exists.

FORMAL SHIFT: From alignment-as-model-property to alignment-as-commitment-topology: the object of evaluation is the distribution of obligation across user, interface, institution, and model, not output safety.

SOURCE FORMALISM: Austin's locutionary/illocutionary/perlocutionary machinery and Winograd-Flores conversation-for-action loops, invoked not diagrammed.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] A commitment graph G = (actors ∪ systems, obligations); speech-act edges may only originate at accountability-bearing nodes. Generative output = edge proposal from a non-bearing node; a design is commitment-sound iff every proposal is countersigned by a bearing node before uptake. "Ontological exemption" = systems are structurally non-bearing.

TENSION: Direct inversion of bajohr.md's chiasmus (FORAGE-PA-002): there, connectionist systems have "semantics without pragmatics"; here they exert pragmatic force (trigger obligations) while lacking the standing to hold it. The two papers split "pragmatics" into force vs answerability without noticing. ricoeur.md dissolves the tension from a third side: meaning never belonged to the intending agent anyway — the trace, not the commitment-holder, is the unit of analysis.

MISSING: Any engagement with legal personhood/agency law, the obvious adjacent literature; also no positive example of a commitment-centered generative interface actually built.

BOUNDARY: Explicitly not a claim that generative AI is trivial or fake — "These systems are extraordinarily powerful cultural and technical artifacts"; the critique targets the surrounding practice redesign, not capability.

CITATION TRAIL: Winograd & Flores (1986); Austin, *How to Do Things with Words*; Heidegger; Maturana/Varela; Dreyfus; Suchman; the section 9 seven-principle program (prediction≠understanding; output≠commitment; design for breakdown; preserve situated judgment; visible provenance; language as action; reject artificial personhood as master metaphor).

TEST: Field experiment: same model, two interfaces — one framing outputs as drafts requiring countersignature with visible provenance, one oracle-style; measure obligation misattribution ("the AI decided") in downstream incident reports.

PLATFORM: Email/document assistants, medical-note summarizers, agentic schedulers — the source's own examples.
