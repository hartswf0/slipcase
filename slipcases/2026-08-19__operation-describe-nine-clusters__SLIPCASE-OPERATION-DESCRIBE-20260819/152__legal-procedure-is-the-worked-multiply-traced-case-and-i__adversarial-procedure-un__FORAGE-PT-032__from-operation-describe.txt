TITLE: Legal procedure is the worked multiply-traced case, and its design concedes that the invariant under inscription is thin

SOURCE: Adversarial testimonial procedure — deposition, cross-examination, and corroboration requirements in common-law practice [UNVERIFIED for specific jurisdictional rules]; read against PROGRAMS/mot.json rescue schema and PROGRAMS/tah.json validation formula

PASSAGE: [PARAPHRASE] Legal systems that must reconstruct a vanished event do not rely on a single record. They take multiple independent accounts, subject each to adversarial questioning, and treat agreement across independent accounts as the ground of reliability while treating any single account as insufficient. [QUOTE] mot.json: "<action> [vanishes as] <event> <trace> [rescues] <the-done>"

RESEARCH OBJECT: An institution built on the assumption the parent could not evaluate. If a single trace rescued the deed, cross-examination would be redundant and corroboration requirements pointless. The existence and elaborateness of adversarial procedure is standing evidence that practitioners who must actually reconstruct events found the single-trace invariant too thin to rely on.

LOCAL MOVE: This child executes the parent's test by finding the domain where multiply-tracing one event is not a research proposal but a centuries-old requirement, and reads the procedure as a measurement of the invariant's size.

SOURCE TERMS: deposition / cross-examination / corroboration / independent account / impeachment / the courtroom of interpretation

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: The corpus's own hermeneutic instrument already invokes "the courtroom of interpretation" as a metaphor for validation. The courtroom is not a metaphor for the validation formula; it is an implementation of it, with the two missing parameters supplied by rule — how many independent accounts, and how much time. Those rules exist, vary by jurisdiction, and were arrived at by adjustment against error.

QUESTION: What do corroboration rules imply about the size of the invariant — how many independent traces do institutions that bear the cost of error actually require?

DEEPER QUESTION: Legal procedure also supplies something neither instrument has: a mechanism for *impeaching* a trace, which means it treats traces as differentially reliable rather than as uniform rescues. Does the corpus need a reliability weight on each trace, and what would set it?

MECHANISM: <EVENT VANISHES> -> [MULTIPLE INDEPENDENT INSCRIPTIONS TAKEN] -> [EACH SUBJECTED TO ADVERSARIAL QUESTIONING] -> [AGREEMENT AND DISAGREEMENT MAPPED] -> <FINDING, PROVISIONAL AND APPEALABLE>

FORMAL SHIFT: <SINGLE TRACE AS RESCUE> -> <PLURAL TRACES UNDER ADVERSARIAL TEST> -> [CORROBORATION THRESHOLD] -> <FINDING WITH A STATED STANDARD OF PROOF>

SOURCE FORMALISM: Corroboration requirements and standards of proof are stated rules in legal practice; they are not quoted here and vary by jurisdiction and matter type.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Read a jurisdiction's corroboration rule as an empirical estimate of k in the validation formula, and its standard of proof as a threshold on the confidence that plural agreement licenses. Then k is not unknown — it has been set, revised, and litigated for centuries in the one institution that pays for getting it wrong.

TENSION: READING A: legal procedure is evidence that the invariant is thin and that plurality is mandatory, so the parent's rescue claim is weak and tah's formula is right to require rival readings. READING B: legal procedure is adversarial because parties have *interests*, not because traces are thin; remove the incentive to lie and a single honest record may be perfectly adequate — in which case the procedure measures adversarial pressure, not inscriptional loss.

MISSING: Any verified statement of a specific corroboration rule. Any case where independent traces of an uncontested event were compared for content overlap rather than for consistency of testimony.

BOUNDARY: Legal reliability rules are shaped by policy, cost and rights as much as by epistemics. Reading them as measurements of an epistemic quantity requires an argument this child has not made.

CITATION TRAIL: [[FORAGE-PT-005]] and [[FORAGE-PT-006]] -> adversarial testimonial procedure -> corroboration thresholds as an estimate of k -> next: eyewitness-reliability research, which measures independent-account agreement directly and reports it to be low.

TEST: Find one uncontested event with several independent contemporaneous records taken without adversarial pressure — a documented meeting, a logged incident. Compute content overlap. Low overlap under no incentive to distort supports Reading A; high overlap supports Reading B and relocates the courtroom's function to interest rather than inscription.
