TITLE: Latent bricolage and epistemic humility: legacy spatial theory breaks on three false assumptions

SOURCE: LATENT SPACE/Latent Space_ Bricolage and Humility.md — "Introduction: The Category Error of Legacy Spatial Theory", "Part V: The Imperative of Epistemic Humility", and the Lévi-Strauss section

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] "They operate on three fundamental assumptions: first, that space is stable enough to be definitively mapped; second, that architecture is authored enough to be controlled by a human master-planner; and third, that environments are material enough to leave a reliable, indexical historical trace." [QUOTE] "The Bricoleur, conversely, is a tinkerer who makes do with \"whatever is at hand\"." [QUOTE] "Interacting with a generative AI model is fundamentally an act of **Latent Bricolage**." [QUOTE] "Entering a generative world model armed with Lynch's demand for objective legibility, Foucault's search for panoptic intent, or Gieryn's quest for physical truth spots ensures analytical failure."

RESEARCH OBJECT: The failure conditions of twentieth-century spatial/media theory when applied to N-dimensional probability manifolds, and the operator posture that survives them.

LOCAL MOVE: Diagnoses a triple category error (mappability, authorship, materiality) and swaps the Engineer/master-architect for Lévi-Strauss's bricoleur as the only viable operator inside latent space.

SOURCE TERMS: latent bricolage; epistemic humility; category error; Engineer vs. Bricoleur (The Savage Mind); master architect; N-dimensional probability manifold; semantic scraps; ontological rupture.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: Expertise inverts — the theorist with the strongest framework (Lynch, Foucault) is the *worst* equipped observer, guaranteed to hallucinate authorship and stability where there is only probability.

QUESTION: Which specific Lynch/Foucault/Offenhuber predictions fail inside a world model — is "analytical failure" demonstrated or asserted?

DEEPER QUESTION: Is epistemic humility a temporary posture (until interpretability matures) or a permanent condition entailed by high-dimensional emergence?

MECHANISM: The operator cannot access source geometry (no polygon edit, no texture file), so control reduces to recombining what the prompt-interface affords: semantic scraps, style fragments, found affordances — ad-hoc synthesis under uninterpretable constraints. Humility = surrendering the demand for legibility, intent, and material trace before analysis begins.

FORMAL SHIFT: From top-down specification (blueprint → world) to opportunistic composition (repertoire → collage); design becomes search within an unknown affordance landscape.

SOURCE FORMALISM: Lévi-Strauss's Engineer/Bricoleur binary from The Savage Mind (1962), applied to prompting.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Engineer: choose w = argmax over a known model; Bricoleur: greedy local search over an unknown affordance function using only the at-hand repertoire R — prompting as R-restricted stochastic hill-climbing.

TENSION: This file's rejection of Gieryn ("quest for physical truth spots ensures analytical failure") sits against the Quasi-Place file's constructive use of Gieryn as its foundational rubric — the region's two flagship documents disagree about whether legacy sociology is a usable audit or a broken instrument. Also rival reading: "disorientation is epistemic and mathematical, rather than purely socio-economic" is a direct attack on the LA School reduction, not a neutral synthesis.

MISSING: A demonstration case of a legacy theory failing empirically; criteria distinguishing humility from resignation; engagement with interpretability progress that might restore partial legibility.

BOUNDARY: Scoped to generative world models (Genie 3 exemplar); the file concedes legacy theories retain "immense historical utility" for built environments.

CITATION TRAIL: Claude Lévi-Strauss, La Pensée Sauvage (1962); Kevin Lynch (imageability); Michel Foucault (panopticism); Dietmar Offenhuber; Gieryn; DeepMind Genie 3 (24 fps real-time photorealistic claim).

TEST: The Genie 3 capability claim is checkable ("synthesizing dynamic, photorealistic, and interactive environments in real-time at twenty-four frames per second"); the category-error thesis is testable only indirectly — e.g., do Lynch-style cognitive-map experiments fail inside generated worlds where they succeed in authored game worlds?

PLATFORM: Genie 3-class world models; prompt-driven generation interfaces.
