ID: FORAGE-PT-024

TITLE: The corpus contains two Geertzes because culture-as-program and action-after-the-event run on clocks eight orders of magnitude apart

SOURCE: Collision of PROGRAMS/thick.json (culture is an extra-genetic instruction system) with PROGRAMS/tda.json (how meaningful action survives the event); pairing registered in PROGRAMS/meta.json <cross_references> as "tda.json <-> thick.json"

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] thick.json: "<culture> [is] <extra-genetic instruction system>" and "<human> [is] <unfinished animal completed through cultural programs>" [QUOTE] tda.json: "How does <meaningful_action> survive after <the_event> disappears?" with the wink rules: "IF <eyelid_contraction> [prepares] <future_parody> THEN OUTPUT <rehearsal>"

RESEARCH OBJECT: One thinker formalised twice, incompatibly, because two different temporal objects were extracted. thick takes the *constitutive* Geertz: programs acquired across a life that make a person capable. tda takes the *momentary* Geertz: a single eyelid contraction whose meaning must be reconstructed after it is gone.

Both are in Geertz. Neither is the other. And the corpus deploys them in one suite.

LOCAL MOVE: This zettel replaces the apparent contradiction (culture is / is not a program) with a timescale diagnosis, which is repairable where a flat contradiction is not.

SOURCE TERMS: extra-genetic instruction system / unfinished animal / cultural programs / meaningful action after the event / eyelid contraction / rehearsal

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: The word program is doing two jobs. In thick it means something acquired over years that constitutes a capacity — closer to a compiler than an instruction. In tda the unit is an act lasting a fraction of a second whose intentional depth must be recovered. Calling both "program" produces the appearance of one theory and the behaviour of two.

QUESTION: Is there one mechanism at two rates, or two mechanisms — one that *makes* an agent and one that *steers* one?

DEEPER QUESTION: If they are two, then every claim of the form "AI is culture intensified" equivocates: intensifying a steering signal and intensifying a constitutive program are different interventions with different politics. The first changes what someone does today; the second changes who they become.

MECHANISM: Constitutive: <SYMBOLIC PROGRAM> -> [ACQUIRED OVER YEARS, ENFORCED BY A COMMUNITY] -> <CAPACITY> -> conduct follows from capacity. Momentary: <ACT> -> [READ NOW AT SOME INTENTIONAL DEPTH] -> <MEANING ASSIGNED> -> next act. The first changes the reader; the second uses one.

FORMAL SHIFT: <CULTURE> -> <TWO OBJECTS AT TWO RATES> -> [INDEX EVERY CLAIM BY TIMESCALE] -> <EQUIVOCATION DISSOLVED>

SOURCE FORMALISM: thick's is-not/is contrast pairs; tda's IF/THEN wink rules.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Index by exponent of seconds. 10^0: read a wink. 10^3: a session's accumulated frame. 10^6: habituation, the trained eye. 10^8: enculturation. thick sits at 10^8; tda at 10^0. Nothing in the corpus occupies 10^3 to 10^6 — which is exactly where habituation, fine-tuning and institutional drift live.

TENSION: READING A: one mechanism, differing only in accumulation — repeated momentary readings become a disposition, so the two Geertzes are the same theory sampled at two rates. READING B: constitution is not accumulation; being made able to see winks is categorically unlike seeing one, and no amount of the second yields the first.

MISSING: Any object in the corpus at the middle timescales. Any exposure-history variable in any instrument.

BOUNDARY: This is a diagnosis of two formalisations, not a claim about which reading of Geertz is correct.

CITATION TRAIL: Geertz, "The Impact of the Concept of Culture on the Concept of Man" and "Thick Description," in The Interpretation of Cultures (1973) — read for whether he permits short-run control. Bourdieu on habitus for the empty middle. [[FORAGE-PT-007]] [[FORAGE-PT-018]]

TEST: Deploy both instruments on one artifact and record the operator's ruling at the seam. If the ruling is always "different timescale, both apply," the repair is an index. If the readings genuinely conflict about the same act at the same scale, the contradiction is real and one instrument must retire.
