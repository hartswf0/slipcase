ID: FORAGE-ZP-011

TITLE: The non-sovereign prompt: system prompts as the constitution of the worldtext, the buried law beneath the visible petition

SOURCE: Z-Port/final-draft-v3.md §3 (fullest version); Z-Port/worldtext-after-ekphrasis.md §5 ("Hidden Prompts, Buried Laws"); Z-Port/image-is-not-end-of-ekphrasis.md §§6–7; Z-Port/final-draft-v4.md ("The Shield as Worldtext")

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] "A system prompt is a buried law; the user prompt is only the visible petition. Together, these system prompts are the constitution of the worldtext — the governing instructions that specify what the world permits, what it forbids, what it defaults to, and how it responds to user input. The user's prompt is the thin surface; the system prompt stack is the thick infrastructure beneath it." (final-draft-v3.md §3) [QUOTE] "The prompt is not sovereign. The prompt is one operator among many. It collaborates with system prompts, training data, latent visual conventions, safety filters, physics engines, interface affordances, and procedural rules. Worldtext names the hanging-together of these elements." (final-draft-v3.md §3) [QUOTE] "A world is not a larger image. A world is an image with rules, durations, permissions, refusals, paths, boundaries, memory, and consequence... Without consequence, worldtext is only scenery with ambition." (final-draft-v3.md §3)

RESEARCH OBJECT: A political-theoretical model of the generative stack: the user prompt as petition, the hidden system-prompt stack as constitution/buried law, and the generated world as governed output. The claim de-sovereignizes the prompt — "the fantasy of word-to-world can easily become a fantasy of magical verbal sovereignty" (worldtext-after-ekphrasis §5) — and asserts an asymmetry of visibility: "The prompt is visible. The infrastructure is not" (image-is-not-end §7). The E research file adds the industry term for the mechanism: platforms enforce standards through "shadow prompting" or prompt rewriting.

LOCAL MOVE: Import juridical vocabulary (law, petition, constitution, permission, refusal, governance) to describe software layers, so that reading a generated world becomes constitutional interpretation — the analysis of what the world's rules allow, rather than what its surface shows.

SOURCE TERMS: buried law; visible petition; constitution of the worldtext; the prompt is not sovereign; one operator among many; hidden scaffolding; shadow prompting (E file); control signal (v4, cf. Wiener 1948); "operation is never innocent" (v4, citing Chun 2011)

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: The user's authorship inverts: the person who "writes the world" contributes the legally weakest text in the stack — a petition — while the unread, unseen corporate text holds constitutional rank. Authorship and authority come apart inside a single act of writing.

QUESTION: Is the constitution metaphor structurally accurate — constitutions are public, contestable, and amendable, while system prompts are secret and unilaterally changed; is "buried law" therefore closer to administrative fiat than constitution, and does the difference matter for critique?

DEEPER QUESTION: If worldtext names "the hanging-together" of user text, system text, training data, and engine rules, who is the author of a generated world in any sense that supports responsibility — and can the authorization question (ZP-010) be answered without access to the buried layers?

MECHANISM: Layered mediation: user prompt passes through the d-1 stack — "<system prompt> <training distribution> <model architecture> <interface defaults> <safety filters> <procedural constraints> <physics or simulation rules> <rendering system>" — each layer adding constraints the user neither sees nor consented to.

FORMAL SHIFT: From dyadic ekphrasis (writer/image or user/model) to a stack model with ranked instruction layers — the first place in the corpus where the unit of analysis is neither prompt nor loop but the whole apparatus.

SOURCE FORMALISM: The d-1/image-is-not-end §6 layered-stack listing (quoted in MECHANISM); v3's permit/forbid/default/respond quadruple as the constitution's clause types.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Output W = G(p_user | L1..Ln) with instruction layers L ranked by precedence; sovereignty(p_user) = marginal influence of p_user on W holding L fixed. The buried-law claim: precedence(L_system) > precedence(p_user) and visibility(L_system) ≈ 0. Constitutional reading of W = inferring the permit/forbid/default/respond clauses of the L-stack from W's behavior alone.

TENSION: READING A: the hidden stack is why worldtext matters — infrastructure is "exactly why worldtext matters" (v2 §3); critique becomes possible because the concept names the whole apparatus. READING B: the hidden stack is why the entire ekphrasis frame falters — if the effective description of the world is mostly corporate boilerplate plus physics middleware, the literary genealogy (Homer to prompt) tracks only the thin visible layer; the neoplatonic reading in portugal-version ("something like a neoplatonic faith in the power of the word") names the temptation the analysis itself flirts with.

MISSING: No actual system prompt is examined anywhere in the corpus (none quoted, none leaked, none reconstructed); the constitution claim rests entirely on the known existence of such layers; no discussion of jailbreaking or prompt injection, the obvious phenomena where petition contests law.

BOUNDARY: Applies to hosted commercial generative platforms; a locally run open-weights model without a system prompt would collapse several layers of the stack and weaken the buried-law asymmetry — a case no draft considers.

CITATION TRAIL: Wiener 1948 (control signal, v4) → Chun 2011 (Programmed Visions: "operation is never innocent," v4) → Meyer (platform realism, shadow prompting via E file) → Davis 2011/2015 (theological residue of speaking worlds into being) → deep-research-report: "the prompt narrows variance inside a preexisting statistical field rather than conjuring worlds ex nihilo."

TEST: Compare identical prompts on a hosted platform vs the same base model self-hosted without system scaffolding; behavioral differences directly measure the constitution's contribution and would give the buried-law claim its first empirical content.

PLATFORM: Hosted text-to-image and world-model platforms (Midjourney, Gemini, Genie, Marble); the E file's "shadow prompting" refers to production prompt-rewriting layers.
