ID: FORAGE-PT-003

TITLE: Descriptive struggle names nine answerability conditions and only two of them are observable from outside

SOURCE: PROGRAMS/dse.json — DESCRIPTIVE_STRUGGLE_ENGINE, <THESIS>

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] "<language> [does-not-become] <thought> by being fluent. <language> [becomes] <thinking-like-description> only when it is made answerable to: { <inward_pressure>, <memory>, <intention>, <metaphor>, <fact>, <history>, <public_criterion>, <correction>, <ethical_consequence> }" [QUOTE] "<generative_AI> [can-produce] <fluent_description> without undergoing <descriptive_struggle>."

RESEARCH OBJECT: The nine-item set, sorted by observability. Public criterion and correction leave traces. Inward pressure, memory, intention, metaphor, fact, history and ethical consequence do not — or leave traces indistinguishable from their simulation.

LOCAL MOVE: The theory locates thinking in answerability rather than in output quality, which defeats fluency as evidence. It then lists the answerabilities without sorting them by whether anyone can check them.

SOURCE TERMS: descriptive struggle / answerable to / inward pressure / public criterion / correction / fluent description without struggle

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: The theory's own criterion cannot be applied to the case it was built for. If struggle is mostly private, then "this system did not struggle" is asserted, not shown — and a system that produced the same text after genuine correction would be indistinguishable from one that did not.

QUESTION: Is descriptive struggle detectable at all, or only its two public residues — visible correction history and answerability to a stated criterion?

DEEPER QUESTION: If only the public two are checkable, this collapses into the concept-possession account, and "struggle" adds moral weight without adding evidence. Is the inward remainder doing philosophical work or consolatory work?

MECHANISM: <DRAFT> -> [PRESSURE FROM NINE SOURCES] -> revision -> [PRESSURE AGAIN] -> ... -> <DESCRIPTION THAT HAS BEEN ANSWERABLE>. Only the revision chain and the stated criterion survive into the artifact.

FORMAL SHIFT: <FLUENT TEXT> -> <REVISION HISTORY + STATED CRITERION> -> [AUDIT] -> <STRUGGLE PARTIALLY EVIDENCED>

SOURCE FORMALISM: The nine-element answerability set.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Partition the nine into OBSERVABLE = {public_criterion, correction} and PRIVATE = the rest. Define public_struggle(text) from revision depth and criterion-answerability only. Then ask empirically whether human "this was struggled over" judgments track public_struggle or exceed it.

TENSION: READING A: struggle is a real process whose absence in machines matters even if unobservable. READING B: an unobservable differentiator is not a criterion; the theory should be rewritten around the observable two, at the cost of its moral force.

MISSING: Any inter-rater study on whether readers can detect struggle. Any account of what a *simulated* revision history would lack.

BOUNDARY: The observability sort is ours. dse does not claim the nine are equally checkable; it simply does not sort them.

CITATION TRAIL: Iris Murdoch, A. C. Lloyd, Gilbert Ryle — "Thinking and Language" (1951), the descriptive-struggle/public-criteria exchange. [[FORAGE-PT-022]] [[FORAGE-PT-023]]

TEST: Take twenty passages: ten with real revision chains, ten single-pass but stylistically matched. Ask judges which were struggled over. If judges cannot beat chance, the private seven are inaccessible and the theory must stand on the public two.
