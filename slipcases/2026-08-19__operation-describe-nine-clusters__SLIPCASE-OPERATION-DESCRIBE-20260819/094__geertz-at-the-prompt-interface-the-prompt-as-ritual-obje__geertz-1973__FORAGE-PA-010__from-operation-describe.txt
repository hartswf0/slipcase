ID: FORAGE-PA-010

TITLE: Geertz at the prompt interface: the prompt as ritual object clothing outputs in an aura of factuality

SOURCE: PAPERS/cyber-03.md, sections 2, 9 ("Geertz at the Prompt Interface"; "The Ethics of Aura")

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] "Clifford Geertz's famous definition of religion as a cultural system offers a surprisingly precise grammar for understanding prompt-based AI interaction. ... This sequence maps almost too cleanly onto generative AI prompting." [QUOTE] "The prompt is a ritual object. The model is the responsive apparatus. The output is the artifact. The user is not outside the system; the user is the officiant, witness, editor, and recursive participant." [QUOTE] "Geertz's aura of factuality becomes, in AI systems, an ethical problem of interface and rhetoric. ... The ritual may continue, but the altar needs labels."

RESEARCH OBJECT: A five-step mapping of Geertz's definition of religion onto prompting: (1) prompt establishes a system of symbols ("Act as a historian," YAML schemas, genre markers); (2) establishes moods and motivations (severe, mythic, cinematic — "They tell the model not only what to say, but what kind of world the response should feel like it belongs to"); (3) formulates a general order of existence ("Every substantial prompt contains a miniature cosmology"); (4) clothes it with an aura of factuality (citations, numbered steps, procedural formats); (5) makes the moods seem uniquely realistic ("A good output does not only answer. It realizes the prompt's atmosphere. It makes the user feel that the ritual worked.").

LOCAL MOVE: Replaces "prompt engineering" with "symbolic world-making under computational conditions," then weaponizes the mapping ethically: the same mechanism that makes prompting powerful makes it dangerous, so AI literacy must include "ritual literacy: the ability to recognize when a symbolic performance is generating belief."

SOURCE TERMS: cybernetic ritual narratology; system of symbols; moods and motivations; aura of factuality; miniature cosmology; accountable enchantment; misrecognized mediation; ritual loop

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: Formatting — YAML blocks, citations, numbered methods stop being neutral scaffolding and become legitimacy-conferring ritual regalia ("Procedural structure can make speculation look like method. The ritual can succeed too well.").

QUESTION: Which symbolic devices contribute most to factual aura (persona? format? citation-shape? tone?) — an ablatable question the paper never tests.

DEEPER QUESTION: Is "accountable enchantment" stable, or self-defeating — does labeling the altar (provenance, uncertainty marks) dissolve the very mood-realization that makes the ritual work, leaving only the "bad tools and bored users" of total disenchantment?

MECHANISM: Prompt-as-invocation constructs a symbolic field; the model, trained on the culture's genre-sediment, completes within the declared cosmology; output realizes the atmosphere; user experiences realization as validity. Diagnostic question-set: "What symbolic devices make it feel true? What genre is lending it authority? What archive does it appear to speak from? What institutional voice is being simulated? What uncertainties are hidden by style? ... Who benefits if the artifact is believed?"

FORMAL SHIFT: Prompt evaluation shifts from instruction-compliance to ritual efficacy: did the output make a declared world feel briefly habitable — and belief-generation becomes a measured side effect, not a bug.

SOURCE FORMALISM: Geertz's five-clause definition of religion as a cultural system, used as a template; the ritual loop "archive → symbolic invocation → model operation → artifact → interpretation → feedback → revised invocation".

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] aura(o) = credence(reader, o) − warrant(o); prompting devices d_i contribute Δaura independent of content; ritual literacy = capacity to estimate Σ Δaura(d_i) and subtract it. Accountable enchantment: maximize mood-realization subject to aura(o) ≤ ε.

TENSION: geertz-01.md applies the same Geertz to the OTHER side of the interface — the prompt as an act to be thickly described ("Thin description says: the user typed a prompt. Thick description asks: what did that prompt count as?"); cyber-03 makes Geertz explain how prompts produce belief, geertz-01 makes Geertz discipline how we read prompts. calvino.md's constraint-design reading (FORAGE-PA-004) is the secular rival: prompt as Oulipian machine vs prompt as liturgical act.

MISSING: Any actual prompt corpus; the "almost too cleanly" self-awareness is flagged but never cashed out — no case where the mapping fails.

BOUNDARY: The paper limits itself to prompt-based interaction and explicitly refuses both disenchantment and mysticism; it does not claim models believe or perform ritual — the ritual is the human-machine exchange.

CITATION TRAIL: Geertz, "Religion as a Cultural System" (the five-part definition); Geertz, "Thick Description" (webs of significance — Conclusion: "The machine is one of the new places where culture performs itself back to us"); McLuhan, Kittler, Flusser, Barthes, Manovich (section 7's media-theory chorus).

TEST: Factorial experiment: same propositional content rendered with/without persona, citations, YAML, numbered method; measure reader credence shift — direct estimate of aura(d_i).

PLATFORM: Chat LLMs; prompt-sharing communities; any interface where formatting conventions travel.
