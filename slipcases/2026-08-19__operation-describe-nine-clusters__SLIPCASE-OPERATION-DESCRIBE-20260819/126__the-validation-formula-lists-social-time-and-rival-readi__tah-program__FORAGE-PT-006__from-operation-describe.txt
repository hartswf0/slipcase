ID: FORAGE-PT-006

TITLE: The validation formula lists social time and rival readings as required inputs, so no interpretation made alone and immediately can be valid

SOURCE: PROGRAMS/tah.json — TEXTUAL_ACTION_HERMENEUTICS v1.1, <core_problem>

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] "<event> disappears. <trace> remains. <trace> becomes readable. <reading> becomes contested. <contestation> demands validation. <validation> never becomes absolute certainty." [QUOTE] "Formula: <action> + <trace> + <social_time> + <rival_readings> [produces] <validated_interpretation>"

RESEARCH OBJECT: A validation function with four arguments, two of which are not available to a single reader in the present. Social time and rival readings are not desiderata in this formula; they are inputs. Remove them and the function does not return.

LOCAL MOVE: The theory makes validation procedural and plural, and then honestly refuses closure ("never becomes absolute certainty").

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: This indicts the standard mode of AI-assisted reading directly and without mentioning it. A single operator producing an interpretation in one session has supplied two of four arguments. By this formula the output is not an invalid interpretation — it is not yet an interpretation at all.

SOURCE TERMS: contestation / validation / social time / rival readings / validated interpretation / never absolute certainty

QUESTION: What is the minimum viable social time and the minimum number of genuinely independent rival readings for validation to begin?

DEEPER QUESTION: Can a model supply rival readings, or does independence require separately situated readers with different stakes — in which case plurality cannot be simulated, only staged?

MECHANISM: <TRACE> -> [READING 1] -> [RIVAL READINGS] -> contestation over social time -> [ARGUMENT FROM THE TRACE] -> <VALIDATED-FOR-NOW INTERPRETATION>

FORMAL SHIFT: <TRACE> -> <PLURAL CONTESTED READINGS OVER TIME> -> [ADJUDICATION] -> <PROVISIONAL VALIDITY>

SOURCE FORMALISM: The four-argument formula and the axiom that validation never reaches certainty.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] validate(action, trace, dt, R) where |R| >= k and dt >= tau. The theory leaves k and tau unspecified; both are empirically estimable as the point where interpretations stop moving.

TENSION: READING A: model-generated rival readings count, because what matters is that alternatives are on the table. READING B: they do not, because independence means different stakes, and a single system's alternatives share a prior — plurality without independence is ventriloquism.

MISSING: k and tau. Any criterion for independence of readings.

BOUNDARY: Ricoeur's argument concerns human interpretive communities; whether a model's alternatives satisfy his plurality condition is exactly what is at issue and cannot be settled by citing him.

CITATION TRAIL: Ricoeur on validation and the "courtroom of interpretation". Inter-annotator agreement methods as an operationalisation of plurality. [[FORAGE-PT-005]] [[FORAGE-PT-021]]

TEST: Vary the number of independent readings (1, 3, 7) and elapsed time (same day, one week, one month) on the same trace. Find where interpretations stabilise. Those values are k and tau, and they turn a philosophical formula into a working protocol.
