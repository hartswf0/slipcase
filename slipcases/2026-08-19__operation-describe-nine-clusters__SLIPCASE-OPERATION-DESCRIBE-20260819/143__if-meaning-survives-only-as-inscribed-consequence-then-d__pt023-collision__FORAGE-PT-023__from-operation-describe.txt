ZETTEL

ID: FORAGE-PT-023

TITLE: If meaning survives only as inscribed consequence, then descriptive struggle — being uninscribed — cannot be part of meaning at all

SOURCE: Collision of PROGRAMS/mot.json and PROGRAMS/tah.json (meaning survives as inscribed public consequence) with PROGRAMS/dse.json (thinking is answerability, mostly inward)

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] mot.json: "<meaning> [does-not-live-inside] <intention> <meaning> [survives-as] <inscribed-public-consequence>" [QUOTE] tah.json: "<action> + <trace> + <social_time> + <rival_readings> [produces] <validated_interpretation>" [QUOTE] dse.json: "<language> [becomes] <thinking-like-description> only when it is made answerable to: { <inward_pressure>, <memory>, <intention> ... }"

RESEARCH OBJECT: A mutual exclusion. The hermeneutic instruments evict intention from meaning and locate it in the public trace. The struggle instrument locates thinking in answerability to intention, memory and inward pressure — none of which is inscribed. By the first pair's own rule, the second's central object is not a candidate for study.

LOCAL MOVE: This zettel refuses to soften the conflict. Both instruments are in the same corpus, deployed in the same hermeneutic suite, and one rules the other's object out of court.

SOURCE TERMS: inscribed public consequence / does-not-live-inside intention / rival readings / inward pressure / answerable to / descriptive struggle

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: The eviction of intention was a *methodological* achievement — it is what let the human sciences read deeds instead of guessing at minds. Accepting it costs the corpus its account of thinking. The corpus wants both the discipline of the trace and the moral weight of the struggle, and the trace argument does not permit the second.

QUESTION: Can a trace evidence answerability without evidencing intention — is there a public signature of having been under correction?

DEEPER QUESTION: The one candidate is *revision*: a preserved sequence of changes made against objections is inscribed, public, and about answerability rather than about intent. If that works, struggle re-enters the hermeneutic frame through its residue instead of through the mind — and the drafts become the primary document.

MECHANISM: <THINKING UNDER PRESSURE> -> [MOSTLY UNINSCRIBED] -> only the final text is traced -> [HERMENEUTIC READING OF THE TRACE] -> struggle invisible -> <CLAIM THAT IT OCCURRED IS UNGROUNDED BY THE CORPUS'S OWN RULE>. Repair path: preserve the revision chain, and the pressure becomes inscribed consequence.

FORMAL SHIFT: <INWARD ANSWERABILITY> -> <REVISION CHAIN AS INSCRIPTION> -> [READING OF CHANGES, NOT OF MINDS] -> <STRUGGLE AS A PUBLIC OBJECT>

SOURCE FORMALISM: mot's rescue schema; tah's four-argument validation formula; dse's nine conditions.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Read the diff, not the mind. For a revision chain d1..dn, code each change as {correction under objection, elaboration, restyling}. The proportion of the first is a public, contestable, plural-readable measure of answerability. It satisfies the trace requirement and preserves the struggle claim.

TENSION: READING A: struggle is real and unstudiable, and the corpus should admit it holds an unfalsifiable commitment. READING B: struggle has a residue — the diff — and the corpus's error is discarding drafts, not holding incompatible theories.

MISSING: Any treatment of revision as a document. Every instrument in the set reads finished artifacts.

BOUNDARY: Ricoeur's distanciation argument concerns the autonomy of the inscribed; it does not forbid reading revision chains, which are themselves inscribed. The exclusion bites only against *uninscribed* struggle.

CITATION TRAIL: Ricoeur, "The Model of the Text" (1971). Murdoch, Lloyd, Ryle, "Thinking and Language" (1951). Genetic criticism and the study of manuscript drafts — an existing discipline built on exactly this move. [[FORAGE-PT-005]] [[FORAGE-PT-003]]

TEST: Take ten documents with full revision history. Code the diffs for correction-under-objection. Ask independent readers to rank the finished texts for evident struggle. If diff-coding predicts the rankings, struggle has a public signature and the drafts are the document.
