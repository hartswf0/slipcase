TITLE: Epistemic debt and the Fragile Expert: the operator-side pathology with an irreversibility threshold

SOURCE: worldtext/syntheses/epistemic-debt.md — "Core Thesis", "The Naur Root", "The Fragile Expert", "Accumulation Mechanics", "The Metacognitive Friction Loop"

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] "**Epistemic debt** is the accumulation of human competence deficits that occurs when AI-mediated outsourcing bypasses the cognitive friction required for schema formation." [QUOTE] "epistemic debt names the condition where **the theory dies while the text lives**."

RESEARCH OBJECT: An operator-side debt metric mirroring technical/continuity debt: the measurable decay of a human's capacity to understand, repair, and extend a system whose outputs they continue to produce — culminating in the Fragile Expert phenotype ("competent without comprehension").

LOCAL MOVE: The file generalizes Naur's Programming as Theory Building beyond programmer departure: [PARAPHRASE, exact table] "Epistemic debt accrues not when experts leave, but when they never form" — the theory can be stillborn, not just orphaned.

SOURCE TERMS: Epistemic debt; Fragile Expert (high functional utility, zero corrective competence, prompt fluency, theory illiteracy, confidence asymmetry); friction bypass; output acceptance; schema atrophy; delegation creep; vocabulary loss; irreversibility threshold; Metacognitive Friction Loop; Explanation Gate; epistemic anchor (the raw/ layer).

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: Confidence becomes a symptom rather than a signal: [QUOTE] "Confidence scales with output quality, not with understanding." The better the AI's outputs, the less the operator's confidence tells you about the operator.

QUESTION: The file's own open question, verbatim: [QUOTE] "At what generation rate does epistemic debt become irreversible — when does the operator lose the theory to repair what they produce?"

DEEPER QUESTION: The irreversibility threshold is defined as [QUOTE] "The operator can no longer formulate the question that would reveal the debt" — a self-referential blindness condition. Can any *internal* instrument detect crossing it, or is the threshold observable only from outside the operator-AI dyad (making solo worldtext practice structurally unable to certify its own health)?

MECHANISM: The Metacognitive Friction Loop: LINT detects contradiction → LLM halts automated integration and flags lint-report.md → operator must make "a DEFINITIVE THEORETICAL RULING — Not a preference. Not a feeling. A ruling — with reasons." → ruling logged in chronicle. [QUOTE] "Each pass through the loop is a micro-act of theory-building. Epistemic debt cannot accrue if the operator is forced to think."

FORMAL SHIFT: From deskilling as diffuse cultural worry to a ledger with accrual mechanisms, rates ("Compound", "Step-function", "Terminal — once vocabulary erodes, the theory cannot be reconstructed"), a defined critical threshold, and monitorable signals with numeric warning levels.

SOURCE FORMALISM: The dual-diagnostic diagram (CONTINUITY DEBT world-side | EPISTEMIC DEBT operator-side → TOTAL SYSTEM FAILURE: "The world is broken AND no one can fix it"); the CRI-governor extension table: [QUOTE] "Operator override rate ... Warning at >30%; Auto-accept rate ... Warning at >50%; Question frequency ... Warning if declining; Vocabulary drift ... Warning if degrading; Repair latency ... Warning if increasing." Diagnostic question: [QUOTE] "Can the operator repair a coherence failure introduced by a system update, without the AI's assistance?"

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Let θ(t) = operator theory-fidelity, g = generation rate, f = friction events per generation. dθ/dt = −k·g·(1−f) + r·f; irreversibility when θ < θ* such that the operator's query distribution no longer contains debt-revealing questions (a coverage condition on the question space, not on θ itself).

TENSION: The feedback-loop claim (continuity debt and epistemic debt mutually accelerate toward "outputs look good and no one knows they're wrong") makes the two debts empirically hard to distinguish at exactly the point where distinguishing matters most. Rival reading: epistemic debt is not a second pathology but continuity debt *as seen from inside* — one process, two coordinate systems.

MISSING: Any measurement study; units for the debt (what is one unit of epistemic debt?); evidence for the specific warning thresholds (>30%, >50%); a recovery protocol beyond "go back to the evidence" via the raw/ epistemic anchor.

BOUNDARY: Scoped to single-operator human-AI systems; the file's Bateson framing (second-order cybernetics as "the only known defense against competence collapse in recursive human-AI systems") is asserted, not derived, and does not cover teams where theory is socially distributed.

CITATION TRAIL: epistemic-debt.md → Naur, Programming as Theory Building (1985) → Bateson, Steps to an Ecology of Mind (1972) → arXiv 2602.20206 (AI-induced deskilling) → dual pair continuity-debt.md → clean-stack-and-artifact-lifecycle.md (friction enforcement at Ingest).

TEST: Checkable empirical claim: operators subjected to forced Explanation-Gate rulings should retain repair ability (fix a seeded coherence failure without AI help) at higher rates than operators allowed to auto-accept — a randomizable protocol the file implies but never runs.

PLATFORM: Any human-AI generation loop with a lint stage; monitor signals require logging operator overrides, accepts, queries, and latencies.
