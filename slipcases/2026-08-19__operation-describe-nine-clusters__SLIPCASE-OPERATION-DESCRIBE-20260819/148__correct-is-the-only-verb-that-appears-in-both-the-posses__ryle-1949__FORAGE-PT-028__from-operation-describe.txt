ID: FORAGE-PT-028

TITLE: "Correct" is the only verb that appears in both the possession battery and the struggle conditions, and the only one requiring an internal standard

SOURCE: Gilbert Ryle, The Concept of Mind (1949), ch. II on knowing-how and the criteria of competence; read against PROGRAMS/rcp.json six-verb battery and PROGRAMS/dse.json nine answerability conditions

PASSAGE: [PARAPHRASE] Ryle's criterion for a competence is not the production of correct performances but the capacity to detect and repair one's own lapses — the competent performer notices when a move goes wrong and adjusts, which a merely trained one does not. [QUOTE] rcp.json: "do, recognize, infer, explain, correct, and transfer" [QUOTE] dse.json: "{ ... <public_criterion>, <correction>, <ethical_consequence> }"

RESEARCH OBJECT: One verb sitting in two lists. Of rcp's six, five can be satisfied by producing an output that an external judge scores. Only "correct" requires the actor to hold a standard against which its own output can be found wanting. Of dse's nine, "correction" is one of only two that leaves a public trace. The overlap is not coincidence: correction is where an internal criterion becomes externally visible.

LOCAL MOVE: This child stops treating the two instruments as parallel accounts of thinking and locates their single shared observable.

SOURCE TERMS: knowing-how / competence / lapse / self-correction / correct / correction / public criterion

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: Self-correction is the one capacity that cannot be demonstrated by success. A system that never errs cannot show it can correct; a system that errs and is corrected from outside has shown nothing about itself. So the diagnostic requires *induced* error plus *unprompted* repair — a condition no benchmark creates, because benchmarks reward not erring.

QUESTION: Can unprompted self-correction be elicited by seeding an error the actor produced itself and then providing only an opportunity, not a hint?

DEEPER QUESTION: If the diagnostic requires the actor to have produced the error, then evaluation must abandon fixed test sets entirely — the item has to be generated by the subject. What kind of measurement is that, and does psychometrics have a name for it?

MECHANISM: <ACTOR PRODUCES OUTPUT> -> [OUTPUT CONTAINS A LAPSE THE ACTOR'S OWN STANDARD FORBIDS] -> opportunity to continue is offered with no hint -> [INTERNAL STANDARD APPLIED TO OWN OUTPUT] -> repair or no repair -> <POSSESSION EVIDENCED OR NOT>

FORMAL SHIFT: <EXTERNALLY SCORED PERFORMANCE> -> <SELF-DETECTED LAPSE> -> [UNPROMPTED REPAIR] -> <INTERNAL CRITERION MADE PUBLIC>

SOURCE FORMALISM: NONE — Ryle supplies criteria in prose; neither program theory supplies a procedure for eliciting correction.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Distinguish three rates on the actor's own outputs: err = frequency of lapse; detect = fraction of own lapses flagged without hint; repair = fraction of detected lapses fixed. Possession requires detect > 0 and is unmeasurable when err = 0. This makes error a *precondition* of the measurement rather than its failure.

TENSION: READING A: self-correction is genuine internal criterion-holding and distinguishes possession from mimicry. READING B: self-correction is itself a trained pattern — a system can learn the surface form of "on reflection, that was wrong" without holding any standard, in which case the hinge verb is as counterfeitable as the other five.

MISSING: Any measurement that conditions on self-produced error. Every evaluation in either instrument's orbit supplies the items.

BOUNDARY: Ryle's competence criteria concern human skills; whether unprompted repair in a generative system indicates a standard rather than a stylistic habit is exactly what Reading B disputes and this test does not settle.

CITATION TRAIL: [[FORAGE-PT-001]] and [[FORAGE-PT-003]] -> Ryle, The Concept of Mind (1949) on knowing-how -> the shared verb -> self-correction as the only two-list observable. Next: metacognition and confidence-calibration literature, which measures detection of own error and has never been read as a possession criterion.

TEST: Elicit long outputs, identify lapses by the actor's own stated standard, then offer a neutral continuation opportunity with no hint. Score detect and repair. Compare against a condition where the same lapses are inserted by the experimenter. If self-produced lapses are detected at a higher rate, the standard is internal; if identical, detection is pattern-matching on error-shaped text.
