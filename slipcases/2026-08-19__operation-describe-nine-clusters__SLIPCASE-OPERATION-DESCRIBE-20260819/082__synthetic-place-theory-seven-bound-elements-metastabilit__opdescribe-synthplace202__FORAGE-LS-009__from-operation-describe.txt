Present it as an operative, political, material, worlding arrangement experienced through trajectory."

RESEARCH OBJECT: A home-grown formal ontology of place, built in a bracketed relational syntax inside a chat session, then compressed into an executable "Golden Record" prompt.

LOCAL MOVE: Reclaims "synthetic" from its pejorative sense (fake) to its etymological sense (syn-thesis, placed-together), making every place — physical or generated — an assembly, and latent spaces just one engine among many.

SOURCE TERMS: Synthetic_Place; Metastable_Place; Operative_Coherence; Situated_Agent; the seven elements (Topology, Affordance, Memory, Meaning, Power, Material_Support, Interface); Synthetic_Place_Engine; worlding medium; Golden Record prompt.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: The theory dissolves the digital/physical divide it grew from — by its own definition a cathedral, a Discord server, and a Genie 3 corridor are the same kind of object, differing only in binding mechanisms.

QUESTION: Why exactly seven elements — the definition block lists six bound elements plus Interface described separately as "the zone of encounter"; is Interface an element or the boundary of the binding?

DEEPER QUESTION: What is the identity criterion for a metastable place across reconfigurations — how much element-drift before it is a different place?

MECHANISM: Heterogeneous elements are "bound into" operative coherence for a situated agent; coherence is metastable — "coherent enough to orient action, never fully closed, always conditional" — and each element is auditable (the Golden Record's seven "Always specify" axes and nine diagnostic questions: "What is being assembled? What is being excluded? ... What does this place cost? Where does it fail, drift, or break?").

FORMAL SHIFT: From place as location to place as a typed binding operation over a heterogeneous element set, with an explicit runtime (the prompt) that can execute the theory as generation or as analysis.

SOURCE FORMALISM: The bracket syntax itself: <Entity> [relation] <Entity> with nesting, e.g. [emerges_when] {...} [are] [bound_into]; plus the Golden Record prompt as a 3000-character executable compression of the ontology.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] SP = bind(E, agent a) where E ⊆ {Top, Aff, Mem, Mean, Pow, Mat, Int}; place-hood is the predicate coherent_for(bind(E), a); metastability: d(bind)/dt ≠ 0 under exogenous pressure while coherent_for holds.

TENSION: Provenance tension the archive must own: the theory's canonical statement is a ChatGPT response to prompts like "Deeper code syntax more novel and non obvious" — rival readings: (a) genuine theory-construction with the model as bricolage instrument (enacting LS-007), or (b) fluent LLM patter retro-dignified as theory. Also textual damage: the export ate bare bracketed terms, leaving holes ("is not a neutral container" lacks its subject), so the theory's authoritative text is partially corrupt.

MISSING: Any application of the nine diagnostic questions to a real case; relation to prior assemblage theory (DeLanda, Latour go unmentioned); versioning between the essay form and the Golden Record form.

BOUNDARY: The theory covers "any scene, environment, world model, latent space, or operative ecology"; it deliberately does not distinguish generated from built places — that indifference is the claim.

CITATION TRAIL: synth-place.json metadata (chatgpt.com conversation link, exported 4/20/2026); internally cites Bricolage and Humility ("Method: use <Bricolage> and <Humility>"); connects to Ian Cheng's worlding and the Shield of Achilles as "a perfect instance of <Synthetic_Place>".

TEST: The Golden Record prompt is directly executable: feed it plus a scene to any capable model and audit whether outputs specify all seven axes and answer the nine diagnostics — a runnable conformance test the archive itself never ran.

PLATFORM: Prompt-level: any LLM or world model; the theory is engine-agnostic by design.
