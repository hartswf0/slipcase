TITLE: "A world is an image with rules, memory, and consequence" — the definition that survived the wreck

SOURCE: Dry-Dock/BULKHEAD-06_shield-as-worldtext.md (Part B); Dry-Dock/watson-shield-as-worldtext.md (aphorism triage); 00_PLIMSOLL-LINE/portugal-draft.md (§ The shield as worldtext — the ONLY Watson sentences retained in the final); KEEL BLOCKS/SYNTH-02_shield-as-world-model.md

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] "The decisive shift is from depicting a scene to specifying a world. In generative AI research, this is the distinction between an image model and a world model. An image has no consequences. A world does. A world model is a predictive engine: a compressed spatiotemporal representation of how states succeed one another and how environments respond to intervention (Ha and Schmidhuber, 2018)." [QUOTE] "A world is not a larger image. A world is an image with rules, memory, and consequence. Without consequence, worldtext is only scenery with ambition." [QUOTE, the paired question] "Image generation asked: what can a sentence make visible? World generation asks: what can a sentence make habitable?" [PARAPHRASE] watson-shield-as-worldtext.md records the forging of the definition: the chiasmus "A picture can lie by resemblance; a world lies by consistency" was executed as incoherent and REPLACED by "An image has no consequences. A world does."

RESEARCH OBJECT: A compact definition of worldhood for generative media with a built-in failure mode: consequence (states respond to intervention) is the necessary condition separating world from image; scale is explicitly rejected as the differentia ("not a larger image"). Plus its interrogative form — visible vs. habitable — which restates the definition as a research program.

LOCAL MOVE: Replace the intuitive image→world gradient (bigger, 3D, immersive) with a categorical test: does intervention have downstream effects the system remembers? If not, whatever its resolution or dimensionality, it is "scenery with ambition."

SOURCE TERMS: image model vs. world model; consequence; rules; memory; habitable; scenery with ambition; predictive engine; intervention.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: Immersion — the whole cultural telos of 3D graphics and VR the drafts recount — turns out to be irrelevant to worldhood: a fully immersive environment without consequence is still an image, and a text adventure with consequence is more world than a VR vista.

QUESTION: How much memory and how much rule-consistency does an environment need before "consequence" is genuine rather than scripted?

DEEPER QUESTION: Is consequence a property of the artifact or of the relationship between artifact and agent — can the same generated environment be a world for one user (whose actions persist) and an image for another (spectating)?

MECHANISM: World models predict state succession and response to intervention (Ha & Schmidhuber); Genie 2 "simulates the consequences of any action ... remembers parts of the world no longer in view" (SYNTH-02); the definition extracts these engineering capabilities into a media-theoretic criterion.

FORMAL SHIFT: From media taxonomy by sensory channel or dimensionality to taxonomy by counterfactual structure: worldhood = the environment supports "what would happen if."

SOURCE FORMALISM: Ha & Schmidhuber's compressed spatiotemporal representation; the visible/habitable question pair.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] World(E) iff exists state space S, transition T: S×A→S (rules), persistence over time of unobserved state (memory), and non-trivial dependence of future observations on past actions (consequence). Image(E) iff T is trivial. "Scenery with ambition" = high-fidelity E with trivial T.

TENSION: READING A (the final portugal-draft): the definition is retained as a modest clarifying remark inside Jay's neoplatonic paragraph ("Ekphrastic hope here reaches beyond the word as natural sign to a neoplatonic affirmation of the power of the word/prompt to create the world"). READING B (Watson's full section): the definition is the hinge of a political argument — consequence is what makes worldtext dangerous, requiring the affordance-critique of SYNTH-03. The final keeps the definition and amputates what it was for.

MISSING: In the final draft the sentence "Without consequence, worldtext is only scenery with ambition" is gone, as is the visible/habitable pair; no operational threshold for consequence is ever given; the "neoplatonic faith in the power of the word" (Jay) is asserted but its theological genealogy (Davis, in SYNTH-03) was cut.

BOUNDARY: The definition presupposes interactive systems; it says nothing about narrative worlds sustained purely in a reader's cognition — which is exactly where the shield lives, a gap the drafts bridge only by analogy.

CITATION TRAIL: Ha & Schmidhuber 2018 → Bruce et al. 2024 (Genie) → World Labs Marble → Genie 3 site quote in portugal-draft: "Genie 3 is a general-purpose world model. It uses simple text descriptions to generate photorealistic environments that can be explored in real-time." (https://deepmind.google/models/genie/)

TEST: Apply the criterion to boundary cases: a 360° video (fails: no T), Minecraft (passes), a diffusion image with inpainting loop (contested — the prompter's iterations create memory outside the artifact). The contested case tests whether consequence must live inside the system.

PLATFORM: Genie 2/3; World Labs Marble; VR/videogame worlds as the pre-generative baseline.
