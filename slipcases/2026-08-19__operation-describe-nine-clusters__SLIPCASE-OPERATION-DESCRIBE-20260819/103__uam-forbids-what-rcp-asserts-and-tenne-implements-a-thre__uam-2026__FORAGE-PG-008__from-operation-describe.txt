TITLE: uam forbids what rcp asserts and tenne implements: a three-way standoff on whether meaning/understanding IS operation

SOURCE: PROGRAMS/uam.json — <Primary Distinctions> D6, D9; <Invariants> I2, I5; PROGRAMS/rcp.json — <CORE_CLAIM>; PROGRAMS/tenne.json — <invariants> I1, I2; PROGRAMS/meta.json — <Assumption Ledger> A2

PASSAGE: [QUOTE uam.json] "D6: <use> != <meaning>. ... D9: <operation> != <understanding>." and "I2: <paper> [must-never-collapse] <meaning> into <use>." [QUOTE rcp.json] "<concept-possession> [is] <operational-mastery-across-an-indefinite-task-field>". [QUOTE tenne.json] "<I1> <meaning> [is] <executable_relation_to_possible_worlds>. <I2> <understanding> [requires] <translation> + <world_model> + <inference>." [QUOTE meta.json A2] "all converge on the same architectural claim: that meaning is public, operational, and context-dependent."

RESEARCH OBJECT: A genuine invariant-conflict triangle. uam (from Ryle-Findlay) makes non-collapse of meaning into use a hard invariant; rcp (also from Ryle!) identifies possession of a concept with operational mastery; tenne identifies meaning with an executable relation. And meta's assumption A2 — marked status="safe" — asserts corpus-wide convergence on "meaning is operational," which uam's I2 exists to deny.

LOCAL MOVE: Test meta's safest assumption against the child that contradicts it, and note the two Ryle-derived theories pulling in opposite directions (uam uses Findlay to discipline reduction; rcp is pure operational Ryle with the Findlay correction absent).

SOURCE TERMS: use; meaning; performed intentionality; operational-mastery; factor-not-slice; executable_relation_to_possible_worlds; Use Idolization (uam failure F2); A2 status safe

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: uam has a named failure mode, F2 <Use Idolization>: "trigger: <paper> [claims] <use> fully explains <meaning>. correction: [insert] <Findlay reversal>." Read as a corpus-level diagnostic, F2 fires on rcp's core claim and on tenne's I1 — and on meta's A2. The corpus contains its own refutation-detector for its own governing assumption, filed under a child theory.

QUESTION: Does rcp actually collapse meaning into use, or only concept-possession into operational mastery — leaving meaning (a property of speech) untouched? uam D9 says operation ≠ understanding; rcp's final compression says "<having-a-concept> [means] <being-able-to-operate>" — the collision is direct on understanding, not necessarily on meaning.

DEEPER QUESTION: Is meta's A2 a factual claim about the corpus (falsified by uam) or a normative admission criterion (theories are admitted insofar as they operationalize)? If the latter, uam was admitted in violation of the corpus's architecture — or as its licensed internal opposition, the theory whose job is to keep A2 honest.

MECHANISM: Lineage effect: rcp formalizes Ryle 1962 alone; uam formalizes the Ryle–Findlay dispute ("Use, Usage and Meaning", 1961 symposium), importing Findlay's counter-thesis as invariants. Formalizing a dispute yields anti-reduction invariants; formalizing a single position yields identity claims.

FORMAL SHIFT: From "the corpus agrees meaning is use" to a precise triage: use as evidence-surface (uam I5: "<use> [is] <evidence surface> not <complete ground>"), mastery as possession-criterion (rcp), executability as meaning-substrate (tenne). Three different logical roles for operation: evidence, criterion, constitution.

SOURCE FORMALISM: uam's numbered distinctions D1–D10 and invariants; rcp's CORE_CLAIM is/is-not chain; tenne's invariant list.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Let O = observable operation, M = meaning, U = understanding. uam: O ⇒-evidence M, ¬(O = M), ¬(O = U). rcp: U =df graded-O over indefinite task field. tenne: M =df executable relation R(utterance, possible worlds); U = translate∘model∘infer. The three are pairwise non-equivalent: rcp and tenne disagree on whether U is graded capacity or pipeline achievement; uam denies both identities but accepts O as sole public evidence — so all three agree on methodology (test by use) while disagreeing on metaphysics (what use is evidence OF). Meta's A2 conflates methodology with metaphysics.

TENSION: Rival reading: no conflict, division of labor — rcp handles concepts (sub-sentential), uam handles speech meaning (sentential), tenne handles machine semantics (system-level). But uam's change test CT3 explicitly maps its dispute onto AI ("<use> -> <operation>, <meaning> -> <understanding> ... Can <system behavior> explain <meaning> without smuggling in <human interpretive intentionality>?"), entering tenne's domain and denying its I1 there. The theories themselves cross the levels the harmonization needs to keep apart.

MISSING: An operator ruling; a revision of A2's status from "safe" to "uncertain"; a fourth position (opek's ArtificialSemantics — meaning weak, nonhuman, consequential) placed into the triangle, which would make it a square.

BOUNDARY: The standoff concerns constitution of meaning/understanding; all parties reject private-object semantics (uam I8, rcp's anti-reification, tenne's visible traces), so the conflict lives entirely inside the public-operational family.

CITATION TRAIL: Ryle & Findlay, "Use, Usage and Meaning" (PAS Supp. 1961) → uam.json; Ryle, "Thinking Thoughts and Having Concepts" → rcp.json; Wong, Grand, Goodman et al., "From Word Models to World Models" (2023) → tenne.json; meta.json A2.

TEST: Construct an agent that passes rcp's full mastery battery (classification+inference+explanation+correction+transfer) and tenne's trace requirements for a concept, then ask uam's question: does anything about meaning remain unexplained? If uam's axes can name a concrete residue (intentional direction not exhibited in any task), the standoff is real; if not, uam's I2 is an invariant without teeth by the corpus's own LAW 7.

PLATFORM: LLM session; rcp's pseudo-program evaluate_concept_possession is directly implementable as an eval harness.
