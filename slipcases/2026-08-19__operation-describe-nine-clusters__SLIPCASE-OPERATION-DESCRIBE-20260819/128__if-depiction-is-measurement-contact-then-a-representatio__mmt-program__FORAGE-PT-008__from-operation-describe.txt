ID: FORAGE-PT-008

TITLE: If depiction is measurement contact, then a representation whose projection rule is lost is not a bad model — it is not a model at all

SOURCE: PROGRAMS/mmt.json — MODEL_MEASURE_TRACTATUS, <Initial Interpretation> and <central command>

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] "<central command> := Do not ask, 'How does a proposition look like the world?' Ask, 'What disciplined arrangement lets one system be laid against another and tested?'" [QUOTE] "<thesis> := <Tractatus> [is-not] <pure logic detached from machines>. <Tractatus> [contains] <a workshop theory of representation>"

RESEARCH OBJECT: Depiction as a *procedure* rather than a property. Resemblance is intrinsic to a picture; laying-against-and-testing requires a rule of projection held by someone. Representation therefore has a maintenance requirement, and it can lapse.

LOCAL MOVE: The theory rewrites the picture theory as a workshop practice — scale, correlation, measurement — and in doing so makes representation depend on a living convention rather than on similarity.

SOURCE TERMS: disciplined arrangement / laid against / tested / model / measure / scale / workshop theory of representation

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: Representations can *die* while remaining fully legible. A technical drawing whose conventions are forgotten still shows lines; it no longer measures anything. Nothing in the visible artifact records whether its projection rule is still held.

QUESTION: What happens to a representation when its rule of projection is lost — does it become a picture, an ornament, or a ruin?

DEEPER QUESTION: If projection rules must be actively held, they are exactly the kind of thing Naur says dies with the team. Then every model is on a decay clock, and the archive's coherence problem is a projection-rule maintenance problem.

MECHANISM: <MODEL> + <PROJECTION RULE> -> [LAY AGAINST WORLD AT SPECIFIED POINTS] -> agreement or disagreement -> <TRUTH-EVALUABLE>. Remove the rule and the final arrow does not fire, while the artifact is unchanged.

FORMAL SHIFT: <RESEMBLANCE> -> <PROJECTION + CONTACT POINTS> -> [TEST] -> <DEPICTION AS A MAINTAINED CAPACITY>

SOURCE FORMALISM: Scale, correlation and measurement contact as the Tractatus's operative machinery, per this theory's reconstruction.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] depicts(M, W) iff there exists a rule R, currently held by some community, and a set of contact points C, such that agreement of M and W at C is decidable. Depiction is thus indexed to a community and a time.

TENSION: READING A (this theory): projection is the essence; resemblance was always a distraction. READING B: some depiction is genuinely resemblance-based and survives loss of all convention — a portrait remains a portrait. Then projection is one mode of depiction, not its definition.

MISSING: A worked case of a lost projection rule. Any statement of what the *minimum* set of contact points is for a given model.

BOUNDARY: This is a contested reading of the Tractatus. It licenses claims about how a measurement-model works, not about what Wittgenstein meant.

CITATION TRAIL: Tractatus 2.1-2.225, 4.01-4.0641. Naur on theory death. [[FORAGE-PT-016]] [[FORAGE-PT-027]]

TEST: Find a technical representation whose convention is genuinely lost (an obsolete notation, an undocumented schematic). Ask expert readers what it depicts. If they can describe the marks but cannot test any claim against the world, projection is doing the work and resemblance is not.
