ZETTEL

ID: FORAGE-PT-013

TITLE: Tool, Author and Mirror are rejected because each puts agency in one place — and the replacement supplies no positive term

SOURCE: PROGRAMS/cyber.json — Agential Cybernetic Media v2.0, <Initial Interpretation>

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] "<GenerativeAI> is misdescribed when treated as <Tool>, <Author>, or <Mirror>." [QUOTE] "<AgentialCyberneticMedium> [cuts] <EntangledReality> [compresses] <CulturalMemory> [predicts] <PossibleContinuations> [generates] <WorldSurfaces> [feeds_back_into] <HumanImagination> [modulates] <FutureAction> [participates_in] <WorldMaking>"

RESEARCH OBJECT: The structure of the three rejections. Tool locates agency in the user; Author locates it in the machine; Mirror locates it in the world. All three are *locations*. The replacement is distribution — but distribution is not a location, so the theory has a negative definition and seven verbs where a positive noun should be.

LOCAL MOVE: It clears the ground by refusing the three available descriptions, then characterises the medium by what it does rather than by what it is.

SOURCE TERMS: Tool / Author / Mirror / AgentialCyberneticMedium / cuts / compresses / predicts / generates / feeds back / modulates / participates

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: Every objection to "AI as tool" in the literature is an objection to a *location* of agency, and the standard reply is to name another location. This theory declines to, which is intellectually honest and leaves it unable to say what would count as evidence in its favour.

QUESTION: What observation would distinguish distributed agency from agency located in one place and merely hard to see?

DEEPER QUESTION: Is "distributed" falsifiable at all, or is it a stance — and if a stance, what does adopting it commit you to that the tool description does not?

MECHANISM: The seven verbs form a loop: cut -> compress -> predict -> generate -> feed back into imagination -> modulate future action -> participate in world-making -> (the modulated action produces new material to compress). The loop closure is the argument for distribution.

FORMAL SHIFT: <LOCATED AGENCY> -> <SEVEN-VERB LOOP> -> [NO SINGLE LOCUS] -> <DISTRIBUTION WITHOUT A POSITIVE TERM>

SOURCE FORMALISM: The seven-relation characterisation.

OUR FORMALIZATION: [OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] An ablation battery. Remove each of {user intent, model weights, archive, interface, feedback path} and ask whether output *character* changes. Tool predicts collapse when the user is removed; Author when the model is; Mirror when the archive is. Distribution predicts partial degradation across all five and total collapse under none.

TENSION: READING A: the loop is real and the ablation asymmetry will show it. READING B: ablation will show one dominant term — most plausibly the archive — and distribution will turn out to be a Mirror description in richer vocabulary.

MISSING: Any positive noun. Any ablation. Any criterion for how much distribution counts as distributed.

BOUNDARY: This concerns the description's evidentiary structure, not whether the seven relations obtain.

CITATION TRAIL: Latour on actants and delegation; Hutchins, Cognition in the Wild, on distributed cognition and its evidence standards. [[FORAGE-PT-012]]

TEST: Run the five-way ablation on one fixed task and score change in output character. The shape of the degradation profile decides between the four descriptions, and it is the first empirical purchase anyone has had on this argument.
