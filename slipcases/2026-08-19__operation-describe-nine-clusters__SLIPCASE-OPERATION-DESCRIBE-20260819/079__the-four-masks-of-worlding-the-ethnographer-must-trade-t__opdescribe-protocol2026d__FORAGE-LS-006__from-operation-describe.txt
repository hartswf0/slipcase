ID: FORAGE-LS-006

TITLE: The four masks of worlding: the ethnographer must trade the Director for the Emissary

SOURCE: LATENT SPACE/Operative Ethnography Protocol Design.md — "The Topology of the Four Masks" (Ian Cheng section)

PASSAGE: [QUOTE] "Cheng posits that the successful creation of a World requires the deliberate orchestration and channeling of four specific creative patterns, which he refers to as \"masks,\" within the creator's own psychology." [QUOTE] "**The Director:** The mask of the Director seeks to control the narrative, define the rigid roadmap, and recover a fixed, singular meaning from complex problems." [QUOTE] "**The Hacker:** The Hacker is a spontaneous, systemic disruptor who devises new tools, tinkers with technical architectures, and discovers operational playbooks and \"tricks\"." [QUOTE] "**The Emissary:** ... Unlike the other masks, the Emissary willfully betrays the creator's desire for absolute control in order to serve the World itself." [QUOTE] "Aliveness is defined as the capacity of a system to perpetuate its own drama, generate surprise, and adapt to rapidly changing environments long after the original author has exited the system."

RESEARCH OBJECT: The role-psychology of world-makers, imported as a methodology map for researchers of generative systems.

LOCAL MOVE: Maps Cheng's artist-facing typology (Director, Cartoonist, Hacker, Emissary) onto the traditional-vs-operative ethnographer distinction: the monograph-writer is a Director; the operative ethnographer must end as an Emissary.

SOURCE TERMS: four masks; Director; Cartoonist; Hacker; Emissary; Worlding; aliveness; threshold of autonomy; Emissaries trilogy.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE: Methodological rigor is recast as a *betrayal problem*: the best observer of a living system is the one who gives up authorial control over what the observation means.

QUESTION: Is the mask sequence a pipeline (adopt each in order) or a portfolio (orchestrate all four simultaneously)? The file says "adoption and shedding" without ordering the middle two.

DEEPER QUESTION: Can an institution (IRB, journal, funder) accept Emissary-mode research whose success criterion is that the studied system outgrows the researcher's framing?

MECHANISM: Director fixes roadmap and singular meaning → Cartoonist compresses into resonant, relatable tokens → Hacker devises probes and tricks against the technical substrate → Emissary "nurtures the system's ongoing changes, accepts the chaotic unknowns of reality as fertile content" and lets the World cross the autonomy threshold. Operative ethnography = initiate encounter, set conditions, step back.

FORMAL SHIFT: From researcher-as-lens to researcher-as-role-stack; validity criteria differ per mask (Director: fidelity; Hacker: reproducibility of tricks; Emissary: system aliveness after exit).

SOURCE FORMALISM: Cheng's four-mask typology plus the "aliveness" threshold from An Emissaries Guide to Worlding.

OUR FORMALIZATION: NONE

TENSION: The Hacker and Emissary masks contradict: the Hacker exists to expose flaws and force the system's hand; the Emissary serves the system's autonomy. The file needs both (probes require Hackers) but names no protocol for when to switch — rival reading: the four masks are not a method but a description of role conflict.

MISSING: Any empirical case of an ethnographer completing the Director→Emissary transition; criteria for measuring "aliveness"; the Cartoonist's research function beyond communication.

BOUNDARY: Cheng's typology concerns art-world simulations (Emissaries trilogy); its transfer to research method is the file's own extension.

CITATION TRAIL: Ian Cheng, An Emissaries Guide to Worlding; Cheng's Emissaries trilogy (live simulations); connected in synth-place.json to the "world watcher" figure.

TEST: Named framework, no specified procedure: "aliveness" has a definition but no measurement; the mask transitions have no protocol. Not currently checkable.

PLATFORM: Live simulations, agent ecosystems, long-running generative worlds.
