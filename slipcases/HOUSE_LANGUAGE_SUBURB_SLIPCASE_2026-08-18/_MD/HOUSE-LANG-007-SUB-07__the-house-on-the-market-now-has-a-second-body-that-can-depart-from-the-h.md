THE HOUSE ON THE MARKET NOW HAS A SECOND BODY THAT CAN DEPART FROM THE HOUSE ITSELF

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
“virtual staging is literally a form of lying.”

[PARAPHRASE]
Wagner argues that digital staging can manipulate perceived scale and appearance, forcing a distinction between the physical room and its marketed image.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
THE REAL-ESTATE REPRESENTATION AS AN OPERATIVE DIGITAL TWIN THAT CAN MODIFY VALUE WITHOUT MODIFYING THE BUILDING.

LOCAL MOVE:
The earlier question was:

How do words become houses?

The listing introduces the reverse operation:

How does a house become representations that subsequently act upon the house?

A property now circulates simultaneously as:

physical dwelling
photograph
floor plan
database row
bed/bath count
square footage
style label
price
staged image
search result.

The market may encounter this representational body before anyone encounters the building.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The house no longer needs to change physically for its perceived rooms, furniture, atmosphere, scale, desirability, or likely inhabitant to change.

Representation has acquired a mutation channel independent of construction.

The marketed house can therefore enter states that the material house has never occupied.

QUESTION:
Which house is economically operative during a sale:

the physical building or its platform-generated representational twin?

DEEPER QUESTION:
When decisions about buying, valuing, renovating, financing, and imitating houses are increasingly made from their representations, can the representational twin begin selecting the future evolution of the physical housing stock?

MECHANISM:
physical house
→ capture / measurement / description
→ listing representation
→ staging / classification / ranking
→ buyer perception
→ price / demand / purchase decisions
→ renovation and development incentives
→ future physical houses

The representation returns causally to the world it represents.

FORMAL SHIFT:
HOUSE → DESCRIPTION

becomes recursive:

HOUSE_t
→ REPRESENTATION(HOUSE_t)
→ MARKET ACTION
→ HOUSE_t+1

Representation is no longer terminal documentation.

It enters the causal loop.

SOURCE FORMALISM:

→ ALTERED MARKET IMAGE

Wagner specifically notes that staging can make space appear larger or smaller.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

H = physical house
R = represented house

R = ENCODE(H)

R' = TRANSFORM(R)

DECISION = MARKET(R')

H may remain unchanged while:

VALUE(H | R') ≠ VALUE(H | R)

Then:

R'
→ economic action
→ eventual mutation of H

TENSION:
Representation can help people imagine occupation.

But the same power allows the imagined occupation to outrun the evidence supplied by the room.

The representation is useful precisely because it does not remain faithful to the current state.

MISSING:

BOUNDARY:
A virtual image is not the physical house.

The claim is not ontological equivalence.

The research question concerns causal agency:

whether decisions made from R substantially determine what subsequently happens to H.
