TITLE:
THE ROOF PROBLEM IS NOT JUST THAT FAILURE MUST BE LEGIBLE; THE MATERIAL WORLD MUST BE ALLOWED TO INVALIDATE THE MODEL.

SOURCE:
Tim Ingold — Making: Anthropology, Archaeology, Art and Architecture — 2013 — Chapter 4, “On Building a House,” pp. 47–48.

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
“the idea is crystalline, the fact fluid”

[PARAPHRASE]
Ingold describes builders as inhabiting the mismatch between architectural conception and a world of changing materials, weather, decay and unforeseen conditions. Plans do not eliminate this discrepancy; builders repeatedly improvise because operations do not simply unfold according to plan.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
LEGIBILITY REQUIRES CONTACT WITH THE WORLD THAT CAN PRODUCE THE FAILURE.

LOCAL MOVE:
The paper’s Roof Problem says deferred formalization works only when correctness is legible from outcomes. Ingold adds a prior condition: an outcome discloses a failure only if the artifact is exposed to the forces under which that failure exists.

SOURCE TERMS:
plan; process; improvise; materials; rain; leak; world; fluid; completion; builder

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The problem is not merely CAN THE USER RECOGNIZE THE FAILURE? Before that: HAS THE GENERATIVE LOOP CREATED THE CONDITIONS UNDER WHICH THE FAILURE CAN APPEAR? A rendered roof cannot visibly leak if it never encountered rain.

QUESTION:
Is failure legibility a property of the artifact, or of the artifact-plus-test-environment?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does deferred formalization eventually require the generation loop to expand from artifact generation into world simulation, execution, instrumentation, and testing?

MECHANISM:
A representation proposes a form; artifact encounters omitted conditions; conditions produce effects; effects become perceptible through inspection/instrumentation; only then can a new constraint be formulated.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<GENERATED ARTIFACT>
→ <ARTIFACT UNDER RELEVANT CONDITIONS>
→ [EXPOSE / MEASURE / NOTICE FAILURE]

SOURCE FORMALISM:

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
GENERATE → EXPOSE TO WORLD W → OBSERVE → DIAGNOSE → CORRECT → RE-EXPOSE. Correctness is indexed: CORRECT(A, W, TEST), not merely CORRECT(A).

TENSION:
Cheap generation makes iteration cheap, but if meaningful exposure is expensive, generation is not the bottleneck. Testing may become the scarce resource; judgement must be split from construction of adequate test conditions.

MISSING:
A taxonomy of FAILURE ACCESSIBILITY: directly legible; legible after interaction; after simulation; only with instrumentation; only after long duration; not reliably legible.

BOUNDARY:
Ingold is discussing architecture, materials, and maintenance, not software validation; transformation into a theory of test environments is [OUR INFERENCE].
