TITLE:
INGOLD’S “EVERY TECHNICAL GESTURE IS A QUESTION” MAKES EXECUTION ITSELF A SPECIFICATION-PRODUCING ACT.

SOURCE:
Tim Ingold — Making: Anthropology, Archaeology, Art and Architecture — 2013 — Chapter 2, “The Materials of Life,” p. 31.

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
“Their every technical gesture is a question, to which the material responds according to its bent.”

[PARAPHRASE]
Ingold describes making as correspondence: practitioners act into materials, perceive how materials respond, and continue in relation to what those responses disclose.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
EXECUTION AS INQUIRY.

LOCAL MOVE:
Ingold turns material operation into an epistemic act. A gesture does not merely realize existing knowledge; it produces information about what can happen next. This is close to deferred formalization, except the next specification can be elicited from resistance and response of the thing being made.

SOURCE TERMS:
technical gesture; question; responds; follow; correspondence; materials; potentials; becoming; forces; flows

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The paper’s loop is DESCRIBE → GENERATE → INSPECT → CORRECT → REGENERATE. Ingold suggests a more general primitive: PROBE → RESPONSE → ADJUST. Generation is an experiment that makes previously unavailable information perceptible.

QUESTION:
Is deferred formalization fundamentally a programming phenomenon, or one instance of the older logic of skilled inquiry through making?

DEEPER QUESTION:
What exactly must an artifact reveal for its production to count as an epistemic probe rather than merely an output?

MECHANISM:
A practitioner performs an operation; material or situation responds; response discloses properties relevant to continuation; next operation adjusts; knowledge and form emerge together.

PROMPT₀ → GENERATION₀ → RESPONSE₀ → INFERENCE₁ → CONSTRAINT₁ → PROMPT₁. Or: ACT → READ CONSEQUENCE → UPDATE → ACT. Deferred formalization becomes a special case of ACTIVE INQUIRY.

TENSION:
“The Prompt Is Not the Program” gives FAILURE a central role; Ingold’s vocabulary is broader. The response can be resistance, affordance, unexpected capacity, deformation, new direction.

MISSING:
A taxonomy of artifact responses beyond PASS/FAIL: FAILURE, SURPRISE, AFFORDANCE, AMBIGUITY, SIDE EFFECT, NEW POSSIBILITY, LATENT DEFECT.
