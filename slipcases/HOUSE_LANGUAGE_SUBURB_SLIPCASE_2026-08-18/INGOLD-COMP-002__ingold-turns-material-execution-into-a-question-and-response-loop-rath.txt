TITLE:
INGOLD TURNS MATERIAL EXECUTION INTO A QUESTION-AND-RESPONSE LOOP RATHER THAN THE TERMINAL STAGE OF A PROGRAM.

SOURCE:
Tim Ingold — Making: Anthropology, Archaeology, Art and Architecture — 2013 — Chapter 2, “The Materials of Life,” p. 31.

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
“making is a process of correspondence”

RESEARCH OBJECT:
MATERIAL RESPONSE AS PART OF EXECUTION.

LOCAL MOVE:
Ingold says practitioners learn materials by following what happens to them; a technical gesture functions as a probe and material response matters for what comes next.

SOURCE TERMS:
follow; technical gesture; responds; correspond; bringing forth; potentials; becoming; forces; flows

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The lineage treats LANGUAGE as the new source of action. Ingold makes RESPONSE the more fundamental unit: if a sentence issues an instruction but nothing downstream can answer back, it may be executable but unable to make competently.

QUESTION:
Does a language truly participate in making when it can only command, or must an operative language also be capable of receiving answers from matter?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Could the decisive transition after “language becomes executable” be LANGUAGE BECOMES CORRESPONDENT: WORDS ⇄ WORLD?

MECHANISM:
Practitioner acts; material variation becomes perceptible; variation changes next gesture; making continues through coupled history.

SOURCE FORMALISM:

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
OPEN LOOP: PROMPT → MODEL → TOOLPATH → MACHINE → MATTER.
PRESSURE: PROMPT → MODEL → ACTION → MATERIAL STATE → SENSE → REVISE REPRESENTATION → NEXT ACTION → NEW MATERIAL STATE ↺.

TENSION:
[[HOUSE-COMP-006]] makes NC language important because statements have machining consequences; Ingold asks whether operations can alter themselves in response to what material actually does.

MISSING:
The boundary between feedback control, adaptive control, error correction, machine vision, material sensing, and craft correspondence.

BOUNDARY:
Ingold does not claim sensor feedback or cybernetic loops constitute correspondence; equivalence requires evidence.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[HOUSE-COMP-000]] → Ingold on correspondence → Deleuze/Guattari → cybernetics → adaptive machining → construction robotics.

TEST:
Fabricate the same irregular material with fixed toolpath and with sensing/replanning; ask whether the adaptive system merely corrects deviation from a fixed target or allows the target itself to change.
