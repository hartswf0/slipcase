TITLE: The Builder’s Game locates meaning in use
ORIGINAL ID: Z09
TYPE: NEW ZETTEL

CLAIM
The builder’s call does not contain its complete operation inside the word; its meaning is inseparable from the activity in which the call and response have a place.

EVIDENCE
In Philosophical Investigations §2, the builder calls names such as “slab” and the assistant brings the learned building stone; later remarks explicitly treat language as woven into activity.

TENSION
The primitive language-game should not be mistaken for a general model in which all language reduces to commands.

QUESTION
What additional practices make an underspecified prompt actionable in contemporary generative systems?
