TITLE: The corrective loop is the unit of skilled prompting
ORIGINAL ID: Z06
TYPE: NEW ZETTEL

CLAIM
Prompting skill is located in repeated cycles of seeing, redescribing, acting, and checking rather than in the quality of a single initial instruction.

EVIDENCE
The user’s “making the right decision over and over again” is better read as recovering an adequate next move from what actually happened. Schön’s reflection-in-action and Suchman’s situated action support this shift.

TENSION
A loop can still fail if feedback is delayed, hidden, misleading, or irreversible.

QUESTION
How can prompting systems expose enough state for users to distinguish model consequence from uncontrolled disturbance?
