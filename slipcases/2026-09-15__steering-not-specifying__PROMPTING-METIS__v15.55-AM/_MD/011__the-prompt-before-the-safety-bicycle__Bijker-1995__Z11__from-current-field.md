TITLE: The prompt before the safety bicycle
ORIGINAL ID: Z11
TYPE: NEW ZETTEL

CLAIM
The prompt may be a pre-closure technical object: instruction, conversation, code, creative material, agent objective, multimodal gesture, and control surface remain bundled under one unstable term.

EVIDENCE
Bijker’s bicycle history shows technical artifacts taking different forms and meanings across relevant social groups before stabilization. The user proposes the prompt as analogous to the bicycle before the safety bicycle.

TENSION
The analogy is diagnostic, not predictive; it does not establish that prompting will converge on one stable form.

QUESTION
Which prompt forms are stabilizing, for whom, and around which problems?
