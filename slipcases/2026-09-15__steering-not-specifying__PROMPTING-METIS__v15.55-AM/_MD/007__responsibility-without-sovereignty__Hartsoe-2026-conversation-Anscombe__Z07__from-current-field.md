TITLE: Responsibility without sovereignty
ORIGINAL ID: Z07
TYPE: NEW ZETTEL

CLAIM
Agency in uncertain systems occupies a middle ground between total authorship and total absolution: actors can be answerable for what they do within conditions they did not choose.

EVIDENCE
The user formulates the distinction as chance = conditions not chosen, consequence = what follows from action within them, and mêtis = the capacity to tell the difference and act anyway. Anscombe’s account of action under descriptions helps separate physical events from the descriptions under which they count as intentional.

TENSION
Responsibility cannot be assigned solely by subjective intention once downstream effects become visible and further action remains possible.

QUESTION
At what points in a generative workflow does newly available knowledge change what the actor is responsible for doing next?
