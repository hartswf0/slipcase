TITLE: House of Dust makes description into a score for realization
ORIGINAL ID: Z12
TYPE: NEW ZETTEL

CLAIM
House of Dust demonstrates a chain in which generated language describes possible architectures and a selected description is taken up as a practical score for physical and social realization.

EVIDENCE
Project histories describe Knowles’s four lists, Tenney’s FORTRAN implementation, computer-generated quatrains, and the later translation of a quatrain into a functional structure that became a site for classes and events.

TENSION
The description does not mechanically compile into architecture; human interpretation, construction knowledge, material constraints, and institutional context fill the gap.

QUESTION
What changes when generative systems increasingly participate in the interpretive steps that once remained human and institutional?
