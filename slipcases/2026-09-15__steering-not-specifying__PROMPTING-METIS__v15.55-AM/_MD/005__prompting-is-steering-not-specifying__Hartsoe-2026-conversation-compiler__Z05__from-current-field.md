TITLE: Prompting is steering, not specifying
ORIGINAL ID: Z05
TYPE: NEW ZETTEL

CLAIM
Prompting is better modeled as steering an indeterminate process than specifying a fully determined outcome.

EVIDENCE
The user contrasts the commander whose orders make reality obey with the navigator who observes what happened and alters the next action. Suchman and Schön provide parallel accounts of plans and professional action that do not prescribe every situated detail.

TENSION
Steering should not become a metaphor that hides where systems constrain, channel, or preempt human action.

QUESTION
Which interface features make steering possible by exposing consequences soon enough for correction?
