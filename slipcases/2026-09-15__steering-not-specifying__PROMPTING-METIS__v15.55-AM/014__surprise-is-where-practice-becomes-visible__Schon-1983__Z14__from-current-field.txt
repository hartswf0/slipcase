TITLE: Surprise is where practice becomes visible
ORIGINAL ID: Z14
TYPE: NEW ZETTEL

CLAIM
Uncertainty and surprise expose tacit knowing and provoke reflection-in-action, making breakdown a productive site for studying skilled prompting.

EVIDENCE
Schön’s reflective practitioner addresses professional action in uncertain, unique, unstable situations and the improvisational reflection elicited by surprise.

TENSION
Not every surprise is recoverable; some systems make errors opaque or consequences irreversible.

QUESTION
Can capability arenas be designed so breakdown and repair are observable without artificially simplifying the practice?
