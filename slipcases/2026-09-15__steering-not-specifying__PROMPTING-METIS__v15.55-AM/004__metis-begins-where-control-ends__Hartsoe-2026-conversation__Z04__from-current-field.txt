TITLE: Mêtis begins where total control ends
ORIGINAL ID: Z04
TYPE: NEW ZETTEL

CLAIM
Mêtis names practical intelligence for unstable circumstances: anticipation, timing, adaptation, and cunning exercised because the situation cannot be fixed in advance.

EVIDENCE
Detienne and Vernant characterize mêtis through shifting, polymorphic situations and navigation; the user develops the helmsman as a model for prompting in uncertain worlds.

TENSION
Mêtis can be romanticized as individual cleverness unless its dependence on material conditions, feedback, and social practices remains visible.

QUESTION
Can mêtis be operationalized as observable repair and course-correction rather than treated as a heroic personality trait?
