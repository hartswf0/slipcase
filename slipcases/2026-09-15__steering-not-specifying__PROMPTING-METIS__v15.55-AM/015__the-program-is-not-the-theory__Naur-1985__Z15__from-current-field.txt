TITLE: The program text is not the theory of the program
ORIGINAL ID: Z15
TYPE: NEW ZETTEL

CLAIM
Program text does not exhaust the practical theory that makes modification, explanation, and continued work possible.

EVIDENCE
Naur argues that the primary activity of programming is theory building rather than text production, especially visible when programs encounter unexpected behavior or changing demands.

TENSION
The analogy to prompting is strongest at the level of practice and maintenance, not because prompts and programs are technically identical.

QUESTION
What tacit or situated theory does a skilled prompter build about a model-and-tool system over repeated use?
