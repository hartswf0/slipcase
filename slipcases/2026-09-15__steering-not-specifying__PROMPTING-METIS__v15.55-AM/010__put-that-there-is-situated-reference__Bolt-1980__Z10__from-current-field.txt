TITLE: Put That There turns deixis into operation through a situated interface
ORIGINAL ID: Z10
TYPE: NEW ZETTEL

CLAIM
“That” and “there” become executable not because their textual semantics are complete but because speech, pointing, visible objects, spatial state, and feedback are coordinated.

EVIDENCE
Bolt’s system combined voice and gesture so pronouns could refer economically; MIT’s project description emphasizes redundant channels, context-sensitive interpretation, early feedback, and easy correction under imperfect recognition.

TENSION
Naturalness in multimodal input can obscure the considerable machinery that resolves reference and constrains valid action.

QUESTION
How should contemporary prompt interfaces surface the hidden contextual machinery that makes language executable?
