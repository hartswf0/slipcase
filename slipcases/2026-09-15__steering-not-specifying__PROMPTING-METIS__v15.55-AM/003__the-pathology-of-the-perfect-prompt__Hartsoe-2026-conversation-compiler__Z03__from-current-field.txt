TITLE: The pathology of the perfect prompt
ORIGINAL ID: Z03
TYPE: NEW ZETTEL

CLAIM
The “perfect prompt” ideal mistakes successful action for complete specification and treats contingency as a defect to be eliminated.

EVIDENCE
The user’s navigation framing makes competence depend on continued adjustment under changing conditions, not on commands that force reality to obey.

TENSION
When outputs diverge, discourse oscillates between blaming the prompt and invoking randomness, while preserving the same control model.

QUESTION
Which HAI evaluation regimes reward control fantasies instead of situated competence?
