WHAT THIS FIELD IS
A source-led research lineage moving from Black Mountain College's productive institutional mess through Black Mountain SOLE, Sugata Mitra's SOLE design, Harrison Owen's Open Space Technology, Jo Freeman on task-relative structure, and the Granny Cloud's embodied infrastructural history.

FINAL PAPER WAGER
Self-organization does not abolish specification; it relocates specification into a constitutional layer of constraints, infrastructure, and revision rights. The important variable is not amount of structure but where conditions are fixed, what trajectories remain open, whether those conditions still fit the task, and who can change the fixing rules.

STRONGEST CONTRADICTION
Systems presented as open repeatedly depend on conditions their rhetoric pushes to the edge: device scarcity, facilitation timing, boundary transcripts, funding, centre access, volunteer availability, participant prior capacity, and asymmetric control over material survival.

STRANGEST STEPPING STONES
014 — SUCCESS BY BYPASS: the celebrated Black Mountain SOLE success did not use the flagship MOOC technology.
021 — THE CLOUD HAD BODIES: a remote pedagogical cloud became unavailable when children could not reach centres and volunteers themselves aged.
019/020 — THE BLANK WALL IS A PROGRAM: Open Space's visible openness depends on a prior procedural frame, including temporary protection of the meta-level.
022 — TASK-RELATIVE STRUCTURE: a coordination form can work until the task changes.

PROVISIONAL SYNTHESIS
023 — STRUCTURED OPENNESS remains a killable hypothesis, not a completion claim. Its constraint ledger is SO=<T,C,X,A>: task, conditions held fixed, trajectories left open, authority/process for revising conditions. If the ledger cannot discriminate cases better than generic structure/freedom language, discard it.
