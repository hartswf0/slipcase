BMC-MESS-006-A-A

TITLE:
Open Space contains a complaint operator that routes failure back to the participant.

SOURCE:
Harrison Owen — A Brief User’s Guide to Open Space Technology — Open Space World — n.d.

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE] “there is exactly one person to complain to. Yourself.”

RESEARCH OBJECT:
A procedural architecture for relocating responsibility. If an issue is absent from the self-generated agenda, the participant cannot treat that absence solely as a failure of organizers; the method routes responsibility back to the person who failed to introduce it.

LOCAL MOVE:
Owen makes self-organization operational not merely by distributing freedom but by attaching responsibility for missing possibilities to participants themselves.

SOURCE TERMS:
“personal responsibility”
“complain”
“yourself”
“passion”
“genuine responsibility”
“in charge”

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Self-organization has a theory of complaint. The location where grievance is allowed to terminate may be as important as the location where decision power begins.

QUESTION:
When does routing complaint toward the participant generate agency, and when does it make structural defects difficult to name?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Could radically participatory institutions contain a built-in failure mode in which every missing support becomes evidence that somebody failed to self-organize it?

MECHANISM:
desired issue/activity/support is absent
→ no central organizer is assigned responsibility for supplying it
→ participant is authorized to create it
→ absence persists
→ procedural logic points back toward participant action
→ structural complaint is translated into personal responsibility.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<missing institutional provision>
→ <available participant initiative>
→ [ROUTE RESPONSIBILITY TO SELF]
→ <act, leave, or own the absence>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
In Owen’s agenda-setting procedure, participants introduce issues they care about, convene the resulting group, and take responsibility for recording its results.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Define:

C(x) = address to which complaint about missing x is routed.

Centralized institution:
C(x) → administrator / designated role.

Open Space:
C(x) → participant who wants x.

This changes not merely governance but causal diagnosis.

A future institution could therefore be mapped by its COMPLAINT ROUTING TABLE.

TENSION:
Inside a short voluntary meeting, this routing may be precisely what makes participant agency possible. Inside a residential educational institution with unequal money, expertise, time, authority, or vulnerability, the same operator could erase differences in actual capacity to create what is missing.

MISSING:
Evidence showing whether Black Mountain SOLE explicitly transferred this responsibility logic from Open Space into its responses to participants asking for teachers, academic programming, funding, or creative infrastructure.

BOUNDARY:
Owen does not claim that institutions should refuse responsibility for structural conditions. The potentially troubling generalization appears only when the procedure is transplanted beyond its original event context.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[BMC-MESS-006-A]]
→ participants feel obligated to make the experiment succeed
→ original Open Space procedure
→ responsibility for missing agenda items terminates at the participant
→ investigate whether commitment became an architecture for absorbing institutional failure.

TEST:
Take documented complaints from Black Mountain SOLE and classify the response: institution changes structure; participant is asked to create the missing provision; participant is encouraged to reinterpret the problem; participant exits. Compare the distribution by complaint type and by participant power.
