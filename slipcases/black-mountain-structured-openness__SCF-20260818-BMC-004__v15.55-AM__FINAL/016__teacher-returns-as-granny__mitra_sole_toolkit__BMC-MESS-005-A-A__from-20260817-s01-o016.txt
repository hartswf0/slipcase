BMC-MESS-005-A-A

TITLE:
The teacher did not disappear from SOLE; she returned as a remote grandmother.

SOURCE:
Sugata Mitra — SOLE Toolkit: How to Bring Self-Organised Learning Environments to Your Community — School in the Cloud — n.d. — pp. 8–10.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] The toolkit specifies “Skype Grannies” who encourage children while also helping develop language fluency and search skills. Elsewhere it describes effective educators as witnesses, supporters, and providers of structure rather than suppliers of answers.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Pedagogical authority can disappear at the level of answer-giving while remaining crucial at the levels of affect, language, search competence, confidence, witnessing, and environmental structure.

LOCAL MOVE:
The toolkit quietly replaces “teacher” with a more peculiar technical role: a person whose function is not primarily to transmit content but to maintain the learner’s ability and desire to continue searching.

SOURCE TERMS:
“Skype Grannies”
“mediators”
“encouraging”
“language fluency”
“search skills”
“witnesses”
“supporters”
“structure providers”
“not answer-suppliers”

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The supposedly minimally invasive environment contains an indispensable human component whose intervention may be difficult to recognize as teaching precisely because it operates through encouragement rather than answers.

QUESTION:
What if the irreducible pedagogical primitive is not instruction but encouragement?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can an institution radically decentralize epistemic authority while increasing its dependence on affective labor?

MECHANISM:
learner encounters difficult question
→ information is technically available
→ linguistic/search/affective barrier prevents productive access
→ mediator encourages, converses, models searching, or sustains confidence
→ learner resumes self-directed investigation
→ resulting knowledge remains attributed to self-organization rather than direct instruction.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<teacher as answer source>
→ <remote encouraging mediator>
→ [SUSTAIN SEARCH CAPACITY]
→ <continued self-directed learning>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
The SOLE Toolkit explicitly includes adult encouragement within student-driven learning, gives Skype Grannies roles in language fluency and search skills, and characterizes educators as witnesses, supporters, and structure providers.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Self-directed learning may require:

INFORMATION ACCESS
+
SEARCH CAPACITY
+
AFFECTIVE PERSISTENCE
+
SOCIAL ENCOURAGEMENT.

Removing ANSWER SUPPLY does not imply:

ADULT FUNCTION = 0.

Instead:

teacher.content_authority ↓
teacher.search_and_affect_scaffolding ↑ or persists.

TENSION:
Calling the role “Granny” may reveal a second hidden operation: skilled pedagogical labor can be reframed as naturalized care, volunteerism, warmth, or personality rather than expertise.

MISSING:
How much intervention Grannies actually performed; whether their effectiveness came from encouragement, English competence, pedagogical judgment, age, distance, novelty, consistency, or some combination.

BOUNDARY:
The toolkit establishes that adult mediation belongs inside the SOLE design. It does not establish which component of that mediation is causally necessary.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[BMC-MESS-005-A]]
→ historical Black Mountain suggests openness may require continuous pedagogical attention
→ SOLE Toolkit
→ supposedly self-organized learning explicitly installs an encouraging mediator
→ follow encouragement as infrastructure rather than as atmosphere.

TEST:
Compare recorded SOLE sessions with no mediator, generic technical facilitator, trained teacher, and Granny-style encouraging mediator. Code interventions by operation and determine which interventions restart stalled inquiry without supplying answers.
