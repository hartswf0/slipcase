TITLE:
The Law of Two Feet changes category when a meeting protocol becomes a school philosophy.

SOURCE:
Harrison Owen — A Brief User’s Guide to Open Space Technology — Open Space World — n.d.; Nora Caplan-Bricker — “This North Carolina Campus Was Meant to Show Off the Future of Online Education” — The New Republic — 2013.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] Owen’s Open Space Technology assumes an interested and committed group, a defined theme, a time, a place, and a leader. Participation should be voluntary and informed. Within that bounded event, participants use the Law of Two Feet to move where they can contribute. Black Mountain SOLE displayed the same Law as a general rule of student participation.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
A rule for mobility inside a temporary voluntary event can mutate when transplanted into an institution. “Leave this conversation when it ceases to be useful” is not necessarily equivalent to “assemble your education by continually exiting activities.”

LOCAL MOVE:
Following the phrase “Law of Two Feet” backward reveals an unexpected source domain: not educational theory but a technology for organizing meetings.

SOURCE TERMS:
“Law of Two Feet”
“voluntary”
“interested and committed”

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A rule that appears radically anti-institutional was originally nested inside a carefully bounded facilitation apparatus.

QUESTION:
What happened to the Law of Two Feet when Black Mountain SOLE moved it from a temporary Open Space event into the ongoing architecture of education?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Which experimental institutions are built from organizational techniques whose original boundary conditions disappear during transplantation?

MECHANISM:
source technique defines bounded event
→ participants voluntarily enter shared theme and temporal container
→ local exit enables movement among conversations
→ technique migrates into educational institution
→ local mobility rule is generalized into learning philosophy
→ original preconditions may no longer remain visible.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Open Space Technology specifies a theme, committed group, time, place, leader, overall framework, voluntary participation, and participant mobility through the Law of Two Feet.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Original operator:

ENTER voluntarily
→ accept FRAME
→ MOVE freely within FRAME
→ EXIT local conversation when non-contributing.

Possible institutional mutation:

FRAME becomes background
→ MOVE/EXIT becomes foreground principle
→ local mobility is interpreted as generalized autonomy.

The operation survives while some of its preconditions become invisible.

TENSION:
The textual resemblance strongly motivates a genealogy search but does not by itself prove how Black Mountain SOLE acquired the Open Space language or whether founders understood its original scope.

MISSING:

BOUNDARY:
This evidence establishes a striking correspondence and a change of context. It does not yet establish direct intellectual influence.
