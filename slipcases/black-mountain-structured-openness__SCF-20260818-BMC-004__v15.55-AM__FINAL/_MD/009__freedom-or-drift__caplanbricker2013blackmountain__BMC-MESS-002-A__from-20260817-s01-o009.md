TITLE:
A productive mess may require participants who already possess the capacity to navigate it.

SOURCE:
Nora Caplan-Bricker — “This North Carolina Campus Was Meant to Show Off the Future of Online Education” — The New Republic — 2013 — https://newrepublic.com/article/116014/black-mountain-sole-mooc-campus-online-learners-has-rough-start

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] SOLE appeared to work best for participants who arrived with plausible projects. Ciara Kosior, who arrived without one, reported feeling adrift; Tara Byrne later said she would have added more leaders and traveling teachers rather than relying entirely on participants themselves.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Tolerance for institutional mess may not be a property of an institution alone. It may depend upon competencies participants bring before entering it: self-direction, project formation, time management, confidence, social capital, and the ability to solicit mentors.

LOCAL MOVE:
Caplan-Bricker shifts the unit of analysis from “how much disorder can the institution sustain?” to “which participants can convert that disorder into usable freedom?”

SOURCE TERMS:
“felt adrift”
“plausible idea”
“do-it-yourself learning”
“more leaders”
“teachers”
“structure”

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The same lack of structure can appear as freedom to one participant and abandonment to another.

QUESTION:
What capacities must a learner already possess before an unstructured learning environment becomes educational rather than merely under-specified?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Do radical pedagogies sometimes mistake competencies produced by previous schooling, privilege, professional experience, or temperament for universal capacities of self-directed learners?

MECHANISM:
formal scaffolding is removed
→ participants must generate goals, schedules, projects, mentors, evaluation criteria, and momentum themselves
→ participants enter with unequal capacities for those operations
→ high-self-direction participants convert openness into opportunity
→ others experience the same openness as drift.

FORMAL SHIFT:
<shared institutional mess>
→ <participant-specific capacity to navigate uncertainty>
→ [SELF-DIRECT]
→ <freedom OR drift>

SOURCE FORMALISM:
The source contrasts participants arriving with established projects against participants seeking a broader educational experience and reports requests for additional leaders and teachers.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]

Let M = environmental under-specification.
Let N_i = navigation capacity of participant i.
Let S = supplied scaffolding.

Usable autonomy for participant i might depend on:

A_i = f(N_i + S - M)

The same M can therefore produce:

A_i > threshold → autonomy
A_j < threshold → drift.

The institutional question is not simply how much M is productive, but for whom.

TENSION:
Historical Black Mountain College also demanded unusual autonomy from students, including deciding when they were prepared for advancement. Yet it retained faculty, intensive teaching, examinations, studios, work programs, and sustained disciplinary expertise. Its “mess” may therefore have contained considerably more scaffolding than the SOLE rhetoric of self-organization suggests.

MISSING:
A precise inventory of the pedagogical scaffolds present at historical Black Mountain College but absent at SOLE.

BOUNDARY:
Kosior’s and Byrne’s experiences cannot establish a universal requirement for teachers or conventional structure. They instead reveal heterogeneity in what learners require from an ostensibly shared environment.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[BMC-MESS-002]]
→ ask how a productive mess remains between excessive order and collapse
→ SOLE participant divergence
→ replace one institution-wide disorder variable with a relation between disorder and participant capacity.

TEST:
Compare participants who flourished and participants who left SOLE. Code prior project clarity, professional experience, self-directed learning history, access to mentors, financial security, disciplinary training, and desired form of education. Test whether success tracks commitment to the ideology or pre-existing capacity to operate without scaffolding.
