Observed on the official Granny Cloud history page:
- The project describes encouragement and access as central to its model.
- Early SOLE labs found internet use constrained by children's English comprehension; the Granny Cloud emerged as remote conversational mediation.
- Earlier centres closed in part because of funding constraints and school apathy.
- During COVID-19, children could not reach centres and often had minimal or no home internet.
- The organization also reports ageing volunteers and health challenges as factors in its June 2022 wind-down.
- The page reports a 2026 restart at one location, Phaltan, with a fresh volunteer team.

This note is a verification receipt, not a local copy of the webpage body.
