Observed on Jo Freeman's author-hosted text:
- Freeman argues that groups inevitably develop structure, whether formal or informal.
- Informal, loose groups may fit consciousness-raising but often founder when the task changes toward coordinated action and the organizational form does not change with it.
- The absence of explicit structure can leave decision rules legible only to insiders and make informal power difficult to contest.
- Freeman proposes explicit, accountable, distributed authority rather than a simple return to rigid hierarchy.
