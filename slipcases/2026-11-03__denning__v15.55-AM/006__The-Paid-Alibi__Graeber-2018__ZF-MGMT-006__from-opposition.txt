ZF-MGMT-006

TITLE:
The deck can be bullshit and still earn every dollar of its fee

SOURCE:
David Graeber — Bullshit Jobs: A Theory — 2018 — final working definition.
Steve Denning — Why Business Schools And Consulting Firms Are Adding So Little Value — 2026 — §6.

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE — Graeber] “the employee feels obliged to pretend that this is not the case”
[QUOTE — Denning] “for legitimacy and political cover”

RESEARCH OBJECT:
A deliverable that fails its stated purpose and succeeds at protecting its buyer.

LOCAL MOVE:
Graeber makes compelled pretense part of the definition. Denning identifies a reason the executive might be satisfied with work the analyst privately considers a sham.

SOURCE TERMS:
“pretend”; “legitimacy”; “political cover.”

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A repeat client can be evidence that the hidden service is excellent. The analyst sold an independent judgment in public and supplied an alibi in private.

QUESTION:
What happens to the engagement when the analyst’s best-supported answer contradicts the sponsor’s preferred decision?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Who inside the engagement can say what the client is really buying without jeopardizing the contract or their job?

MECHANISM:
[OUR INFERENCE] The buyer commissions ostensibly open analysis. The team learns which conclusions can survive review. The final deck gives a prior decision the appearance of having been independently reached.

FORMAL SHIFT:
Predetermined decision → apparently open inquiry → [filter findings through sponsor review] → signed endorsement.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Score an engagement separately on whether findings changed the decision, whether attribution changed its defensibility, and whether staff felt obliged to misrepresent the work.

TENSION:
An independent review can genuinely confirm the prior decision. The decisive distinction is whether the reviewers had permission and practical means to reach the opposite result.

MISSING:
The original brief, discarded findings, review comments, and private accounts from the people who did the work.

BOUNDARY:
One foreclosed engagement supports a claim about that assignment. Graeber’s job-level diagnosis requires evidence about the employee’s role and the pretense it demands.

CITATION TRAIL:
Graeber’s final working definition and “Box-tickers.” Denning §6. Follow the statement of work into redlines, board minutes, and analyst interviews.

TEST:
Find one analytically material result that cut against the sponsor’s preference. Trace whether it reached the decision packet intact and whether anyone could act on it.
