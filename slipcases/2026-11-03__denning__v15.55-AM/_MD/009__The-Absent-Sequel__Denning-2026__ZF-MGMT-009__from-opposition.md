TITLE:
The MBA case can end exactly where accountability begins

SOURCE:
Steve Denning — Why Business Schools And Consulting Firms Are Adding So Little Value — 2026 — opening discussion of the case method.
Dan Davies — Taming the Unaccountability Machine — “Control and communication.”

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE — Denning] “the vantage point of a single top executive”
[QUOTE — Davies] “the decided-upon”

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Whether a teaching case gives people subject to a decision any power to reopen it.

LOCAL MOVE:
Denning describes a restriction of viewpoint. Davies adds a separate question about whether the people living with a decision can communicate what the decision-maker’s model discarded.

SOURCE TERMS:
“case method”; “single top executive”; “the decided-upon.”

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A case can include moving testimony from workers and customers and still give them no standing. Their voices can become more material for the executive to interpret while the executive retains every decision right.

QUESTION:
What happens if students must design the appeal that could overturn their own recommendation?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does the exercise train managers to exercise judgment, or to treat other people’s judgment as data they alone are entitled to weigh?

MECHANISM:
[OUR INFERENCE] The case packet fixes what enters as evidence. The assignment hands the student a decision right. The exercise ends at recommendation, so consequences and appeals fall outside the graded task.

FORMAL SHIFT:
Organizational conflict → bounded executive case → [choose and defend a decision] → grade issued before the consequences arrive.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
NONE

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX] Give a second group the same case plus a later harm report from an affected actor. Grade whether that actor can trigger review, introduce new evidence, and secure a binding revision.

TENSION:
A focused executive case can be a legitimate exercise in decision-making under pressure. Repetition and grading determine whether the omitted feedback becomes a durable lesson.

MISSING:
Actual case packets, grading rubrics, sequels, and evidence of how graduates transfer the exercise to work.

BOUNDARY:
The pasted article describes a tendency. Whether any particular course teaches unaccountability turns on its instructional design and assessment.

CITATION TRAIL:
Read an original teaching case with its instructor guide and any follow-up case. Locate the last moment at which an affected actor can change the outcome.

TEST:
Compare two versions of the same case. Both include affected actors’ testimony. Only one gives those actors a binding right to trigger review. Check whether students change their original decision or merely add sympathetic language.
