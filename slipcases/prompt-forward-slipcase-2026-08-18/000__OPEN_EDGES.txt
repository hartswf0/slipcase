OPEN EDGES

Compiled from MISSING, BOUNDARY, QUESTION, DEEPER QUESTION, and TEST fields.

## PF-001 — Disclosure templates do not adjudicate disclosure thresholds.
QUESTION: What artifact teaches researchers how qualitative AI-disclosure standards are actually applied?
DEEPER: Does AI governance need adjudicated exemplars, or are discipline-specific precedents supposed to perform that work?
MISSING: Published examples containing interaction, artifact, disclosure decision, and rationale.
BOUNDARY: The policy's lack of adjudicated exemplars does not establish that no such precedents exist elsewhere at Georgia Tech.
TEST: Find ten real disclosure decisions and compare which properties actually predict the judgment.

## PF-002 — Amount of interaction and amount of contribution are different variables.
QUESTION: Should AI scholarship distinguish interaction volume, informational influence, and intellectual judgment?
DEEPER: Can informational contribution capture choices such as rejection, source selection, or diagnosis that leave little lexical trace in the output?
MISSING: Selection, rejection, validation, and orchestration that do not survive as output text.
BOUNDARY: The paper does not claim to measure scholarly authorship.
TEST: Construct pairs where a five-word prompt has high causal leverage and a thousand-word prompt has low leverage, then compare token-count and contribution metrics.

## PF-003 — Governance can regulate prompts without giving them a scholarly ontology.

MISSING: Evidence that this deferral causes actual misclassification.

TEST: Compare how the same prompt artifact is classified by a policy office, an IRB, a software venue, and a dissertation committee.

## PF-004 — Contribution taxonomies already fracture the simple human-author category.
QUESTION: Could AI-assisted scholarship be described first through contribution roles rather than percentage-of-AI labels?

BOUNDARY: CRediT cannot simply be treated as an AI authorship standard.
TEST: Take twenty AI-assisted research workflows and attempt to encode them using only current CRediT roles; record recurrent residue.
