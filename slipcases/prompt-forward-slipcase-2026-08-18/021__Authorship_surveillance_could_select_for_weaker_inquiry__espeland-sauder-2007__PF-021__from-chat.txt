TITLE:
Authorship surveillance could select for weaker inquiry.

SOURCE:
Espeland and Sauder — Rankings and Reactivity — 2007.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] Evaluated actors adapt behavior to the categories and incentives produced by measurement systems.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
If “looking authorial” is rewarded, researchers may optimize the record rather than the inquiry.

LOCAL MOVE:
The source supplies the mechanism by which an evaluative category can reshape practice.

SOURCE TERMS:
reactivity; incentives; commensuration.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A transparency policy could theoretically reduce exploratory use while increasing superficially compliant behavior.

QUESTION:
Does perceived AI-authorship scrutiny reduce the willingness to solicit strong counterarguments, unfamiliar ideas, or machine initiative?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Could a governance regime accidentally reward epistemically weaker human-AI collaboration?
