# Authorship, Control, Contribution

Derived map of content; immutable cards remain authoritative.

- [[PF-004]] Contribution taxonomies already fracture the simple human-author category.
- [[PF-005]] Intellectual labor in a prompt does not automatically become copyright authorship.
- [[PF-034]] Ablation establishes causal dependence, not scholarly contribution.
- [[PF-038]] Contribution can be decomposed without forcing the verbs back into one author noun.
- [[PF-042]] Selection counts as authorship only when the selection itself is sufficiently creative.
- [[PF-043]] Orchestration can be a contribution role without becoming writing authorship.
- [[PF-048]] A prompt can be a contribution without being a sufficient authorship claim.
- [[PF-056]] Explicit selection rules are not necessary for selection to be creative.
- [[PF-057]] Expressive arrangement and process orchestration are different kinds of control.
- [[PF-072]] A committee can inspect contribution roles without requiring human production of every word.
- [[PF-082]] Prompt sophistication does not guarantee expressive control.
