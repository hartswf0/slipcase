# Prompt as Epistemic Instrument

Derived map of content; immutable cards remain authoritative.

- [[PF-007]] The blueprint analogy overstates how much a prompt controls execution.
- [[PF-009]] Prompt robustness belongs to a prompt–model relation, not to the prompt alone.
- [[PF-010]] A prompt becomes instrument-like only when its calibration conditions are specified.
- [[PF-036]] A method can be linguistic without every linguistic instruction becoming method.
- [[PF-051]] A good prompt can be robust, effective, interpretable, or transferable—and these need not coincide.
- [[PF-052]] Calibration turns “prompt as instrument” into an ensemble claim.
- [[PF-079]] Scientific instruments can themselves embody knowledge rather than merely subtract from human knowledge.
- [[PF-080]] An effective instrument can embody others' knowledge without transferring their contribution to its user.
- [[PF-083]] A prompt must earn epistemic-object status rather than receive it by metaphor.
- [[PF-090]] The prompt may be an epistemic instrument precisely where it cannot be reduced to either prose or output.
