ZETTEL

ID: PF-072

TITLE:
A committee can inspect contribution roles without requiring human production of every word.

SOURCE:
NISO — CRediT Role Descriptors — n.d.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] CRediT permits explicit attribution of conceptualization, methodology, validation, writing, software, supervision, and other research work.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The dissertation defense could shift from word-origin purity toward demonstrable contribution architecture.

LOCAL MOVE:
The taxonomy provides a vocabulary for asking what the researcher actually did.

SOURCE TERMS:
conceptualization; methodology; validation; writing; software.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A dissertation committee already evaluates several kinds of work that are not sentence production.

QUESTION:
Which contribution roles must remain substantially the doctoral candidate's for a prompt-forward dissertation to qualify as their research?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can degree requirements be stated as nondelegable contribution roles?

MECHANISM:
<research workflow>
→ <role decomposition>
→ [committee evaluates candidate's roles]
→ <degree judgment>

FORMAL SHIFT:
NONE

SOURCE FORMALISM:
CRediT role taxonomy.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
NONDELEGABLE PHD ROLES = ?.

TENSION:
The answer is institution-specific and may include original writing itself.

MISSING:
Graduate-school rules specifying nondelegable intellectual work.

BOUNDARY:
CRediT does not define doctoral requirements.

CITATION TRAIL:
Georgia Tech dissertation policies; graduate-school authorship rules.

TEST:
Translate current doctoral evaluation criteria into contribution roles and identify which ones AI could or could not mediate.
