ZETTEL

ID: PF-013

TITLE:
The final text cannot distinguish workflows that leave the same surface artifact.

SOURCE:
Zhu et al. — Humanly: A Configurable and Traceable Environment for Human-AI Collaborative Writing — 2026.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] Humanly begins from the premise that the finished text alone cannot reveal how it was produced and records writing and AI-assistance activity to produce a traceable certificate.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Production mode is underdetermined by product.

LOCAL MOVE:
The system moves evidence from textual detection to process capture.

SOURCE TERMS:
traceable; human-AI collaborative writing; writing process; certificate.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The difference between a three-word request followed by wholesale acceptance and a long iterative process may exist almost entirely outside the final prose.

QUESTION:
Which process variables are sufficient to distinguish consequentially different AI-assisted workflows?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can a trace reveal intellectual control, or only observable interaction?

MECHANISM:
<writing process>
→ <captured events>
→ [summarize/attest]
→ <process evidence>

FORMAL SHIFT:
NONE

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Trace capture and certificate.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
PRODUCT + TRACE > PRODUCT for process classification.

TENSION:
The more evidence a system captures, the more privacy, gaming, and surveillance problems it may introduce.

MISSING:
Validation that trace features correspond to intellectual contribution rather than mere activity.

BOUNDARY:
Humanly does not settle legal or institutional authorship.

CITATION TRAIL:
HaLLMark; DraftMarks; process mining.

TEST:
Generate identical final texts using controlled workflows with radically different human involvement and test which trace features discriminate them.
