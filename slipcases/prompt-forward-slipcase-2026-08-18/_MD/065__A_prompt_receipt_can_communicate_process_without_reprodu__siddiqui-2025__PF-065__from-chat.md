ZETTEL

ID: PF-065

TITLE:
A prompt receipt can communicate process without reproducing the entire process.

SOURCE:
Siddiqui et al. — DraftMarks — 2025.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] DraftMarks transforms detailed human-AI interaction data into visual process traces such as revision intensity and AI-origin markings.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Process evidence can be compressed into legible representations for readers.

LOCAL MOVE:
The system treats trace communication as a design problem.

SOURCE TERMS:
process traces; human-AI co-writing; transparency; revision.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A receipt does not have to be a transcript.

QUESTION:
What representation gives a dissertation committee enough process evidence without drowning it in chat logs?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Which compression choices create misleading impressions of agency or effort?

MECHANISM:
<interaction events>
→ <derived metrics/marks>
→ [visualize]
→ <reader inference>

FORMAL SHIFT:
NONE

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Skeuomorphic process-trace visualization.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
RECEIPT = selective visualization of contribution-relevant provenance.

TENSION:
Any visualization encodes a theory of what matters.

MISSING:
Committee-specific evidence requirements.

BOUNDARY:
DraftMarks does not validate its traces as misconduct evidence.

CITATION TRAIL:
HaLLMark; Humanly; provenance visualization.

TEST:
Give committees raw logs, summary disclosure, and visual prompt receipts for the same workflows; compare accuracy and confidence.
