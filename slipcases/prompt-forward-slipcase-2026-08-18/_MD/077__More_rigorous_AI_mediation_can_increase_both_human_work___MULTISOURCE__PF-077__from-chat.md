TITLE:
More rigorous AI mediation can increase both human work and measured AI significance.

SOURCE:
Xie et al. — Measuring Human Contribution in AI-Assisted Content Generation — 2026; Georgia Institute of Technology — Interim AI Policy — 2026.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] Human contribution varies across AI-assisted workflows, while Georgia Tech treats magnitude/significance of AI use as relevant to disclosure.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
High AI significance and high human contribution can coexist.

LOCAL MOVE:
The two sources make a two-axis model possible.

SOURCE TERMS:
human contribution; magnitude; significance; disclosure.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A researcher who builds elaborate scaffolding may legitimately have “more AI involvement” and “more human contribution” simultaneously.

QUESTION:
Do current disclosure practices mistakenly treat those axes as inversely related?

DEEPER QUESTION:
What cases most clearly break the zero-sum intuition?
