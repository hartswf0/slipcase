ID: PF-050

TITLE:
Contribution thresholds should attach to roles before they attach to “AI use” globally.

SOURCE:
NISO — CRediT Role Descriptors — n.d.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] CRediT distinguishes multiple kinds of contribution rather than measuring one undifferentiated amount of research participation.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
A prompt can contribute differently depending on whether it performs conceptualization, methodology, software, validation, or writing.

LOCAL MOVE:
The taxonomy makes role the primary descriptor.

SOURCE TERMS:
roles; conceptualization; methodology; software; validation; writing.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
“Substantive AI use” may conflate substantive assistance in radically different research roles.

QUESTION:
Should disclosure name where AI entered the contribution topology before estimating magnitude?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Would “AI-assisted methodology” and “AI-assisted prose drafting” trigger different concerns even at equal magnitude?

TENSION:
Adding role specificity increases reporting complexity.

MISSING:
AI extensions to contributor-role vocabularies.

BOUNDARY:
CRediT describes human contributions, not AI disclosure.

CITATION TRAIL:
Machine-readable contribution statements; AI policy schemas.

TEST:
Compare evaluator judgments for identical “30% AI involvement” scenarios placed in different research roles.
