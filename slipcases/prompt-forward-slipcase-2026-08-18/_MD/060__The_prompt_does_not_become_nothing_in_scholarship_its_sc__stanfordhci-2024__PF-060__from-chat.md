ZETTEL

ID: PF-060

TITLE:
The prompt does not become “nothing” in scholarship; its scholarly status is simply unstable.

SOURCE:
StanfordHCI — genagents repository — 2024.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] The project stores all LLM prompts in a named prompt-template directory within its simulation engine.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
At least some academic projects already preserve prompts as inspectable research-software artifacts.

LOCAL MOVE:
The repository provides a counterexample to the absolute claim that prompts vanish from the scholarly record.

SOURCE TERMS:
prompt_template; simulation_engine; repository.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The stronger problem is not absence but inconsistency of recognition across scholarly genres.

QUESTION:
When do academic venues require, reward, ignore, or suppress prompt artifacts?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is “the scholarly record” itself too singular a category?

TENSION:
“In the scholarly record, it becomes nothing” is refuted by prompt-containing academic repositories.

MISSING:
Systematic survey across publication genres.

BOUNDARY:
One repository cannot establish broad academic practice.

CITATION TRAIL:
Prompt appendices; reproducibility checklists; model cards.

TEST:
Sample 200 LLM papers and record whether prompts appear in article, appendix, code, dataset, or nowhere.
