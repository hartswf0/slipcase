TITLE:
A clean provenance record may be evidence of optimization for inspection.

SOURCE:
Espeland and Sauder — Rankings and Reactivity — 2007.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] Public measures can induce actors to reorganize practices around what the measure recognizes.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Staged transparency is a possible second-order effect of audit systems.

LOCAL MOVE:
The source suggests that a record should be analyzed not only for what it contains but for the incentives under which it was produced.

SOURCE TERMS:
reactivity; commensuration; public measures.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
“Good provenance” may become a performance target.

QUESTION:
What makes a provenance trace credible once producing a favorable trace itself has institutional value?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can audit systems authenticate process without incentivizing process theater?

MECHANISM:

MISSING:

TEST:
