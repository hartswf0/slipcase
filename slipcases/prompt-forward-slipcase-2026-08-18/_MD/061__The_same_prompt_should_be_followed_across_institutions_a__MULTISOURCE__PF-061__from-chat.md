ID: PF-061

TITLE:
The same prompt should be followed across institutions as a comparative object, not presumed to expose contradiction.

SOURCE:
Beurer-Kellner, Fischer, and Vechev — Prompting Is Programming — 2023; Georgia Institute of Technology — Interim AI Policy — 2026.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] LMQL operationalizes prompt-based programs; Georgia Tech regulates AI use through disclosure and responsibility criteria.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
A single artifact can be subjected to different institutional questions without those questions necessarily being inconsistent.

LOCAL MOVE:
The collision becomes a comparative research design.

SOURCE TERMS:
programming; constraints; disclosure; significance.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The real object may be the translation of one artifact between regimes.

QUESTION:
What exactly changes when a prompt moves from software artifact to dissertation method to compliance evidence?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Which properties survive the translation, and which are created by the receiving institution?

TENSION:

MISSING:

BOUNDARY:
