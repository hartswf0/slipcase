ID: PF-057

TITLE:
Expressive arrangement and process orchestration are different kinds of control.

SOURCE:
U.S. Copyright Office — Copyright and Artificial Intelligence, Part 2 — 2025.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] The Office recognizes human authorship in sufficiently creative selection, coordination, arrangement, or modification of generated material.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
One can control the expressive arrangement of outputs without having controlled their generation.

LOCAL MOVE:
The report locates authorship in post-generation composition under some conditions.

SOURCE TERMS:
selection; coordination; arrangement; modification.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
“Orchestration” contains at least two operations: coordinating the production process and arranging the expressive result.

QUESTION:
Which form is doing the work in the director/conductor analogy?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can process orchestration be scholarly method while expressive orchestration is authorship?

MECHANISM:
<generation process>
→ <process coordination>

→ <work>

FORMAL SHIFT:

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
ORCHESTRATION_PROCESS ≠ ORCHESTRATION_EXPRESSION.

TENSION:
The two forms often occur together in prompt-forward work.

MISSING:
Separate provenance markers.

BOUNDARY:
The copyright report speaks to expressive authorship, not research-method credit.

CITATION TRAIL:
CRediT supervision/methodology; film directing; editorial authorship.

TEST:
Create cases varying process control and expressive arrangement independently and ask evaluators to assign contribution roles.
