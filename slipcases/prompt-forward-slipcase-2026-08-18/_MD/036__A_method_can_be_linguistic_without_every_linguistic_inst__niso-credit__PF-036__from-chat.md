ID: PF-036

TITLE:
A method can be linguistic without every linguistic instruction becoming method.

SOURCE:
NISO — CRediT Role Descriptors — n.d. — Methodology.

PASSAGE:
[PARAPHRASE] CRediT defines methodology as development or design of methodology and creation of models.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Research method is identified by function in inquiry, not by whether its carrier is code, prose, or material apparatus.

LOCAL MOVE:
The taxonomy recognizes method-design as distinct scholarly contribution.

SOURCE TERMS:
methodology; development; design; models.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The claim “research instruments can be words” is too broad until a procedure identifies which words systematically organize inquiry.

QUESTION:
What operational properties make a prompt methodological?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Must a methodological prompt be reusable, explicit, discriminating, and linked to an epistemic objective?

MECHANISM:
<instruction>
→ <repeatable inquiry procedure>

FORMAL SHIFT:
NONE

SOURCE FORMALISM:
CRediT role definition.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
PROMPT-AS-METHOD requires relation to evidence-producing procedure.

TENSION:
Many prompts assist writing without functioning as methods.

MISSING:
Admission criteria separating method prompts from convenience prompts.

BOUNDARY:
CRediT does not recognize prompts specifically.

CITATION TRAIL:
Interview-guide methodology; computational notebooks; experimental protocols.

TEST:
Blindly classify a mixed set of prompts as writing assistance or methodology using explicit criteria, then assess agreement.
