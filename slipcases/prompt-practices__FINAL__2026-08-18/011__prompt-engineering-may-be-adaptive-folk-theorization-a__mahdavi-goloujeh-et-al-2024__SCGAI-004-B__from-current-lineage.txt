ID:
SCGAI-004-B

TITLE:
Prompt engineering may be adaptive folk theorization: a successful rule can become false while its words remain unchanged.

SOURCE:
Michael Ann DeVito — “Adaptive Folk Theorization as a Path to Algorithmic Literacy on Changing Platforms” — Proceedings of the ACM on Human-Computer Interaction 5(CSCW2), Article 339 — 2021 — https://doi.org/10.1145/3476080

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE] “Because platforms constantly change, users must constantly re-evaluate their folk theories.”

RESEARCH OBJECT:
[OUR INFERENCE] Prompt communities can be understood as adaptive folk theorists of opaque changing systems. Their product is a provisional causal belief: X seems to make the machine do Y—here, now, under these conditions.

LOCAL MOVE:
Replace PROMPT TIP AS STABLE TECHNIQUE with PROMPT RULE AS VERSION-DEPENDENT FOLK THEORY.

SOURCE TERMS:
folk theories; folk theorization; algorithmic literacy; platform change; causal powers; mechanistic fragments; experimentation

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A prompt rule can die without changing a character. Yesterday’s technique becomes today’s superstition when the platform changes beneath preserved words; prompt folklore can contain fossils of systems that no longer exist.

QUESTION:
How much contemporary prompt advice describes current behavior, and how much is fossilized knowledge from earlier model versions?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can there be stable prompt expertise when the object is opaque and repeatedly altered without preserving the conditions under which knowledge was learned?

MECHANISM:
system behavior/change → observation/foraging → folk theorization → adaptation → further observation → theory revision; for models: M_t → heuristic H_t → update M_(t+1) → anomaly → H_(t+1).

FORMAL SHIFT:
RULE X→Y becomes temporally indexed hypothesis P(Y|X,M_t,C_t) appears elevated.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
DeVito describes complexity from basic awareness to causal powers, mechanistic fragments, and mechanistic ordering, and discusses endogenous testing versus exogenous information.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
PROMPT-FOSSIL = a circulating prompting rule whose originating system conditions no longer obtain but whose linguistic form continues to circulate.

TENSION:
DeVito studies algorithmically driven social platforms, not generative models; the transfer must be tested rather than assumed.

MISSING:
Version-aware histories of prompt advice linking a rule to the exact model/configuration under which it was learned.

BOUNDARY:
“Prompt fossil” and application to generative AI are [OUR INFERENCE], not DeVito’s terminology.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[SCGAI-004]] → community experiments without defining literature → DeVito 2021 → folk theories under platform change → distinguish experimental knowledge from ritual.

TEST:
Re-run falsifiable old prompt rules across archived/current versions and classify persistent, version-bound, reversed, absent, or untestable effects.

PLATFORM:
Opaque and changing algorithmic platforms / generative AI systems
