FINAL PAPER
TITLE: When Failure Is Evidence
SUBTITLE: Separating Instruction from Experiment in Generative AI Prompting
AUTHOR: Watson Hartsoe
STATUS: Final Working Paper, 18 August 2026

CURRENT WAGER
Some prompts seek artifacts; others seek information about the system. A generation can therefore fail as a product while succeeding as evidence. Prompt literacy under opaque, changing generative systems includes experimental literacy: deliberately varying inputs, comparing outcomes, preserving conditions, and revising causal beliefs.
