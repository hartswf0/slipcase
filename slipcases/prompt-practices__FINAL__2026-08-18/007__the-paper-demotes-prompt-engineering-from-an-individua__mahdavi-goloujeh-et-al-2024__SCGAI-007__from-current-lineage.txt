ZETTEL

ID:
SCGAI-007

TITLE:
The paper demotes prompt engineering from an individual technical skill to a socio-technical practice.

SOURCE:
Atefeh Mahdavi Goloujeh, Anne Sullivan, and Brian Magerko — “The Social Construction of Generative AI Prompts” — CHI EA ’24 — 2024 — https://doi.org/10.1145/3613905.3650947

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE] The authors argue for “repositioning prompt engineering not merely as a technical skill defined by efficacy standards in an individual’s mind, but rather as a socio-technical practice.”

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Effectiveness against a model becomes only one dimension of a practice also organized by ownership, learning, collaboration, originality, circulation, and influence.

LOCAL MOVE:
Expand evaluation beyond OUTPUT EFFICACY.

SOURCE TERMS:
technical skill; efficacy standards; socio-technical practice; distributed cognition; distributed creativity; social practice; originality; collaboration

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A prompt can be technically effective while socially illegitimate, derivative, unshareable, pedagogically useless, or corrosive to the community that produced its knowledge.

QUESTION:
What counts as prompt quality once technical efficacy is no longer the sole criterion?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does “prompt engineering” remain coherent if competence includes social learning, attribution, community participation, norm navigation, and collective experimentation?

MECHANISM:
Prompt practice is shaped by model-facing efficacy constraints and community-facing norms, motivations, knowledge flows, and ownership judgments.

FORMAL SHIFT:
QUALITY(prompt)=MODEL PERFORMANCE becomes QUALITY(prompt practice)=MODEL PERFORMANCE + SOCIAL RELATIONS + KNOWLEDGE EFFECTS + NORMATIVE CONSEQUENCES.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
The authors connect findings to distributed cognition, distributed creativity, and technologies-as-social-practice, organizing participation through four context-dependent orientations.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Q(p)={efficacy, learnability, provenance, shareability, adaptability, originality, contribution_to_collective_knowledge}

TENSION:
Calling prompting socio-technical may deepen analysis but can stretch the object until nearly every surrounding cultural activity is included.

MISSING:
A criterion for deciding which social relations belong inside prompting and which merely constitute context.

BOUNDARY:
The paper argues for reframing prompt engineering but does not provide a complete ontological boundary.

CITATION TRAIL:
[[SCGAI-2024]] → socio-technical practice → Hutchins distributed cognition → Glăveanu distributed creativity → Suchman social practice → compare units of analysis.

TEST:
Forage Hutchins, Glăveanu, and Suchman separately and identify the unit that performs cognition, creativity, or practice.
