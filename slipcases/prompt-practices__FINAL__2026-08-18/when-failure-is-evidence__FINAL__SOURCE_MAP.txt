WHEN FAILURE IS EVIDENCE — CLAIM–SOURCE MAP
Working Paper · 2026-08-17
Author: Watson Hartsoe

STATUS
Derived scholarly interpretation. Not evidence.

MELTED INTERPRETATION
The prior paper, “Prompt Receipts,” foregrounded archival and zettel machinery. That interpretation is preserved but no longer governs the public argument. The new paper follows the stronger crossing: epistemic action × collaborative prompting × folk theorization × model version drift × evaluation practice.

CLAIM 01
CLAIM: Prompt executions can serve different primary functions: obtaining an artifact or obtaining information about system behavior.
TYPE: COMPILER-SYNTHESIS
ZETTEL: SCGAI-004-A
SOURCE: Kirsh & Maglio 1994
CITEKEY: kirsh1994distinguishing
SUPPORT: Source distinguishes pragmatic from epistemic action and shows actions can improve informational state without directly improving task state.
INFERENCE: Apply the functional distinction to generative prompting.

CLAIM 02
CLAIM: A generation can be pragmatically poor yet epistemically useful.
TYPE: COMPILER-SYNTHESIS
ZETTEL: SCGAI-004-A
SOURCE: Kirsh & Maglio 1994
CITEKEY: kirsh1994distinguishing
SUPPORT: Epistemic actions may appear physically disadvantageous while improving cognition.
INFERENCE: An undesirable model output can still discriminate among prompt hypotheses.

CLAIM 03
CLAIM: Prompt communities sometimes act as informal experimental institutions.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED + INTERPRETATION
ZETTEL: SCGAI-004
SOURCE: Mahdavi Goloujeh, Sullivan & Magerko 2024
CITEKEY: mahdavigoloujeh2024social
SUPPORT: Participants report collective experiments, iterative prompt modification, peer learning, and group exploration where defining literature was absent.
INFERENCE: These practices constitute distributed empirical inquiry, though not necessarily controlled experimentation.

CLAIM 04
CLAIM: Non-experts often explore prompt designs opportunistically rather than systematically.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED
SOURCE: Zamfirescu-Pereira et al. 2023
CITEKEY: zamfirescu2023johnny
SUPPORT: The study reports opportunistic exploration, overgeneralization, and barriers arising from human-to-human instructional expectations.

CLAIM 05
CLAIM: Prompt knowledge can behave like adaptive folk theory in opaque changing systems.
TYPE: COMPILER-SYNTHESIS
ZETTEL: SCGAI-004-B
SOURCE: DeVito 2021
CITEKEY: devito2021adaptive
SUPPORT: Folk theories guide action under opaque algorithms; platform change requires repeated re-evaluation; structural theorization can enable experimentation and adaptation.
INFERENCE: Prompt heuristics can be treated as provisional causal beliefs rather than timeless rules.

CLAIM 06
CLAIM: Model-version changes can invalidate otherwise unchanged prompt rules.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED + INTERPRETATION
ZETTEL: WWP-20260817-09
SOURCE: Midjourney Version documentation, accessed 2026-08-17
CITEKEY: midjourney2026version
SUPPORT: V8.1 and V8.2 had different default periods and documented behavior differences.
INFERENCE: A stable string does not guarantee stable operational semantics.

CLAIM 07
CLAIM: Current production guidance increasingly formalizes experimental discipline through snapshots and evaluation suites.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED
SOURCE: OpenAI Prompt Engineering + Working with Evals, accessed 2026-08-17
CITEKEYS: openai2026promptengineering; openai2026evals
SUPPORT: Documentation recommends model snapshots and tests/evals when iterating prompts or upgrading models.

CLAIM 08
CLAIM: Automated prompt generation shifts human work from wording toward task specification and evaluation.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED + INTERPRETATION
ZETTEL: WWP-20260817-06
SOURCE: OpenAI Prompt Generation, accessed 2026-08-17
CITEKEY: openai2026promptgeneration
SUPPORT: Playground uses meta-prompts to generate/improve prompts from task descriptions.
INFERENCE: Durable expertise may move toward defining goals, examples, and criteria.

CLAIM 09
CLAIM: Generative control can be specified through iterative selection rather than language alone.
TYPE: SOURCE-SUPPORTED + INTERPRETATION
ZETTEL: WWP-20260817-05
SOURCE: Midjourney Style Creator, accessed 2026-08-17
CITEKEY: midjourney2026stylecreator
SUPPORT: Style Creator learns from selected and unselected sample images; the text prompt generates previews but does not guide the Style Creator itself.
INFERENCE: The deeper common structure is intervention → observation → discrimination → update, not wording alone.

OPEN TESTS
1. Classify real prompt executions by artifact-seeking, information-seeking, or mixed intent.
2. Compare expert and novice intervention quality: number of variables changed, reuse of controls, retention of negative results, and version awareness.
3. Re-run historical prompt rules across model versions to separate persistent effects from prompt fossils.
4. Test interfaces that make branching comparison and condition visibility cheap.

BOUNDARIES
- Kirsh & Maglio do not study generative AI.
- DeVito does not study generative models.
- Mahdavi Goloujeh et al. study 19 U.S.-based MidJourney users.
- Vendor documentation establishes present platform mechanisms, not general laws of all generative systems.
