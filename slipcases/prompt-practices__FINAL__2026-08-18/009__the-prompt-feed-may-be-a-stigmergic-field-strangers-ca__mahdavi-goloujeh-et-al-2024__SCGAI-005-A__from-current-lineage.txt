SCGAI-005-A

TITLE:
The prompt feed may be a stigmergic field: strangers can coordinate through traces without ever collaborating directly.

SOURCE:
Francis Heylighen — “Stigmergy as a Universal Coordination Mechanism I: Definition and Components” — Cognitive Systems Research 38 — 2016 — pp. 4–13 — https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cogsys.2015.12.002

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE] “the trace left by an action in a medium stimulates subsequent actions.” (p. 4)

RESEARCH OBJECT:
[OUR INFERENCE] Public prompt-plus-image artifacts can be investigated as environmental modifications: traces in a medium that stimulate later action without requiring direct communication, shared plans, or mutual awareness.

LOCAL MOVE:
Replace PROMPT SHARING AS HUMAN-TO-HUMAN COMMUNICATION with PROMPT POSTING AS ENVIRONMENTAL TRACE-MAKING.

SOURCE TERMS:
stigmergy; action; medium; trace; coordination; subsequent action; feedback; self-organization

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A prompt feed may not merely display a community; it may be part of the mechanism that produces one. Prompt culture can organize more like a trail system than a conversation.

QUESTION:
Can observable prompt convergence arise through environmental traces even when direct interpersonal communication is absent?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is the relevant unit people + traces + feed architecture + model outputs rather than “community” alone?

MECHANISM:
ACTION → TRACE IN MEDIUM → TRACE STIMULATES SUBSEQUENT ACTION → NEW TRACE → FEEDBACK → COORDINATED PATTERN.

FORMAL SHIFT:
SOCIAL INFLUENCE USER_A → USER_B becomes STIGMERGIC INFLUENCE USER_A → ENVIRONMENTAL TRACE → USER_B → NEW TRACE → USER_C.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Heylighen defines action, agent, medium, trace, and coordination; stigmergic feedback can coordinate without central control or direct communication.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION — NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
POST → visible trace → encounter → local salience → reuse/mutation → new post → amplification/suppression → emergent convention.

TENSION:
Mahdavi Goloujeh et al. foreground motivations and orientations; stigmergy makes intention less causally central. Prompt culture may be intentional exchange or partially self-organizing trace ecology.

MISSING:
Longitudinal exposure data distinguishing interpersonal transmission from indirect trace-mediated propagation and identifying which feed affordances matter.

BOUNDARY:
Heylighen does not study generative-AI prompting. The analogy is [OUR INFERENCE] unless action → persistent trace → stimulated action is demonstrated.

TEST:
Track distinctive fragments with author IDs, replies, follows, and exposure; test whether trace exposure predicts reuse after controlling direct social ties.
